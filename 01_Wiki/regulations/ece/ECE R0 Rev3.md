# UN Regulation No. 0 (Revision 3) - International Whole Vehicle Type Approval (IWVTA)

## 1. Scope
1.1. This Regulation applies to vehicles of category M1. It specifies requirements for the type approval of a whole vehicle.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **Manufacturer** and **Manufacturer's representative**
- **IWVTA class**: A group of vehicles which does not differ in essential respects.
- **IWVTA type**: A group of vehicles which can be approved within one IWVTA. Belongs to the same IWVTA class and has the same level of conformity to Annex 4 requirements.
- **International Whole Vehicle Type Approval (IWVTA)**: Approval of an IWVTA type to this Regulation.
- **Universal IWVTA (U-IWVTA)**: An IWVTA where all applicable UN Regulations listed in Annex 4, Part A, Section I are complied with according to the specified or later version.
- **IWVTA of Limited recognition (L-IWVTA)**: An IWVTA where not all listed UN Regulations are complied with, or are complied with according to an earlier version.
- **Information document, Information folder, Information package**
- **Declaration of Conformance (DoC)**: Information for a single vehicle certifying to which UN Regulations and versions the IWVTA type is approved at production.
- **Component** and **Vehicle system**
- **Type approval certificate**

## 3. Application for Approval
3.1. Submitted by the manufacturer to the approval authority.
3.2. Required documentation includes the information folder (Annex 5) and relevant type approval certificates.
3.3. Documentation shall be in English, with translation available upon request.
3.4. Representative vehicle(s) must be submitted for inspection.
3.5. Manufacturer assigns a unique type designation to each IWVTA type.
3.6. All variants and versions within one IWVTA type are covered by a single application.
3.7. Applications for all IWVTA types within one IWVTA class must be filed with the same approval authority.

## 4. Approval
4.1. Follows procedures in Annex 3.
4.2. Granted if the IWVTA type meets relevant provisions.
4.3. A type approval number is assigned per Annex 8.
4.4. Notice of approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal is communicated via a secure internet database (or transitional paper/electronic means).
4.5. A type approval marking must be affixed to every conforming vehicle, per Annex 2.
4.6. The approval mark cannot be replaced by a Unique Identifier (UI).
4.7. Vehicle systems referenced in an IWVTA need no separate approval marking. Components must be marked as per their respective UN Regulations.
4.8. Marking must be legible and indelible.
4.9. Should be placed on or near the vehicle data plate if fitted.
4.10. U-IWVTA uses Annex 2, Section I template; L-IWVTA uses Section II.
4.11. All variants/versions within one IWVTA type are approved within one IWVTA.
4.12. Approvals for all IWVTA types within one IWVTA class are handled by the same authority.
4.13. A new IWVTA class is created upon the first approval of an IWVTA type within it.

## 5. Specifications
5.1. **Required Certificates**: An IWVTA type must meet the requirements of this Regulation and those in Annex 4, Part A, Section I, demonstrated by type approval certificates.
    - 5.1.2. For U-IWVTA: Certificates for all applicable UN Regulations per the listed or later version.
    - 5.1.3. For L-IWVTA: One or more certificates per 5.1.2. may be omitted or be for an earlier version.
5.2. **Declaration of Conformance (DoC)**: Manufacturers must provide/upload information to generate a DoC for each vehicle in the UN secure internet database (applicable from a future WP.29 specified date).

## 6. Test Procedure
6.1. If compliance is demonstrated via required certificates covering all variants/versions, no further testing is required for those items.

## 7. Modification of IWVTA Type and Modification of Approval
7.1. Changes follow the procedure in Schedule 3 of the 1958 Agreement.
7.2. An existing IWVTA can only be modified if: vehicles belong to the same IWVTA class; the status (U/L) does not change; and after modification, all vehicles comply with the same version of each included UN Regulation.
7.3. If conditions are not met, a new IWVTA must be issued or the existing one split.
7.4. Procedures for when new requirements enter into force (via amendments to Annex 4): Manufacturer notifies the approval authority with options (type not affected, maintain U-IWVTA via extension, replace with L-IWVTA, or split the approval).
7.5. Rules for transitioning a L-IWVTA to a U-IWVTA.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Complies with Schedule 1 of the 1958 Agreement. The vehicle must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. Verification focuses on whole vehicle and assembly level.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
9.1. Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or a marked vehicle does not conform.
9.2. Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the granting authority, which then notifies other Contracting Parties.

## 11. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate the designated Technical Services and approval authorities.

## 12. Introductory and Transitional Provisions
12.1. From 9 months after entry into force, Contracting Parties shall accept an IWVTA and not request separate approvals for UN Regulations certified by it.
12.2. Transitional provisions of UN Regulations in Annex 4 (and later versions) must be respected when granting approval.
12.3. From the entry into force of the 01 series of amendments, no Contracting Party shall refuse to grant or accept IWVTA under this amended Regulation.
12.4. From 1 September 2019, Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept IWVTA to preceding series first issued after that date.

## 13. Special Provisions for Contracting Parties Applying this Regulation
13.1. Contracting Parties shall accept a U-IWVTA as evidence of compliance for all approved systems/equipment/parts, regardless of whether they apply the individual UN Regulations. National requirements for items not covered by Annex 4 still apply.
13.2. Contracting Parties may issue IWVTA subject to Article 2 of the 1958 Agreement.
13.3. For granting an IWVTA, Contracting Parties shall accept type approvals issued according to UN Regulations in Annex 4, Part A, Section I. For U-IWVTA, they accept approvals per the listed version or any later version submitted.
13.4. For placing equipment/spare parts on the market for IWVTA-accepted vehicles, Contracting Parties shall accept type approvals to the listed UN Regulations as evidence of compliance, unless they notify non-acceptance for specific UN Regulations.
13.5. Contracting Parties may accept L-IWVTA by notifying the UN Regulations and versions they will accept. They shall then accept L-IWVTA that includes at least the type approvals matching their notification.

## Annexes Overview
- **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval notifications.
- **Annex 2**: Templates for type approval marking (U-IWVTA and L-IWVTA).
- **Annex 3**: Procedures for the IWVTA process (verification, inspections).
- **Annex 4**: **List of requirements for U-IWVTA (Category M1 vehicles)**. Contains a table listing 57 UN Regulations (e.g., R35, R43, R13-H, R94, R95, R100, R137) with their required series of amendments. Notes indicate when requirements apply only if a system is fitted (*).
- **Annex 5**: Information document template for application (general, construction, masses, power plant, transmission, etc.).
- **Annex 6**: Specifications for the Declaration of Conformance (DoC) process and appendices (declaration form, conformance list, translation sheet).
- **Annex 7**: Defines IWVTA class, type, variant, and version based on manufacturer, body structure, level of conformity, power plant, axles, mass, engine capacity, etc.
- **Annex 8**: Format for the IWVTA type approval number (e.g., `E4*0R03/U*0025/01*02`).
- **Annex 9**: Notification form for when new requirements enter into force for an existing U-IWVTA.
