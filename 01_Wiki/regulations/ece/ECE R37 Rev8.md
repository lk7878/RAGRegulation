# UN Regulation No. 37 (Revision 8) – Filament Lamps for Vehicles

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to filament lamps shown in Annex 1 and intended for use in approved lamp units of power-driven vehicles and of their trailers.

## 2. Administrative Provisions

### 2.1. Definitions
- **Category**: Describes different basic designs of standardized filament lamps (e.g., "H4", "P21W").
- **Type**: Filament lamps within the same category differing in trade name/mark, bulb/cap design affecting optical results, rated voltage, or halogen type. A selective-yellow bulb/outer bulb solely for colour change does not constitute a new type.

### 2.2. Application for Approval
- Submitted by the trade name/mark owner or accredited representative.
- Must include drawings, technical description, and five samples of each colour applied for.
- Simplified submission for types differing only by trade name/mark from an already approved type.

### 2.3. Inscriptions
Filament lamps must bear on the cap or bulb:
- Applicant's trade name/mark.
- Rated voltage (exceptions for small bulbs).
- International designation of the relevant category.
- Rated wattage.
- Space for the approval mark.
- Halogen lamps meeting UV requirements marked with a "U".

### 2.4. Approval
- Granted if samples meet requirements.
- An approval code is assigned, indicating the amendment series and a unique identifier.
- An international approval mark (truncated circle with "E" and country number, plus approval code) must be affixed.
- Marks must be legible and indelible.

## 3. Technical Requirements

### 3.1. Definitions
Includes terms: Rated voltage/wattage, Test voltage, Objective values, Standard (étalon) filament lamp, Reference luminous flux, Measuring luminous flux, Reference axis/plane, Filament light source.

### 3.2. General Specifications
- Samples must conform to the Regulation.
- Must be designed for normal use and free of faults.

### 3.3. Manufacture
- Bulbs must be free of scores/spots impairing performance.
- Must have standard caps per IEC 60061.
- Caps must be strong and firmly secured.

### 3.4. Tests
- Filament lamps must be aged at test voltage.
- Coated bulbs must withstand a wipe test.
- Filament position/dimensions measured at 90-100% of test voltage.
- Electrical/photometric measurements at test voltage(s).
- Luminous flux values specified for white light; selective-yellow must be ≥85% of white light flux.

### 3.5. Filament Position and Dimensions
- Geometric shapes as per Annex 1 data sheets.
- Line filament position/shape checked per data sheets.
- Luminous centre determination per Annex 4 for point filaments.
- Filament length definition and measurement methods specified.

### 3.6. Colour
- Emitted light colour is white unless otherwise specified.
- Colour definitions from UN Regulation No. 48 apply.
- Colour measured per Annex 5; values must lie within tolerance area. For white light, deviation from Planckian locus ≤0.020 in x/y.

### 3.7. UV Radiation
Halogen lamps must meet specified UV radiation limits (k1 ≤ 2×10⁻⁴ W/lm, k2 ≤ 2×10⁻⁶ W/lm).

### 3.8. Observation Concerning Selective-Yellow Colour
Approval may be granted for both white and selective-yellow light versions. Contracting Parties may prohibit either on their registered vehicles.

### 3.9. Check on Optical Quality
(Applies only to categories R2, H4, HS1).
- Tested in a standard headlamp to verify passing-beam light distribution compliance.
- Specific requirements for 12V, 6V, 24V lamps and selective-yellow lamps.

### 3.10. Standard Filament Lamps
- Additional requirements in Annex 1 data sheets.
- Bulbs of white light standard lamps must not alter CIE coordinates of a 2,856 K source by more than 0.010 in x/y.
- For amber/red standard lamps, bulb temperature changes must not affect luminous flux impairing photometric measurements.

## 4. Conformity of Production
- Approved lamps must conform to the approved type.
- Manufacturer must implement quality control procedures, have necessary test equipment, record results, and analyze test data.
- At least the tests prescribed in Annex 6 must be carried out.
- Type Approval Authority may verify control methods and conduct spot checks (criteria in Annexes 8 & 9). Normal inspection frequency is biennial.

## 5. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or if a lamp bearing the mark does not conform. Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 6. Production Definitively Discontinued
If manufacture ceases, the approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority, which notifies other Parties.

## 7. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## 8. Transitional Provisions
- Approvals under preceding amendments remain valid, but current production must comply with the latest amendments 12 months from their application date.
- Table of correspondence between old and new designations (e.g., P25-1 to P21W).
- From specified dates (linked to Supplement entries into force), certain lamp categories (listed in Annex 1, Group 3) may only be used for replacement parts in vehicles already in use.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Sheets for filament lamps. Contains detailed dimensional drawings, tolerances, cap types, and electrical/photometric characteristics for numerous lamp categories, organized into three groups:
    - **Group 1**: Without general restrictions (e.g., H1, H4, H7, HB3).
    - **Group 2**: Only for use in signalling lamps, cornering lamps, reversing lamps and rear registration plate lamps (e.g., P21W, P21/5W, R5W, W5W).
    - **Group 3**: For replacement purposes only (see transitional provisions 8.3., 8.4.) (e.g., C5W, H1 (6V only), R2, T4W).
- **Annex 2**: Communication form.
- **Annex 3**: Example of the arrangement of the approval mark.
- **Annex 4**: Luminous centre and shapes of filament lamps.
- **Annex 5**: Checking the colour of filament lamps.
- **Annex 6**: Minimum requirements for quality control procedures by the manufacturer.
- **Annex 7**: Sampling and compliance levels for manufacturer test records.
- **Annex 8**: Minimum requirements for spot checks by the Type Approval Authority.
- **Annex 9**: Compliance approved by spot check.
