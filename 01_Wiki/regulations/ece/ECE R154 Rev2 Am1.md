# UN Regulation No. 154 (WLTP)

## 1. Introduction
The intention of this Regulation is to establish uniform provisions concerning the approval of motor vehicles with regard to the emissions of light-duty vehicles based on the new World harmonized Light vehicle Test Procedure (WLTP) included in UN GTR No. 15 and the updated Evaporative Emissions test procedure (Type 4 test) which has been developed in UN GTR No. 19. It will enable Contracting Parties (CPs) to issue and accept approvals based on these new type approval tests.

The WLTP Type 1 test replaces both the current Type 1 test in UN Regulation No. 83 and UN Regulation No. 101, whilst the updated Evaporative Emissions test procedure (Type 4 test) replaces that currently in UN Regulation No 83.

In addition, this new Regulation includes an update to the Type 5 test for verifying the durability of pollution control devices and updated On-Board Diagnostic (OBD) requirements. These updates are in order to reflect the changes from the previous NEDC based Type 1 test to the new WLTP Type 1 test.

The 02 series of this Regulation covers two sets of requirements – termed Level 1A and Level 1B. Level 1A is based on a four phase test cycle (Low, Medium, High and Extra-High), whilst Level 1B is based on a three phase test cycle (Low, Medium and High), with different type 1 limits applying to these different levels. The majority of the regulatory text is applicable to both Level 1A and Level 1B. Where the requirements are specific to either Level 1A or Level 1B the relevant sections are labelled accordingly. This series of amendments covers regional requirements and does not require mutual recognition by other Contracting Parties.

The 03 series of this Regulation includes a harmonised procedure which contains the most stringent procedures/limits which shall be subject to full mutual recognition. A type approval to the 03 series shall therefore be accepted by all CPs having adopted this Regulation.

## 3. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation, extensive definitions are provided covering:
*   **General terms:** Vehicle type with regard to emissions, engine capacity/displacement, approval of a vehicle.
*   **Test equipment:** Accuracy, precision, calibration, total hydrocarbons (THC), non-methane hydrocarbons (NMHC), response time.
*   **Road load and dynamometer setting:** Aerodynamic drag, mass in running order, reference mass, target road load, vehicle coastdown mode, etc.
*   **Vehicle types:** Pure electric vehicle (PEV), hybrid electric vehicle (HEV), off-vehicle charging hybrid electric vehicle (OVC-HEV), not off-vehicle charging hybrid electric vehicle (NOVC-HEV), fuel cell hybrid vehicle (FCHV), bi-fuel vehicle, flex fuel vehicle, mono-fuel vehicle.
*   **Powertrain:** Powertrain, auxiliary devices, peripheral devices, drivetrain.
*   **General:** Criteria emissions, cycle energy demand, defeat device, driver-selectable mode, predominant mode.
*   **PM/PN:** Particle number emissions (PN), particulate matter emissions (PM).
*   **WLTC:** Rated engine power, maximum speed.
*   **Evaporative emissions:** Fuel tank system, fuel system, butane working capacity (BWC), permeability factor (PF), sealed fuel tank system, evaporative emissions.
*   **On-Board Diagnostics (OBD):** OBD system, OBD family, malfunction indicator (MI), malfunction, OBD driving cycle, warm-up cycle, access, unrestricted, deficiency.
*   **Ambient Temperature Correction Test (Annex B6a):** Active heat storage device, insulation materials.

## 4. Application for Approval
*   The application shall be submitted by the manufacturer or authorized representative to the Type Approval Authority.
*   The application shall be drawn up according to the model information document in Annex A1.
*   Additional information required includes details on the OBD system, malfunction indicator, anti-tampering provisions, and, where applicable, particulars of the OBD family.
*   A vehicle representative of the type to be approved must be submitted to the Technical Service for testing.

## 5. Approval
*   If the vehicle type meets all requirements, approval shall be granted.
*   An approval number is assigned with a specific format: `E[CountryCode]*154R[Series]/[Supplement]/[Level]*[SeqNumber]*[Extension]` (e.g., E11*154R01/01/02*0123*01).
*   An international approval mark must be affixed to every conforming vehicle.
*   Additional requirements are specified for approval of flex fuel vehicles, mono-fuel/bi-fuel gas vehicles, OBD systems, and on-board fuel/energy consumption monitoring (OBFCM) devices.

## 6. Specifications and Tests
### 6.1. General Requirements
*   Vehicles must be designed to comply under normal use conditions throughout their useful life.
*   Prohibits the use of defeat devices, with specific exceptions.
*   Specifies requirements for fuel tank inlet orifices, prevention of evaporative emissions from missing caps, and electronic system security.

### 6.2. Test Procedure
*   **Table A** specifies the test requirements (Type 1, ATCT, Type 4, Type 5, OBD, OBFCM) applicable to different vehicle categories (M1, N1, N2) and fuel types (petrol, diesel, LPG, NG/biomethane, hydrogen, electric).
*   Defines family identifiers for various test families (Interpolation, Road Load, Evaporative, Durability, OBD, etc.).

### 6.3. Type 1 Test (WLTP)
*   Shall be performed according to the procedures in Annexes Part B (WLTC, gear selection, reference fuels, road load, test equipment, calculations).
*   Defines **Interpolation Families** for pure ICE vehicles, HEVs, PEVs, and FCHVs based on common powertrain/emission characteristics.
*   Defines **Road Load Families** and **Road Load Matrix Families**.
*   Defines **Periodically Regenerating Systems (Ki) Families**.
*   Defines **Gas Fuelled Vehicles (GFV) Families**.
*   Specifies additional test requirements for vehicles fuelled by LPG/NG/biomethane and flex-fuel vehicles.
*   **Emission Limits:** Provided in **Table 1A** (for 4-phase WLTP) and **Table 1B** (for 3-phase WLTP) for CO, THC, NMHC, NOx, THC+NOx, PM, and PN, differentiated by vehicle category (M1, N1 class I/II/III, N2) and fuel type (Positive Ignition / Compression Ignition).

### 6.6. Type 4 Test (Evaporative Emissions)
*   Shall be carried out on all vehicles with a petrol tank according to Annex C3.
*   **Emission Limit:** Evaporative emissions shall be less than **2.0 g/test**.
*   Defines **Evaporative Emission Families** based on fuel system characteristics.

### 6.7. Type 5 Test (Durability of Pollution Control Devices)
*   Represents an ageing test up to a target useful life of **160,000 km**, conducted per Annex C4.
*   Manufacturers may use assigned **deterioration factors** (multiplicative for Table 1A limits, additive for Table 1B limits) as an alternative to testing.
*   Defines **Durability Families** based on engine and pollution control system parameters.

### 6.8. On-Board Diagnostics (OBD) Test
*   Shall be carried out per Annex C5.
*   Defines **OBD Families** based on engine, emission control system, and OBD monitoring characteristics.
*   **OBD Thresholds:** Provided in **Table 4A** and **Table 4B** for CO, NMHC, NOx, and PM, which trigger the malfunction indicator.

### 6.9. Vehicles Using a Reagent for Exhaust After-Treatment
*   Must meet requirements in Appendix 6.
*   Defines **Exhaust after-treatment system using reagent (ER) Families**.

## 7. Modification and Extension of Type Approval
*   Describes the process for notifying modifications and obtaining extensions of approval.
*   Specifies conditions for extending approval for tailpipe emissions (Type 1), evaporative emissions (Type 4), durability (Type 5), and OBD without further testing, based on family definitions.

## 8. Conformity of Production (COP)
*   Every production vehicle must conform to the approved type.
*   The manufacturer must implement documented control plans and conduct necessary tests.
*   Defines **COP Families** for Type 1, Type 4, and OBD tests.
*   Specifies **test frequencies** for manufacturer verification and authority audits.
*   **Checking Conformity for Type 1 Test:** Tests are performed on a minimum of 3 randomly selected production vehicles from the COP family. The statistical method for pass/fail decision is described in Appendix 2 and Figure 8/1.
*   **Checking Conformity for Type 4 Test:** At least one vehicle per year from the COP family is tested.
*   Procedures for handling non-conformity are outlined.

## Appendices & Annexes (Referenced)
*   **Appendix 1:** Type 1 test CoP verification for specific vehicle types.
*   **Appendix 2:** Verification of conformity of production for Type 1 test - statistical method.
*   **Appendix 3:** Run-in test procedure to determine run-in factors.
*   **Appendix 4:** Conformity of production for Type 4 test.
*   **Appendix 5:** Devices for monitoring on board the vehicle the consumption of fuel and/or electric energy (OBFCM).
*   **Appendix 6:** Requirements for vehicles that use a reagent for the exhaust after-treatment system.
*   **Annexes Part A (Administrative):** A1 (Information document, test reports), A2 (Communication form), A3 (Approval mark).
*   **Annexes Part B (WLTP Test Procedures):** B1 (WLTC), B2 (Gear selection), B3 (Reference fuels), B4 (Road load), B5 (Test equipment), B6 (Type 1 test procedures), B6a (Ambient Temperature Correction Test), B7 (Calculations), B8 (PEV, HEV, FCHV).
*   **Annexes Part C (Evap, Durability, OBD):** C3 (Type 4 - Evaporative emissions), C4 (Type 5 - Durability), C5 (OBD).
