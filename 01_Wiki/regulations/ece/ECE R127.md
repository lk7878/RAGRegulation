# UN Regulation No. 127 - Pedestrian Safety

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to motor vehicles of categories M1 and N1.
However, vehicles of category N1 where the driver’s position "R-point" is either forward of the front axle or longitudinally rearwards of the front axle transverse centreline by a maximum of 1,100 mm, are exempted.
This Regulation does not apply to vehicles of category M1 above 2,500 kg maximum mass and which are derived from N1 category vehicles, and where the driver’s position "R-point" is either forward of the front axle or longitudinally rearwards of the front axle transverse centreline by a maximum of 1,100 mm.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Adult headform test area**: Area on the outer surfaces of the front structure bounded by a wrap around distance (WAD) of 1,700 mm at the front, the rear reference line for adult headform at the rear, and the side reference lines.
*   **Child headform test area**: Area bounded by the front reference line for child headform (WAD1000), the WAD1700 line at the rear, and the side reference lines.
*   **Bonnet leading edge**: Edge of the front upper outer structure.
*   **Bonnet top**: Area bounded by the bonnet leading edge reference line, the bonnet rear reference line, and the side reference lines.
*   **Bumper**: Front, lower, outer structure of a vehicle.
*   **Bumper test area**: Frontal surface of the bumper limited by two longitudinal vertical planes intersecting the corners of the bumper and moved 66 mm parallel and inboard.
*   **Front structure**: All outer structures of the vehicle except the windscreen, windscreen header, A-pillars and structures rearward of these.
*   **Ground reference plane**: Horizontal plane passing through the lowest points of contact for all tyres.
*   **Head Injury Criterion (HIC)**: Calculated result of accelerometer time histories.
*   **Normal ride attitude**: Vehicle positioned with its mass in running order, tyres inflated, suspension set for 40 km/h.
*   **Wrap Around Distance (WAD)**: Geometric trace on the outer surface described by a flexible tape held in a vertical longitudinal plane.
*   **Vehicle type with regard to pedestrian protection**: Category of vehicles which, forward of the A-pillars, do not differ in essential respects such as structure, main dimensions, materials of outer surfaces, or component arrangement.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application shall be submitted by the manufacturer or authorized representative, accompanied by an information document (Annex 1, Part 1) and a representative vehicle.

## 4. Approval
An approval number is assigned. An international approval mark must be affixed to conforming vehicles.

## 5. Specifications
### 5.1. Legform Test to Bumper
Requirements depend on lower bumper height at the test position:
*   **< 425 mm**: Must meet lower legform test requirements.
*   **≥ 425 mm and < 500 mm**: Manufacturer may choose lower or upper legform test requirements.
*   **≥ 500 mm**: Must meet upper legform test requirements.

**5.1.1. Lower Legform to Bumper Test:**
*   Maximum dynamic knee bending angle ≤ 19°.
*   Maximum dynamic knee shearing displacement ≤ 6.0 mm.
*   Acceleration at upper end of tibia ≤ 170 g (with possible nominated zones up to 250 g).

**5.1.2. Upper Legform to Bumper Test:**
*   Instantaneous sum of impact forces ≤ 7.5 kN.
*   Bending moment ≤ 510 Nm.

### 5.2. Headform Tests
**5.2.1. Child and Adult Headform Tests:**
*   HIC ≤ 1000 over two thirds of the combined child and adult headform test areas.
*   HIC ≤ 1700 for the remaining areas.
*   If only a child headform test area exists, HIC ≤ 1000 over two thirds, and ≤ 1700 for the remainder.

**5.2.2. Child Headform Impact:**
*   HIC ≤ 1000 over a minimum of one half of the child headform test area.
*   HIC ≤ 1700 for the remaining areas.

## 6. Modification of Vehicle Type and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified. The Type Approval Authority may grant an extension or require further tests.

## 7. Conformity of Production
Vehicles must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. Inspection frequency is once every two years.

## 8. Penalties for Non-conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn for non-compliance.

## 9. Production Definitively Discontinued
The manufacturer must inform the approval authority.

## 10. Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties shall communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Information document and communication forms.
*   **Annex 2**: Arrangements of approval marks.
*   **Annex 3**: General test conditions (temperature, humidity, vehicle preparation).
*   **Annex 4**: Test impactor specifications for lower legform, upper legform, child headform, and adult headform.
*   **Annex 5**: Test procedures for lower/upper legform to bumper and child/adult headform impacts.
*   **Annex 6**: Certification procedures for the impactors (static/dynamic tests, drop tests).
