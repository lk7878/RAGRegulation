# R122am1c1e

**Document Reference:** E/ECE/324 Rev.2/Add.121/Amend.1/Corr.1 / E/ECE/TRANS/505
**Date:** 3 May 2010

This document is an **Erratum (Corrigendum 1)** to **Amendment 1** of **Addendum 121** (Regulation No. 122) under the 1958 Agreement.

**Agreement Context:**
*   **Agreement:** Concerning the Adoption of Uniform Technical Prescriptions for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts...
*   **Revision:** 2 (including amendments effective 16 October 1995)
*   **Addendum:** 121 (Regulation No. 122)
*   **Amendment:** 1
*   **Corrigendum:** 1

**Subject:** Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles of categories M, N and O with regard to their heating systems.

**Correction Content:**
The erratum specifies a single typographical correction in the original Amendment 1 document:
*   **Location:** Annex 9, paragraph 3.1.
*   **Correction:** Replace "OT" with "OX".

**Note:** The former title of the 1958 Agreement was "Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Uniform Conditions of Approval and Reciprocal Recognition of Approval for Motor Vehicle Equipment and Parts", done at Geneva on 20 March 1958.
