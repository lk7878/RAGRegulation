**UN Regulation No. 44 - Revision 3 - Amendment 10**

**Supplement 17 to the 04 series of amendments – Date of entry into force: 29 May 2020**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of restraining devices for child occupants of power-driven vehicles ("Child Restraint Systems")**

*This document is meant purely as documentation tool. The authentic and legal binding text is: ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2019/108.*

**Amendment Details:**

Paragraph 7.1.4.2.2. is amended to read:
"7.1.4.2.2. The vertical component of the acceleration from the abdomen towards the head, "-z", when the measurement procedures defined in paragraph 8.5 of this document are followed, shall not exceed 30 g except during periods whose sum does not exceed 3 ms. The measurement procedures defined in paragraph 8.5 of this document follow those of ISO 6487 with SAE J211 sign convention, as shown in Figure A.

Figure A
SAE J211 sign convention"
