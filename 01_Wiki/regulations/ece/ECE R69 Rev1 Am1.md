# Regulation No. 69 - Amendment 1

**Addendum 68: Regulation No. 69**
**Revision 1 – Amendment 1**
Supplement 5 to the 01 series of amendments
Date of entry into force: 24 October 2009

**UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF REAR MARKING PLATES FOR SLOW-MOVING VEHICLES (BY CONSTRUCTION) AND THEIR TRAILERS**

## Technical Amendments

### Definitions (Paragraph 2)
*   Insert a new paragraph **2.4.4.**:
    > "2.4.4. 'Colour of the reflected light of the device.' The definitions of the colour of the reflected light are given in paragraphs 2.30. and 2.31. of Regulation No. 48."
*   Amend paragraph **2.5.** to read:
    > "2.5. Fluorescence
    > 2.5.1. When certain substances are brought near … than ordinary colours."
*   Insert a new paragraph **2.5.2.**:
    > "2.5.2. 'Colour of the fluorescent light of the device.' The definitions of the colour of the fluorescent light are given in paragraph 2.32. of Regulation No. 48."

### Annex 6 - Photometric and Colorimetric Tests
*   Amend paragraph **2.1.** to read:
    > "2.1. When measured with a spectrophotometer in accordance with the provisions of CIE document No. 15 (1971) and illuminated with the CIE Standard illuminant D at an angle of 45º to the normal and viewed along the normal (45/0 geometry), the colour of the material in new condition shall be within the limits according to paragraph 2.31. of Regulation No.48."
*   Insert a new paragraph **2.1.1.**:
    > "2.1.1. Luminance factor for red colour shall be ≥ 0.3."
*   Amend paragraph **2.2.** to read:
    > "2.2. When illuminated by the CIE Standard Illuminant A at an entrance angle β1 = β2 = 0º or, if this produces a colourless surface reflection, an angle β1 = ± 5º, β2 = 0º, and measured at an observation angle of 20’, the colour of the material in new condition shall be within the limits according to paragraph 2.30. of the Regulation No. 48."
*   Amend paragraph **3.1.** to read:
    > "3.1. When measured with a spectrophotometer in accordance with the provisions of CIE document No. 15 (1971) and illuminated polychromatically with the CIE Standard Illuminant D at an angle 45º to the normal and viewed along the normal (geometry 45/0), the colour of the material in new condition shall be within the limits according to paragraph 2.32. of the Regulation No. 48."

### Annex 6 - Photometric and Colorimetric Tests (Continued)
*   Insert a new paragraph **3.1.1.**:
    > "3.1.1. Luminance factor shall be ≥ 0.3."

### Annex 8 - Durability of Photometric and Colorimetric Characteristics
*   Amend paragraph **1.3.** to read:
    > "1.3. Colour fastness – The colours of the exposed specimen shall still meet the requirements specified in Annex 6."
