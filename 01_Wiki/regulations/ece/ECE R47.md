# Regulation No. 47

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the emission of gaseous pollutants from positive-ignition engines of two-wheeled or three-wheeled vehicles with an unladen weight of less than 400 kg, a maximum design speed not exceeding 50 km/h and a cylinder capacity not exceeding 50 cm³.

## 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation:
- **2.1. "Approval of a vehicle"**: means the approval of a vehicle type with regard to the limitation of the emission of gaseous pollutants from the engine.
- **2.2. "Vehicle type"**: means a category of power-driven vehicles which do not differ in such essential respects as the equivalent inertia determined in relation to the reference weight, and the engine and vehicle characteristics as defined in annexes.
- **2.3. "Reference weight"**: means the weight of the vehicle in running order, increased by a uniform figure of 75 kg. The weight of the vehicle in running order is its total unladen weight with all tanks filled to within 90 per cent of their maximum capacity.
- **2.4. "Gaseous pollutants"**: means carbon monoxide, hydrocarbons and nitrogen oxides, the last-named being expressed in nitrogen dioxide (NO₂) equivalent.

## 3. Application for Approval
- **3.1.** The application for approval of a vehicle type with regard to limitation of the emission of gaseous pollutants from its engine shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or by his duly accredited representative.
- **3.2.** It shall be accompanied by the required documents in triplicate and particulars including a description of the engine type and particulars concerning the vehicle as shown in annex 2.
- **3.3.** A vehicle representative of the vehicle type to be approved shall be submitted to the technical service for the tests referred to in paragraph 5.

## 4. Approval
- **4.1.** If the vehicle type submitted for approval meets the requirements of paragraphs 5 and 6, approval shall be granted.
- **4.2.** An approval number shall be assigned to each type approved.
- **4.3.** Notice of approval or refusal shall be communicated to the Parties applying this Regulation.
- **4.4.** An international approval mark shall be affixed to every conforming vehicle, consisting of a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the country which has granted approval, and the number of this Regulation, followed by the letter "R", a dash and the approval number.
- **4.5.-4.8.** Details on the placement, legibility, and examples of the approval mark.

## 5. Specifications and Tests
### 5.1. General
The components liable to affect the emission of gaseous pollutants shall be so designed, constructed and assembled as to enable the vehicle in normal use to comply with this Regulation.

### 5.2. Description of tests
The vehicle shall be subjected to Type-I and Type-II tests.

#### 5.2.1. Type-I test (verifying the average emission of gaseous pollutants in a congested urban area)
- **5.2.1.1.** The vehicle shall be placed on a dynamometer bench. A test lasting a total of 448 seconds and comprising four cycles shall be carried out. Each cycle comprises seven phases (idling, acceleration, steady speed, deceleration, etc.). Exhaust gases shall be diluted with air and samples collected for analysis.
- **5.2.1.1.2.** The test shall be carried out by the method described in annex 4.
- **5.2.1.1.3.** Subject to provisions, the test shall be repeated three times. In each test, the mass of the carbon monoxide and the mass of the hydrocarbons obtained shall be less than the amounts shown in the tables below. The measurement of the mass per km of nitrogen oxides is for information only.

**Emission Limits (Type-I Test):**
- **Two-wheeled vehicles:**
    - Mass of carbon monoxide: 8 g/km
    - Mass of hydrocarbons: 5 g/km
- **Three-wheeled vehicles (other than those referred to in paragraph 7.4):**
    - Mass of carbon monoxide: 15 g/km
    - Mass of hydrocarbons: 10 g/km

- **5.2.1.1.3.1.** For each pollutant, one of the three results may exceed the limit by not more than 10%, provided the arithmetical mean of the three results is below the prescribed limit.
- **5.2.1.1.4.** Conditions for reducing the number of required tests are specified.

#### 5.2.1.2. Type-II test (verifying the emission of carbon monoxide and unburnt hydrocarbons at idling speed)
- **5.2.1.2.1.** The mass of carbon monoxide and the mass of unburnt hydrocarbons emitted with the engine idling during one minute shall be noted.
- **5.2.1.2.2.** This test shall be carried out by the method described in annex 5.

## 6. Modifications of the Vehicle Type
- **6.1.** Every modification shall be notified to the administrative department which may require further tests.
- **6.2.** Confirmation or refusal of approval shall be communicated.

## 7. Extension of Approval
Conditions under which approval may be extended to vehicle types differing in reference weight, overall gear ratios, or from two-wheeled to three-wheeled vehicles are specified in paragraphs 7.1 to 7.5.

## 8. Conformity of Production
- **8.1.** Every vehicle bearing an approval mark shall conform to the approved type.
- **8.2.-8.3.** Procedures for verifying conformity, including sampling and statistical evaluation.
- **Conformity of Production Limits (Type-I Test):**
    - **Two-wheeled vehicles:**
 - Mass of carbon monoxide: 9.6 g/km
 - Mass of hydrocarbons: 6.5 g/km
    - **Three-wheeled vehicles (other than those referred to in paragraph 7.4):**
 - Mass of carbon monoxide: 18 g/km
 - Mass of hydrocarbons: 13 g/km

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met.

## 10. Production Definitely Discontinued
The holder of the approval must inform the authority if production ceases.

## 11. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments
Parties shall communicate the names and addresses of responsible technical services and administrative departments.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Essential characteristics of the engine and information concerning the conduct of tests.
- **Annex 2**: Communication concerning the approval (or refusal/withdrawal) of a vehicle type.
- **Annex 3**: Arrangements of the approval mark.
- **Annex 4**: Type-I test procedure (detailed description of the operating cycle, equipment, preparation, testing, sampling, analysis, and calculation of emissions).
- **Annex 5**: Type-II test procedure (measurement at idle).
- **Annex 6**: Specifications of reference fuels.
