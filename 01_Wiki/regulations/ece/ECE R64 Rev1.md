# UN Regulation No. 64 (Revision 1)

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the approval of vehicles of category M1 and N1 when equipped with:
- A temporary-use spare unit; and/or
- Run-flat tyres and/or a run-flat system; and/or
- A tyre pressure monitoring system (TPMS).

For TPMS, applies to M1 up to 3,500 kg and N1, with all axles equipped with single tyres. Spare wheel and tyre substitute units in the form of run-flat tyres or a run-flat system in a totally deflated condition are treated as temporary-use spare units.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **Vehicle type**: Category of vehicles with similar essential aspects (e.g., axle loads, spare unit characteristics, drive method, suspension, braking, wheel/tyre size, wheel offset for temporary-use spare units; or trade name, vehicle features influencing TPMS, TPMS type and design for TPMS).
- **Wheel**: Complete wheel (rim + disc).
- **Tyre types**: Normal tyre, temporary-use spare tyre, run-flat tyre (self-supporting), run-flat system (extended mobility system).
- **Flat tyre running mode**: Tyre operating at inflation pressure between 0 and 70 kPa.
- **Basic tyre function**: Normal capability of an inflated tyre in supporting load, transmitting driving/steering/braking forces.
- **Standard spare unit**: Assembly of wheel and tyre identical to those fitted for normal operation.
- **Temporary-use spare unit**: Assembly not meeting "Standard spare unit" definition. Five types are defined (Type 1 to Type 5), differing in tyre type, wheel offset, tyre structure, size, or use of deflated run-flat systems.
- **Maximum mass / Maximum axle load**: Technically permissible values stated by manufacturer.
- **Run-Flat Warning System (RFWS)**: System informing driver a tyre is in flat tyre running mode.
- **Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS)**: System evaluating tyre inflation pressure/variation and transmitting information to user while vehicle is running.
- **Pressures**: Cold tyre inflation pressure, Recommended cold inflation pressure (P_rec), In-service operating pressure (P_warm), Test Pressure (P_test).

## 3. Application for Approval
Submitted by vehicle manufacturer or accredited representative, accompanied by vehicle description (Annex 1) and a representative vehicle sample.

## 4. Approval
Granted if vehicle meets requirements of paragraph 5. Approval number assigned (first two digits indicate series of amendments). Approval mark affixed to vehicle (circle with "E" + country code, regulation number "64" + "R"/"P"/"RP", approval number). Examples in Annex 2.

## 5. Specifications and Tests
### 5.1. General (for temporary-use spare units)
- Tyres must be approved per UN R030.
- Load capacity: At least half of highest maximum axle load (or specific axle if restricted).
- Design speed: At least 120 km/h for Types 1, 2, 3.
- Warning symbols: Permanent display on wheel outer face indicating maximum speed (80 km/h/50 mph for Types 1,2,3; 120 km/h/75 mph for Type 4 on M1). Specific size/color requirements.
- Distinctive color/pattern on wheel/tyre when mounted.
- Only one temporary-use spare unit permitted per vehicle (except for run-flat systems).
- **Run-Flat Warning System (RFWS)**: Required for vehicles equipped with run-flat tyres/systems (unless TPMS meeting para 5.3 is fitted). Must operate from 40 km/h to max design speed. Optical yellow warning signal, activation requirements, malfunction indication. Test requirements in Annex 4.
- Inflation device required if spare stored deflated (inflate within 10 min).

### 5.2. Braking Test
Vehicles with temporary-use spare units must meet requirements of Annex 3 (braking and deviation test from 80 km/h or 120 km/h depending on spare unit type and vehicle category, with engine disconnected, per UN R13 or R13-H).

### 5.3. Tyre Pressure Monitoring Systems (TPMS)
- Applies to M1 up to 3,500 kg and N1 with all axles on single tyres.
- Must comply with UN R10 (EMC).
- Operate from 40 km/h or below up to max design speed.
- **Three test categories** (detailed in Annex 5):
    1.  **Puncture test** (incident-related pressure loss): Warning within 10 min after pressure in one tyre reduced by 20% or to min 150 kPa (whichever higher).
    2.  **Diffusion test** (gradual pressure loss): Warning within 60 min cumulative driving after pressure in any tyre (up to four) reduced by 20%.
    3.  **Malfunction test**: Warning within 10 min after system malfunction (unless blocked by external influence).
- **Warning indication**: Optical signal per UN R121. Bulb check, visible by daylight, verifiable from driver's seat. Malfunction indication may use same signal (flashing then continuous). Flashing may indicate reset status.

## 6. Supplementary Information (Owner's Manual)
- **For temporary-use spare unit**: Risks, speed restrictions (80 km/h for Types 1,2,3,5; 120 km/h for Type 4), instruction to reinstall standard unit ASAP, prohibition on using more than one temporary spare (Types 1,2,3), specified inflation pressure, inflation procedure if stored deflated.
- **For RFWS/TPMS**: Statement of system fitment, reset instructions (if applicable), image of tell-tale(s), significance of warning and corrective actions.
- If no manual, information must be displayed prominently on vehicle.

## 7. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified. Authority may confirm compliance or require further tests.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Procedures per Appendix 2 of the 1958 Agreement. Annual verification by approval authority.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn.

## 10. Production Definitely Discontinued
Holder must inform approval authority.

## 11. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments
Communicated to UN Secretariat.

## 12. Transitional Provisions
- From entry into force of 01 series (2008-02-03): No refusal to grant approval under 01 series.
- 36 months after 01 series entry into force: Approvals only granted if meeting 01 series requirements for temporary-use spare units/run-flat systems.
- From entry into force of 02 series (2010-08-19): No refusal to grant approval under 02 series.
- From 2012-11-01: May refuse national/regional approval for M1 (≤3,500 kg) and N1 (single tyres) if not meeting 02 series.
- From 2014-11-01: May refuse first registration of such vehicles if not meeting 02 series.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval actions.
- **Annex 2**: Examples of approval marks (Model A: temporary-use spare unit "R"; Model B: TPMS "P"; Model C: combined "RP").
- **Annex 3**: Braking and deviation test procedure for vehicles with temporary-use spare units (test conditions, performance criteria based on UN R13/R13-H).
- **Annex 4**: Test requirements for Run-Flat Warning System (RFWS) (test conditions, procedures for detecting flat tyre running mode and system failure).
- **Annex 5**: Test requirements for Tyre Pressure Monitoring Systems (TPMS) (detailed test conditions, procedures for puncture, diffusion, and malfunction tests, including learning phase, deflation, and detection phases).
