# Regulation No. 135 - Pole Side Impact Performance (PSI)

## 1. Scope
1.1. This Regulation applies to:
    (a) Category M vehicles with a gross vehicle mass of up to 3,500 kg; and
    (b) Category N vehicles where the acute angle alpha (α), measured between a horizontal plane passing through the centre of the front axle and an angular transverse plane passing through the centre of the front axle and the R-point of the driver's seat, is less than 22.0 degrees; or the ratio between the distance from the driver's R-point to the centre of the rear axle (L101-L114) and the centre of the front axle and the driver's R-point (L114) is less than 1.30.
1.2. Other Category M and Category N vehicles with a gross vehicle mass of up to 4,500 kg may also be approved if requested by the manufacturer.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Approval of a vehicle type**: The full procedure whereby a Contracting Party certifies that a vehicle type meets the technical requirements.
*   **Gross vehicle mass**: The maximum mass of the fully laden solo vehicle, based on its construction and design performances, as declared by the manufacturer.
*   **Hydrogen-fuelled vehicle**: Any motor vehicle that uses compressed gaseous hydrogen as a fuel to propel the vehicle.
*   **Passenger compartment**: The space for occupant accommodation, bounded by the roof, floor, side walls, doors, outside glazing and front bulkhead and the plane of the rear compartment bulkhead or the plane of the rear-seat back support.
*   **R-point**: A design reference point with coordinates determined in relation to the designed vehicle structure.
*   **Vehicle type**: A category of vehicles, the design characteristics of which do not differ in such essential respects as the type of protective system(s), front seat(s), vehicle width, wheelbase, structure of side walls, type of door latches and hinges, type of fuel system(s), unladen vehicle mass, rated cargo and luggage mass, and sitting of the engine.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or their duly accredited representative, accompanied by detailed documentation including vehicle description, drawings, mass specifications, and descriptions of side interior fittings and protective systems. A representative vehicle shall be submitted for testing.

## 4. Approval
If the vehicle type meets the requirements, approval shall be granted. An approval number is assigned, and an international approval mark must be affixed to conforming vehicles.

## 5. Requirements
5.1. A representative vehicle shall be tested in accordance with Annex 3, using a WorldSID 50th percentile adult male dummy. The test is generally conducted on the driver's side.
5.2. The test results are satisfactory if the requirements of paragraphs 5.3, 5.4, and 5.5 are met.
5.3. **WorldSID 50th Percentile Adult Male Performance Requirements:**
    *   **Head Injury Criterion (HIC) 36**: Shall not exceed 1,000.
    *   **Shoulder**: Peak lateral shoulder force shall not exceed 3.0 kN.
    *   **Thorax**: Maximum thorax rib deflection shall not exceed 55 mm.
    *   **Abdomen**: Maximum abdominal rib deflection shall not exceed 65 mm. Resultant lower spine acceleration shall not exceed 75 g (except for intervals whose cumulative duration is not more than 3 ms).
    *   **Pelvis**: Peak pubic symphysis force shall not exceed 3.36 kN.
5.4. **Door Latch and Hinge System Integrity Requirements:**
    *   Any side door which impacts the pole shall not separate totally from the vehicle.
    *   Any non-impacting door (excluding trunk lids) shall remain latched; the latch shall not separate from the striker; hinge components shall not separate; and neither latch nor hinge systems shall pull out of their anchorages.
5.5. **Fuel System Integrity Requirements:**
    *   **For liquid fuels**: Fuel ballast leakage shall not exceed 142 grams in the first 5 minutes post-impact, and 28 grams per minute from 5 to 30 minutes post-impact.
    *   **For compressed hydrogen-fuelled vehicles**: The average hydrogen leakage rate shall not exceed 118 NL per minute; hydrogen concentration in enclosed spaces shall not exceed 4.0% (or 3.0% for helium) within 60 minutes; and the hydrogen storage container(s) shall remain attached at a minimum of one point.

## 6. Modification of Vehicle Type and Extension of Approval
Modifications affecting key design characteristics must be notified to the Approval Authority, which may grant an extension or require further tests.

## 7. Conformity of Production
Vehicles must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. The approval holder must carry out checks, and the granting authority may verify conformity control methods.

## 8. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if production conformity requirements are not met.

## 9. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the authority if production of an approved type ceases.

## 10. Technical Services and Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties shall communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN secretariat.

## 11. Transitional Provisions
Outlines the acceptance of approvals under the original regulation and the 01 series of amendments, with key dates including 1 September 2016.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval notifications.
*   **Annex 2**: Arrangement of the approval mark.
*   **Annex 3**: Dynamic pole side impact test procedure (purpose, definitions, vehicle preparation, test procedure).
*   **Annex 4**: Seat adjustment and installation requirements for the WorldSID 50th percentile adult male dummy.
*   **Annex 5**: Description of the three-dimensional H-point machine (3-D H machine).
*   **Annex 6**: Test conditions and procedures for the assessment of post-crash hydrogen fuel system integrity.
*   **Annex 7**: Impact reference line.
*   **Annex 8**: Impact angle.
*   **Annex 9**: Pitch and roll angle references.
*   **Annex 10**: Determination of WorldSID 50th percentile adult male performance criteria (calculation methods for HIC, forces, deflections, and accelerations).
