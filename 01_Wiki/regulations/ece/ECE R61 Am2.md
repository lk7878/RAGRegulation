# Amendment 2 to Regulation No. 61

**Date of Entry into Force:** 15 July 2013
**Communication Date:** 6 August 2013

## Summary
This document constitutes Amendment 2 to UNECE Regulation No. 61. It introduces new definitions, modifies approval procedures, and updates technical requirements related to external projections on commercial vehicles.

## Key Amendments

### 1. New Definitions (Paragraph 2)
- **2.9. "Bumper"**: Defined as the front, lower, outer structure of a vehicle, including all structures intended for protection in low-speed frontal collisions and any attachments.
- **2.10. "Bumper cover"**: Defined as the non-rigid outer surface of a bumper, typically extending across the full width of the vehicle front.

### 2. Type Approval (Paragraph 4.1.)
- Approval is granted if the vehicle meets the requirements of paragraphs 5 and 6.
- A category N1 vehicle derived from category M1 may be approved based on the technical specifications of **Regulation No. 26**, subject to acceptance by the Technical Service and Type Approval Authority.

### 3. Technical Requirement Update (Paragraph 6.5.2.)
- Rigid surfaces of front protective devices facing outwards must have a radius of curvature of **not less than 5 mm**.
- If a bumper cover is fitted, it must have a radius of curvature of **not less than 2.5 mm**.

### 4. Modification of Vehicle Type (Paragraphs 7 to 7.2.)
Introduces detailed procedures for handling modifications to an approved vehicle type:
- **7.1.** Notifies the Type Approval Authority of any modification.
- **7.1.1. Revision**: For modifications unlikely to have an appreciable adverse effect. Requires updated documentation.
- **7.1.2. Extension**: For modifications requiring further tests, changes to the communication document, or approval to a later amendment series.
- **7.2.** Requires communication of approval confirmation/refusal (specifying alterations) to applying Parties and updating of the information package index.

### 5. Withdrawal of Approval (Paragraph 9.2.)
- If a Party withdraws an approval, it must immediately notify other applying Contracting Parties using the prescribed communication form (Annex 1).

### 6. Production Discontinuation (Paragraph 10.)
- The approval holder must inform the granting Type Approval Authority upon completely ceasing manufacture of an approved vehicle type. The authority then notifies other applying Parties.

### 7. Annex 1 - Communication Form
- The model communication form is amended to include options for "Approval extended", "Approval refused", "Approval withdrawn", and "Production definitively discontinued".
- The title specifies the subject as approval "of a vehicle type with regard its external projections, pursuant to Regulation No. 61".

---
**Note:** This amendment is Supplement 2 to the original version of Regulation No. 61. The parent Agreement is the "Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Uniform Technical Prescriptions for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts..." (Revision 2).
