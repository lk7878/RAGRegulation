# UN Regulation No. 118 (Revision 3)

## 1. Scope
1.1. This Regulation applies to the burning behaviour (ignitibility, burning rate and melting behaviour) and to the capability to repel fuel or lubricants of materials used in vehicles of categories M3, Classes II and III.
1.2. Type approvals are granted according to:
    *   **Part I** - Approval of a vehicle type with regard to the burning behaviour and/or the capability to repel fuel or lubricant of the components used in the interior compartment, the engine compartment and any separate heating compartment and with regard to the burning behaviour of electric cables and cable sleeves or cable conduits used for protecting electric cables in the vehicle.
    *   **Part II** – Approval of a component with regard to its burning behaviour and/or its capability to repel fuel or lubricant installed in the interior compartment, the engine compartment or any separate heating compartment.

## 2. Definitions: General
*   **2.1. "Manufacturer"**: The person or body responsible to the Type Approval Authority for all aspects of the type approval process and for ensuring conformity of production.
*   **2.2. "Interior compartment"**: Any compartment intended for passengers, drivers and/or crew bounded by the interior facing surface(s) of the ceiling, floor, front/rear/side walls, doors, and outside glazing.
*   **2.3. "Engine compartment"**: The compartment in which the engine is installed and in which a combustion heater may be installed.
*   **2.4. "Separate heating compartment"**: A compartment for a combustion heater located outside the interior and engine compartments.
*   **2.5. "Production materials"**: Products in the form of bulk materials or preformed components supplied for incorporation in a vehicle or for vehicle maintenance/repair.
*   **2.6. "Seat"**: A structure, complete with trim, intended to seat one adult person.
*   **2.7. "Group of seats"**: A bench-type seat, or separate but side-by-side seats.
*   **2.8. "Bench seat"**: A structure, complete with trim, intended to seat more than one adult person.
*   **2.9. "Material installed in a vertical position"**: Materials installed such that its slope exceeds 15% from the horizontal when the vehicle is in running order.
*   **2.10. "Electrical cable"**: A single-core or multi-core cable, enabling the transfer of electrical signals.
*   **2.11. "Cable sleeve"**: Any component that enfolds single cables to a multi-core cable or electrical harness.
*   **2.12. "Cable conduit"**: Any component that covers electrical cables to guide, route, or fasten them to the vehicle.

## 3. Application for Approval
3.1. Submitted by the manufacturer.
3.2. Accompanied by an information document (Annex 1 for vehicles, Annex 2 for components).
3.3. Submission to the Technical Service:
    *   3.3.1. For vehicle approval: A representative vehicle.
    *   3.3.2. For components already approved: List of type approval numbers and designations.
    *   3.3.3. For components without approval: Specified number of samples (see Annexes 6-9), plus one for reference. Complete devices (e.g., seats) require one full sample.

## 4. Approval
4.1. Granted if the type meets the requirements.
4.2. An approval number is assigned. Its first two digits indicate the series of amendments (e.g., 03, 04).
4.3. Notice communicated to Contracting Parties via forms in Annex 3 or 4.
4.4. **Vehicle Approval Mark**: An international mark consisting of a circle with "E" and country number, followed by "118RI", a dash, and the approval number. Placed near the vehicle data plate.
4.5. **Production Materials Marking**: Packaging marked with a circle with "E" and country number, followed by "118RII", a dash, and the approval number. May include symbols for installation direction (↔ horizontal, ↕ vertical, both) and "V" if it meets para. 6.2.2 requirements.
4.6. Components may be marked as per 4.5. Complete devices (e.g., seats) include the symbol "CD".
4.7. Examples in Annex 5.

## 5. Part I: Vehicle Type Approval
5.1. **Definition**: "Vehicle type" means vehicles that do not differ in essential respects like the manufacturer's type designation.
5.2. **Specifications**:
    *   5.2.1. Materials inside and up to 13 mm beyond the interior compartment, in the engine/separate heating compartments, and electric cables/sleeves/conduits must meet Part II requirements.
    *   5.2.2. Materials/equipment must be installed to minimize flame development and propagation.
    *   5.2.3. Materials must be installed according to their intended purpose and passed tests.
    *   5.2.4. Adhesives should not exacerbate burning behaviour. Pre-approved adhesives (listed in Annex 2) allow composite materials to be considered as meeting requirements. Unlisted adhesives require testing of the material-adhesive-support combination.

## 6. Part II: Component Approval
6.1. **Definitions**:
    *   6.1.1. "Type of a component": Components not differing in essential respects (manufacturer's designation, intended use, base material, layers, etc.).
    *   6.1.2. "Burning rate": Quotient of burnt distance and time (mm/min).
    *   6.1.3. "Composite material": Material composed of several layers intimately held together.
    *   6.1.4. "Exposed face": Side facing the passenger/engine/heating compartment when mounted.
    *   6.1.5. "Upholstery": Combination of interior padding and surface finish for seat cushioning.
    *   6.1.6. "Interior lining(s)": Material(s) constituting surface finish and substrate of ceiling, wall, or floor.
    *   6.1.7. "Insulation material(s)": Material(s) used to reduce heat transfer and for sound-proofing in engine/separate heating compartments.
    *   6.1.8. "Capability to repel fuel or lubricant": Capability when measured per Annex 9.
6.2. **Specifications**:
    *   **6.2.1. Horizontal Burning Test (Annex 6)**: For materials installed horizontally in the interior compartment and insulation materials installed horizontally in engine/separate heating compartments. Requirement: Burning rate ≤ 100 mm/min or flame extinguishes before the last measuring point.
    *   **6.2.2. Melting Behaviour Test (Annex 7)**: For materials installed >500 mm above seat cushion/on ceiling, and insulation materials in engine/separate heating compartments. Requirement: No flaming drops that ignite cotton wool.
    *   **6.2.3. Vertical Burning Test (Annex 8)**: For materials installed vertically in the interior compartment and insulation materials installed vertically in engine/separate heating compartments. Requirement: Burning rate ≤ 100 mm/min or flame extinguishes before destruction of the first marker thread.
    *   **6.2.4. Alternative Compliance**: Materials achieving CFE ≥ 20 kW/m² per ISO 5658-2 are deemed to comply with 6.2.2 and 6.2.3, provided no burning drops are observed.
    *   **6.2.5. Fuel/Lubricant Repellence Test (Annex 9)**: For all insulation materials in engine/separate heating compartments. Requirement: Weight increase ≤ 1 g.
    *   **6.2.6. Electrical Cable Flame Propagation Test (Annex 10 / ISO 6722-1:2011)**: For any cable >100 mm. Flame application time varies by cable type/size. Requirement: Combustion flame extinguishes within 70 seconds and ≥50 mm insulation remains unburned at the top.
    *   **6.2.7. Cable Sleeve/Conduit Test**: For any sleeve/conduit >100 mm. Test per Annex 8 (vertical burning). Requirement: Burning rate ≤ 100 mm/min or flame extinguishes before destruction of the first marker thread.
    *   **6.2.8. Exemptions**: Metal/glass parts; small seat accessories (<200 g non-metallic material each, <400 g total per seat); small surface/volume elements; elements from which a standard sample cannot be extracted.

## 7. Modification of Type and Extension of Approval
7.1. Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing.
7.2. Confirmation/refusal communicated to Contracting Parties.
7.3. Extensions assigned a serial number and communicated.

## 8. Conformity of Production
8.1. Production must conform to the approved type.
8.2. Type Approval Authority verifies control methods at least every two years.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity
9.1. Approval may be withdrawn for non-conformity.
9.2. Withdrawal notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
10.1. Manufacturer informs the Type Approval Authority, which notifies other Parties.

## 11. Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
11.1. Parties communicate names and addresses to the UN Secretariat.

## 12. Transitional Provisions
(Summarizes complex phase-in rules for 01, 02, 03, and 04 series of amendments, including dates for mandatory application and acceptance of previous approvals.)

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Information document for vehicle approval.
*   **Annex 2**: Information document for component approval.
*   **Annex 3**: Communication form (vehicle approval).
*   **Annex 4**: Communication form (component approval).
*   **Annex 5**: Arrangements of approval marks.
*   **Annex 6**: Test to determine the horizontal burning rate of materials.
*   **Annex 7**: Test to determine the melting behaviour of materials.
*   **Annex 8**: Test to determine the vertical burning rate of materials.
*   **Annex 9**: Test to determine the capability of materials to repel fuel or lubricant.
*   **Annex 10**: Test to determine the resistance to flame propagation of electrical cables.
