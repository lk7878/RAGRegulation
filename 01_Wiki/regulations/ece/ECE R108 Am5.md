**Amendment 5 to UN Regulation No. 108**

**Date of entry into force:** 3 January 2021

This document constitutes Amendment 5 to UN Regulation No. 108. The authentic and legally binding text is: ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2020/73.

**Amendments:**

1.  **Insert a new paragraph 2.36:** Defines "Retreader" as the person or body responsible to the Type Approval Authority (TAA) for all aspects of type-approval under this Regulation and for ensuring conformity of production.
2.  **Renumber former paragraph 2.36 to 2.36.1.**
3.  **Amend paragraph 2.49:** Updates the definition of "Standard Reference Test Tyre (SRTT)" to refer to ASTM standard E1136 – 17 for size P195/75R14, designated as "SRTT14".
4.  **Amend paragraph 4.3:** Clarifies that at the TAA's request, the Retreader shall submit tyre samples or test report copies from technical services.
5.  **Amend paragraph 7.2:** Revises the classification criteria for "snow tyre for use in severe snow conditions". A retreaded tyre must meet the performance requirements of paragraph 7.2.1 based on test methods in Annex 9 comparing:
    *   Mean fully developed deceleration (mfdd) in braking,
    *   Or average traction force,
    *   Or average acceleration,
    against the SRTT14. Performance is indicated by a snow grip index.
6.  **Amend paragraph 7.2.1:** Specifies minimum snow grip index values for Class C1 tyres compared to SRTT14:
    *   **Brake on snow method:** 1.07
    *   **Spin traction method:** 1.10
7.  **Amend Annex 9, Paragraph 3.4.1.1:** Provides formulas for computing the arithmetic mean (𝒂̅), corrected sample standard deviation (𝝈), and coefficient of variation (CV) of the mfdd for each tyre braking test.
8.  **Amend Annex 9, Paragraph 3.4.1.2:** Details the calculation of weighted averages (wa) for the SRTT14 results when testing candidate tyres in sequence (e.g., R1–T–R2 or R1–T1–T2–R2).
9.  **Amend Annex 9, Paragraph 3.4.1.3:** Defines the snow grip index (SG) of a candidate tyre as the quotient of its average mfdd and the applicable weighted average of the SRTT14.
10. **Amend Annex 9, Paragraph 3.4.2:** Outlines statistical validation requirements:
    *   Examination of test data for normality, drift, and outliers.
    *   Examination of consistency in SRTT14 test results.
    *   Calculation of a coefficient of validation CVal (SRTT) between consecutive SRTT14 test groups, which must not differ by more than 5%.
    *   The coefficient of variation CV for any braking test must be less than 6%.
    *   If conditions are not met, tests must be repeated after re-grooming the test course.
11. **Amend Appendix 2:** Updates the test report template to include:
    *   **Part 1 – Report:** Fields for the Retreader's name/address, brand/trade description, and the snow grip index relative to SRTT.
    *   **Part 2 – Test data:** A table for recording details of the SRTT and candidate tyres (brand, size, pressure, loads, etc.).
    *   **Test results table:** For recording mfdd values per run, calculating means, standard deviations, CV, CVal, SRTT weighted average, and the resulting snow grip index.
