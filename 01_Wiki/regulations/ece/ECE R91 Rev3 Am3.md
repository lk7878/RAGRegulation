# UN Regulation No. 91 - Side marker lamps

**Document Reference:** E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.90/Rev.3/Amend.3 / E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.1/Add.90/Rev.3/Amend.3

**Authentic Legal Text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2018/113/Rev.1

**Amendment Series:** 01 series of amendments

**Date of Entry into Force:** 15 October 2019

## Transitional Provisions (Paragraph 15)

The amendment modifies Paragraph 15 of the regulation to establish the following transitional rules:

**15.1 Cessation of New Approvals:**
Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall cease to grant new approvals to UN Regulation No. 91 **24 months after the official date of entry into force of the new UN Regulation on Light-Signalling Devices (LSD)**.

**15.2 Extension of Existing Approvals:**
Contracting Parties shall not refuse to grant extensions of approval for devices approved under this or any previous series of amendments of this Regulation.

**15.3 Approvals for Replacement Parts:**
Contracting Parties shall continue to grant approvals for devices based on this or any previous series of amendments, **provided the devices are intended as replacement parts for fitting to vehicles already in use**.

**15.4 Fitting/Use of Replacement Parts:**
Contracting Parties shall continue to allow the fitting or use on an in-use vehicle of a device approved to this Regulation (as amended by any previous series), **provided the device is intended for replacement**.

---
**Note 1:** This amendment does not require changes to the approval number (referencing TRANS/WP.29/815, paragraph 82).

**Note 2:** The "new UN Regulation on Light-Signalling Devices (LSD)" is referenced as document ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2018/157.
