**Note Content**

This document is **UN Regulation No. 137, Revision 1, Amendment 1 (Supplement 1 to the 01 series of amendments)**. It falls under the framework of the **1958 Agreement** concerning the adoption of harmonized technical regulations for wheeled vehicles.

*   **Core Subject:** The regulation establishes uniform technical provisions for the approval of passenger cars regarding their performance in a **frontal collision**, with a particular focus on the **restraint system**.
*   **Document Status:** This specific document is **Amendment 1** to Revision 1 of the regulation. The authentic and legally binding text is referenced as document **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2018/77**.
*   **Entry into Force:** This amendment entered into force on **29 December 2018**.

**Technical Amendment Detail**

The amendment contains a specific modification to the regulation's annexes:
*   **Annex 3, Paragraph 5.2.4.1.** is amended to read: **"5.2.4.1. The axial compressive force is measured with a CFC of 600."**
    *   This change specifies the Channel Frequency Class (CFC) filter to be used for measuring axial compressive force during testing.
