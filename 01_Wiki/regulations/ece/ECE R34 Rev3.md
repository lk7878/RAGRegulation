# UN Regulation No. 34 Revision 3

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies:
1.1. **Part I**: To the approval of vehicles of categories M, N and O1 with regard to the tank(s) for liquid fuel.
1.2. **Part II-1**: At the request of the manufacturer, to the approval of vehicles of categories M, N and O approved to Part I or IV of this Regulation fitted with liquid fuel tank(s) with regard to the prevention of fire risks in the event of a frontal and/or lateral collision as well as to the approval of vehicles of categories M1 and N1, which are of a total permissible mass exceeding 2.8 tonnes, and categories M2, M3, N2, N3 and O, fitted with tank(s) for liquid fuel, which have been approved to Part I or IV of this Regulation with regard to the prevention of fire risks in the event of a rear collision.
    **Part II-2**: To the approval of vehicles of categories M1 and N1, which are of a total permissible mass not exceeding 2.8 tonnes, fitted with liquid fuel tank(s) approved to Part I or IV of this Regulation with regard to the prevention of fire risks in the event of a rear collision.
1.3. **Part III**: To the approval of tanks for liquid fuel as separate technical units.
1.4. **Part IV**: To the approval of vehicles with regard to the installation of approved tanks for liquid fuel.

## 2. Application for approval
Details the required documentation and samples to be submitted by the vehicle or tank manufacturer for approval under the different Parts of the Regulation.

## 3. Approval
Describes the approval process, assignment of approval numbers, communication of approval/refusal, and the format and placement of the international approval mark for vehicles and tanks approved under Parts I, II, III, and IV.

## Part I - Approval of vehicles with regard to their fuel tanks
### 4. Definitions
Includes definitions for "Approval of a vehicle", "Vehicle type", "Passenger compartment", "Tank", "Capacity of the fuel tank", and "Liquid fuel".

### 5. Requirements for liquid fuel tanks
Specifies requirements for tank construction (corrosion resistance, pressure resistance, venting, material), installation (separation from passenger compartment, secure fixing, filler hole location, protection from collision, avoidance of ignition hazards from static electricity). Tanks must be made of fire-resistant metallic material or plastic complying with Annex 5.

### 6. Tests of liquid fuel tanks
6.1. **Hydraulic test**: Tank subjected to internal pressure of at least double the working pressure (min. 30 kPa) for one minute. No cracking or leaking allowed.
6.2. **Overturn test**: Tank rotated about a longitudinal axis while filled to 90% and 30% capacity. Specific rotation sequence and dwell times specified.

## Part II-1 - Approval of vehicles with regard to the prevention of fire risks in the event of collision
### 7. Definitions
Includes definitions for "Approval of a vehicle", "Vehicle type", "Transverse plane", and "Unladen mass".

### 8. Requirements for the installation of liquid fuel tanks
Covers protection of fuel installation components from ground contact and abnormal stress, design of electrical installation to prevent fire risks.

### 9. Tests on the vehicle
Specifies performance requirements during frontal (Annex 3), lateral (referencing UN R95), and rear-end (Annex 4) collision tests:
- No more than slight leakage on collision.
- If continuous leakage after collision, rate ≤ 30 g/min.
- No fire maintained by fuel.
- Battery must remain secured.

## Part II-2 - Approval of vehicle with regard to the prevention of fire risks in the event of rear collision
Applies definitions and requirements of Part II-1. The vehicle test shall be performed according to Annex 4, meeting the performance requirements of paragraph 9.

## Part III - Approval of tanks for liquid fuel as separate technical units
### 10. Definitions
Includes definitions for "Tank", "Capacity of the fuel tank", "Liquid fuel", "Approval of a tank", and "Type of tank". A tank may be approved with or without its accessories, and for universal or specific vehicle use.

### 11. Requirements for liquid fuel tanks
Tanks must comply with the requirements of paragraphs 5.1., 5.2., 5.3., 5.9., 5.12., 6.1. and 6.2. when equipped with normally attached accessories.

## Part IV - Approval of vehicles with regard to the installation of approved fuel tank(s)
### 12. Definitions
Includes definitions for "Approval of a vehicle" and "Vehicle type".

### 13. Requirements for the installation of liquid fuel tanks
Vehicles installing tanks approved under Part III must comply with the installation requirements of paragraphs 5.4., 5.5., 5.6., 5.7., 5.8., 5.10., and 5.11.

## 14. Modifications of the type of vehicle or tank
Procedures for notifying the Type Approval Authority of modifications and potential need for further testing.

## 15. Conformity of production
Requires serially-produced vehicles or tanks bearing the approval mark to conform to the approved type, verified through random checks.

## 16. Penalties for non-conformity of production
Approval may be withdrawn if production does not conform or if vehicles fail checks. Other Contracting Parties must be notified.

## 17. Transitional provisions
Outlines phased implementation dates for the 02 and 03 series of amendments regarding grant and acceptance of approvals.

## 18. Names and addresses of Technical Services conducting approval tests, and of Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Communication forms for approval of vehicles (Appendix 1) and tanks (Appendix 2).
- **Annex 2**: Examples of arrangement of approval marks for different Parts of the Regulation.
- **Annex 3**: Procedure for frontal collision test against a barrier (purpose, installations, vehicle state, collision velocity).
- **Annex 4**: Procedure for rear-end collision test (purpose, impactor specifications, propulsion methods, vehicle state).
- **Annex 5**: Testing of fuel tanks made of plastic material, including:
    - Collision resistance at low temperature.
    - Mechanical strength (hydraulic test at elevated temperature).
    - Fuel permeability.
    - Resistance to fuel.
    - Resistance to fire (exposure to flame with/without screen).
    - Resistance to high temperature.
    - Marking requirements.
    - Appendices 1 and 2 provide test setup diagrams and firebrick specifications.
