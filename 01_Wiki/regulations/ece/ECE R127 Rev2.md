# UN Regulation No. 127 (Revision 2)
**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of motor vehicles with regard to their pedestrian safety performance**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to motor vehicles of categories M1 and N1.
However, vehicles of category N1 where the driver’s position "R-point" is either forward of the front axle or longitudinally rearwards of the front axle transverse centreline by a maximum of 1,100 mm, are exempted.
This Regulation does not apply to vehicles of category M1 above 2,500 kg maximum mass and which are derived from N1 category vehicles, and where the driver’s position "R-point" is either forward of the front axle or longitudinally rearwards of the front axle transverse centreline by a maximum of 1,100 mm.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Adult headform test area**: Area on the front structure bounded by WAD1700 (or 82.5 mm rearward of the bonnet leading edge), WAD2100 (or 82.5 mm forward of the bonnet rear reference line), and 82.5 mm inside the side reference line.
*   **Assessment Interval (AI)**: For the flexible lower legform impactor, the time interval from first contact to the last zero crossing of all femur and tibia bending moments after their first local maximum.
*   **Bonnet leading edge reference line**: Geometric trace of contact points between a 1,000 mm straight edge (inclined 50° rearwards) and the bonnet front surface.
*   **Bonnet rear reference line**: Geometric trace of the most rearward contact points between a 165 mm sphere and the front structure while maintaining contact with the windscreen.
*   **Bonnet top test area**: Comprises the child and adult headform test areas.
*   **Bumper test area**: The front vehicle fascia between the left and right corners of the bumper (minus 42 mm inboard of each corner), or between the outermost ends of the bumper beam (minus 42 mm inboard of each end), whichever is wider.
*   **Child headform test area**: Area on the front structure bounded by WAD1000 (or 82.5 mm rearward of the bonnet leading edge), WAD1700 (or 82.5 mm forward of the bonnet rear reference line), and 82.5 mm inside the side reference line.
*   **Corner of bumper**: The outermost point of contact of a specified corner gauge with the vehicle's outer contour/front fascia.
*   **Front structure**: All outer structures of the vehicle forward of the windscreen, A-pillars, and structures rearward of these (includes bumper, bonnet, wings, etc.).
*   **Head Injury Criterion (HIC)**: Calculated from accelerometer data using a specified formula (HIC shall not exceed limit values).
*   **Lower bumper reference line**: Geometric trace of lowermost contact points between a 700 mm straight edge (inclined 25° forwards) and the bumper.
*   **Measuring point**: The point on the vehicle's outer surface selected for assessment (test point/impact point).
*   **Normal ride attitude**: Vehicle position with mass in running order, tyres inflated, suspension set for 40 km/h.
*   **Side reference line**: Geometric trace of the highest contact points between a 700 mm straight edge (inclined inwards 45°) and the sides of the front structure.
*   **Upper bumper reference line**: Geometric trace of the uppermost contact points between a 700 mm straight edge (inclined rearwards 20°) and the bumper.
*   **Vehicle type with regard to pedestrian protection**: Category of vehicles which do not differ forward of the A-pillars in structure, main dimensions, materials of outer surfaces, or component arrangement in a way that negatively affects test results.
*   **Wrap Around Distance (WAD)**: Geometric trace on the outer surface described by a flexible tape held taut from a point level with the ground reference plane below the bumper front face.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application shall be submitted by the manufacturer or authorized representative, accompanied by an information document (Annex 1, Part 1) and a representative vehicle.

## 4. Approval
If the vehicle meets the requirements, approval is granted. An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate the series of amendments). An international approval mark (as per Annex 2) must be affixed to conforming vehicles.

## 5. Specifications
### 5.1. Legform Test to Bumper
Requirements depend on lower bumper height at the test position:
*   **< 425 mm**: Must meet flexible lower legform requirements.
*   **≥ 425 mm and < 500 mm**: Manufacturer may choose flexible lower legform OR upper legform requirements.
*   **≥ 500 mm**: Must meet upper legform requirements.

**5.1.1. Flexible lower legform to bumper:**
*   Maximum dynamic MCL elongation ≤ 22 mm.
*   Maximum dynamic ACL and PCL elongation ≤ 13 mm.
*   Maximum dynamic tibia bending moment ≤ 340 Nm (with possible relaxation to 380 Nm in nominated zones up to 264 mm total width).

**5.1.2. Upper legform to bumper:**
*   Sum of impact forces ≤ 7.5 kN.
*   Bending moment on impactor ≤ 510 Nm.

### 5.2. Headform Tests
**5.2.1. Child and adult headform tests:**
*   HIC ≤ 1000 over two thirds of the bonnet top test area.
*   HIC ≤ 1700 for the remaining area.
*   If only a child headform test area exists, HIC ≤ 1000 over two thirds, and ≤ 1700 for the remainder.

**5.2.2. Child headform impact:**
*   HIC ≤ 1000 over a minimum of one half of the child headform test area.
*   HIC ≤ 1700 for the remaining area.

## 6. Modification of Vehicle Type and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may grant an extension or require further tests.

## 7. Conformity of Production
Approved vehicles must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. Conformity control inspections should occur at least once every two years.

## 8. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met.

## 9. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority if production of an approved type ceases.

## 10. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## 11. Transitional Provisions
*   From the entry into force of the 02 series of amendments, Contracting Parties shall not refuse approvals under this amended version.
*   From 31 December 2017, only type approvals meeting the 02 series amendments shall be granted.
*   Extensions of approvals granted under earlier versions shall not be refused.
*   Approvals to earlier versions shall continue to be accepted.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Information document and communication forms.
*   **Annex 2**: Arrangements of approval marks.
*   **Annex 3**: General test conditions (temperature, humidity, site, vehicle preparation).
*   **Annex 4**: Test impactor specifications (flexible lower legform, upper legform, child/adult headform).
*   **Annex 5**: Test procedures for flexible lower legform, upper legform, and headform impactors.
*   **Annex 6**: Certification procedures for all impactors (static and dynamic tests).
