# UN Regulation No. 54 (Revision 3 - Amendment 5)

## 1. Scope
This Regulation covers new pneumatic tyres designed primarily for vehicles of categories M2, M3, N, O3 and O4. However, it does not apply to tyre types identified by speed category symbols corresponding to speeds below eighty (80) km/h.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Type of tyre**: Tyres which do not differ in essential characteristics such as manufacturer’s name, tyre-size designation, category of use, structure, speed category symbol, load-capacity indexes, and tyre cross-section.
*   **Category of use**: Normal tyre, Snow tyre, Special use tyre (including Professional off-road tyre).
*   **Structure**: Diagonal (bias-ply) or Radial.
*   **Tyre-size designation**: Includes nominal section width, nominal aspect ratio (or outer diameter), and a conventional number "d" denoting the nominal rim diameter.
*   **Load-capacity index**: One or two numbers indicating the load the tyre can carry.
*   **Speed category**: A symbol indicating the speed at which the tyre can carry the load indicated by its associated load-capacity index (e.g., F=80 km/h, G=90 km/h, up to H=210 km/h).
*   **Tyre Class**:
    *   **Class C2**: Load capacity index in single formation ≤ 121 and speed category symbol ≥ "N".
    *   **Class C3**: Load capacity index in single formation ≥ 122; OR load capacity index ≤ 121 and speed category symbol ≤ "M".

## 3. Markings
Tyres submitted for approval must bear specified markings on the sidewall(s), including:
*   Manufacturer’s name or Brand name/trademark.
*   Tyre-size designation.
*   Structure indication ("R" for radial, optional "D" or no marking for diagonal).
*   Speed-category symbol(s).
*   Load-capacity indices.
*   "M+S", "M.S" or "M&S" for snow tyres or certain special use tyres.
*   "TUBELESS" if designed for tubeless use.
*   Date of manufacture (four-digit group: week and year).
*   Regroovable symbol or word if applicable.
*   Inflation pressure index ("PSI") for endurance tests (kPa for tyres first approved after 1 Jan 2018).
*   Inscriptions for special use tyres ("MPT", "ML", "ET", "POR").
*   Prefix "LT" or suffixes "C"/"LT" for certain light truck tyres.
*   Suffix "CP" for tyres designed for motor caravans.
*   Inscription "FRT" for free-rolling tyres on trailer axles.

## 4. Application for Approval
The application, submitted by the manufacturer, must specify details such as tyre-size designation, manufacturer's name, category of use, structure, speed category, load-capacity indexes, dimensions, and test parameters. It must be accompanied by sketches/photographs and a test report or samples.

## 5. Approval
If the tyre type meets the requirements, approval is granted with an assigned approval number. An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country distinguishing number, plus approval number) must be affixed to conforming tyres. Provisions are included for subsequent retreading and upgrading of service descriptions in accordance with UN Regulation No. 109.

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. Dimensions of Tyres
*   Formulas are provided for calculating section width (`S`) and outer diameter (`D`).
*   Tyre dimensions must be measured according to the procedure in **Annex 6**.
*   Tolerances are specified for overall width and outer diameter, which vary for radial vs. diagonal tyres, normal vs. special use tyres, and snow tyres.

### 6.2. Load/Speed Endurance Test
*   Each tyre type must undergo at least one load/speed endurance test per the procedure in **Annex 7**.
*   After the test, the tyre must not exhibit tread, ply, or cord separation, chunking, or broken cords. The outer diameter change must be ≤ 3.5%.
*   Additional tests are required for tyres with additional load/speed combinations beyond the nominal values.

### 6.3. Tread Pattern
*   **Special use tyres** must have a block tread pattern with specific minimum tread depth and void-to-fill ratio (≥35%).
    *   C2 tyres: tread depth ≥ 11 mm.
    *   C3 tyres: tread depth ≥ 16 mm.
*   **Professional off-road tyres** must meet the above tread pattern requirements and have maximum speed ratings of ≤ Q for C2 tyres and ≤ K for C3 tyres.

## 7. Modification and Extension of Approval
Modifications to an approved tyre type must be notified. Changes to the tread pattern do not necessitate retesting. Extensions of approval must be communicated.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Production must conform to the approved type. The Type Approval Authority verifies conformity control methods at least once every two years per production facility.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if production does not conform or if samples fail tests. Withdrawal must be notified to other contracting parties.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority upon ceasing production, who then notifies other parties.

## 11. Names and Addresses
Contracting parties must communicate the details of their Technical Services, test laboratories, and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval actions.
*   **Annex 2**: Arrangement of the approval mark.
*   **Annex 3**: Examples of tyre marking arrangements.
*   **Annex 4**: List of load-capacity indices and corresponding loads (in kg).
*   **Annex 5**: Tyre-size designation and dimensions tables (Part I: European tyres; Part II: United States tyres).
*   **Annex 6**: Method of measuring pneumatic tyres.
*   **Annex 7**: Procedure for load/speed endurance tests (including test programmes and pressure index conversion table).
*   **Annex 8**: Table for variation of load capacity with speed.
*   **Annex 9**: Communication form for upgrading service description for retreading.
