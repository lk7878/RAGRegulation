**UN Regulation No. 13 - Revision 8 - Amendment 1**

**Supplement 11 to the 11 series of amendments – Date of entry into force: 9 October 2014**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles of categories M, N and O with regard to braking**

This amendment introduces provisions for automated connectors used for the brake electric/electronic interface between towing and towed vehicles.

**Key Amendments:**

1.  **New Definitions (Paragraphs 2.39., 2.40.):**
    *   **"Brake electric/electronic interface"**: The part of a separable electrical/electronic connection between the towing vehicle and the towed vehicle dedicated to the braking system.
    *   **"Automated connector"**: A system through which the electric and pneumatic connection between the towing vehicle and towed vehicle is made automatically without direct human intervention.

2.  **Electromagnetic Compatibility (Paragraph 5.1.1.4.):** Clarifies that braking system effectiveness, including the electric control line, must not be adversely affected by magnetic or electrical fields, demonstrated by compliance with UN Regulation No. 10 (specific amendment series apply based on vehicle charging capability).

3.  **Electric Control Line Requirements (Paragraph 5.1.3.6.):**
    *   Must conform to ISO 11992-1 and 11992-2:2003 (including Amendment 1:2007).
    *   For automated connectors, they must provide at least the same number of pins as the ISO 7638 connector and meet the requirements of the new **Annex 22**.
    *   Specifies exclusive use of data contacts for braking (priority) and running gear functions (ISO 11992-2), and exclusive use of power supply for these functions and related trailer information transfer.

4.  **Prohibition of Non-Automatic Shut-off Devices (Paragraph 5.1.3.8.):** Shut-off devices which are not automatically actuated are not permitted.

5.  **Allocation of Flexible Hoses and Cables (New Paragraph 5.1.3.9.):** Specifies that for tractor/semi-trailer combinations, flexible hoses and cables are part of the power-driven vehicle; for other cases, part of the trailer. This requirement is not applicable for automated connectors.

6.  **Connector Requirements for Vehicles Towing Trailers with ABS (Paragraph 5.2.1.23.):** Power-driven vehicles authorized to tow ABS-equipped trailers must be equipped with either an ISO 7638:2003 connector and/or an automated connector meeting **Annex 22** requirements.

7.  **Connector and Marking Requirements for Trailers (Paragraph 5.2.2.17.):**
    *   Trailers with an electric control line (and O3/O4 trailers with ABS) must be fitted with either an ISO 7638:2003 connector and/or an automated connector meeting **Annex 22**.
    *   Failure warning signals from the trailer must be activated via these connectors.
    *   Trailers equipped with an ISO 7638 connector must be indelibly marked to indicate braking system functionality when the connector is connected/disconnected. The marking must be visible during connection.
    *   A note specifies that for trailers equipped with both connector types, the marking must indicate that the ISO 7638 connector should not be connected when the automated connector is in use.

8.  **Information Document and Approval Form (Annex 2):** Adds new entries to the approval form (items 14.15., 14.15.1., 14.15.2.) regarding the presence of an automated connector, its compliance with Annex 22, and its category (A/B/C/D).

9.  **Test Procedures (Annexes 6, 13, 19):** Amendments to test procedures (response time measurement, anti-lock system EMC verification) to account for automated connectors and reference the new EMC compliance requirement from paragraph 5.1.1.4.

10. **New Annex 22: Requirements for the brake electric/electronic interface of an automated connector.**
    *   **Categories:** Defines four categories (A, B, C, D) for automated connectors based on vehicle combination type (tractor/semi-trailer vs. others) and compliance with standardized interfaces (Appendices 2 & 3). Category A (compliant with ISO 13044-2:2013) and Category C (to be defined) ensure compatibility within their category.
    *   **General Requirements:** The automated connector must achieve the same functional requirements as the ISO 7638 connector. Specifics include:
 *   Electrical characteristics and functionality of contacts must match ISO 7638.
 *   Exclusive use of data contacts and power supply for braking (priority), running gear functions, and related trailer information.
 *   Maximum cable length specifications for semi-trailer combinations.
 *   Requirements for vehicles equipped with both ISO 7638 and automated connectors (single functional path, priority to automated connector for automatic selection).
 *   Trailers with automated connectors must have a spring braking system (per Annex 8).
 *   Manufacturer must submit an information document describing functionality, limitations, category, and (for categories B & D) compatibility identification means.
 *   Vehicle handbook must warn about checking connector compatibility.
 *   Vehicles must have an indelible marking visible to the driver specifying the automated connector category (and type for categories B & D).
    *   **Appendix 1:** Provides example diagrams for electrical and pneumatic connections in various configurations (automated/manual mode, mixed equipment scenarios).
    *   **Appendix 2:** Specifies that Category A couplings shall conform to ISO 13044-2:2013.
    *   **Appendix 3:** Reserved for future definition of Category C standards.
