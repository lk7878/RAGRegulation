**Regulation No. 55 - Revision 1 – Amendment 2**

**Supplement 2 to the 01 series of amendments - Date of entry into force: 30 January 2011**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of mechanical coupling components of combinations of vehicles**

This document constitutes Amendment 2 to Revision 1 of Addendum 54 (Regulation No. 55) under the Revised 1958 Agreement.

**Key Amendments:**

1.  **New Definition (Paragraph 2.16):**
    *   Defines "Secondary coupling device" as a chain, wire rope, etc., fitted to a Class B coupling head. Its purpose is to prevent the trailer drawbar from touching the ground and provide some residual steering action on the trailer in the event of separation of the main coupling.

2.  **Annex 1 - New Paragraph 9.4:**
    *   Adds a requirement for the approval documentation to indicate whether a Class B coupling head is intended to be fitted to an unbraked O1 trailer (Yes/No).

3.  **Annex 5 - Amendments:**
    *   **Paragraph 1.5:** Requires manufacturers of towing brackets to incorporate attachment points for secondary couplings or devices necessary to enable the trailer to be stopped automatically in case of main coupling separation.
    *   **Paragraph 2.1:** Specifies that coupling heads of Class B50 must retain prescribed characteristics. Furthermore, coupling heads for a tractive force up to 800 kg and intended for unbraked O1 trailers must be fitted with a secondary coupling device or at least have attachment point(s) for one. These attachment points must be positioned so as not to restrict the normal articulation of the coupling.

4.  **Annex 6 - New Paragraph 3.2.4:**
    *   Specifies that the secondary coupling device attachment points mentioned in Annex 5, paragraph 2.1 must withstand a static force equivalent to 2D with a maximum of 15 kN.
