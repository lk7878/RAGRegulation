**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of mechanical coupling components of combinations of vehicles**

**Document Information**
*   **Authentic Legal Text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2020/27
*   **Document Reference:** E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.54/Rev.2/Amend.5 / E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.1/Add.54/Rev.2/Amend.5
*   **Agreement Context:** Addendum 54 to the Agreement concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical United Nations Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts... (Revision 3, including amendments effective 14 September 2017).
*   **Amendment Series:** 02 series of amendments.
*   **Entry into Force:** 25 September 2020.

**Amendment Content**

This document introduces Amendment 5 to UN Regulation No. 55, Revision 2, specifically modifying the transitional provisions.

**Paragraph 13. Amendments and Additions**

Paragraph 13 is amended to read: "13. Transitional provisions".

New paragraphs 13.1. to 13.5. are inserted after paragraph 13:

**13.1.** From the official date of entry into force of the 02 series of amendments, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall not refuse to grant or accept type approvals under the Regulation as amended by the 02 series.

**13.2.** From 1 September 2021, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation are not obliged to accept type approvals issued to the preceding series of amendments.

**13.3.** Notwithstanding paragraph 13.2., Contracting Parties shall continue to accept type approvals for mechanical coupling devices and components issued according to the preceding series of amendments.

**13.4.** Notwithstanding paragraph 13.2., Contracting Parties shall continue to accept type approvals issued according to the preceding series of amendments for vehicles not affected by the changes introduced by the 02 series.

**13.5.** Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall not refuse to grant type approvals according to any preceding series of amendments to this Regulation or extensions thereof.
