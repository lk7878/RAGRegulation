**Regulation No. 12 (R012) - Revision 4, Amendment 1**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to the protection of the driver against the steering mechanism in the event of impact.**

This document constitutes **Amendment 1** to **Revision 4** of **Addendum 11 (Regulation No. 12)** under the 1958 Agreement.

*   **Date of Document:** 6 August 2013
*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 15 July 2013 (as Supplement 2 to the 04 series of amendments)
*   **Parent Agreement:** Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Uniform Technical Prescriptions for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts... (Revision 2, incl. amendments effective 16 October 1995). Formerly known as the "Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Uniform Conditions of Approval and Reciprocal Recognition of Approval for Motor Vehicle Equipment and Parts" (Geneva, 20 March 1958).

**Textual Amendments:**

1.  **Paragraph 2.16.2.** is amended to read:
    > "2.16.2. 'Passenger compartment … provided for protecting the occupants from direct contact with high voltage live parts.'"

2.  **Paragraph 13.3.6.** is amended to read:
    > "13.3.6. Where … Parties applying this Regulation may refuse national approval of such vehicles ..."

3.  **Editorial Correction:** Throughout the entire text of the Regulation, the acronym **"RESS"** is to be corrected to read **"REESS"**.
