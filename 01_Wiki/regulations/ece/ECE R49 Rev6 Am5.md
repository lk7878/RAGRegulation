# UN Regulation No. 49 Revision 6 - Amendment 5

**Supplement 5 to the 06 series of amendments**
**Date of entry into force: 19 July 2018**

This document constitutes Amendment 5 to Revision 6 of UN Regulation No. 49, concerning measures against the emission of gaseous and particulate pollutants from compression-ignition and positive ignition engines for use in vehicles.

## Main Amendments

### 1. General Provisions (Paragraph 4.)
*   **4.6.2. (Market Fuels):** Amended to specify requirements when manufacturers permit engine families to run on market fuels not complying with reference fuels or CEN standards (e.g., FAME B100, B20/B30 blends, paraffinic fuel). Manufacturers must declare the fuels, demonstrate compliance on declared fuels, and be liable for in-service conformity on those fuels and their blends.
*   **4.11.5. (Replacement Pollution Control Devices):** Amended to state that such devices shall be type-approved according to specific testing requirements in Annex 13.
*   **4.12.3.3.6. & 4.12.3.3.7. (Approval Mark for Gaseous Fuels):** Amended to detail the letters and digits to be included in the approval mark for natural gas/biomethane fuelled engines and dual-fuel engines to indicate the approved gas range(s) and engine type.
*   **4.12.7.2.:** Amended (text incomplete in OCR).

### 2. Transitional Provisions (Paragraph 13.)
*   **13.2.4. & 13.2.5.:** New paragraphs. Contracting Parties may refuse type approval for non-compliance with this Supplement 5 (with exceptions) from its entry into force, and **shall grant type approval only if compliant from 1 September 2018**.
*   **13.3.4.:** New paragraph. Type approvals granted under the 06 series which do not comply with paragraph 13.2.5. **shall cease to be valid from 1 September 2019**.
*   **13.4.4.:** New paragraph. Modified in-service testing requirements do not apply retroactively to engines/vehicles not approved according to those requirements.

### 3. Test Procedures and Technical Requirements
*   **Annex 3, Table 1:** Amended table detailing implementation dates, power thresholds, and requirements for OBD monitors, reagent quality, and IUPR for different vehicle/engine categories (Characters A, B, C, D).
*   **Annex 4 (Measurement of Emissions):**
    *   **7.8.4. (Drift Verification):** Amended definitions of "test cycle" for drift check procedures.
    *   **8.4.1.7. (Carbon Balance Method):** Amended exhaust mass flow calculation formula.
    *   **9.3.9.4.1. (Sample Dryer Efficiency):** Amended humidity specification for CLD analysers.
    *   **9.4.2. (Dilution System Requirements):** Amended temperature and residence time requirements for particulate sampling.
    *   **9.5.5. (Total System Verification):** Amended u-factor for propane in CVS verification.
    *   **10.4.2. (Particle Number Determination - Partial Flow):** Amended calculation formula.
*   **Appendix 3, A.3.2. (Regression Analysis):** Amended formulas for slope, intercept, standard error, and coefficient of determination.
*   **Annex 8 (In-Service Conformity):**
    *   **2.1., 2.3., 4.1., 4.4.1., 4.4.2., 4.4.2.1., 4.4.3.:** Amendments related to test representativeness, vehicle payload, and requirements for lubricating oil, fuel, and reagent.
    *   **4.5., 4.5.1., 4.5.2., 4.5.3., 4.5.4.:** Amended and new paragraphs defining trip composition (urban/rural/motorway shares) and average speeds for different vehicle categories (M1, N1, N2, M2, M3, N3). Paragraph 4.5.3. deleted and 4.5.4. renumbered.
    *   **4.6.5., 4.6.10.:** Amendments on test duration and conditions for voiding a trip.
*   **Annex 8 - Appendix 1 (PEMS Testing):**
    *   **A.1.1., A.1.2.2., A.1.2.6.1., A.1.2.6.2., A.1.3.2.1.:** Amendments on measurement principles, data parameters, test start/run conditions, and data validation criteria.
    *   **A.1.4.1., A.1.4.2.2., A.1.4.3.1.:** Major amendments on the moving averaging window method. Introduces two sets of criteria for selecting valid windows based on power threshold (20%->15% or 10%) and maximum window duration, with applicability tied to the dates in paragraphs 13.2.5. and 13.3.4.
*   **Annex 8 - Appendix 2 (PEMS Equipment):** Amendments to gas analyser specifications and EFM installation requirements.
*   **Annex 9A (OBD):** Amendments to OBD threshold limits and their equivalence with other regulations.
*   **Annex 10 - Appendix 1:** New paragraph A.1.2.3. requiring manufacturers to ensure vehicles can be tested with PEMS by independent parties. Amendment to A.1.3.1. on vehicle payload for PEMS demonstration.
*   **Annex 13 (Replacement Pollution Control Devices):**
    *   **4.3.2.4.:** Amendment linking durability testing to Appendix 4.
    *   **4.3.5., 4.6. to 4.6.5.:** New paragraphs on fuel requirements for testing and compatibility requirements with NOx control measures.
    *   **Appendix 4 (Durability Procedure):** Completely new, detailed procedure for evaluating emissions performance of replacement pollution control devices. Includes data collection phase, service accumulation schedule calculation (thermal and lubricant ageing), and specific test sequences.
    *   **Appendices 5-9:** New appendices providing the thermal ageing sequence, a chassis dynamometer/on-road test cycle, and examples/flowcharts for the service accumulation schedule.
