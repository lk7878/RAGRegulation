# Regulation No. 106
**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of pneumatic tyres for agricultural vehicles and their trailers**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation covers new pneumatic tyres designed primarily, but not only, for agricultural and forestry vehicles (power-driven vehicles in category T1), agricultural machines (power-driven and trailed) and agricultural trailers, and identified by speed category symbols corresponding to speeds of 65 km/h (speed symbol "D") and below.

It does not apply to tyre types designated primarily for other purposes, such as:
(a) Construction application (tyres marked "Industrial" or "IND" or "R4"or "F3");
(b) Earth-moving equipment;
(c) Industrial and lift trucks.

## 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation:
*   **2.1. "Type of agricultural tyre"**: A category of tyres which do not differ in essential respects such as manufacturer, tyre-size designation, category of use, structure, speed category symbol, load capacity index, and tyre cross-section.
*   **2.3. "Structure of a tyre"**: Technical characteristics of the tyre carcass (Diagonal/Bias-ply, Bias-belted, Radial).
*   **2.15. "Tyre-size designation"**: A designation showing nominal section width (mm), nominal aspect ratio, structure indicator ("-"/"D", "R", "B"), nominal rim diameter, and optional markings (e.g., "IMP", "FRONT", "IF", "VF", "CFO", "CHO").
*   **2.20. "Tractor-drive wheel tyre"**: Tyre designed for driven axles of agricultural/forestry tractors, suitable for sustained high torque service.
*   **2.21. "Tractor steering wheel tyre"**: Tyre designed for non-driven axles of agricultural/forestry tractors.
*   **2.22. "Implement tyre"**: Tyre designed primarily for agricultural machines/implements (category S) or agricultural trailers (category R).
*   **2.26. "Service description"**: Association of a load capacity index with a speed category symbol.
*   **2.28. "Load-capacity index"**: A number indicating the load the tyre can carry in single formation at its rated speed (list in Annex 4).
*   **2.29. "Speed category"**: Reference speed expressed by a symbol (A2:10 km/h, A4:20, A6:30, A8:40, B:50, D:65).
*   **2.30. "Table: Variation of load capacity with speed"**: Tables in Annex 7 showing maximum load rating variations at speeds different from the rated speed category symbol.

## 3. Markings
Tyres must bear the following markings, clearly and legibly moulded on both sidewalls (except date of manufacture and approval mark on one sidewall only):
*   Manufacturer's trade name or mark.
*   Tyre-size designation.
*   Indication of structure (optional "RADIAL" for radial, "BIAS-BELTED" for bias-belted).
*   Service description (load index + speed symbol). For implement tyres, supplemented with relevant application symbol (traction or trailer).
*   Supplementary service description (if applicable).
*   Specific inscriptions (e.g., "DEEP"/"R-2" for special tread, "F-1"/"F-2" for steering tyres, "LS-1" to "LS-4" for forestry, "I-3" for implement traction tread, "IMPLEMENT", "TUBELESS", "IF"/"VF", "CFO"/"CHO").
*   Pictogram with maximum inflation pressure for bead seating (Annex 11).
*   Date of manufacture (four digits: week and year).
*   Type approval mark (model in Annex 2).

## 4. Application for Approval
Submitted by the holder of the trade name or mark, specifying details including tyre size, trade name, category of use, structure, speed symbol, load index, rim configuration, inflation pressures, and submitting technical file/samples as requested.

## 5. Approval
*   Granted if requirements of paragraph 6 are met.
*   An approval number is assigned.
*   An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country number, Regulation number, and approval number) must be affixed to conforming tyres.

## 6. Requirements
*   **6.1. & 6.2. Section Width and Outer Diameter Calculations**: Formulas provided for general tyres. For tyres listed in Annex 5, values are given in tables.
*   **6.3. & 6.4. Tolerances**: Specified for overall width and outer diameter, with coefficients (a, b) varying by category of use (steering, drive, implement, forestry) and structure (radial, diagonal). Specific tolerances for Annex 5 tyres may apply.
*   **6.5. Test Procedures**:
    *   Annex 6: Method for measuring tyre dimensions.
    *   Annex 8: Test procedure to assess tyre resistance to bursting (2.5x specified pressure, min 6 bar, max 10 bar, held for 10 min).
    *   Annex 9: Load/speed test procedure for tyres marked with speed symbol "D" (test drum or trailer method).

## 7. Modification of Tyre Type and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority. Changes to tread pattern do not necessitate repeating tests.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Tyres must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. Conformity control methods are subject to verification by the Type Approval Authority at least once every two years.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not complied with.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority.

## 11. Names and Addresses
Parties to the Agreement shall communicate details of Technical Services, test laboratories, and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form (Model).
*   **Annex 2**: Arrangement of approval mark.
*   **Annex 3**: Arrangement of tyre markings (with examples and minimum heights for drive wheel, steering wheel, implement, and forestry tyres).
*   **Annex 4**: List of load capacity indices (LI) and corresponding maximum mass (kg).
*   **Annex 5**: Tables of theoretical rim, outer diameter and nominal section width for specific tyre size designations (Agricultural steering wheels, Drive wheel tyres - normal/low section, Implement tyres - normal/low section, High flotation tyres).
*   **Annex 6**: Test method for measuring tyre dimensions.
*   **Annex 7**: Variation of load capacity with speed tables for:
    *   Part A: Drive wheel tyres for agricultural tractors.
    *   Part B: Steering wheel tyres.
    *   Part C: Implement tyres.
    *   Part D: Tyres for forestry machines.
*   **Annex 8**: Test procedure to assess tyre resistance to bursting.
*   **Annex 9**: Load/speed test procedure.
*   **Annex 10**: Tyre classification code (optional marking, e.g., F-1, I-3, R-2, LS-1).
*   **Annex 11**: Example pictogram for maximum inflation pressure during bead seating.
