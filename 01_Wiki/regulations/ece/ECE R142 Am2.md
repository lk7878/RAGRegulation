**UN Regulation No. 142 (Amendment 2) - Tyre Installation**

**1. Scope**
This Regulation applies to vehicles of categories M, N and O1 with regard to the installation of their tyres. It does not apply to:
*   Vehicles whose conditions of use are incompatible with the characteristics of tyres of class C1, C2 or C3.
*   The installation of:
    *   Temporary use spare units.
    *   Run-flat tyres/systems in flat tyre running mode.
    *   Extended mobility tyres in flat tyre running mode.
    *   Tyre pressure monitoring systems (TPMS).

**2. Definitions**
Key definitions include:
*   **Vehicle type with regard to tyre installation:** Vehicles not differing in essential aspects such as tyre types, tyre size designations, wheel dimensions/offsets, speed/load capabilities, and wheel guard characteristics.
*   **Tyre Classes:**
    *   **C1:** Conforms to UN R30.
    *   **C2:** Conforms to UN R54, load index ≤121, speed symbol ≥ 'N'.
    *   **C3:** Conforms to UN R54, load index ≥122 OR load index ≤121 and speed symbol ≤ 'M'.
*   **Type of tyre:** A range not differing in tyre class (C1/C2/C3) and the specific type characteristics defined in UN R30 (C1) or UN R54 (C2/C3).
*   **Normal tyre:** Tyre or run-flat tyre for normal on-road use.
*   **Snow tyre:** Tyre designed for better performance in snow than a normal tyre.
*   **Snow tyre for severe conditions:** Snow tyre designed for severe conditions, meeting UN R117 requirements.
*   **Special use tyre:** Tyre for mixed on-/off-road or special duty.
*   **Standard unit:** Assembly of wheel and tyre for normal operation.
*   **Standard spare unit:** Identical to a standard unit in size, offset, and structure (may differ in wheel material/fixing design).
*   **Temporary-use spare unit:** Does not meet standard spare unit definition, as described in UN R64.

**3. Application for Approval**
Submitted by the manufacturer or authorized representative, accompanied by required documents and a representative vehicle or simulation tool.

**4. Approval**
*   Granted if requirements are met.
*   An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments (e.g., 01).
*   An international approval mark (circle with 'E', country code, "142R", approval number) must be affixed to each vehicle.
*   Details are communicated to Contracting Parties.

**5. Specifications**
*   **5.1 General:** Tyres must meet this Regulation and the applicable requirements of UN R30, R54, and R117.
*   **5.2 Performance Requirements:**
    *   **5.2.1 Tyre Fitment:** Tyres on the same axle must be of the same type. All normal tyres must have the same structure. Wheel arches must allow unrestricted movement with the largest permissible tyres.
    *   **5.2.2 Load Capacity:** Tyre maximum load rating must be at least:
 *   Single formation (same type): Half of the technically permissible maximum axle mass.
 *   Single formation (different types): Half of the mass for the relevant axle.
 *   Dual C1 tyres: 0.27 times the axle mass.
 *   Dual C2/C3 tyres: 0.25 times the axle mass (using dual load index).
 *   Determined per UN R30 (C1) or UN R54 load-speed tables (C2/C3).
 *   Manufacturer must provide info on suitable replacement tyres.
    *   **5.2.3 Speed Capacity:** Tyres must bear a speed category symbol compatible with the vehicle's maximum design speed, considering load-speed combinations in UN R30/R54.
 *   **Exceptions (with speed warning label if needed):**
 *   Snow tyres for severe conditions (must be ≥160 km/h or > design speed).
 *   Special use tyres.
 *   Vehicles with speed limitation devices (SLD) per UN R89 (M2/M3/N2/N3) or onboard speed limitation (M1/N1). Tyre speed symbol must be compatible with the set limitation speed.
 *   Manufacturer must provide info on suitable replacement tyres.
    *   **5.2.4 Special Cases:**
 *   **Towing (M1/N1):** Rear tyre load may be exceeded by ≤15%. Handbook must specify max speed (≤100 km/h) and increased tyre pressure (+20 kPa min.).
 *   **Trailers (O1/O2, ≤100 km/h, C1 tyres):** Load rating must be ≥0.45 x axle mass (single) or ≥0.24 x axle mass (dual). A speed warning label is required.
 *   **Special Vehicles (C2/C3):** Load capacity coefficients apply instead of speed-load tables (e.g., 1.15 for buses per UN R107, 1.10 for certain urban N2 vehicles ≤60 km/h). Speed capacity requirements do not apply.
 *   **Exceptional Vehicles:** Vehicles incompatible with C1/C2/C3 tyres may use tyres approved under UN R75 or R106, subject to approval authority agreement.
    *   **5.2.5 Spare Wheels/Tyres:** If provided, must be either:
 *   A standard spare unit.
 *   A temporary-use spare unit (not permitted for most M2/M3/N2 vehicles >7500 kg).
 *   Vehicles with temporary-use spare units or run-flat tyres must comply with UN R64.
 *   Specific fitting instructions must be in the handbook.

**6. Modification and Extension of Approval**
Procedures for notifying the Type Approval Authority of modifications, classified as "revisions" (minor) or "extensions" (requiring further checks or involving regulation changes).

**7. Conformity of Production**
Manufactured vehicles must conform to the approved type. Conformity control methods are subject to verification by the approval authority at least biennially.

**8. Penalties for Non-Conformity**
Approval may be withdrawn for non-conformity, with notification to other Contracting Parties.

**9. Production Discontinued**
Manufacturer must inform the approval authority if production of an approved type ceases.

**10. Transitional Provisions**
*   From 30 Sep 2021: Contracting Parties must grant/accept approvals under the 01 series.
*   From 6 Jul 2022: Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept new approvals to the preceding series.
*   Until 6 Jul 2022: Must accept approvals to the preceding series issued before that date.
*   Extensions of existing approvals to preceding series may continue to be granted.

**11. Technical Services and Approval Authorities**
Contracting Parties must communicate the relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

**Annexes**
*   **Annex 1:** Information document template for approval application.
*   **Annex 2:** Communication form for granting, extending, refusing, or withdrawing approval.
*   **Annex 3:** Example of the arrangement of the approval mark.
