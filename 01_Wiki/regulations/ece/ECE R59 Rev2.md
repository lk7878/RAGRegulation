**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of replacement silencing systems**

**1. Scope**
This Regulation covers exhaust replacement silencing systems for vehicles of categories M and N.

**2. Definitions**
For the purpose of this Regulation:
*   **2.1. "Silencing systems"** means a complete set of components necessary for limiting the sound produced by an engine, its intake and exhaust (the exhaust manifold(s), the catalyst(s) and emission after-treatment device(s) are not considered part of the sound reduction system; these parts belong to the engine).
*   **2.2. "Exhaust silencing system"** means a complete set of components necessary for limiting the sound produced by the engine of a motor vehicle and its exhaust of a motor engine.
*   **2.3. "Exhaust silencing system or exhaust silencing system component with variable geometry"** means an exhaust silencing system or exhaust silencing system component containing one or more moving parts or devices which, by changing the exhaust silencing system or exhaust silencing system component geometry, may change its sound reduction performance.
*   **2.4. "Exhaust silencing system component"** means one of the separate components which together form the exhaust silencing system (e.g. silencer proper, expansion chamber, resonator).
*   **2.5. "Exhaust silencing system of different types"** means exhaust silencing systems which significantly differ in respect of at least one of the following: trade names or trademarks of their components; the characteristics of the materials constituting their components, except for the coating of those components; the shape or size of their components; the operating principles of at least one of their components; the assembly of their components; the number of exhaust silencing systems or components.
*   **2.6. "Exhaust replacement silencing system or components of said system"** means any part of the exhaust silencing system defined in paragraph 2.2. above intended for use on a vehicle, other than a part of the type fitted to this vehicle when submitted for type approval pursuant to this Regulation.
*   **2.7. "Design family of exhaust replacement silencing systems or replacement systems components"** means exhaust silencing systems or components thereof belonging to the same design family if all of the characteristics according to paragraph 6.4.1. are the same.
*   **2.8. "Approval of an exhaust replacement silencing system or components of said exhaust system"** means the approval of the whole or part of an exhaust silencing system adaptable to one or several specified types of motor vehicles, as regards the limitation of their noise level.
*   **2.9. "Vehicle type"** means a category of motor vehicles which does not differ in such essential respects as: the type of engine, number and capacity of cylinders, number and type of carburettors or injection system, arrangement of valves or the type of electric motor; "Rated maximum net power" Pn; the silencing system.

**3. Application for approval**
*   **3.1.** The application shall be submitted by the manufacturer or representative.
*   **3.2.** It shall be accompanied by documents in triplicate including: a description of the intended vehicle type(s); a description and drawings of the system; mounting instructions; an information document (Annex 1 Appendix).
*   **3.3.** The Technical Service may request samples of the system, the original system, a representative vehicle, and/or a separate engine for testing.

**4. Markings**
*   **4.1.** Each component (excluding tubes and mounting accessories) shall bear the trade name/mark and commercial description.
*   **4.2.** The marking shall include the character "A", "B", or "C" indicating the phase of Regulation No. 51 limit values the system is intended for.
*   **4.3.** Markings shall be clear and indelible.

**5. Approval**
*   **5.1.** Approval is granted if the system meets the requirements of paragraph 6.
*   **5.2.** An approval number is assigned.
*   **5.3.** Notice of approval or refusal is communicated to applying Parties.
*   **5.4.** An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country number, Regulation No. 59R, dash, approval number) shall be affixed.
*   **5.5.** The mark must be legible and indelible when mounted.
*   **5.6.** A component may bear multiple approval marks.

**6. Specifications**
*   **6.1. General specifications:** The system must be designed for normal use conditions, resistant to vibration and corrosion. Prescriptions against tampering and for multi-mode systems are given.
*   **6.2. Specifications regarding sound levels:** Sound reduction performance is verified per methods in Regulation No. 51. Limits for noise increase over the original system or type approval value are specified for both moving and stationary vehicle tests (e.g., not more than 1 dB(A) for moving vehicle). Additional requirements for systems with variable geometry are outlined.
*   **6.3. Measurement of the vehicle performances:** The back pressure of the replacement system must not exceed that of the original system by more than 25%. Test methods using an engine or a vehicle are described.
*   **6.4. Additional specifications regarding replacement silencing systems or components containing acoustically absorbing fibrous materials:** Conditions for using fibrous materials are defined. If not exempt, the system must undergo conditioning (10,000 km road operation, test bench conditioning, or pulsation conditioning) and subsequent noise check.

**7. Extension of approval**
The manufacturer may request an extension of approval to other vehicle types, following the procedure in paragraph 3.

**8. Modification of the type of exhaust silencing system**
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further tests.

**9. Conformity of production**
Production must conform to the approved type. The holder of approval must ensure quality control procedures, have necessary test equipment, record results, and allow inspections. For COP, the limit values apply with an additional margin of 1 dB(A).

**10. Information intended for users and technical inspection**
Each system must be accompanied by a paper document containing information as specified in Annex 6 (e.g., approval number, manufacturer details, intended vehicles, mounting instructions).

**11. Penalties for non-conformity of production**
Approval may be withdrawn if COP requirements are not met.

**12. Production definitively discontinued**
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority if production ceases.

**13. Names and addresses of Technical Services responsible for conducting approval tests, and of Type Approval Authorities**
Contracting Parties shall communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.
