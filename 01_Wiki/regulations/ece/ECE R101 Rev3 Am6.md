**Document Information**
*   **Agreement:** Concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical United Nations Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts which can be Fitted and/or be Used on Wheeled Vehicles and the Conditions for Reciprocal Recognition of Approvals Granted on the Basis of these United Nations Regulations (Revision 3, including amendments in force as of 14 September 2017).
*   **Regulation:** Addendum 100 – UN Regulation No. 101, Revision 3 – Amendment 6.
*   **Supplement:** Supplement 7 to the 01 series of amendments.
*   **Official Entry into Force:** 10 October 2017.
*   **Document Date:** 7 December 2017.
*   **Authentic Text Reference:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2017/44 (1622624).

**Scope**
This regulation establishes uniform provisions for the type approval of:
1.  Passenger cars powered solely by an internal combustion engine, or by a hybrid electric power train, with regard to the measurement of **carbon dioxide emissions, fuel consumption, and/or electric energy consumption and electric range**.
2.  Vehicles of categories M and N powered solely by an electric power train, with regard to the measurement of **electric energy consumption and electric range**.

**Amendment Content**
The amendment inserts a new paragraph **13.5.**, which states:

"13.5. As from the official date of entry into force of Supplement 7 to the 01 series of amendments to UN Regulation No. 101, and by way of derogation to the obligations of Contracting Parties during the transitional period set out in paragraphs 13.1. to 13.4. above, the Contracting Parties applying this Regulation and also applying in their national/regional territory the provisions on the Worldwide Harmonized Light Vehicle Test Procedure (WLTP) set out in UN Global Technical Regulation No. 15 may no longer accept type approvals granted on the basis of this Regulation as an alternative to compliance with their national/regional legislation."

**Historical Note**
The document references the former titles of the underlying 1958 Agreement, noting its evolution from the original 1958 version to Revision 2 in 1995.
