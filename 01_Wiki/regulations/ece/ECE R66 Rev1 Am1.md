**Regulation No. 66 - Revision 1 - Amendment 1**

**Scope**
1.1. This Regulation applies to single-deck rigid or articulated vehicles belonging to Classes II or III.
1.2. At the request of the manufacturer, this Regulation may also apply to single-deck rigid or articulated vehicles belonging to Classes other than II or III.

**Footnotes**
*   **Footnote 1/:** As defined in Annex 7 to the Consolidated Resolution on the Construction of vehicles (R.E.3), (document TRANS/WP.29/78/Rev.1/Amend.2 as last amended by Amend.4).
