# UN Regulation No. 52 (Revision 3)
**UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF M2 AND M3 SMALL CAPACITY VEHICLES WITH REGARD TO THEIR GENERAL CONSTRUCTION**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to single-deck rigid vehicles of categories M2 and M3 designed and constructed for the carriage of seated or standing persons and having a capacity not exceeding 22 passengers in addition to the driver.

**Note:** Technical provisions for the carriage of passengers with reduced mobility are outside the scope of this Regulation until harmonized provisions are finalized.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Vehicle:** Single-deck vehicle of category M2 or M3 for carriage of seated/standing persons (≤22 passengers + driver). Two classes:
    *   **Class A:** Designed to carry standing passengers.
    *   **Class B:** Not designed to carry standing passengers.
*   **Low floor vehicle:** Vehicle where ≥35% of standing area is a single step-free area.
*   **Service door, Emergency door, Emergency window, Escape hatch, Exit, Gangway, Access passage, Driver's compartment.**
*   **Unladen kerb mass (MK), Unladen mass (MV), Technical maximum mass (MT), Technically permissible maximum axle mass.**
*   **Passenger, Passenger with reduced mobility, Passenger compartment.**
*   **Automatically operated service door, Starting prevention device, Driver operated service door.**

**Measurement Condition:** Unless stated otherwise, measurements are made with the vehicle at its unladen kerb mass (MK) on smooth, horizontal ground (kneeling system not in operation).

## 3. Application for Approval
Submitted by the manufacturer or representative. Must include detailed description, drawings, and particulars such as masses, baggage provisions, passenger areas (S0, S1), number of seating places (Ps), total intended passengers (N), and vehicle class (A or B). A representative vehicle must be submitted for testing.

## 4. Approval
*   Granted if the vehicle meets the specifications in paragraph 5.
*   An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate the series of amendments).
*   An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, regulation number "52R", approval number, and class letter A or B) must be affixed to conforming vehicles.
*   Examples of approval mark arrangements are given in Annex 2.

## 5. Specifications
### 5.1. Load Distribution Between Axles and Loading Conditions
Specifies minimum front axle load percentages for unladen and laden conditions for Class A and B vehicles. Defines laden condition for calculation purposes.

### 5.2. Area Available for Passengers
Defines methods to calculate:
*   **S0:** Total surface area available for passengers.
*   **S1:** Surface area available for standing passengers (Class A only), with specific exclusions (e.g., areas with slope >8%, clear height <190 cm, space in front of seats).

### 5.3. Passenger Capacity
The total number of passengers (N) is calculated based on seating places (Ps), standing area (S1), and mass constraints. Formulas and assumed values for passenger mass (Q), standing area per passenger (Ssp), and baggage loads (L, R) are provided for Class A and B.
Vehicles must be clearly marked inside with the number of seating places (Ps), total passengers (N), and number of wheelchair spaces (if any).

### 5.4. Strength of the Superstructure
For Class B vehicles only, the structure must be strong enough to withstand a static roof load equal to the vehicle's technical maximum mass (MT).

### 5.5. Protection Against Fire Risks
Comprehensive requirements covering:
*   **Engine compartment:** Use of materials, prevention of fuel/oil accumulation, heat barriers.
*   **Fuel filler-holes:** Location, accessibility, cap design, leak prevention.
*   **Fuel tanks:** Location, protection, construction, pressure testing.
*   **Fuel-feed systems:** Placement, protection, and design of lines.
*   **Emergency switch (if fitted):** Location, marking, and required functions (engine stop, battery isolation, hazard lights).
*   **Electrical equipment and wiring:** Insulation, circuit protection, routing.
*   **Batteries:** Security, compartment ventilation.
*   **Fire extinguishers and first-aid equipment:** Minimum rating (8A or 21B), storage space.
*   **Materials:** Flammable materials near exhaust must be shielded.

### 5.6. Exits
#### 5.6.1. Number
*   Minimum two doors (service + emergency, or two service).
*   Minimum total number of exits (doors + escape hatches counted as exits) based on passenger capacity (≤16 passengers: 3 exits; >16: 4 exits). At least one must be an escape hatch.
*   Driver's compartment requires two exits if isolated.

#### 5.6.2. Siting
*   Service door(s) on traffic side or rear.
*   At least one exit on each side.
*   At least one exit in both forward and rearward halves of passenger space.
*   At least one exit in rear/front face unless an escape hatch is fitted.

#### 5.6.3. Minimum Dimensions
Specifies minimum dimensions for:
*   **Service door:** Entry height (Class A: 165 cm, Class B: 150 cm), aperture height, width (single: 65 cm, double: 120 cm) with permitted reductions.
*   **Emergency door:** Height: 125 cm, Width: 55 cm.
*   **Emergency window:** Aperture area: 4000 cm² (must inscribe 50x70 cm rectangle).
*   **Escape hatch:** Aperture area: 4000 cm² (must inscribe 50x70 cm rectangle).
*   Special reduced dimensions apply to Class B vehicles ≤3.5 tonnes with ≤12 seats and unobstructed access to two doors.

#### 5.6.4. – 5.6.10. Technical Requirements for Doors, Windows, Hatches, and Steps
Detailed requirements for operation, controls, safety devices, and markings for:
*   Manually-operated, power-operated, and automatically-operated service doors (including clamping force limits ≤150 N, with peak ≤300 N).
*   Emergency doors and windows (operation, hinges, breakable glass, dimensions).
*   Escape hatches.
*   Retractable steps (synchronization, safety interlocks, strength).

#### 5.6.11. Markings
Emergency exits and their controls must be clearly marked with text or symbols and instructions, in a language determined by the registering country.

### 5.7. Interior Arrangements
#### 5.7.1. Access to Service Door
Defines a dual-panel test gauge and procedure to ensure clear access from the door into the vehicle. Includes provisions for crew folding seats and exceptions for small Class B vehicles.

#### 5.7.2. Access to Emergency Doors
Defines a two-cylinder test gauge for clear access.

#### 5.7.3. & 5.7.4. Access to Emergency Windows and Escape Hatches
Specifies test gauges and requirements for clear access.

#### 5.7.5. Gangways
Defines a gauging device (two cylinders with a cone) that must pass freely. Dimensions differ for Class A (overall H: 190 cm) and Class B (overall H: 150 cm).

#### 5.7.6. Slope of the Gangway
Max longitudinal slope: Class A: 8%, Class B: 12.5%. Max transversal slope: 5% for all.

#### 5.7.7. Steps
Specifies maximum/minimum heights and minimum depths for steps at doors and inside the vehicle. Maximum height of first step from ground, minimum step area (800 cm²), and max slope (5%).

#### 5.7.8. Passenger Seats and Space
*   **Clear Space:** Minimum free space above each seating position (90 cm from seat cushion, 135 cm from floor; reduced for small Class B vehicles and seats next to driver).
*   **Seat Dimensions:** Minimum widths for individual and continuous seats.
*   **Seat Cushion:** Height (40-50 cm from floor) and depth (≥35 cm).
*   **Seat Spacing:** Minimum distances between rows (≥65 cm) and between facing seats (≥130 cm).
*   **Foot Space:** Minimum clear foot space required.
*   **Seats for Disabled:** At least two specially designated and equipped seats must be provided.

### 5.8. Artificial Interior Lighting
Required for passenger compartment, steps, access to exits, exit markings/controls, and obstacle areas.

### 5.9. Handrails and Handholds
*   **General:** Strength, design, dimensions (section: 2-4.5 cm), clearance (≥4 cm), non-slip surface.
*   **Class A Vehicles:** Must be provided so that any standing passenger can reach at least two handholds (80-190 cm high), with at least one ≤150 cm high.
*   **Service Doors:** Handholds required on each side, with specific positioning for persons on ground or steps.
*   **Handicapped:** Handrails/holds leading to designated disabled seats must account for their needs.

### 5.10. Guarding of Stepwells
A guard (min. height 80 cm) or safety-belt must be fitted where seated passengers could be thrown into a stepwell during heavy braking.

### 5.11. Luggage Racks
Driver must be protected from falling objects.

### 5.12. Trap Doors
Must be secure, not openable without tools, with projections ≤8 mm above floor.

### 5.13. Reversing Lights
Required if a service door is in the rear face, conforming to UN R048.

## 6. Modification and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified. The approval may be extended after evaluation.

## 7. Conformity of Production
Vehicles bearing the approval mark must conform to the approved type. Random checks are to be conducted.

## 8. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn for non-conformity.

## 9. Production Definitely Discontinued
Manufacturer must inform the approval authority.

## 10. Transitional Provisions
*   No new approvals under this Regulation after 1 April 2008.
*   Extensions of existing approvals permitted.
*   From 12 August 2010, Contracting Parties may refuse first registration of vehicles not meeting UN R107 (02 series).

## 11. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments
To be communicated to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Model communication form for approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal.
*   **Annex 2:** Examples of approval mark arrangements.
*   **Annex 3:** Explanatory diagrams for access, gangways, seats, steps, handhold testing, etc.
*   **Annex 4:** Guidelines for measuring the closing forces of power-operated doors (definitions, measurement points, method, device specifications).
