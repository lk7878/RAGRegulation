# UN Regulation No. 132 (Revision 1) - Retrofit Emission Control Devices (REC)

## 1. Purpose
This Regulation provides a harmonized method for the classification, evaluation and approval of Retrofit Emission Control (REC) systems for particulate matter (PM), for oxides of nitrogen (NOx), or for both PM and NOx, and for the determination of the levels of emissions from compression-ignition (CI) engines used in applications within the scope indicated in paragraph 2. It provides a framework for approval of REC for different applications with corresponding environmental performance levels and for the identification in Type Approval of those levels.

## 2. Scope
This Regulation applies to Retrofit Emission Control devices (REC) to be installed on:
*   Category M2, M3 and N vehicles and their CI engines, excluding those vehicles approved according to Regulation No. 83.
*   CI engines having a net power higher than 18 kW but not more than 560 kW installed in non-road mobile machinery, operated under variable speed.
*   CI engines having a net power higher than 18 kW but not more than 560 kW installed in non-road mobile machinery, operated under constant speed.
*   CI engines having a net power higher than 18 kW but not more than 560 kW installed in category T vehicles.

## 3. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Retrofit Emission Control device (REC):** Any particulate reduction system, NOx-reduction system or combination of both used for retrofit purposes, including any sensors and software essential to its operation.
*   **Class I REC:** Intended to control particulate matter emissions only, and which does not increase the direct NO2 emissions.
*   **Class IIA or IIB REC:** Intended to control particulate matter emissions only, and which does not increase the direct tailpipe NO2 emissions by more than a specified percentage.
*   **Class III REC:** Intended to control NOx emissions only.
*   **Class IV REC:** Intended to control both particulate matter emissions and NOx emissions.
*   **Engine baseline emission:** The emissions of a given engine or engine system without any retrofit emission control device.
*   **Reduction efficiency:** The ratio between the emissions downstream of the REC system and the engine baseline emissions.
*   **NOx Control Diagnostic system (NCD):** A system of the REC capable of detecting and identifying NOx control malfunctions.

## 4. Application for Approval
The application for type-approval of a REC shall be submitted by the manufacturer or by his duly accredited representative. Each application shall be accompanied by specified information, including the manufacturer’s name, identifying part number, application range, instruction manual, and end-user service manual. The applicant shall provide the Information Document in accordance with Annex 1 and sample(s) of the REC to be tested.

## 5. Markings and Labels
All REC shall be marked with the trade name or mark of the retrofit manufacturer and the identifying part number. All REC shall be identified by a permanently fixed label containing the approval mark, which includes the REC Class (I, IIA, IIB, III, or IV). The approval mark consists of a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the country which has granted the approval, the number of this Regulation (132), followed by the letter "R", a dash and the approval number, and the Class of the REC.

## 6. Approval
Type approval shall be granted if the REC meets the requirements of this Regulation. An approval number shall be assigned to each approved REC.

## 7. General Requirements
A REC approved under this Regulation shall be designed, constructed, and capable of being mounted to enable compliance throughout its normal life under normal conditions of use. It must be durable and resistant to corrosion, oxidation, vibration, and mechanical stress. Key requirements include:
*   Provision of installation documents.
*   Prohibition of devices that bypass or reduce the efficiency of the REC.
*   Fitting of an on-board operator warning system.
*   Specific requirements for REC requiring a reagent or additive (e.g., reagent tank sampling point, reagent level indicator, freeze protection).
*   Requirements for NOx control measures and a NOx Control Diagnostic (NCD) system for applicable REC classes.
*   Provision of written maintenance and operation instructions to drivers/operators.

## 8. Performance Requirements
REC approved under this Regulation shall meet criteria for emission limit values, reduction levels, NO2 emission requirements, and secondary emissions requirements.
*   **Limit Values:** The retrofitted engine system shall not exceed the emission limits for the relevant pollutants of a more stringent emission stage as specified in Regulation No. 49 or Regulation No. 96.
*   **Reduction Levels:** The minimum reduction efficiency for "Reduction level 01" is 90% for PM mass and 60% for NOx.
*   **NO2 Emissions:** Specific limits on NO2 emission increases are defined for each REC Class (I, IIA/IIB, III, IV).
*   **Particle Number:** Class I, IIA/IIB, and IV REC approved to PM Reduction Level 01 shall provide a particle number reduction efficiency of at least 97%.
*   **Secondary Emissions:** The REC shall not increase secondary emissions to concentrations known to be hazardous to health. For Class III and IV REC, ammonia emissions shall not exceed a mean value of 25 ppm.

## 9. Durability Requirements
The applicant shall declare that the REC will comply over a useful life of 200,000 km or 6 years (for M2, M3, N vehicles) or 4,000 operating hours or 6 years (for other applications). A 1,000-hour durability test (field or engine test bed) is required. After the durability run, the REC system must still meet the performance requirements.

## 10. Application Range
The application range describes the range of engines or engine systems to which the REC family may be applied. It is initially limited to the engine family of the test engine but may be extended to other engines from the same or different manufacturers if specific technical criteria (e.g., cylinder displacement, aspiration method, baseline emission stage) are met.

## 11. Modifications to Engine Baseline Emissions
Modifications of engine operation parameters which might affect engine baseline emissions must be kept within the original engine manufacturer's limits. Modifications to the original engine manufacturer's emission control system are generally not permitted, with specific exceptions (e.g., written permission from OEM, replacement of an existing diesel oxidation catalyst under certain conditions for Class I/II REC). The performance of any original On-Board Diagnostics (OBD) system shall not be compromised.

## 12. Choice of the Test Engines and REC Combination
The test engines shall originate from an engine family corresponding to the intended application range of the REC. The test engine and REC combination must meet specific criteria regarding power output, space velocity, and catalytic material concentration.

## 13. Specifications Concerning Emissions Measurement
Gaseous and particulate emissions shall be measured by the methods described in the applicable annexes of Regulation No. 49 and Regulation No. 96.

## 14. Particulate Reduction REC Family
Defines the criteria for grouping particulate reduction REC into families for approval purposes. Features such as working principle, design characteristics, type of regeneration, and catalytically active materials must not deviate from the tested system within specified tolerances.

## 15. NOx Reduction REC Family
Defines the criteria for grouping NOx reduction REC into families. Features such as critical dimensions of active elements, catalyst substrate material, control strategy, and reagent introduction must not deviate from the tested system within specified tolerances.

## 16. NOx and PM Reduction REC Family
For combined systems, both the PM reduction elements and NOx reduction elements must conform to the respective family criteria in paragraphs 14 and 15, and their relative location must be the same as in the tested device.

## 17. Fuel and Specific Fuel Consumption
Testing shall be conducted with commercially available fuel. The specific fuel consumption of the engine fitted with the REC during applicable test cycles shall be not more than 4% greater than in the non-retrofitted condition.

## 18. Operating Behaviour and Safety Hazard
The REC must be designed to be used without exposing operators or bystanders to any safety hazard and without impairment of the operating behaviour of the vehicle or machine beyond specified limits. The REC manufacturer shall assess potential safety hazards arising from installation.

## 19. Noise Emissions
The applicant shall provide evidence that retrofitting a REC will not lead to an increase of the vehicle's noise emissions.

## 20. Installation of a REC
The REC manufacturer shall provide written installation guidelines and operation and maintenance instructions in accordance with Annex 11, including requirements for language, safety reminders, maintenance instructions, and reagent specifications.

## 21. Modification and Extension of Approval of a REC
Every modification relevant to this Regulation must be notified to the Type Approval Authority for assessment. Confirmation or refusal of approval shall be communicated to other Contracting Parties.

## 22. Conformity of Production
Conformity of production procedures shall comply with the 1958 Agreement. Manufacturers must submit data for initial assessment, which may involve appraisal of a certified quality system (e.g., ISO 9001) or a company assessment.

## 23. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Type approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not complied with.

## 24. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority if production of an approved REC type ceases.

## 25. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties shall communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## 26. Transitional Provisions
From the official date of entry into force of the 01 series of amendments (22 January 2015), Contracting Parties applying these amendments shall not refuse to grant/accept approvals under them, and may refuse approvals for REC not meeting the new requirements. Approvals to previous levels may continue to be granted for use in countries applying those requirements.

## Annexes
The regulation includes 12 annexes detailing procedures and requirements:
1.  Information Document
2.  Communication Form
3.  Addendum to Communication
4.  Arrangement of REC Type-Approval Mark
5.  Test of a Particulate Reduction REC (Class I or II)
6.  Test of a NOx Reduction REC (Class III)
7.  Tests of a PM and NOx Reduction REC (Class IV)
8.  Test Sequences
9.  Limit Value Equivalence Tables
10. Requirements of the NOx Control Diagnostic System
11. Installation and Operation Instructions
12. Specific Requirements Regarding Approval Relative to Regulation No. 49 06 Series Limits
