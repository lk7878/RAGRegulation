# Regulation No. 9 (Revision 3)

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to vehicles of category L2, L4 and L5 with regard to noise.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **Approval of a vehicle**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to the noise level and the original exhaust system as a technical unit.
- **Vehicle type**: A category of motor vehicles which do not differ in essential respects such as body lines/materials, dimensions, engine type (ignition type, cylinders, power), gear ratios, and exhaust system arrangement.
- **Exhaust or silencing system(s)**: A complete set of components necessary for limiting the noise made by a motor vehicle and its exhaust.
- **Original exhaust or silencing system**: The system with which the vehicle is equipped on acceptance or extension of acceptance.
- **Kerb mass**: The mass of the vehicle ready for normal operation, including full equipment, instruments, liquids (fuel tank at least 90% full), and auxiliary equipment.
- **Maximum speed**: The maximum vehicle speed as defined in ISO 7117:1995.

## 3. Application for Approval
- Submitted by the manufacturer or accredited representative.
- Must include documents in triplicate: description of vehicle type, list of exhaust/silencing system components, drawings of the system and its position, detailed component drawings.
- A representative vehicle and, upon request, a sample exhaust/silencing system must be submitted to the Technical Service.

## 4. Markings
Components of the exhaust/silencing system must bear indelible, legible marks including:
- Manufacturer's trade name/mark.
- Trade description.
- Identifying part numbers.
- For all original silencers, an "E" mark followed by the country code of the component type approval.
- Packing of original replacement systems must be marked "original part" with make, type, and the "E" mark.

## 5. Approval
- Granted if the vehicle meets requirements in paragraphs 6 and 7.
- An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments (e.g., 07 for the 07 series).
- Notice of approval/extension/refusal is communicated to contracting parties via a form (Annex 1).
- An international approval mark (a circle with "E" and country code, plus regulation number and approval number) must be affixed to each approved vehicle.
- The mark must be legible, indelible, and placed close to or on the vehicle data plate.

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. General Specifications
- The vehicle, engine, and exhaust system must be designed to comply with the Regulation under normal use and vibration.
- The exhaust system must resist corrosive action.
- The vehicle must display the manufacturer's name and the target engine speed/result of the stationary test (as per Annex 3, para. 3.2).

### 6.2. Specifications Regarding Sound Levels
- Noise is measured by two methods described in **Annex 3**: for the vehicle in motion and when stationary (unless the engine doesn't operate when stationary).
- **Maximum permissible sound levels**:
    - Categories L4 and L5: **80 dB(A)**
    - Category L2: **76 dB(A)**
- Values are entered in the test report and Annex 1 form.

### 6.3. Additional Prescriptions (Tamperability & Multi-mode Systems)
- Exhaust systems must not easily permit removal of silencing parts (e.g., baffles). Removal should cause permanent damage.
- Manually adjustable multi-mode systems must meet requirements in all modes. Reported noise levels are from the mode with the highest levels.
- Manufacturers must not use devices/procedures solely for passing tests that are not operational during typical on-road use.

## 7. Modification and Extension of Approval
- Any modification to the vehicle type or exhaust system must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing.
- Extension of approval is communicated to other parties via Annex 1 form.

## 8. Conformity of Production
- Every vehicle with an approval mark must conform to the approved type and fitted with the approved exhaust system.
- To verify conformity, a vehicle from the series is tested. Production conforms if measured levels do not exceed the approval test value by more than **3 dB(A)**, nor the prescribed limits (para. 6.2.1.3) by more than **1 dB(A)**.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
- Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met.
- Withdrawal must be notified to other contracting parties via Annex 1 form.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
- The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority if production ceases. The Authority then notifies other parties.

## 11. Transitional Provisions (07 Series of Amendments)
- From the entry into force date (03 Nov 2013), contracting parties shall not refuse approvals under the 07 series.
- From 24 months after entry into force, only vehicle types meeting the 07 series requirements shall be granted type approvals.
- Extensions for existing types approved under earlier amendments shall not be refused.
- For 24 months after entry into force, national/regional approval of vehicles type-approved to earlier amendments shall not be refused.
- After 24 months, contracting parties are not obliged to accept vehicles type-approved to earlier amendments for national/regional approval.

## 12. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Authorities
Contracting parties must communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
**Annex 1**: Communication form for approval, extension, refusal, withdrawal, or production discontinuation. Includes details like trade name, vehicle type, manufacturer, engine specs, sound levels, etc.

**Annex 2**: Examples of approval mark arrangements.

**Annex 3**: **Methods and instruments for measuring the noise made by motor vehicles**.
- **1. Measuring Instruments**: Specifies Class 1 sound level meters (IEC 61672-1:2002), calibration requirements (IEC 60942:2003), and instrumentation for speed and meteorological measurements.
- **2. Conditions of Measurement**: Covers vehicle condition (test mass = kerb mass ± 75 ± 5 kg), tyre selection, test site requirements (free sound field, specific surface per Annex 4), and ambient noise corrections.
- **3. Methods of Measurement**:
    - **3.1. Vehicle in Motion**: Microphone at 7.5 m from vehicle path, 1.2 m high. Vehicle accelerates between lines AA' and BB'. Steady approach speed is the lowest of: 50 km/h, 3/4 rated engine speed, or 3/4 governed max speed. Gear selection rules specified.
    - **3.2. Stationary Vehicle**: For in-service testing. Microphone at 0.5 m from exhaust outlet reference point at 45°. Engine speed held at 3/4 rated speed (or 1/2 if >5000 min-1). Procedures for multi-outlet and multi-mode systems.
- **4. Interpretation of Results for Vehicles in Motion**: Results rounded to nearest whole dB. Average of four readings (two per side) must not exceed permissible limits.
- **5. Original Exhaust (Silencing) System**:
    - Requirements for silencers with fibrous materials (must be asbestos-free, retained, or meet heat/chemical resistance specs).
    - Conditioning methods (road operation, pulsation, test bench) to simulate aging.
    - Marking requirements ("E" mark, "original part" on packaging).
    - Intake silencers/filters, if essential for compliance, are considered part of the system.

**Appendix to Annex 3**: Figures for test positions (in motion, stationary), pulsation test apparatus, and exhaust outlet reference point.

**Annex 4**: **Specifications for the test site**.
- Based on ISO 10844:1994.
- **Surface Requirements**:
    - Residual voids content ≤ 8%, OR sound absorption coefficient α ≤ 0.10.
    - Texture depth ≥ 0.4 mm.
    - Must be homogeneous.
- **Test Surface Design**: Dense asphaltic concrete, max chipping size 8 mm, wearing course ≥30 mm, straight penetration grade bitumen. Provides aggregate grading curve and design guidelines.
- **Test Methods**: For voids content, sound absorption (impedance tube), and texture depth (volumetric).
- **Stability and Maintenance**: Surface matures after 4 weeks. Loose debris must be removed. Salt for de-icing not recommended.
- **Documentation**: Requirements for documenting the test surface construction and test results.
