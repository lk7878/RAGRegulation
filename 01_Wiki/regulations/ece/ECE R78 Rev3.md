# UN Regulation No. 78 (Revision 3)

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to vehicles of category L (L1, L2, L3, L4, L5). These categories do not include:
(a) Vehicles with a Vmax of < 25 km/h;
(b) Vehicles equipped for disabled riders.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **2.1. "Antilock Brake System (ABS)"**: A system which senses wheel slip and automatically modulates braking pressure to limit wheel slip.
*   **2.5. "Brake system"**: The combination of control, transmission, and brake parts used to reduce speed, halt, and hold a vehicle stationary.
*   **2.6. "Combined brake system (CBS)"**: A service brake system where actuation of a single control operates brakes on multiple wheels, with specific definitions per vehicle category.
*   **2.12. "Gross vehicle mass" or "maximum mass"**: The technically permissible maximum laden mass declared by the manufacturer.
*   **2.17. "Peak braking coefficient (PBC)"**: A measure of tyre-to-road surface friction based on maximum deceleration of a rolling tyre.
*   **2.22. "Split service brake system (SSBS)"**: A brake system operating on all wheels, consisting of two or more subsystems, designed so that a single failure in any subsystem does not impair others.
*   **2.27. "Vehicle type"**: A sub-category of L-category vehicles defined by specific characteristics such as category, mass, braking device type, axle arrangement, engine, gearing, and tyre dimensions.
*   **2.28. "Vmax"**: The maximum speed attainable under defined conditions or measured per ISO 7117:1995.
*   **2.30. "Emergency braking signal"**: A logic signal indicating emergency braking as specified in the Regulation.
*   **2.31. "Braking Signal"**: A logic signal indicating when stop lamp illumination is required/allowed.
*   **2.32. "Electric Regenerative Braking System"**: A braking system that converts kinetic energy to electrical energy during deceleration and is not part of the service braking system.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application, submitted by the manufacturer or representative, must include descriptive documents, component lists, diagrams, drawings, and a representative vehicle for testing.

## 4. Approval
If requirements are met, an approval number is assigned. An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, plus "78R-XXXXX") must be affixed to conforming vehicles. Examples are provided in Annex 2.

## 5. Specifications
### 5.1. Brake System Requirements
*   **5.1.2./5.1.3. Control Operation**: Service and secondary brake controls must be operable by a seated rider with hands on steering.
*   **5.1.4. Parking Brake System**: If fitted, must hold the vehicle on an 18% slope via a separate, mechanically held control.
*   **5.1.5.-5.1.8. System Requirements by Vehicle Category**:
    *   **L1, L3**: Two separate service brake systems or a split service brake system (SSBS), with brakes on front and rear wheels.
    *   **L4**: Must comply with L1/L3 requirements. A sidecar wheel brake is not required if performance is met.
    *   **L2, L6**: Parking brake plus either: two separate service brake systems (except CBS) operating all wheels; an SSBS; or a CBS operating all wheels with a secondary system (which may be the parking brake).
    *   **L5, L7**: Parking brake plus a foot-actuated service brake system operating on all wheels via either an SSBS or a CBS with a secondary system.
*   **5.1.10. Hydraulic Systems**: Must have sealed separate reservoirs with minimum capacity and visible fluid level.
*   **5.1.12. Split System Warning**: A red warning lamp for hydraulic failure or low fluid level.
*   **5.1.13. ABS Warning**: A yellow warning lamp for ABS malfunction.
*   **5.1.14. EMC**: Braking effectiveness must not be adversely affected by magnetic/electrical fields (compliance with UN R10).
*   **5.1.15. Emergency Braking Signal**: Defines activation (≥6 m/s² deceleration or ABS fully cycling above 50 km/h with ≥2.5 m/s²) and deactivation (<2.5 m/s²) criteria.
*   **5.1.16. Disabling ABS**: Generally not permitted. Derogation allowed for "off-road" modes under strict conditions (vehicle stationary, deliberate rider action, automatic re-enablement, specific tell-tale).
*   **5.1.17. Braking Signal for Stop Lamps**: Defines generation upon service brake application. For electric vehicles with regenerative braking, specifies signal generation based on deceleration thresholds (≤0.7 m/s²: not generated; >0.7 & ≤1.3 m/s²: may be generated; >1.3 m/s²: shall be generated).
*   **5.1.18. Rider-Selectable Rear ABS Deactivation**: Permitted if indicated by a specific tell-tale.
### 5.2. Durability
Wear must be compensated (automatic/manual adjustment). Friction material thickness must be visible or assessed by a device. No detachment or leakage during/after tests.
### 5.3. Measurement of Dynamic Performance
Three methods: Mean Fully Developed Deceleration (MFDD), Stopping Distance (with correction formula), or Continuous Deceleration Recording.
### 5.4. Brake Lining Materials
Shall not contain asbestos.

## 6. Tests
Braking tests (conditions, procedures, performance) are prescribed in **Annex 3**.

## 7. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be communicated to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Manufactured vehicles must conform to the approved type. The approval holder must ensure quality control, have test equipment, record results, and allow inspections.

## 9. Transitional Provisions
Outlines the applicability and acceptance of approvals under the 04 and 05 series of amendments, with key dates:
*   From **1 September 2018**: New approvals must meet 04 series.
*   From **1 September 2021**: Parties need not accept approvals to preceding series for national/regional approval.
*   From **1 September 2023**: Parties need not accept UN type-approvals to preceding series first issued after this date (with exceptions for types unaffected by 05 series changes).

## 10. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if production does not comply.

## 11. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority upon ceasing production.

## 12. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal. Includes an Appendix with a data list for UN Regulation No. 90 approvals.
*   **Annex 2**: Examples of approval mark arrangements.
*   **Annex 3**: **Test conditions, procedures and performance requirements**. Contains detailed specifications for:
    *   Test surfaces (high/low friction), ambient conditions, preparation (burnishing).
    *   Test sequence: Dry stop (single control), Dry stop (all controls), High speed, Wet brake, Heat fade, and specific tests for Parking brake, ABS, Partial failure (SSBS), Power-assisted system failure, CBS failure.
    *   Performance tables with stopping distance formulas and MFDD values for various vehicle categories and brake system types.
    *   **Appendix 1**: Alternative method for determining Peak Braking Coefficient (PBC).
