# Regulation No. 123 - Adaptive Front-lighting Systems (AFS)

**Document Reference:** E/ECE/324/Rev.2/Add.122/Rev.1/Amend.1/Corr.1 / E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2/Add.122/Rev.1/Amend.1/Corr.1

**Issuing Body:** United Nations Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE)

**Parent Agreement:** Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Uniform Technical Prescriptions for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts which can be fitted and/or be used on Wheeled Vehicles and the Conditions for Reciprocal Recognition of Approvals Granted on the Basis of these Prescriptions (Revision 2, including amendments effective 16 October 1995).

**Scope:** This document contains uniform technical provisions for the approval of adaptive front-lighting systems (AFS) for motor vehicles.

**Amendment Details:** This is Corrigendum 1 to Supplement 1 of the 01 series of amendments to Addendum 122 (Regulation No. 123, Revision 1 – Amendment 1).

**Key Content (from OCR):**
The corrigendum includes a specific amendment to **Annex 3, Table 1**, which details the photometric requirements for the passing beam. The table is to be amended as indicated.
