# Regulation No. 83 (Revision 5, 07 series of amendments)

## 1. Scope
This Regulation establishes technical requirements for the type approval of motor vehicles. In addition, it lays down rules for in-service conformity, durability of pollution control devices and On-Board Diagnostic (OBD) systems.

**1.1.** This Regulation applies to vehicles of categories M1, M2, N1 and N2 with a reference mass not exceeding 2,610 kg. At the manufacturer's request, type approval may be extended to M1, M2, N1 and N2 vehicles with a reference mass not exceeding 2,840 kg which meet the conditions laid down in this Regulation.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Vehicle type:** A group of vehicles that do not differ in equivalent inertia and engine/vehicle characteristics.
*   **Reference mass:** The unladen mass of the vehicle increased by a uniform figure of 100 kg for test purposes.
*   **Gaseous pollutants:** Exhaust gas emissions of carbon monoxide (CO), oxides of nitrogen (NOx), and hydrocarbons (HC) with specified fuel-specific carbon-hydrogen-oxygen ratios.
*   **Particulate pollutants (PM & PN):** Components removed from diluted exhaust gas at ≤52°C, including particulate number (PN) for particles >23 nm.
*   **Exhaust emissions:** For PI engines: gaseous and particulate pollutants. For CI engines: gaseous pollutants, PM, and PN.
*   **Evaporative emissions:** Hydrocarbon vapours lost from the fuel system (tank breathing and hot soak losses).
*   **On-Board Diagnostic (OBD):** A system for emission control capable of identifying likely malfunction areas via fault codes.
*   **Defeat device:** Any design element that reduces emission control effectiveness under normal operating conditions, with specific exceptions.
*   **Approval of a vehicle:** Approval of a vehicle type with regard to the limitation of exhaust, evaporative, and crankcase emissions, durability, and OBD, categorized as Approval B (petrol/LPG/NG/biofuels), Approval C (diesel/biofuel), or Approval D (LPG/NG).
*   **Periodically regenerating system:** An anti-pollution device requiring regeneration less than every 4,000 km, during which emission standards may be exceeded.
*   **Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV):** A vehicle that draws propulsion energy from both a consumable fuel and an on-vehicle electrical energy/power storage device.
*   **Mono-fuel, Bi-fuel, Flex-fuel, and Alternative fuel vehicles:** Definitions for vehicles designed to run on one fuel, two separate fuels one at a time, mixtures of fuels, or gaseous/non-mineral oil derived fuels.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application, submitted by the manufacturer, must include detailed information on the vehicle, its OBD system (including functional description, malfunction indicator, in-use performance compliance plan, anti-tampering provisions), and, if applicable, vehicle family particulars. A representative vehicle must be submitted for testing.

## 4. Approval
If the vehicle meets the requirements, approval is granted and assigned an approval number indicating the amendment series. An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, Regulation number, approval number, and vehicle category/class letter) must be affixed to conforming vehicles. Specific requirements are outlined for vehicles fuelled by LPG/NG/biomethane, flex-fuel vehicles, and OBD systems.

## 5. Specifications and Tests
**5.1. General Requirements:** Components must be designed to comply under normal use and vibration. Defeat devices are prohibited. Specific requirements cover fuel tank inlet orifices, prevention of evaporative emissions from missing filler caps, electronic system security against unauthorized modification, and provisions for roadworthiness inspection.

**5.2. Test Procedure (Table A):** Specifies the required tests based on engine type and fuel:
*   **PI engines & HEVs with PI engines:** Type I (cold start exhaust), II (idle CO), III (crankcase), IV (evaporative), V (durability), VI (low temp CO/HC), OBD, engine power.
*   **PI engines fuelled with LPG/NG:** Type I, II, III, IV (where applicable), V, VI (where applicable), OBD, engine power.
*   **CI engines & HEVs with CI engines:** Type I, V, OBD.

**5.3. Description of Tests:**
*   **Type I Test (Verifying exhaust emissions after a cold start):** Conducted on a chassis dynamometer over a combined urban/extra-urban cycle. Gaseous, PM, and PN emissions are measured. Limits are specified in **Table 1** (see below). The test flow chart determines the required number of tests (1, 2, or 3) based on initial results.
*   **Type II Test (CO at idling speed):** For PI engines. CO at normal idle ≤0.3% vol; at high idle (≥2000 min⁻¹) ≤0.2% vol with Lambda 1 ± 0.03.
*   **Type III Test (Crankcase emissions):** For PI engines. The crankcase ventilation system must not permit emissions into the atmosphere.
*   **Type IV Test (Evaporative emissions):** For PI engines (except those fuelled solely with LPG/NG). Evaporative emissions must be <2 g/test.
*   **Type VI Test (Low ambient temperature emissions):** For PI engines at -7°C. CO and HC limits are specified in **Table 2**.
*   **Type V Test (Durability):** An ageing test of 160,000 km. Alternatively, assigned deterioration factors from **Table 3** may be used.
*   **OBD Test:** Required for all vehicles.
*   **Emission data for roadworthiness testing:** For PI engines, CO content, engine speed, and Lambda value at idle and high idle must be recorded and supplied.

## 6. Modifications of the Vehicle Type
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing.

## 7. Extensions to Type Approvals
Rules for extending approvals to other vehicles based on parameters such as reference mass, transmission ratios, periodically regenerating system characteristics, evaporative emission system design, durability test parameters, and OBD system identity.

## 8. Conformity of Production (COP)
Vehicles must conform to the approved type. Statistical procedures for verifying COP for Type I tests are detailed in **Appendices 1 and 2**, based on whether the manufacturer's production standard deviation is satisfactory. Procedures for Type III, IV, and OBD checks are also outlined.

## 9. In-Service Conformity
Sets out requirements for auditing the in-service performance of vehicles regarding tailpipe emissions and OBD (including In-Use Performance Ratio - IUPRM). Manufacturers must collect and report information from vehicles in use. The Type Approval Authority audits based on this information and surveillance testing. Procedures for vehicle selection, testing, and evaluation are detailed in **Appendices 3, 4, and 5**.

## 10. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if COP requirements are not met.

## 11. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority.

## 12. Transitional Provisions
The 07 series of amendments entered into force on 22 January 2015. Staged implementation dates are provided for new type approvals and new vehicles to comply with the new Type I limits and OBD thresholds (preliminary and final).

## 13. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Appendices & Annexes
The regulation includes numerous detailed appendices and annexes covering test procedures, statistical methods, fuel specifications, OBD requirements, and special provisions for various technologies (e.g., hybrids, periodically regenerating systems, alternative fuels).
