# UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF VEHICLES WITH REGARD TO THE BEHAVIOUR OF THE STRUCTURE OF THE IMPACTED VEHICLE IN A REAR-END COLLISION

**Regulation No. 32**
**Revision 1**
**Addendum 31**

## 1. SCOPE
This Regulation applies to the behaviour of the structure of the passenger compartment of private (passenger) cars when subjected to a rear-end collision.

## 2. DEFINITIONS
### 2.1. In defining the dimensions the following simplified terminology is used:
- **Transverse plane**: A vertical plane perpendicular to the median longitudinal plane of the vehicle.
- **Longitudinal plane**: A plane parallel to the median longitudinal plane of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle's "reference plane"**: A plane related to the vehicle and horizontal when the vehicle in the state defined in paragraph 2.2.4. below rests on a horizontal plane.

### 2.2. For the purposes of this Regulation
- **Approval of a vehicle**: The approval of a vehicle type with regard to the behaviour of the structure of the passenger compartment in a rear-end collision.
- **Vehicle type**: A category of power-driven vehicles which do not differ in such essential respects as:
    - The length and width of the vehicle in so far as they have an effect on the results of the impact test prescribed in this Regulation.
    - The structure, dimensions, lines and materials of the part of the vehicle rearward of the transverse plane through the "R" point of the rearmost seat.
    - The lines and inside dimensions of the passenger compartment in so far as they have an effect on the results of the impact test prescribed in this Regulation.
    - The siting of the engine (front, rear, centre).
- **Passenger compartment**: The space for occupant accommodation, bounded by the roof, floor, side walls, doors, outside glazing, front bulkhead and the plane of the rear compartment bulkhead or the plane of the rear seat back support.
- **Unladen kerb weight**: The weight of the vehicle in running order, unoccupied and unladen but complete with fuel, coolant, lubricant, tools and a spare wheel (if provided as standard equipment by the vehicle manufacturer).
- **Capacity of the fuel tank**: The fuel-tank capacity as specified by the manufacturer.

## 3. APPLICATION FOR APPROVAL
The application for approval of a vehicle type shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or by his duly accredited representative. It shall be accompanied by documents in triplicate, including a detailed description of the vehicle type, drawings, and particulars of the vehicle's unladen kerb weight. A representative vehicle shall be submitted for testing.

## 4. APPROVAL
If the vehicle meets the requirements, approval shall be granted and an approval number assigned. An international approval mark (a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the country which has granted approval, and the Regulation number followed by "R", a dash and the approval number) shall be affixed to every conforming vehicle.

## 5. REQUIREMENTS
1. When the vehicle has undergone the test, the lengthwise displacement shall not exceed 75 mm.
2. After the test, no rigid component in the passenger compartment shall constitute a risk of serious injury to the vehicle's occupants.
3. The side doors of the vehicle shall not open under the effect of the impact.
4. Except in the case of a vehicle not having a roof of rigid construction, the opening of a number of doors sufficient to enable all the occupants to emerge shall, after the impact, continue to be possible without the use of tools.

## 6. TEST
The vehicle's compliance with the requirements shall be checked by the method set out in Annexes 3 and 4 to this Regulation.

## 7. MODIFICATIONS AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL OF THE VEHICLE TYPE
Every modification of the vehicle type shall be notified to the administrative department which approved that vehicle type. Confirmation or refusal of approval shall be communicated to the Parties to the Agreement.

## 8. CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Every vehicle bearing an approval mark shall conform to the approved vehicle type. Random checks shall be conducted to verify conformity.

## 9. PENALTIES FOR NON-CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Approval may be withdrawn if the conformity of production requirement is not complied with or if the vehicle fails the tests.

## 10. PRODUCTION DEFINITELY DISCONTINUED
If the holder of the approval completely ceases to manufacture the approved type, the granting authority shall be informed.

## 11. NAMES AND ADDRESSES OF TECHNICAL SERVICES CONDUCTING APPROVAL TESTS, AND OF ADMINISTRATIVE DEPARTMENTS
The Parties to the Agreement shall communicate the names and addresses of the technical services and administrative departments to the UN Secretariat.

## ANNEXES
- **Annex 1**: Communication form concerning approval, extension, refusal, withdrawal, or production discontinuation.
- **Annex 2**: Arrangements of approval marks.
- **Annex 3**: Procedure for determining the "H" point and the actual torso angle for seating positions in motor vehicles.
    - **Appendix 1**: Description of the Three-dimensional "H" point machine (3-D H machine).
    - **Appendix 2**: Three-dimensional reference system.
    - **Appendix 3**: Reference data concerning seating positions.
- **Annex 4**: Procedure for rear-end impact test.
    - Describes the purpose, scope, test installations (testing ground, impactor specifications), procedures, and measuring instruments for the rear-end collision simulation test.
