This document constitutes **Amendment 3** to **Revision 2** of **UN Regulation No. 75**, concerning the approval of pneumatic tyres for L-category vehicles. It entered into force on 9 February 2017.

The authentic and legally binding text is document **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2016/55**.

### Key Amendments to Regulation No. 75

This amendment modifies specific paragraphs within the regulation:

*   **Paragraph 6.1.1.1.** is amended to specify the formula for calculating tyre section width (`S`), including definitions for measured section width (`S`), nominal section width (`S1`), rim widths (`A`, `A1`), and the constant factor `K = 0.4`.
*   **Paragraph 6.1.2.1.** is amended to define the formula for calculating tyre outer diameter (`D = d + 2H`), where `H` (nominal section height) is derived from the nominal section width (`S1`) and the nominal aspect ratio (`Ra`).
*   **Paragraph 6.1.4.** is retitled to "Tyre section width specification".
*   **Paragraph 6.1.4.2.** is amended regarding permissible excess of section width, referencing limits in Annex 5 or specified percentages for sizes not in the annex.
*   **Paragraph 6.1.5.** is amended and expanded to cover tyre outer diameter specifications:
    *   **6.1.5.1.** states the outer diameter must be within the `D_min` and `D_max` values specified in Annex 5.
    *   **6.1.5.2.** provides formulae to calculate `D_min` and `D_max` for tyre sizes not listed in Annex 5, using factors `a` and `b` applied to the nominal section height `H`.
