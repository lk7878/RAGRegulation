# UN Regulation No. 148

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the approval of the following light-signalling lamps for power-driven vehicles and their trailers:
- Rear-registration plate illuminating lamps
- Direction indicator lamps
- Position lamps
- Stop lamps
- End-outline marker lamps
- Reversing lamps
- Manoeuvring lamps
- Rear fog lamps
- Parking lamps
- Daytime running lamps
- Side marker lamps

## 2. Definitions
- **Lamps of different types**: Lamps differing in essential respects such as trade name/mark, optical system characteristics, light source category/identification code, lamp category, variable intensity control, or sequential activation of light sources. A change in light source or filter colour does not constitute a change of type.
- Definitions from the latest series of amendments to UN Regulation No. 48 apply unless otherwise specified.

## 3. Administrative Provisions
### 3.1. Application for Approval
- Submitted by the holder of the trade name/mark or accredited representative.
- Must include detailed drawings, technical description, and samples as specified.

### 3.2. Approval
- Separate approval required for each lamp type listed in paragraph 1.
- Approval granted if the lamp meets the requirements of this Regulation.
- An approval number is assigned and communicated to Contracting Parties.

### 3.3. Approval Mark
- Consists of a circle with "E" and country distinguishing number, approval number, lamp symbol(s), Regulation number (e.g., 148R), and series of amendments.
- May include additional symbols (e.g., arrows for mounting direction, "D" for independent lamp assemblies, "Y" for interdependent lamp systems).
- May be replaced by a **Unique Identifier (UI)** linked to a secure UNECE database, eliminating the need for conventional E-marking on the lamp itself if the UI is used.
- Lamps must bear the trade name/mark, light source category/identification code, and rated voltage.

### 3.4. Modifications and Extension of Approval
- Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing.

### 3.5. Conformity of Production
- Production must conform to the approved type.
- Procedures and sampling must comply with Annexes 4 and 5.
- For non-replaceable filament light sources, documentation demonstrating compliance with IEC 60809 lifetime requirements is required.

### 3.6. Penalties for Non-Conformity
- Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met.

## 4. General Technical Requirements
### 4.1. Reference Regulations
Requirements from UN Regulations Nos. 48, 53, 74, or 86 (and their amendments) apply where pertinent and feasible to verify at lamp type approval.

### 4.2. Design and Construction
Lamps must be designed to ensure satisfactory operation and retain prescribed characteristics under normal use and vibration.

### 4.3. Light Sources
- **Replaceable light sources**: Must be approved per UN Regulation No. 37 or No. 128. Holders must conform to IEC 60061.
- **Light source modules**: Must be tamperproof, non-interchangeable with replaceable light sources, and fitted only in the correct position.

### 4.4. Independent and Interdependent Lamps
- **"D" marked lamps**: Applicable to assemblies of two independent lamps (e.g., position, stop, end-outline marker lamps).
- **"Y" marked lamps**: Applicable to interdependent lamp systems (e.g., position, stop, daytime running lamps).

### 4.5. Grouped, Combined, or Reciprocally Incorporated Lamps
- Approved position lamps are also deemed approved as end-outline marker lamps.
- Specific rules apply for lamps reciprocally incorporated with other functions (e.g., stop and position lamps).

### 4.6. Failure Provisions
- For lamps with multiple light sources, failure of one source must either maintain minimum intensity or activate a failure tell-tale (with certain conditions and exceptions for specific lamp types).
- For variable intensity lamps (categories R2, RM2, S2, S4, F2, 2b), failure of the control must automatically revert the lamp to steady luminous intensity requirements.

### 4.7. Test Conditions
- Specifies voltage application methods for different lamp types (replaceable light sources, non-replaceable light sources, systems with control gear).
- Defines determination of apparent surface and special provisions for stop lamps mounted inside vehicles.

### 4.8. Photometric Measurements
- **Measurement provisions**: Details on distance, angular aperture, and handling of multiple mounting positions.
- **Measurement methods**: Procedures for lamps with multiple light sources, and specific stabilization times for reversing/manoeuvring lamps (1 min, 10 min) and other lamps (1 min, 30 min). Direction indicators are tested in flashing mode.
- **Intensity requirements**: Must meet minimum and maximum values within angular fields defined in Annex 2, according to standard light distribution tables in Annex 3.
- **Specific rules** for independent lamp assemblies ("D"), interdependent lamp systems ("Y"), variable intensity control performance, and intensity ratios for reciprocally incorporated lamps.

### 4.9. Colour of Light Emitted
- Colour must be measured within the field defined in the relevant Annex 3 table. No sharp colour variation outside this field.

## 5. Specific Technical Requirements
Provides detailed photometric (luminous intensity) and colorimetric requirements for each lamp type, including minimum and maximum values at reference axis (H-V) and within specified angular fields. Key requirements are summarized in tables within the text for:
- **5.1 Front position lamps (A, MA) & Front end-outline marker lamps (AM)**: White light (MA may be amber). Min intensity at HV: 4 cd.
- **5.2 Rear position lamps (R1, R2, MR) & Rear end-outline marker lamps (RM1, RM2)**: Red light. Min intensity at HV: 4 cd for R1/RM1/MR; 4 cd for R2/RM2 (variable).
- **5.3 Parking lamps (77R)**: White (forward), red (rearward), amber (side). Min intensity at HV: 2 cd.
- **5.4 Daytime running lamps (RL)**: White light. Min intensity at HV: 400 cd. Subject to heat resistance test (Annex 6).
- **5.5 Stop lamps (S1, S2, S3, S4, MS)**: Red light. Min intensity at HV ranges from 25 cd (S3/S4) to 60 cd (S1/S2/MS).
- **5.6 Direction-indicator lamps (1, 1a, 1b, 2a, 2b, 5, 6, 11, 11a, 11b, 11c, 12)**: Amber light. Min intensity varies by category (e.g., 175 cd for cat.1, 0.6 cd for cat.5). Specifies failure provisions and allows sequential activation under certain conditions.
- **5.7 Side marker lamps (SM1, SM2)**: Amber light (can be red if combined with rear lamps). Min intensity at reference axis: 4.0 cd (SM1), 0.6 cd (SM2).
- **5.8 Reversing lamps (AR)**: White light. Min intensity at HV: 80 cd.
- **5.9 Rear fog lamps (F1, F2)**: Red light. Min intensity along HH/VV axis: 150 cd. Subject to heat resistance test (Annex 6).
- **5.10 Manoeuvring lamps (ML)**: White light. Max intensity: 500 cd.
- **5.11 Rear-registration plate illuminating lamps (L, LM1)**: Colourless light. Specifies luminance and uniformity requirements.

## 6. Transitional Provisions
- Contracting Parties shall continue to accept type approvals to preceding series of amendments if the changes introduced by the latest series do not affect the pertinent lamp function (as indicated by the change index).

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval notifications.
- **Annex 2**: Diagrams and tables defining light distribution angles (horizontal and vertical) for different lamp categories.
- **Annex 3**: Standard light distribution tables and measurement points for photometric verification of each lamp type.
- **Annex 4**: Minimum requirements for conformity of production (CoP) control procedures, including acceptable deviations.
- **Annex 5**: Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector for CoP verification.
- **Annex 6**: Heat resistance test procedure for rear fog lamps and daytime running lamps.
- **Annex 7**: Examples of approval mark arrangements.
