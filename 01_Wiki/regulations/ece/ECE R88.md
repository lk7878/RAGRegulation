## REGULATION TEXT

### 1. SCOPE
These provisions apply to fixed retroreflecting devices incorporated in the tyre sidewalls for use on mopeds and bicycles to provide a visual impression of circles of light; thus giving easy recognition of a two-wheeled vehicle.

### 2. DEFINITIONS
*Note: The definitions of technical terms are those adopted by the International Commission on Illumination (CIE), see Technical Report CIE Publication 54, Retroreflection Definition and Measurement, 1982.*

#### 2.1 General Definitions
-   **2.1.1 Retroreflection**: Reflection in which radiation is returned to directions close to the direction from which it came, this property being maintained over wide variations of the direction of the incident radiation.
-   **2.1.2 Retroreflector**: A surface or device from which, when directionally irradiated, a relatively large portion of the reflected radiation is retroreflected.
-   **2.1.3 Retroreflecting Tyre**: Means a tyre ready for use and comprising retroreflecting annuli moulded on to each sidewall of the tyre.

#### 2.2 Geometric Definitions (See figure of annex 1)
-   **2.2.1 Reference Centre**: A point at the centre of the wheel on which the tyre is mounted.
-   **2.2.2 Illumination Axis**: A line segment from the reference centre to the source.
-   **2.2.3 Observation Axis**: A line segment from the reference centre to the photometer head.
-   **2.2.4 Observation Angle (Symbol α)**: The angle between the illumination axis and the observation axis. Maximum range: 0 ≤ α < 180°.
-   **2.2.5 Observation Half-Plane**: The half-plane which originates on the illumination axis and which contains the observation axis.
-   **2.2.6 Reference Axis**: The axis of the wheel on which the tyre is mounted.
-   **2.2.7 Entrance Angle (Symbol β)**: The angle from the illumination axis to the reference axis. Range: 0 ≤ β ≤ 180°. Characterized by two components β1 and β2.
-   **2.2.8 First Axis**: An axis through the reference centre and perpendicular to the observation half-plane.
-   **2.2.9 First Component of the Entrance Angle (Symbol β1)**: The angle from the illumination axis to the plane containing the reference axis and the first axis. Range: -180° < β1 ≤ 180°.
-   **2.2.10 Second Component of the Entrance Angle (Symbol β2)**: The angle from the plane containing the observation half-plane to the reference axis. Range: -90° ≤ β2 ≤ 90°.
-   **2.2.11 Second Axis**: An axis through the reference centre and perpendicular to both the first axis and the reference axis.

#### 2.3 Definition of Principal Photometric Terms
-   **2.3.1 Coefficient of Luminous Intensity (Symbol R)**: The quotient obtained from dividing the luminous intensity (I) of the retroreflector in the direction of observation by the illuminance (E⊥) at the retroreflector on a plane perpendicular to the direction of the incident light. `R = I / E⊥`. Expressed in millicandelas per lux (mcd.lx⁻¹).
-   **2.3.2 Angular Diameter of the Retroreflecting Device (Symbol θ)**: Means the angle subtended by the greatest dimension of the visible area of the retroreflective annulus, either at the centre of the source of illumination or at the centre of the receiver.

#### 2.4 Definition of a Type of Retroreflecting Device
-   **2.4.1** Retroreflecting devices of different "types" means devices which differ in such essential respects as:
-   **2.4.2** The trade name or mark;
-   **2.4.3** The parts affecting the properties of the retroreflecting device to which the Regulation applies.

### 3. APPLICATION FOR APPROVAL
-   **3.1** The application shall be submitted by the holder of the trade name or mark or their representative, accompanied by:
    -   **3.1.1** Detailed drawings (triplicate) showing the approval mark position, dimensions of the film and tyre, and recommended maximum tyre pressure.
    -   **3.1.2** A brief technical description of the retroreflective annulus and tyre materials.
    -   **3.1.3** Samples of the retroreflective tyre (number specified in annex 4).

### 4. MARKINGS
-   **4.1** Every tyre submitted for approval must bear:
    -   **4.1.1** The applicant's trade name or mark.
    -   **4.1.2** The recommended maximum tyre pressure in bar.
-   **4.2** A space for the approval mark must be provided and shown on drawings.
-   **4.3** Markings must be clearly legible, indelible, and visible when mounted.

### 5. APPROVAL
-   **5.1** Granted if samples meet requirements.
-   **5.2** An approval number is assigned. Its first two digits indicate the amendment series.
-   **5.3** Notices of approval/refusal/etc. are communicated to applying Parties via a form in annex 2.
-   **5.4** An international approval mark must be affixed to conforming tyres, consisting of:
    -   **5.4.1.1** A circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the country's distinguishing number.
    -   **5.4.1.2** The Regulation number "88R", a dash, and the approval number.
-   **5.5** Annex 3 shows an example arrangement.

### 6. MODIFICATIONS OF THE RETROREFLECTIVE TYRE TYPE AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL
-   **6.1** Modifications must be notified. The approving authority may require further tests.
-   **6.2** Confirmation/refusal is communicated as per 5.3.
-   **6.3** Extensions are assigned a series number and communicated.

### 7. CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
-   **7.1** Tyres bearing the mark must conform to the approved type.
-   **7.2** Mechanical/geometrical divergences must be within manufacturing tolerances.
-   **7.3** Conformity is accepted if a random specimen's photometric measurements are ≥80% of spec.
-   **7.4** If 7.3 fails, five more specimens are tested. The average must meet spec, with no individual measurement <50% of spec.

### 8. PENALTIES FOR NON-CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Withdrawal of approval must be notified to other applying Parties via the annex 2 form.

### 9. PRODUCTION DEFINITELY DISCONTINUED
The approval holder must inform the authority upon ceasing manufacture, who then notifies other Parties.

### 10. GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS
-   **10.1** Must function satisfactorily in normal use with no detrimental defects.
-   **10.2** The retroreflective annulus must be a continuous circle moulded into each sidewall.
-   **10.3** The annulus must not be replaceable.
-   **10.4** The annulus surfaces must be easy to clean (not rough, no obstructive protuberances).

### 11. SPECIAL SPECIFICATIONS (TESTS)
-   **11.1** Tyres must satisfy colorimetric, photometric, physical, and mechanical requirements in annexes 5-8. Test procedures are in annex 4.

### 12. NAMES AND ADDRESSES OF TECHNICAL SERVICES...
Contracting Parties must communicate the names and addresses of their technical services and administrative departments to the UN Secretariat.
