# UN Regulation No. 51 (Revision 2) - Vehicle Noise Emissions

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to vehicles of category M and N1 with regard to noise.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Vehicle type:** A category of vehicles that do not differ in essential aspects such as bodywork, engine type, transmission, and noise reduction system.
*   **Noise reduction system:** The complete set of components for limiting vehicle and exhaust noise.
*   **Maximum mass:** The technically permissible maximum mass declared by the manufacturer.
*   **Rated engine power:** Engine power in kW measured per UN Regulation No. 85.
*   **Mass in running order (m_ro):** Unladen vehicle mass including driver, fuel, coolant, oils, tools, and spare wheel.
*   **Power to mass ratio index (PMR):** A dimensionless quantity used for acceleration calculation.
*   **Target acceleration (a_urban):** Acceleration at partial throttle in urban traffic.
*   **Reference acceleration (a_wot_ref):** Required acceleration during the acceleration test.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application must be submitted by the manufacturer and include:
*   Description of the vehicle type.
*   List and drawings of the noise reduction system components.
*   At the Technical Service's request, a sample noise reduction system and an engine.

## 4. Markings
Components of the noise reduction system must bear the trade name/mark of the manufacturer and a trade description. Markings must be legible and indelible.

## 5. Approval
*   Type approval is granted if the vehicle meets the specifications when tested according to **Measurement Method A** (Annex 3). From 1 July 2007, results from **Measurement Method B** (Annex 10) must also be recorded for monitoring.
*   An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments (e.g., 02).
*   An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, plus regulation number and approval number) must be affixed to approved vehicles.

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. General Specifications
The vehicle, engine, and noise reduction system must be designed to withstand normal use and vibration. The noise reduction system must resist corrosion.

### 6.2. Sound Level Specifications
#### 6.2.1. Methods of Measurement
Noise is measured by two methods described in Annex 3:
1.  **Vehicle in motion** (acceleration test).
2.  **Vehicle stationary** (exhaust outlet test).
Vehicles > 2800 kg with compressed air brake equipment must undergo an additional compressed air noise test (Annex 6).

#### 6.2.2. Sound Level Limits (Measurement Method A)
Limit values in dB(A) are specified per vehicle category, engine power, and mass. Key limits include:
*   **M1 (≤9 seats):** 74 dB(A)
*   **M2, M3 (≤3.5t):** 76-77 dB(A)
*   **M2, M3 (>3.5t):** 78-80 dB(A) (depending on engine power)
*   **N1 (≤3.5t):** 76-77 dB(A) (depending on mass)
*   **N2, N3 (>3.5t):** 77-80 dB(A) (depending on engine power)

**Adjustments:**
*   +1 dB(A) for M1 and N1 vehicles with direct-injection diesel engines.
*   +1 or +2 dB(A) for off-road vehicles >2t (depending on engine power).
*   +1 dB(A) for high-performance M1 vehicles under specific conditions.

### 6.3. Specifications for Exhaust Systems with Fibrous Materials
Requirements of Annex 5 apply. Systems must be conditioned (e.g., road operation, test bench, or pulsation test) unless the gas does not contact the material or the design family is proven durable.

## 7. Modification and Extension of Approval
Any modification must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing. Extensions are communicated to other Parties.

## 8. Conformity of Production
The manufacturer must ensure effective quality control and that production vehicles conform to the approved type. Checks are outlined in Annex 7.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if production does not conform.

## 10. Production Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the authority if production ceases.

## 11. Transitional Provisions
From 1 October 1995, only vehicles meeting the 02 series of amendments could be granted ECE approval. From 1 October 1996, Contracting Parties could refuse first registration of non-compliant vehicles.

## 12. Technical Services and Authorities
Parties must communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Communication form for approval, extension, refusal, withdrawal, or production discontinuation.
*   **Annex 2:** Examples of approval mark arrangements.
*   **Annex 3:** **Measurement Method A** - Methods and instruments for measuring noise (moving and stationary vehicles). Includes detailed test procedures, site requirements, and gear selection rules.
*   **Annex 4:** Classification of vehicles (Categories M1, M2, M3, N1, N2, N3).
*   **Annex 5:** Requirements for silencing systems containing acoustically absorbing fibrous materials, including conditioning procedures.
*   **Annex 6:** Compressed air noise measurement method and limit (72 dB(A)).
*   **Annex 7:** Checks on conformity of production.
*   **Annex 8:** Specifications for the test site (based on ISO 10844:1994), including surface characteristics (voids content, sound absorption, texture depth).
*   **Annex 9:** Vehicle and test data form for Measurement Method B.
*   **Annex 10:** **Measurement Method B** - Alternative method for measuring noise. Includes detailed procedures for acceleration and constant speed tests, gear selection based on PMR, and calculation of partial power factor (k_p).
