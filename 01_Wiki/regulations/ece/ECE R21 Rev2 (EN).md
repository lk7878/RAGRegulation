# Regulation No. 21: Uniform Provisions Concerning the Approval of Vehicles with Regard to Their Interior Fittings

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the interior fittings of passenger cars with regard to:
1.1. the interior parts of the passenger compartment other than the rear-view mirror or mirrors;
1.2. the arrangement of the controls;
1.3. the roof and the sliding roof, and
1.4. the seat-back and the rear parts of seats.

## 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation,
2.1. "approval of a vehicle" means the approval of a vehicle type with regard to its interior fittings;
2.2. "vehicle type" with regard to the interior fittings... means power-driven vehicles which do not differ in such essential respects as the lines and constituent materials of the bodywork of the passenger compartment and the arrangement of the controls;
2.3. "reference zone" means the head-impact zone as defined in annex 1...;
2.4. "level of the instrument panel" means the line defined by the points of contact of vertical tangents to the instrument panel;
2.5. "roof" means the upper part of the vehicle extending from the upper edge of the windscreen to the upper edge of the rear window and bounded at the sides by the upper framework of the side-walls;
2.6. "belt line" means the line constituted by the transparent lower contour of the side windows of the vehicle;
2.7. "convertible car" means a vehicle where, in certain configurations, there is no rigid part of the vehicle body above the belt line...;
2.8. "vehicle with opening roof" means a vehicle of which only the roof or part of it can be folded back or be opened, or may slide, leaving the existing structural elements of the vehicle above the belt line;
2.9. "folding (tip-up) seat" means an auxiliary seat intended for occasional use and which is normally folded out of the way.

## 3. Application for Approval
3.1. The application for approval... shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or by his duly accredited representative.
3.2. It shall be accompanied by... a detailed description of the vehicle type... accompanied by a photograph or an exploded view of the passenger compartment.
3.3. The following shall be submitted to the technical service...: a vehicle representative of the vehicle type... or the part or parts of the vehicle regarded as essential for the checks and tests...

## 4. Approval
4.1. If the vehicle type... meets the requirements of paragraph 5..., approval... shall be granted.
4.2. An approval number shall be assigned to each type approved...
4.3. Notice of approval... shall be communicated to the Parties... by means of a form conforming to the model in annex 2...
4.4. ...every vehicle conforming to a vehicle type approved... shall bear an international approval mark consisting of: a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the country which has granted approval; the number of this Regulation, followed by the letter "R", a dash and the approval number...
4.5.-4.8. (Details on placement and arrangement of approval marks)

## 5. Requirements
### 5.1. Forward interior parts of the passenger compartment above the level of the instrument panel...
5.1.1. The reference zone... shall not contain any dangerous roughness or sharp edges...
5.1.2. Vehicle parts within the reference zone... shall be energy-dissipating, as prescribed in annex 4...
5.1.3. The lower edge of the instrument panel shall... be rounded to a radius of curvature of not less than 19 mm.
5.1.4. Switches, pull-knobs and the like... which project from 3.2 mm to 9.5 mm... shall have a cross sectional area of not less than 2 cm²... and shall have rounded edges with a radius of curvature of not less than 2.5 mm.
5.1.5. If these components project more than 9.5 mm... they shall be so designed... as to be able, under the effect of a longitudinal horizontal force of 37.8 daN... either to retract... or to become detached...
5.1.6. In the case of a projection comprising a component made of non-rigid material of less than 50 shore A hardness mounted on a rigid support, the requirements... shall apply only to the rigid support.

### 5.2. Forward interior parts of the passenger compartment below the level of the instrument panel...
5.2.1. ...components... such as switches, the ignition key, etc. shall comply with the requirements of paragraphs 5.1.4. to 5.1.6.
5.2.2. The handbrake control... shall be so placed that when it is in the position of rest there is no possibility of the occupants... striking against it...
5.2.3. Shelves and other similar items shall be so designed... that the supports in no case have protruding edges...
5.2.4. If the items... contain a part made of material less than 50 shore A hardness... the above requirements... shall apply only to the rigid support.

### 5.3. Other interior fittings in the passenger compartment in front of the transverse plane passing through the torso reference line...
5.3.1. Scope: The requirements... apply to control handles, levers and knobs and to any other protruding objects...
5.3.2. Requirements: If the items... are so placed that occupants... can contact them, they shall meet the requirements...
5.3.2.1. their surface terminates in rounded edges, the radii of curvature being not less than 3.2 mm;
5.3.2.2. control levers and knobs shall be so designed... that, under the effect of a... force of 37.8 daN either the projection... is reduced to not more than 25 mm... or the said fittings become detached or bent...
5.3.2.3. the handbrake control... and the gear lever... have... a surface area of not less than 6.5 cm²... the radius of curvature being not less than 3.2 mm.
5.3.3. The requirements... shall not apply to a floor-mounted handbrake control...
5.3.4. The other elements of the vehicle's equipment... are not subject to any regulation if they are situated below a horizontal line passing through the "H" point...
5.3.4.1. Components mounted on the roof... shall have a radius of curvature not less than 3.2 mm and... the width of the projecting parts shall not be less than the amount of their downward projection; alternatively, these components shall pass the energy-dissipating test...
5.3.5. If the parts... comprise a component made of material of less than 50 shore A hardness... the above requirements shall apply only to the rigid support.

### 5.4. Roof
5.4.1. Scope: The requirements... apply to the inner face of the roof.
5.4.2. Requirements:
5.4.2.1. That part of the inner face of the roof... shall exhibit no dangerous roughness or sharp edges... The width of the projecting parts shall not be less than the amount of their downward projection and the edges shall have a radius of curvature of not less than 5 mm. In particular, the rigid roof sticks or ribs... shall not project downwards more than 19 mm.
5.4.2.2. If the roof sticks or ribs do not meet the requirements... they shall pass the energy-dissipating test...
5.4.2.3. The metal wires which stretch the lining of the roof and the frames of the sun visors shall have a maximum diameter of 5 mm or be able to absorb the energy...

### 5.5. Vehicles with an opening roof
5.5.1. Requirements:
5.5.1.1. The following requirements and those of paragraph 5.4. above shall apply to vehicles with an opening roof when the roof is in the closed position.
5.5.1.2. In addition, the opening and operating devices shall:
5.5.1.2.1. be so designed and constructed as to exclude accidental or inopportune operation as far as possible;
5.5.1.2.2. their surfaces shall terminate in rounded edges, the radii of curvature being not less than 5 mm;
5.5.1.2.3. be accommodated, when in the position of rest, in areas which cannot be touched by a sphere 165 mm in diameter. If this condition cannot be met... under the effect of a force of 37.8 daN... either the projection... shall be reduced to not more than 25 mm... or the devices shall become detached...

### 5.6. Convertible vehicles
5.6.1. In the case of convertible vehicles, only the underside of the top of the roll-bar and the top of the windscreen frame... shall comply with the requirements of paragraph 5.4. The system of folding rods or links... shall... exhibit no dangerous roughness or sharp edges...

### 5.7. Rear parts of seats anchored to the vehicle
5.7.1. Requirements:
5.7.1.1. The surface of rear parts of seats shall exhibit no dangerous roughness or sharp edges...
5.7.1.2. ...that part of the back of the front seat which is in the head-impact zone... shall be energy-dissipating...
5.7.1.2.1. In the case of separate front seats, the rear passengers' head-impact zone shall extend for 10 cm on either side of the seat centreline...
5.7.1.2.2. In the case of front bench seats, the impact zone shall extend between the longitudinal vertical planes 10 cm outboard of the centreline of each designated outboard seating position.
5.7.1.2.3. In the head impact zone outside the limits... the seat frame structure shall be padded... and... shall have a radius of curvature of at least 5 mm.
5.7.2. These requirements shall not apply to the rearmost seats, to seats facing sideways or rearwards, to back-to-back seats or to folding (tip-up) seats.
5.7.3. The requirements... shall be considered to be satisfied in the case of head restraints that either are part of a vehicle type approved under Regulation No. 17... or are approved under Regulation No. 25...

### 5.8. Other not mentioned fittings
5.8.1. The requirements of paragraph 5. shall apply to such fittings not mentioned in previous paragraphs as, according to their location, are capable of being contacted by the occupants...

## 6. Modifications and Extension of Approval of the Vehicle Type
6.1. Every modification of the vehicle type shall be notified to the administrative department which approved the vehicle type.
6.2. Confirmation or refusal of approval... shall be communicated... to the Parties...
6.3. The competent authority issuing the extension of approval shall assign a series number for such an extension and inform thereof the other Parties...

## 7. Conformity of Production
7.1. Every vehicle bearing an approval mark... shall conform to the vehicle type approved.
7.2. ...a vehicle bearing the approval mark... shall be taken from the series.
7.3. Production shall be deemed to conform... if the requirements of paragraph 5. above are met.

## 8. Penalties for Non-conformity of Production
8.1. The approval... may be withdrawn if the requirement... is not complied with...
8.2. If a Party... withdraws an approval... it shall forthwith so notify the other Contracting Parties...

## 9. Production Definitely Discontinued
If the holder of the approval completely ceases to manufacture a type of vehicle... he shall inform thereof the authority which granted the approval.

## 10. Names and Addresses of Technical Services...
The Parties... shall communicate to the United Nations Secretariat the names and addresses of the technical services... and of the administrative departments...
