**Amendment Details**

This document is **Amendment 1** to **Revision 2** of **UN Regulation No. 125**. The authentic legal text is **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2016/15**. It entered into force on **8 October 2016**.

**Key Changes to Paragraph 5.1.3.**

The amendment modifies and adds to the existing paragraph 5.1.3., which lists permitted obstructions within the driver's mandatory field of direct vision.

1.  **Revised Paragraph 5.1.3.:** Clarifies that, apart from listed obstructions (A-pillars, window division bars, aerials, indirect vision devices covering their mandatory field, wipers), there shall be no other obstructions. The text explicitly references new sub-paragraphs 5.1.3.3. and 5.1.3.4.

2.  **New Paragraph 5.1.3.1. (Camera Exemption):** Extends the obstruction exemptions to cameras (including their holders and housings) mounted on the vehicle exterior when part of a camera-monitor system. A system replacing a Class I rear-view mirror receives the same exemption.

3.  **New Paragraph 5.1.3.2. (Monitor Exemption):** For vehicles with approved rear-view mirrors that can be optionally replaced by camera-monitor devices, the obstruction exemptions also apply to the monitors, provided:
    *   **(a)** The monitor's obstruction of the direct view does not exceed the level of obstruction caused by the corresponding exterior rear-view mirror (including its housing and holder).
    *   **(b)** The monitor is positioned as close as practicable to the location of the rear-view mirror it replaces.
    *   A footnote references document **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/GRSG/88, paragraph 46** for information on the period of application of this paragraph.

4.  **Renumbering:** Existing paragraphs 5.1.3.1. to 5.1.3.2.2. are renumbered as paragraphs 5.1.3.3. to 5.1.3.4.2.
