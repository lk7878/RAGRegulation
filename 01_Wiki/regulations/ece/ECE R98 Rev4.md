# UN Regulation No. 98 (Revision 4)

## A. Administrative Provisions

### Scope
This Regulation applies to headlamps utilizing gas-discharge light sources, for vehicles of categories M and N and L3.

### 1. Definitions
For the purpose of this Regulation:
1.1. The definitions given in UN Regulation No. 48 and its series of amendments in force at the time of application for type approval shall apply to this Regulation.
1.2. "Lens" means the outermost component of the headlamp (unit) which transmits light through the illuminating surface.
1.3. "Coating" means any product or products applied in one or more layers to the outer face of a lens.
1.4. "Matched pair" means the set of lamps of the same function on the left- and right-hand side of the vehicle.
1.5. Headlamps of different "types" are headlamps which differ in such essential respects as:
    1.5.1. The trade name or mark.
    1.5.2. The characteristics of the optical system.
    1.5.3. The inclusion or elimination of components capable of altering the optical effects by reflection, refraction, absorption and/or deformation during operation.
    1.5.4. Suitability for right-hand or left-hand or for both traffic systems.
    1.5.5. The kind of beam produced (passing-beam or driving-beam or both).
    1.5.6. However, a device intended for the installation on the left side of the vehicle and the corresponding device intended for the installation on the right side of the vehicle shall be considered to be of the same type.
1.7. References made in this Regulation to standard (étalon) filament light source(s) and gas-discharge light source(s) shall refer to Regulations Nos. 37 and 99 respectively, and to their series of amendments in force at the time of application for type approval.

### 2. Application for Approval of a Headlamp
2.1. The application for approval shall be submitted by the owner of the trade name or mark of the headlamp or by his duly accredited representative.
2.2. Every application shall be accompanied by drawings, technical specifications, and samples as specified.
2.3. The materials making up the lens shall be accompanied by the test report of the characteristics of these materials and coatings if they have already been tested.
2.4. In the case of a type of lamp differing only by the trade name or mark from a type that has already been approved, a simplified submission is allowed.

### 3. Markings
3.1. Headlamps submitted for approval shall bear legibly and indelibly the trade name or mark of the applicant.
3.2. They shall comprise, on the lens and on the main body, spaces of sufficient size for the approval mark and the additional symbols.
3.3. Headlamps designed to satisfy the requirements of both right-hand and left-hand traffic shall bear markings indicating the two settings ("R/D" for right-hand traffic, "L/G" for left-hand traffic).
3.4. All beams may bear on their light-emitting surface a centre of reference as shown in Annex 6.
3.5. In the case of lamps with LED module(s), the lamp shall bear the marking of the rated voltage and rated wattage and the light source module specific identification code.
3.6. LED module(s) submitted along with the approval of lamp shall bear the trade name or mark and the specific identification code.
3.7. If an electronic light source control gear which is not part of a LED module is used to operate a LED module(s), it shall be marked with its specific identification code(s), the rated input voltage and wattage.

### 4. Approval
4.1. General provisions for granting approval, assignment of approval numbers, and communication of approval decisions.
4.2. Composition of the approval mark, including the international approval marking (circle with "E" and country number), approval number, and additional symbols (e.g., arrows for traffic direction, "DC"/"DR"/"DCR" for beam type, "PL" for plastic lens, luminous intensity reference mark).
4.3. Arrangement of the approval mark for independent, grouped, combined, or reciprocally incorporated lamps.

## B. Technical Requirements for Headlamps

### 5. General Specifications
5.1. Each sample shall comply with the specifications set forth in paragraphs 6. to 8.
5.2. Headlamps shall be made to retain their prescribed photometric characteristics and remain in good working order under normal use and vibration. They shall be fitted with an aiming device.
5.3. Headlamps designed for both traffic systems must have only two distinct settings and preclude inadvertent shifting.
5.4. Illumination configuration for different traffic conditions and requirements for adapting beams.
5.5. Requirements for devices that provide alternate beams or bend lighting (robustness, failure mode).
5.6. Complementary tests according to Annex 4 to ensure no excessive change in photometric performance in use.
5.7. Light transmitting components made of plastic material shall be tested according to Annex 5.
5.8. Light sources: Gas-discharge headlamps shall only be equipped with replaceable gas-discharge light source(s) approved according to UN Regulation No. 99. Filament light sources, if used, must be approved according to UN Regulation No. 37. Light sources must be fixable only in the correct position.
5.9. The headlamp and ballast system shall not generate radiated or power line disturbances causing malfunction of other vehicle systems.
5.10. The test house may require additional samples or equipment.
5.11. The test procedure shall be under the manufacturer's mounting specifications.
5.12. Headlamps with LED modules must comply with Annex 11.
5.13. As an alternative, headlamps may conform with the latest version of UN Regulation No. 149 as it relates to headlamps equipped with gas-discharge light sources.

### 6. Illumination
6.1. General provisions for measurement (25 m distance, photoelectric cell, coordinate system), light source aging, and color (white).
6.2. Provisions concerning passing beams, including the definition and aiming of the "cut-off", permitted light sources (one gas-discharge plus optional additional sources for bend lighting or IR), test voltages, and detailed photometric requirements at specified test points and zones (see tables in OCR).
6.3. Provisions concerning driving beams, including aiming, permitted light sources (multiple possible), and photometric requirements at specified test points, including maximum luminous intensity (I_M) limits (43,800 cd min, 215,000 cd max) and reference mark calculation.
6.4. Provisions concerning movable reflectors, requiring compliance at all specified positions and after tilting.

### 7. Gauging of Discomfort and/or Disability
The discomfort and/or disability caused by the passing beam of headlamps shall be gauged (subject of a future recommendation).

## C. Further Administrative Provisions

### 8. Modification of the Headlamp Type and Extension of Approval
Procedures for notifying modifications, requiring further tests if necessary, and communicating extensions.

### 9. Conformity of Production
Requirements for the manufacturer's quality control procedures, including access to equipment, record-keeping, analysis, and specific tests (at least those in Annex 8). Authority verification and sampling rights.

### 10. Penalties for Non-conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met, with notification to other Contracting Parties.

### 11. Production Definitively Discontinued
Procedure for notifying the authority and other Contracting Parties if manufacture ceases.

### 12. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties shall communicate relevant information to the UN Secretariat.

### 13. Transitional Provisions
13.1. As from 24 months after the official date of entry into force of UN Regulation No. 149, Contracting Parties shall cease to grant approvals to this Regulation.
13.2. Extensions of approval to this and any previous series of amendments shall not be refused.
13.3. Approvals for replacement devices based on this and previous amendments shall continue to be granted.
13.4. Fitting or use of approved replacement devices on vehicles in use shall continue to be allowed.

## Annexes
The Regulation includes multiple annexes detailing specific procedures and requirements:
- **Annex 1:** Communication form for approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal.
- **Annex 2:** Examples of approval mark arrangements.
- **Annex 3:** Spherical coordinate measuring system and test point locations (Figures A, B, C).
- **Annex 4:** Tests for stability of photometric performance in operation (including test cycles, voltages, and cut-off line drift test). Includes an Appendix with operational period overviews.
- **Annex 5:** Requirements for lamps with plastic lenses – testing of lens/material samples and complete lamps. Includes Appendices 1-4 on test order, measurement of diffusion/transmission, spray testing, and adhesive tape adherence.
- **Annex 6:** Centre of reference (optional mark).
- **Annex 7:** Voltage markings.
- **Annex 8:** Minimum requirements for conformity of production control procedures.
- **Annex 9:** Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector.
- **Annex 10:** Instrumental verification of the "cut-off" for passing beam headlamps (referenced).
- **Annex 11:** Requirements for LED modules and headlamps including LED modules (referenced).
