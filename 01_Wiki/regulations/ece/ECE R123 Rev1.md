# Regulation No. 123
## Uniform provisions concerning the approval of adaptive front-lighting systems (AFS) for motor vehicles

## Contents
### A. Administrative provisions
0. **Scope**: This Regulation applies to adaptive front-lighting systems (AFS) for motor vehicles of categories M and N1.
1. **Definitions**: Includes definitions for "Adaptive front-lighting system", "Class" of a passing beam (C, V, E, W), "Mode", "Bending mode", "Lighting unit", "Installation unit", "System control", "Neutral state", "Signal", "Signal generator", "Supply and operating device", "System reference axis", "Lens", "Coating", "Aiming", "Adjustment", "Traffic-change function", "Substitute function".
2. **Application for approval of a system**: Specifies the information and documentation required from the applicant, including drawings, technical description, safety concept, samples, and test materials.
3. **Markings**: Requirements for trade name/mark, approval marks, and additional symbols on installation units and LED modules.
4. **Approval**: Procedures for granting approval, composition of the approval mark, and arrangements for independent and grouped/combined lamps.

### B. Technical requirements for systems
5. **General specifications**: Covers conformity to specifications, adjustment devices, light sources (replaceable/non-replaceable, LED modules), traffic direction adaptation, mechanical durability, failure modes, traffic-change function, and installation instructions.
6. **Illumination**:
    - 6.1. **General provisions**: Each system must provide a class C passing beam and may incorporate additional classes/modes. Specifies photometric measurement conditions.
    - 6.2. **Provisions concerning the passing beam**: Requirements for cut-off, aiming, and photometric values for classes C, V, E, W and bending modes. Includes requirements for traffic-change functions.
    - 6.3. **Provisions concerning the driving beam**: Aiming, photometric requirements (minimum and maximum luminous intensity values at specified test points), and provisions for lateral movement.
    - 6.4. **Other provisions**: Requirements for systems with adjustable lighting units, including testing for different mounting positions.
7. **Colour**: The colour of the light emitted shall be white.

### C. Further administrative provisions
8. **Modification of the system type and extension of approval**
9. **Conformity of production**: Procedures and minimum requirements.
10. **Penalties for non-conformity of production**
11. **Production definitely discontinued**
12. **Names and addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments**
13. **Transitional provisions**: Relating to the 01 series of amendments.

## Annexes
1. **Communication form** for approval/refusal/extension/withdrawal.
2. **Examples of arrangements of approval marks**.
3. **Passing beam photometric requirements** (Tables and figures specifying values and zones for classes C, V, E, W).
4. **Tests for stability of photometric performance of systems in operation** (clean and dirty tests, change in cut-off line position).
5. **Minimum requirements for conformity of production control procedures**.
6. **Requirements for systems incorporating lenses of plastic material** (testing of lens/material samples and complete systems).
7. **Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector**.
8. **Passing beam "cut-off" and aiming provisions**.
9. **Photometric measurement provisions**.
10. **Description forms**.
11. **Requirements for LED modules and adaptive front-lighting systems (AFS) including LED modules**.

## Appendices to Annexes
- **Appendix 1** (to Annex 6): Chronological order of approval tests.
- **Appendix 2** (to Annex 6): Method of measurement of the diffusion and transmission of light.
- **Appendix 3** (to Annex 6): Spray testing method.
- **Appendix 4** (to Annex 6): Adhesive tape adherence test.
