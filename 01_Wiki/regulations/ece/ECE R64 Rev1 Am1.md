**Amendment Details**

This document is **Amendment 1** to **Revision 1** of **Addendum 63** to the 1958 Agreement, concerning **Regulation No. 64**.

**Key Amendments:**

1.  **Paragraph 5.1.4.1.1.** is amended to specify that its requirements apply only to a **type 4 temporary-use spare unit** (as defined in paragraph 2.10.4) supplied for use on M1 and N1 category vehicles.

2.  **Annex 3, Paragraphs 2.3. to 2.3.1.2.** are amended, and new paragraphs are inserted, detailing braking performance requirements for vehicles fitted with temporary-use spare units. The amendments differentiate requirements based on:
    *   Vehicle category (M1 or N1).
    *   The type of temporary-use spare unit (types 1, 2, 3, 5 vs. type 4).
    *   The applicable base braking regulation (UN Regulation No. 13 or No. 13-H).
    *   Prescribed test speed (80 km/h or 120 km/h).

    Specific requirements are provided for **stopping distance** and **mean fully developed deceleration (d<sub>m</sub>)** under various combinations of the above conditions.

    A new **paragraph 2.3.1.3** is inserted for N1 category vehicles fitted with a type 4 spare unit tested at 120 km/h under UN Regulation No. 13.

    Former paragraphs 2.3.1.3 and 2.3.1.4 are renumbered to **2.3.1.4 and 2.3.1.5** and amended to cover vehicles approved under UN Regulation No. 13-H fitted with temporary-use spare units, with separate criteria for types 1/2/3/5 (80 km/h test) and type 4 (120 km/h test).
