**Uniform Provisions Concerning the Approval of:**
I. Rear underrun protective devices (RUPDs)
II. Vehicles with regard to the installation of an RUPD of an approved type
III. Vehicles with regard to their rear underrun protection (RUP)

**Amendment Details:**
This document constitutes **Addendum 57: Regulation No. 58, Revision 2 – Amendment 2**, and is the **Supplement 2 to the 02 series of amendments**. It entered into force on **18 November 2012**.

**Key Amendments:**
*   **Paragraph 31.3.** is amended to specify that, starting 18 months after the entry into force of this amendment, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation:
    *   (a) May refuse type approval for components not meeting the 02 series of amendments.
    *   (b) Shall grant approvals only for components meeting the 02 series of amendments.
    *   (c) May prohibit the fitting of components not meeting the 02 series of amendments.
*   **Paragraph 31.5.** is amended to specify that, as from 48 months after the entry into force of this amendment, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation:
    *   (a) Shall grant vehicle type approvals only if the vehicle meets the requirements of paragraph 2.3.(b), 2.3.(c), or Part III as amended by the 02 series.
    *   (b) May refuse national/regional type-approval and first registration of vehicles not meeting those requirements.
