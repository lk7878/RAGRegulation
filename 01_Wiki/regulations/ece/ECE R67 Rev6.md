# UN Regulation No. 67 (Revision 6)

**Uniform provisions concerning:**
I. Approval of specific equipment of vehicles of categories M and N using liquefied petroleum gases in their propulsion system
II. Approval of vehicles of categories M and N fitted with specific equipment for the use of liquefied petroleum gases in their propulsion system with regard to the installation of such equipment

**Date:** 30 January 2023
**Agreement:** Concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical United Nations Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts...
**Revision:** 6
**Incorporates text up to:** Supplement 1 & 2 to the 02 series of amendments (entry into force 15 Oct 2019, 29 May 2020) and the 03 series of amendments (entry into force 29 May 2020).

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to:
1.1. **Part I:** Approval of specific equipment of vehicles of category M and N using LPG in their propulsion system.
1.2. **Part II:** Approval of vehicles of category M and N fitted with specific LPG equipment with regard to the installation of such equipment.

## 2. Definition and classification of components
LPG components are classified based on maximum operating pressure and function (see Figure 1 flowchart in regulation):
- **Class 0:** High pressure parts containing liquid LPG with pressure > 3,000 kPa.
- **Class 1:** High pressure parts containing liquid LPG at vapour pressure up to 3,000 kPa.
- **Class 2:** Low pressure parts containing vaporized LPG (max op. pressure < 450 kPa and > 20 kPa above atmospheric).
- **Class 2A:** Low pressure parts for a limited range (max op. pressure < 120 kPa and > 20 kPa above atmospheric).
- **Class 3:** Shut-off valves and pressure relief valves operating in the liquid phase.
Components designed for pressure < 20 kPa above atmospheric are not subject to this Regulation.

### 2.1. Pressure Definitions
- **Pressure:** Relative vs. atmospheric.
- **Service pressure:** Settled pressure at 15°C.
- **Test pressure:** Pressure during approval test.
- **Working pressure (WP):** Max design pressure for strength determination.
- **Operating pressure:** Pressure under normal conditions.
- **Maximum operating pressure:** Max pressure that might arise.
- **Classification pressure:** Max allowable operating pressure per class.

### 2.2. Specific Equipment
Includes: container, container accessories, vaporizer/pressure regulator, shut-off valve, gas injection device/injector/gas mixing piece, gas dosage unit, flexible hoses, filling unit, non-return valve, gas-tube pressure relief valve, filter unit, pressure/temperature sensor, fuel pump, service coupling, electronic control unit, fuel rail, pressure relief device, multi-component, gas tubes and couplings.

### 2.3. - 2.24. Detailed Definitions
Definitions for container types (standard cylindrical, special, all-composite), batch, type, and all listed specific equipment components (e.g., 80% stop valve, level indicator, multivalve, gas-tight housing, LPG-system, interconnected LPG-system (ICS)).

## Part I - Approval of Specific LPG Equipment

### 3. Application for approval
Submitted by holder of trade name/mark or representative. Requires detailed description, drawings, verification of compliance, and samples.

### 4. Markings
Components must bear manufacturer's trade name/mark, type, and for non-metallic parts, manufacturing month/year. Must have space for approval mark (see Annex 2A). Containers require a marking plate with serial number, capacity, "LPG", test pressure, "maximum degree of filling: 80%", approval year/month, approval mark, and "PUMP INSIDE" if applicable. Additional marks (H1, H2, H3) for certain remotely controlled valves.

### 5. Approval
Granted if samples meet requirements. An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate series of amendments). Communication via form in Annex 2B. International approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, Regulation No. "67R", dash, approval number) must be affixed. For Class 0, working pressure (WP) must also be marked.

### 6. Specifications for LPG Equipment Components
#### 6.1. General Provisions
Equipment must function correctly and safely. Materials must be LPG compatible. Parts influenced by LPG, high pressure, or vibrations must undergo relevant tests. Installation must comply with EMC requirements (UN R10, 02 series).

#### 6.2. - 6.16. Component-Specific Provisions
Table 1 maps components to their respective approval annexes:
- Containers: Annex 10
- Container Accessories (80% stop valve, level indicator, pressure relief valve, PRD, etc.): Annex 3, 4, 7
- Fuel Pump: Annex 4
- Vaporizer/Pressure Regulator: Annex 6
- Shut-off/Non-return/Gas-tube pressure relief valves, Service couplings: Annex 7
- Flexible Hoses: Annex 8
- Filling Unit: Annex 9
- Gas Injection Devices/Injectors/Gas Mixing Piece: Annex 11
- Gas Dosage Units: Annex 12
- Pressure/Temperature Sensors: Annex 13
- Electronic Control Unit: Annex 14
- LPG Filter Units: Annex 5
- Gas Tubes (non-seamless, special materials) & couplings: Annex 15
- Multi-component: Applicable annexes for single components.

#### 6.17. General Design Rules
Detailed technical requirements for:
- 80% stop valve (float strength, cut-off flow rate, marking).
- Electrical operated devices (isolation, protection degree IP40/IP54, hermetic sealing).
- Valves activated by electric/external power (must be "closed" when power off, endurance test for ICS valves).
- Heat exchange medium (compatibility, pressure resistance).
- Components with high & low pressure parts (prevent overpressure in low pressure side).
- Pumps (prevent overpressure).
- Pressure regulator/vaporizer (prevent gas flow when not operating).
- Gas-tube pressure relief valve (opening pressure, leakage).
- Pressure relief valve (discharge valve) (mounting, opening pressure, flow capacity, leakage).
- Pressure relief device (fuse) (opening temperature, flow capacity, mounting).
- Fuel pump heat dissipation.
- Filling unit (non-return valve, contamination protection, design per Annex 9 figures, flow rates, impact test, location).
- Level indicator (type, float strength).
- Gas-tight housing (outlet cross-section, leak-proofness, pressure resistance).
- Remotely controlled service valve with excess flow valve (pressure resistance, leak rate, bypass, cut-off flow).
- Non-return valves for ICS (endurance test, marking).

#### 6.18. Compatibility with Petrol
For interconnected LPG-systems, components that may contact petrol must meet requirements in Annex 19 and bear a specific approval mark (N, M, or NM).

### 7. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which decides on retesting. Communication via Annex 2B.

### 8. (Not allocated)

### 9. Conformity of Production
Procedures per Agreement Appendix 2. Includes production controls, minimum test requirements (Annexes 8, 10, 16). Specific tests for containers (pressure, radiographic, mechanical) and hose assemblies (pressure test).

### 10. Penalties for Non-conformity
Approval may be withdrawn. Notification via Annex 2B.

### 11. Transitional Provisions (Components)
Timelines for application of 01 and 02 series of amendments regarding granting/accepting approvals and sale of components.

### 12. Production Definitively Discontinued
Manufacturer must inform authority. Notification via Annex 2B.

### 13. Names and Addresses
Parties to communicate details of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to UN Secretariat.

## Part II - Approval of Vehicles Fitted with LPG Equipment

### 14. Definitions
- Approval of a vehicle: Approval of vehicle type regarding installation of LPG equipment.
- Vehicle type: Based on manufacturer, type designation, and essential design/construction aspects (chassis, LPG installation).
- Commanded stop phase: Period of automatic engine stop/start for fuel saving.

### 15. Application for Approval
Submitted by vehicle manufacturer or representative. Requires description per Annex 1 and a representative vehicle.

### 16. Approval
Granted if vehicle meets requirements of paragraph 17. Approval number assigned. Communication via form in Annex 2D. International approval mark affixed (similar to Part I, see Annex 2C for arrangement).

### 17. Requirements for Installation
#### 17.1. General
Equipment must not exceed design working pressure. All parts must be type approved per Part I (except integrated ECU covered by vehicle approval). Materials suitable for LPG. Proper fastening, no leaks. Protected against damage. External inspection possible without tools. No unauthorized appliances connected (exceptions for heating systems on certain heavy vehicles and service coupling for mono-fuel vehicles without limp-home). M2/M3 vehicles require identification plates (Annex 17). Service coupling requires sticker (Annex 18).

#### 17.2. Further Requirements
Components shall not project beyond vehicle external surface (filling unit exception: ≤10mm). No component (except container) may extend beyond vehicle's lower edge unless another part is lower within 150mm. Minimum 100mm distance from heat sources unless shielded.

#### 17.3. The LPG System
Lists mandatory and optional components. Container fittings may be combined. Shut-off valve may be combined with regulator/vaporizer.

#### 17.4. Installation of Fuel Container
Permanently installed, not in engine compartment. Correct position per manufacturer. No metal-to-metal contact except at fixings. Secured via permanent points or frame/straps. Minimum 200mm above road surface unless protected. Mounting must absorb specified accelerations (20g/8g for M1/N1, etc.) – calculation accepted.

#### 17.5. Further Requirements to Container
If multiple containers connected to single delivery tube: non-return valve downstream of service valve and tube pressure relief valve required, unless service valve backflow pressure >500kPa and control prevents simultaneous opening (overlap ≤2 min).

#### 17.6. Accessories to Container
- **Service valve with excess flow:** Mounted directly on container. Automatically closed when engine not running (exceptions for liquid injection purge ≤10s and during commanded stop phases). If closed during commanded stop, must comply with Annex 3, para. 4.7.
- **Pressure relief valve:** Mounted in vapour space, discharges to atmosphere or gas-tight housing.
- **80% stop valve & Level indicator:** Suitable and correctly positioned.
- **Gas-tight housing:** Required unless container outside and fittings protected. Must vent to atmosphere downwards (not into wheel arch/at heat source). Minimum clear opening 450 mm². Leak-tight at 10 kPa. Properly secured.

#### 17.7. Gas Tubes and Hoses
- **Seamless tubes (copper/stainless/coated steel):** Copper must be sleeved, max dimensions specified.
- **Other seamless/non-seamless tubes:** Must comply with Annex 15 tests.
- **Non-metallic pipes:** Must comply with para. 6.7.
- **Hoses allowed if compliant.**
- Secured to avoid vibration/stresses. Protected at fixings and passages. Not at jacking points.

#### 17.8. Gas Connections
Soldered/welded/bite-type compression joints not permitted (except for parts of detachable couplings). Compatible fittings regarding corrosion. Stainless with stainless. Distributing-blocks corrosion-resistant. Appropriate joints (compression, flanges) that don't damage tube. Minimum number of joints, accessible for inspection.

#### 17.8.8. Passenger/Enclosed Luggage Compartment
Gas tube/hose length minimized (e.g., only from container to vehicle side). No gas connections except on gas-tight housing and filling unit connection with venting sleeve. Exceptions for M2/M3 with vented sleeve/ducting.

#### 17.9. Remotely Controlled Shut-off Valve
Installed near pressure regulator/vaporizer (may be incorporated). May be elsewhere if return system provided. Cuts off fuel when engine not running or other fuel selected (delay ≤2s allowed). Exceptions for liquid injection purge ≤10s and during commanded stop phases. If closed during commanded stop, must comply with Annex 7, para. 1.7.

#### 17.10. Filling Unit
Secured against rotation, protected from dirt/water. If container inside passenger/luggage compartment, filling unit must be outside.

#### 17.11. Fuel Selection System and Electrical Installation
Electrical components fused (accessible without tools). No power conducted by gas tube. Electrical parts in pressure >20kPa isolated (no current through LPG). Cables protected. Protection degrees: IP40 inside boot/passenger compartment, IP54 elsewhere. Vehicles with multiple fuels require selection system. No sparks in gas-tight housing.

#### 17.12. Pressure Relief Device
Fitted to container to discharge into gas-tight housing if present and compliant.

#### 17.13. Specific Provisions for Interconnected LPG-Systems (ICS)
- **LPG-Diesel (Dual-fuel):** Prevent LPG flow into diesel tank and diesel into LPG container (e.g., two non-return valves per para. 6.17.14.1).
- **LPG-Petrol (Bi-fuel):**
    1. Prevent LPG into petrol tank (e.g., two non-return valves per para. 6.17.14.1).
    2. Prevent petrol overfilling LPG tank (>80%): Requires ECU complying with Annex 14, para. 6 and a remotely controlled shut-off valve per para. 6.17.3.3.
    3. Limit petrol content in LPG tank to ≤16% of actual volume (procedures in Annex 20, fault indication required).
    4. Components contacting petrol must comply with para. 6.18.
    5. ECU from (2) required if flushed volume ≤0.4L **or** LPG container capacity >20L.

### 18. Conformity of Production
Procedures per Agreement Appendix 2. Type Approval Authority verifies controls annually.

### 19. Penalties for Non-conformity
Approval may be withdrawn. Notification via Annex 2D.

### 20. Modification and Extension of Approval
Modifications notified. Authority decides on further testing. Communication via Annex 2D.

### 21. Production Definitively Discontinued
Notification via Annex 2D.

### 22. Transitional Provisions (Vehicle Installation)
Timelines for application of 01, 02, and 03 series of amendments regarding granting/accepting approvals, fitting of components, and first registration of vehicles.

### 23. Names and Addresses
Parties to communicate details to UN Secretariat.

## Annexes (Listed in Contents)
The regulation includes 23 Annexes (1-20, with some having appendices) detailing essential characteristics, approval mark arrangements, communication forms, and specific technical provisions and test procedures for each type of component and for vehicle installation aspects.
