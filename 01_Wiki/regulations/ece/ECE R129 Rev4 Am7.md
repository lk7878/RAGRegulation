**Amendment Details**

This document constitutes **Amendment 7** to **UN Regulation No. 129 (Revision 4)**, specifically as **Supplement 7 to the 03 series of amendments**. It was issued on 21 February 2023 and entered into force on 4 January 2023.

The authentic and legally binding text is referenced as document **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2022/65**.

**Technical Amendment**

The amendment modifies paragraph **6.6.4.5.1.** concerning the main injury assessment criterion for head containment during lateral impact testing of Enhanced Child Restraint Systems (ECRS).

The revised text specifies the positioning of side protection relative to the dummy's head center of gravity at the test start and defines the head containment assessment criteria for the first 80 ms of the impact:
-   (a) No head contact with the door panel.
-   (b) No part of the head shall pass beyond a defined vertical longitudinal "head containment plane" (detailed in Annex 6, Appendix 3, Figure 1). This criterion is noted as for monitoring purposes only for tests conducted with a Q10 dummy.
