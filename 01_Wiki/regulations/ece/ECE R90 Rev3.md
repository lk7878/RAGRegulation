# Regulation No. 90

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the basic braking function of the following replacement parts:
1. **Replacement brake lining assemblies** intended for use in friction brakes forming part of a braking system of vehicles of category M, N, L and O which have a type approval in accordance with Regulations Nos. 13, 13-H or 78.
2. **Replacement drum brake linings** designed to be riveted to a brake shoe for fitment to and use on vehicles of category M, N, N, O or O having a type approval in accordance with Regulation No. 13.
3. **Replacement brake lining assemblies used for separate parking brake systems** being independent of the vehicle service brake system will be subject only to the technical prescriptions defined in Annex 8 of this Regulation.
4. **Replacement brake drums and discs** intended for use in friction brakes forming part of a braking system of vehicles of category M, N and O which have a type approval in accordance with Regulation No. 13 or Regulation No. 13-H.

**Exclusions:**
*   Original brake discs and brake drums, fitted at time of manufacturing of the vehicle and original replacement discs and drums intended for the servicing of the vehicle are not subject to this Regulation.
*   This Regulation does not apply to "Special parts", as defined in paragraph 2.3.4.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Manufacturer:** The organization which can assume technical responsibility for the parts and can demonstrate it possesses the necessary means to achieve conformity of production.
*   **Replacement part:** Either a replacement brake lining assembly type, a replacement drum brake lining type, a replacement drum brake lining, a replacement brake drum or a replacement brake disc.
*   **Original part:** Either an original brake lining, an original brake lining assembly, an original drum brake lining, an original brake drum or an original brake disc.
*   **Brake lining assembly:** A component of a friction brake which is pressed against a drum or disc to produce the friction force. Includes shoe assemblies (drum brakes) and pad assemblies (disc brakes).
*   **Original brake disc/brake drum:** In the case of motor vehicles, is a brake disc/drum covered by the vehicle braking system type approval according to Regulation No. 13 or 13-H. For trailers, it is covered by the vehicle braking system type approval or is part of a brake for which the axle manufacturer owns a relevant test report.
*   **Replacement parts categories for discs/drums:**
    *   **Original replacement brake discs/drums:** Original parts intended for servicing, carrying an indelible identification code.
    *   **Identical brake disc/drum:** Chemically and physically identical in every respect to the original part, except for the absence of the vehicle manufacturer mark.
    *   **Equivalent brake disc/drum:** Identical to the original in all dimensions, geometric features, basic design, and material sub-group.
    *   **Interchangeable brake disc/drum:** Has the same interface dimensions as the original but may differ in design, material composition and mechanical properties.
*   **Special brake disc/drum:** A replacement brake disc/drum not covered by the definitions for original, identical, equivalent, or interchangeable parts.

## 3. Application for Approval
An application for approval of a replacement part for specific vehicle type(s) shall be submitted by the manufacturer or their accredited representative. The holder of a vehicle type approval may also submit an application for parts conforming to the type recorded in their approval documentation.

The application must include detailed descriptions, diagrams, and, for discs/drums, information on material composition, mechanical properties, and manufacturing process. A specified number of samples must be provided for testing (see tables in OCR pages 10-11).

## 4. Approval
If the replacement part meets the requirements, approval shall be granted.

**Approval Number Format:** Comprises four groups of digits: `XX Y ZZZZ / AAAA`
*   `XX`: Series of amendments (e.g., 02).
*   `Y`: Category of replacement part (A=lining assembly, B=drum lining, C=disc, D=drum).
*   `ZZZZ`: Identifies the manufacturer and type of the part.
*   `AAAA`: Identifies the variant/shoe/backplate/specific dimension.

**Approval Mark:** An international approval mark consisting of a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the country which granted approval, the Regulation number "90R", a dash, and the approval number.

## 5. Specifications and Tests
### 5.1. General
A replacement part must be designed so that when substituted for the original part, the vehicle's braking efficiency accords with that of the approved vehicle type. Specific requirements include performance similar to the original part, adequate mechanical characteristics, no asbestos in linings, and sufficient deformation resistance for discs/drums.

### 5.2. Requirements for Brake Linings
Performance requirements are detailed in annexes based on vehicle category:
*   **Annex 3:** Vehicles of categories M1, M2, N1.
*   **Annex 4:** Vehicles of categories M3, N2, N3.
*   **Annex 5:** Vehicles of categories O1, O2.
*   **Annex 6:** Vehicles of categories O3, O4.
*   **Annex 7:** Vehicles of category L.
*   **Annex 8:** Parking brake lining assemblies for systems independent of service brakes.

**Mechanical characteristics tests** include shear strength (ISO 6312), compressibility (ISO 6310), and material hardness (ISO 2039-2) with specified minimum values.

### 5.3. Requirements for Brake Discs/Drums
Parts are classified as Original replacement, Identical, Equivalent, or Interchangeable, with differing test requirements:

| Part Type | Performance tests per R13/13-H | Comparison test with original part frictional properties | Integrity tests (high load & thermal fatigue) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Original replacement | No | No | No |
| Identical | No | No | No |
| Equivalent | No | No | **Dynamometer test** |
| Interchangeable | Vehicle test or alt. dynamometer test | Vehicle test or alt. dynamometer test | **Dynamometer test** |

*   **Geometric and Material Requirements:** Equivalent parts must be identical to the original in dimensions and material sub-group. Interchangeable parts must have the same interface dimensions. Specific tolerances for thickness variation, run-out, roughness, etc., are provided for different vehicle categories.
*   **Material Sub-groups:** Four sub-groups of cast iron are defined based on chemical composition (Carbon, Silicon, Manganese, Chromium, Copper content), hardness (HBW), and tensile strength.
*   **Test Groups and Variants:** Defines criteria for grouping similar discs/drums for testing purposes. The variant with the highest ratio of kinetic energy to replacement part mass within a test group is tested.
*   **Test Reports:** A test report with content at least as defined in Annex 13 must be produced.

## 6. Packaging and Marking
*   **Linings:** Must be marketed in sealed axle sets. Packaging must show quantity, manufacturer, type, approved vehicles/axles/brakes, and the approval mark. Each part must permanently display the approval mark, date of manufacture/batch number, and make/type.
*   **Discs/Drums:** Each unit sold must display part number, vehicle/axle application information, and fitting instructions. Every approved disc/drum must be durably marked with the manufacturer's name/trademark, the approval number, a traceability indication, and the minimum thickness (disc) or maximum inside diameter (drum).

## 7. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Any modification must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing. Confirmations or refusals of extended approval must be communicated.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Approved parts must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. The manufacturer must ensure suitable production controls, including regular quality assurance tests as prescribed (e.g., in Annex 9). The Competent Authority may verify these controls.

## 9. Penalties for Non-conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met. Withdrawals must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the authority if manufacture ceases, who then notifies other Parties.

## 11. & 12. Administrative and Transitional Provisions
Details communication of Technical Services and Approval Authorities, and outlines the validity of approvals under previous amendment series.

## Annexes
The regulation contains 13 Annexes detailing communication forms, approval mark arrangements, and specific technical requirements and test procedures for different vehicle categories and part types.
*   **Annex 1A, 1B:** Communication forms for linings and discs/drums.
*   **Annex 2:** Arrangements of approval marks.
*   **Annex 3-8:** Performance requirements and test procedures for brake linings/assemblies for specific vehicle categories (M1,N1; M3,N2,N3; O1,O2; O3,O4; L; parking brakes).
*   **Annex 9:** Special additional procedures for conformity of production (friction behaviour testing for linings, and control parameters for discs/drums).
*   **Annex 10:** Illustrations for geometric checks.
*   **Annex 11, 12:** Requirements and integrity tests for replacement brake discs/drums for vehicles of categories M/N and category O, respectively.
*   **Annex 13:** Model test report for a replacement brake disc/drum.
