# UN Regulation No. 140

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of passenger cars with regard to Electronic Stability Control (ESC) Systems**

*Date of entry into force as an annex to the 1958 Agreement: 22 January 2017*

## 1. Scope
1.1. This Regulation applies to the approval of vehicles of category M₁ and N₁ with regard to their electronic stability control system.
1.2. This Regulation does not cover:
    1.2.1. Vehicles with a design speed not exceeding 25 km/h;
    1.2.2. Vehicles fitted for invalid drivers.

## 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation, key definitions include:
*   **"Approval of a vehicle"**: The approval of a vehicle type with regard to electronic stability control.
*   **"Electronic Stability Control (ESC) System"**: A system that improves vehicle directional stability by automatically controlling braking torques individually on wheels to induce a correcting yaw moment, is computer-controlled with a closed-loop algorithm to limit oversteer and understeer, directly determines yaw rate and estimates side-slip, monitors driver steering inputs, and can modify propulsion torque.
*   **"Oversteer"**: A condition where the vehicle's yaw rate is greater than the yaw rate that would occur at the vehicle's speed as a result of the Ackerman steer angle.
*   **"Understeer"**: A condition where the vehicle's yaw rate is less than the yaw rate that would occur at the vehicle's speed as a result of the Ackerman steer angle.
*   **"Static stability factor (SSF)"**: One-half the track width divided by the height of the center of gravity (SSF = T/2H).

## 3. Application for Approval
The application shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or representative, accompanied by documents describing the vehicle type, ESC components, diagrams, and drawings. A representative vehicle shall be submitted for testing.

## 4. Approval
If the vehicle meets requirements, approval shall be granted with an approval number. An international approval mark shall be affixed to conforming vehicles.

## 5. General Requirements
5.1. Vehicles equipped with ESC shall meet functional (paragraph 6) and performance (paragraph 7) requirements under specified test conditions and procedures.
5.1.1. As an alternative, certain M₁ and N₁ vehicles with a mass > 1,735 kg may be equipped with a vehicle stability function meeting Regulation No. 13, Annex 21 requirements.
5.2.-5.3. The ESC shall be designed to withstand normal use, vibration, corrosion, and ageing.
5.4. Effectiveness shall not be adversely affected by magnetic or electrical fields (compliance with Regulation No. 10).
5.5. Safety aspects related to braking are included in the overall assessment per Regulation No. 13-H.
5.6. Provisions for periodic technical inspection, including visual check of warning signals.

## 6. Functional Requirements
The ESC system shall:
6.1. Be capable of applying braking torques individually to all four wheels.
6.2. Be operational over the full speed range during all driving phases (except when disabled by driver, speed <20 km/h, during initial self-test, or in reverse).
6.3. Remain capable of activation even if ABS or traction control is activated.

## 7. Performance Requirements
During specified tests (Sine with Dwell), the vehicle with ESC engaged shall satisfy:
7.1. **Directional Stability (1 sec)**: Yaw rate 1 second after steering input completion ≤ 35% of first peak yaw rate after steering wheel angle changes sign.
7.2. **Directional Stability (1.75 sec)**: Yaw rate 1.75 seconds after steering input completion ≤ 20% of that first peak yaw rate.
7.3. **Responsiveness**: Lateral displacement ≥ 1.83 m (vehicles ≤3500 kg) or ≥ 1.52 m (vehicles >3500 kg) 1.07 seconds after Beginning of Steer (BOS).
7.4. **ESC Malfunction Detection**: A tell-tale must warn the driver of any ESC malfunction affecting control or response signals. Specific requirements for tell-tale illumination, function check, and extinguishing are provided.
7.5. **ESC Off and Other System Controls**: An "ESC Off" control is permitted if specific conditions are met, including that the system returns to the default compliant mode at each new ignition cycle (with limited exceptions for certain 4WD modes).
7.6. **ESC Off Tell-tale**: Required if an "ESC Off" control is installed, with specifications for its operation.
7.7. **ESC System Technical Documentation**: Required documentation includes system diagrams, written explanation of operation, logic diagrams, and understeer information.

## 8. Test Conditions
Specifies ambient conditions (temperature, wind speed), road test surface (dry, uniform, PBC of 0.9), and vehicle conditions (mass, tyre pressure, use of outriggers for safety, automated steering machine specifications).

## 9. Test Procedure
Detailed procedure including:
9.1.-9.3. Tyre inflation, tell-tale and "ESC Off" control checks.
9.4. Brake conditioning.
9.5. Tyre conditioning.
9.6. Slowly increasing steer procedure to determine steering wheel angle "A".
9.7.-9.8. Preparation for main test.
9.9. **Sine with Dwell Test**: Primary test for oversteer intervention and responsiveness. Conducted in two series (clockwise/anticlockwise) with increasing steering amplitudes.
9.10. ESC malfunction detection test.
9.11. Post data processing for performance metrics (filtering, zeroing, determining BOS/COS, calculating yaw rate ratios and lateral displacement).

## 10. Modification of Vehicle Type or ESC System and Extension of Approval
Procedures for notifying the Type Approval Authority of modifications, which may be classified as a "revision" or an "extension".

## 11. Conformity of Production
Manufactured vehicles must conform to the approved type. The Type Approval Authority verifies control methods periodically.

## 12. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn for non-conformity, with notification to other Contracting Parties.

## 13. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Authority upon ceasing production.

## 14. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties shall communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form model.
*   **Annex 2**: Examples of approval mark arrangements.
*   **Annex 3**: Provisions for using dynamic stability simulation as an alternative to physical testing.
*   **Annex 4**: Specifications and validation requirements for the dynamic stability simulation tool.
*   **Annex 5**: Model test report for the vehicle stability function simulation tool.
