**Corrigendum Details**

This document is **Corrigendum 1** to **Revision 6** of **UN Regulation No. 46**.

**Date of Entry into Force:** 14 March 2018

**Specific Amendment:**

The corrigendum amends **Annex 3, item 9**.

**Change:** Replaces the letter "S" with class "VII", while keeping the reference to footnote 2 unchanged.

**Amended Text (Item 9):**
"9. Brief description
Identification of the device: mirror, camera/monitor, other devices for indirect vision of
Classes I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII 2
Symbol …… "

**Background Information:**

*   This corrigendum is issued under the framework of the **1958 Agreement** (as revised), concerning the adoption of harmonized UN Regulations for wheeled vehicles and reciprocal recognition of approvals.
*   The authentic and legally binding text for this corrigendum is referenced as **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2018/17**.
