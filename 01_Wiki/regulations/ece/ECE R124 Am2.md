**Amendment 2 to UN Regulation No. 124**

**Date of entry into force:** 30 September 2021

**Amendment Details:**

This amendment modifies Annex 8, Paragraph 3, specifically the table under "Acceptance criteria".

**Revised Text (Annex 8, Paragraph 3, Table - "Acceptance criteria"):**

The test shall be considered satisfactory if there is not any visible fracture penetrating through the wheel surface and if there is not total loss of inflation pressure due to tyre depressurization within one minute of completing the test. Fractures and indentations caused by the direct contact with the falling weight are acceptable.

In the case of wheels with demountable rims or other components that can be dismantled, if threaded fastenings that are close to the spoke or ventilation holes fail the wheel is to be considered as having failed the test.
