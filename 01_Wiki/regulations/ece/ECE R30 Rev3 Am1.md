**Amendment 1 to Revision 3 of UNECE Regulation No. 30**

This document constitutes Amendment 1 to Revision 3 of UNECE Regulation No. 30, concerning the approval of pneumatic tyres for motor vehicles and their trailers. It entered into force on 10 November 2007.

The amendment introduces and clarifies requirements for tyres identified by specific "tyre to rim fitment configuration" symbols, primarily "A" or "U". The changes affect definitions, marking rules, and technical calculation methods.

**Key Amendments:**

1.  **Definitions (Paragraphs 2.10.1, 2.19.1):**
    *   For tyres with fitment configuration "A" or "U", the term "bead" is defined as the area of the tyre which seats on the rim.
    *   The term "tyre to rim fitment configuration" is defined as the type of rim the tyre is designed for, with non-standard rims identified by symbols such as "CT", "TR", "TD", "A", or "U".

2.  **Marking Requirements (Paragraph 3.4.1, Annex 3):**
    *   For tyres with fitment configuration "A" or "U", the required markings may be placed anywhere on the outside sidewall.
    *   Annex 3 specifies the marking format for such tyres (e.g., `185-560 R 400A` or `185-560 R 400U`), where the final letter indicates the fitment configuration. The order of markings in the size designation is also clarified.

3.  **Technical Calculations and Dimensions (Paragraphs 6.1.1.3, 6.1.2.3, 6.1.4.2.4, 6.1.5.1):**
    *   **Load Capacity (K factor):** For tyres "A" or "U", the K factor used in load capacity calculations is set to 0.6.
    *   **Outer Diameter:** For these tyres, the outer diameter for calculation purposes is the value specified in the tyre size designation on the sidewall.
    *   **Overall Width:** For these tyres, the overall width in the lower area is defined as the nominal rim width (as per manufacturer's note) plus 20 mm.
    *   **Section Height:** The rules for calculating the nominal section height (H) for sizes in Annex 5 and for tyres "A" or "U" are amended.
