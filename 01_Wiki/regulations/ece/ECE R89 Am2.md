# UN Regulation No. 89 (Amendment 2)

**Supplement 2 to the original version of the Regulation - Date of entry into force: 30 January 2011**

## 1. Scope and Definitions

### 1.1. Scope
- **Part I:** Applies to vehicles of categories M2, M3, N2, and N3 equipped with a Speed Limitation Device (SLD), and to vehicles of categories M3 and N3 equipped with an Adjustable Speed Limitation Device (ASLD) which have not been separately approved according to Part III of this Regulation. Also applies to vehicles whose component parts can be regarded as totally or partially fulfilling the function of an SLD or ASLD.
- **Part II:** Applies to the installation on vehicles of categories M2, M3, N2, and N3 of SLDs, and the installation on vehicles of categories M3 and N3 of ASLDs which have been type-approved to Part III of this Regulation.
- **Part III:** Applies to SLDs intended to be fitted to vehicles of categories M2, M3, N2, and N3, and ASLDs intended to be fitted to vehicles of categories M3 and N3.

### 1.2. Definitions
- **1.2.1.** A Speed Limitation Device (SLD) or function (SLF) shall limit the maximum speed of vehicles of categories M2, M3, N2, and N3.
- **1.2.2.** When fitted, an Adjustable Speed Limitation Device (ASLD) or function (ASLF) shall limit the speed of vehicles of categories M3 and N3 to a speed voluntarily set by the driver when it is activated.
- Paragraph 1.2.3. is deleted.

## 5. Technical Requirements

### 5.1. Requirements for Vehicles
Specifies requirements for vehicles of categories M2, M3, N2, and N3 equipped with a Speed Limitation Function (SLF).

*This note contains the amendments specified in UNECE document E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.88/Amend.2 (and its TRANS counterpart), which entered into force on 30 January 2011. It amends the original UN Regulation No. 89 concerning speed limitation devices for vehicles.*
