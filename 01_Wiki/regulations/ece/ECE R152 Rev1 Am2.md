This document is Amendment 2 to Revision 1 of Addendum 151 to the 1958 Agreement, concerning UN Regulation No. 152. It amends the uniform provisions for the approval of motor vehicles with regard to the Advanced Emergency Braking System (AEBS) for M and N category vehicles. The authentic legal text is ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2021/16.

**Key Amendments:**

**5. Requirements**
*   **5.1.4.1.3.** Added: Requires illumination of a warning signal upon detection of non-electrical failures (e.g., sensor blindness/misalignment).
*   **5.1.4.3.** Deleted.
*   **5.1.6. False reaction avoidance:** Amended to require system design minimizing false collision warnings and avoiding unnecessary emergency braking, with assessment per Annex 3, Appendix 2 scenarios.
*   **5.2. Specific Requirements (Car-to-car scenario):** Amended requirements for collision warning and speed reduction by braking demand. Includes a table specifying maximum allowable relative impact speeds (km/h) for M1 vehicles under defined conditions (e.g., flat, dry road, specific illumination). Example values: At 42 km/h relative speed, max impact speed is 10.00 km/h (max mass) and 0.00 km/h (mass in running order). Values extend up to 60 km/h relative speed with max impact speed of 35.00 km/h.
*   **5.2.2. Specific Requirements (Car-to-pedestrian scenario):** Amended requirements for collision warning and speed reduction. Includes tables specifying maximum allowable impact speeds (km/h) for M1 and N1 vehicles under defined conditions (e.g., perpendicular crossing pedestrian, dry road, illumination ≥2000 Lux). For M1: e.g., at 60 km/h subject speed, max impact speed is 35.00 km/h. For N1: e.g., at 40 km/h subject speed, max impact speed is 10.00 km/h (max mass) and 0.00 km/h (mass in running order); at 60 km/h, values are 40.00 km/h and 35.00 km/h respectively.
*   **5.4. Deactivation:** Amended. Added **5.4.2.3.**: If AEBS deactivation results from manually switching off ESC, it requires at least two deliberate driver actions. Added **5.4.4.**: Allows AEBS function suspension or strategy adaptation during active automated longitudinal control (e.g., ALKS) without driver indication, provided collision avoidance capability is at least equivalent to manual operation.
*   **5.5.7.** Amended: Specifies that an optical signal for temporary AEBS unavailability (e.g., due to weather) shall be constant, and may be the failure warning signal.

**6. Test Procedures**
*   **6.1. Test Conditions:** Amended. Added **6.1.6.**: Allows tests under deviating (suboptimal) conditions at manufacturer's request and Technical Service agreement, provided performance requirements are still met.
*   **6.3. Test Targets:** Amended. Specifies the target for vehicle detection tests shall be a series production M1 saloon or a "soft target" representative per ISO 19206-3:2020.
*   **6.4.1. Warning and Activation Test with a Stationary Vehicle Target:** Amended. Includes tables specifying subject vehicle test speeds (km/h) and tolerances for M1 and N1 categories.
*   **6.5. Warning and Activation Test with a Moving Vehicle Target:** Amended. Includes tables specifying subject vehicle test speeds (km/h) and tolerances for M1 and N1 categories, with target vehicle at 20 km/h.
*   **6.6. Warning and Activation Test with a Pedestrian Target:** Amended. Specifies pedestrian target crosses perpendicularly at 5 km/h. Includes tables specifying subject vehicle test speeds (km/h) and tolerances for M1 and N1 categories.

**Annex 3, Appendix 2 – False Reaction Scenarios**
*   Previous paragraphs 1-3 deleted.
*   New introductory paragraph added, requiring assessment of system strategies to minimize false reactions. Manufacturers must explain strategies and provide evidence (simulation, test data). Defines formulas for calculating **overlap ratio** (between subject and related vehicle) and **offset ratio** (between subject vehicle and stationary object).
*   Four new assessment scenarios added with detailed parameters and example conditions:
    1.  **Scenario 1: Left turn or Right turn at the intersection** – Subject vehicle turns in front of a stopped oncoming vehicle.
    2.  **Scenario 2: Right turn or Left turn of a forward vehicle** – Subject vehicle goes straight while followed vehicle turns.
    3.  **Scenario 3: Curved road with guard pipes and a stationary object** – Subject vehicle navigates a small-radius curve past a stationary object outside guardrails.
    4.  **Scenario 4: Lane change due to road construction** – Subject vehicle changes lanes in front of a lane-reduction signboard.
