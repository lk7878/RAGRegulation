**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles of categories M, N and O with regard to their heating systems**

**Amendment 3**
*Date of entry into force: 26 July 2012*

This document constitutes Amendment 3 to Addendum 121 (Regulation No. 122) of the Revised 1958 Agreement. It amends Annex 8 of the regulation.

**Annex 8 – Safety requirements for LPG combustion heaters and LPG heating systems**

**1. LPG heating systems for road use in motor vehicles and their trailers**

1.1. If an LPG heating system in a motor vehicle or trailer can also be used when the vehicle is in motion, the LPG combustion heater and its supply system shall comply with the following requirements:
    1.1.1. The LPG combustion heater shall comply with the requirements of the harmonized standard **EN 624:2011** (Specifications for dedicated LPG appliances. Room sealed LPG space heating equipment for installation in vehicles and boats).
    1.1.2. For permanently installed LPG containers, all components in contact with liquid phase LPG (from the filling unit to the vaporiser/pressure regulator) and the associated liquid phase installation shall comply with the technical requirements of **Regulation No. 67, Parts I and II and Annexes 3 to 10, 13 and 15 to 17**. However, the installation of an LPG container in vehicles of category O shall comply with the technical requirements of the harmonized standard **EN 1949:2011**.
    1.1.3. The gaseous phase installation of the LPG heating system in a vehicle shall comply with the requirements of the harmonized standard **EN 1949:2011** (Specifications for the installation of LPG systems for habitation purposes in leisure accommodation vehicles and accommodation purposes in other vehicles).
    1.1.4. The LPG supply system shall be designed to supply LPG with the required pressure and in the correct phase for the installed heater. Simultaneous withdrawal of LPG from the permanently installed container in either gaseous or liquid phase is permitted. There shall be **no gas installation connection between the motor vehicle and trailer**.
    1.1.5. The liquid outlet of the permanently installed LPG container supplying the heater shall be fitted with a remotely controlled service valve with an excess flow valve as per Regulation No. 67, paragraph 17.6.1.1. This valve shall be controlled to **automatically close within five seconds of the vehicle engine stopping**, regardless of the ignition switch position. If the heater or LPG supply system is activated within these five seconds, operation may continue. The heating system can always be restarted. This requirement does not apply to trailers. Trailers shall have a label near the filling point instructing to switch off the heater while refilling the LPG container.
    1.1.6. If LPG is supplied in the gaseous phase from a permanently installed container or portable cylinder(s), appropriate provisions shall ensure that:
 1.1.6.1. No liquid LPG can enter the pressure regulator or heater (a separator may be used).
 1.1.6.2. No uncontrolled release due to accidental disconnection can occur. A device shall be installed to stop LPG flow directly after or in a cylinder/container-mounted regulator. If the regulator is mounted remotely, a device shall be installed directly before the hose/pipe from the cylinder/container (high-pressure protection) and an additional device may be installed in or after the regulator for low-pressure protection.
    1.1.7. If LPG is supplied in the liquid phase, the vaporiser and pressure-regulator unit shall be appropriately heated by a suitable heat source.
    1.1.8. In motor vehicles using LPG for propulsion, the heater may be connected to the same LPG container supplying the engine, provided the propulsion system's safety requirements are met. If a separate container is used for heating, it shall have its own filling unit.

**2. LPG heating systems for stationary use only in motor vehicles and their trailers**

2.1. An LPG heating system intended for use only when the vehicle is stationary shall comply with:
    2.1.1. Permanent labels shall be attached to the portable LPG cylinder storage compartment and near the heating system control device, instructing that the heater shall not be in operation and the cylinder valve shall be closed when the vehicle is in motion.
    2.1.2. The LPG combustion heater shall comply with paragraph 1.1.1. above.
    2.1.3. The gaseous phase installation shall comply with paragraph 1.1.3. above.
