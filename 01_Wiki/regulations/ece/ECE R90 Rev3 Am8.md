# UN Regulation No. 90 - Revision 3 - Amendment 8

**Supplement 8 to the 02 series of amendments – Date of entry into force: 8 October 2022.**

This document contains amendments to **UN Regulation No. 90** concerning the approval of replacement brake lining assemblies, drum-brake linings, discs, and drums for power-driven vehicles and their trailers.

## Amendments

### Annex 14
*   **Table A14/2.2.5** is amended to read as follows:

    | Disc Diameter [mm] | Disc Thickness [mm] | Tangential force F [kN] min |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | ≥ 150 < 250 | ≤ 3 | ≥ 8 |
    | | >3 ≤ 4 | ≥ 10 |
    | | > 4 | ≥ 12 |
    | ≥ 250 < 350 | ≤ 4 | ≥ 8 |
    | | > 4 ≤ 5 | ≥ 11 |
    | | > 5 | ≥ 14 |

### Annex 15
*   A new paragraph **1.5. Definition of bell mounting face** is inserted:
    > "Bell mounting face" means the surface of a brake disc that goes in contact with wheel hub.
    > The mounting surface is calculated between the disc inner diameter T(i) and a diameter T(o) defined as the max diameter of area subjected to flatness restrictions as indicated on disc drawing (see figure 1).
    > In case indications on the drawing are missing, T(o) is defined as follows:
    > T(o) = T(k) + B + 10mm

*   A new paragraph **1.6. Definition of reference disc** is inserted:
    > Within each discs group, the reference disc is the one which has the highest ratio of kinetic energy (considering all the replacement applications foreseen) and its mass, as described in paragraph 5.3.6.

*   Paragraph **2.3. Braking surface lightening** is amended:
    > Any solution is allowed (holes, slots, wave, etc.) provided that the ratio between the vehicle kinetic energy to the mass of the disc braking surface, must be same or higher than the reference disc (with tolerance of – 20 per cent maximum).
    > **Example:**
    > Outer diameter 300 mm,
    > radial width of the braking surface 36.5 mm ≥ total area A = 302 cm²
    > Lightening on the braking surface: 64 holes diameter 7 mm ≥ total area B = 24.6 cm²
    > δ = Braking surface material specific weight
    > Braking Surface Mass (BSM)= (A-B) * Th * δ
    > Vehicle kinetic energy K.E.= ½ m V² (as defined in para. 5.3.6)
    > Braking Surface Lightening (BSL) ratio = K.E. / BSM

*   Paragraph **2.6. Spokes of the bell with full/empty ratio** is amended:
    > – measured on the average circumference between end of the mounting face and max. diameter of the bell same or higher than the reference disc (with a tolerance of -20 per cent max.), thickness same or higher than the reference disc (with a tolerance of -15 per cent max) and same mechanical properties, as specified in the international standard for materials, with respect to the reference disc.

*   Paragraph **2.9. Outer diameter included in the range of 100 mm** is amended and includes the following table:

    | Range [mm] | One piece | Composite fixed | Floating discs |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | ≥ 150 < 250 | X | X | X |
    | ≥ 250 < 350 | X | X | X |
