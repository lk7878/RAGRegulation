## Amendment Content

**Paragraph 1., amended to read:**
"1. Scope
This Regulation applies to electrified vehicles of categories M and N which can be propelled in the normal mode, in reverse or at least one forward drive gear, without an internal combustion engine operating1 in respect to their audibility."
