# UN Regulation No. 127 - Revision 2 - Amendment 2 (03 series of amendments)

**Document:** E/ECE/324/Rev.2/Add.126/Rev.2/Amend.2 - E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2/Add.126/Rev.2/Amend.2
**Authentic Text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2022/70
**Date of Entry into Force:** 4 January 2023

## Summary
This document constitutes **Amendment 2** to **Revision 2** of **UN Regulation No. 127**, introducing the **03 series of amendments**. It amends the uniform technical provisions for the approval of motor vehicles concerning pedestrian safety performance.

## Key Amendments

### 1. Definitions (Paragraph 2.)
*   **2.1. "Adult headform bonnet top test area"**: Amended definition specifying boundaries using Wrap Around Distance (WAD) 1700/2500 or distances from reference lines.
*   **2.10. "Bonnet top test area"**: Amended to clarify it comprises child and adult headform test areas.
*   **2.16. "Child headform bonnet top test area"**: Amended definition specifying boundaries using WAD 1000/1700 or distances from reference lines.
*   **2.43. "Wrap Around Distance (WAD)"**: Amended to include procedures for describing WAD1000, WAD1700, and WAD2500.
*   **New Definitions 2.44. to 2.48.**:
    *   **2.44. "Windscreen test area"**: Defines the test area on the windscreen, its boundaries, measurement methods, and specifies use of child/adult headform impactors based on WAD1700.
    *   **2.45. "Cowl monitoring area"**: Defines this area located between the bonnet and windscreen test areas, with separate boundaries for adult and child head tests.
    *   **2.46. "Opaque obscuration"**: Defines areas of glazing preventing light transmission.
    *   **2.47. "Shade band"**: Defines areas of glazing with reduced light transmittance.
    *   **2.48. "Atypical windscreen fracture behaviour"**: Defines criteria based on headform acceleration characteristics or glass breakage patterns.

### 2. Approval Requirements and Tests (Paragraph 5.)
*   **5.2.1. Child and adult headform tests**: Amended performance criteria for Head Injury Criterion (HIC). Requirements now apply to the combined bonnet top and windscreen test area, as well as the bonnet top test area separately. The cowl monitoring area is excluded from performance assessment and is for monitoring only. Specific criteria added for vehicles with only a child headform test area.

### 3. Transitional Provisions (New Paragraphs 11.5. to 11.13.)
*   **11.5.**: Mandates acceptance of type approvals under the 03 series from their entry into force.
*   **11.6.**: From **7 July 2024**, Contracting Parties may refuse approvals to earlier series *first issued* after this date.
*   **11.7.**: Until **7 July 2026**, Contracting Parties must accept approvals to earlier series *first issued* before 7 July 2024.
*   **11.8.**: From **7 July 2026**, Contracting Parties may refuse all approvals to earlier series.
*   **11.9. to 11.11.**: Contain phased provisions for the **WAD 2100 boundary** and tests for atypical windscreen fracture:
    *   Until **1 September 2028**: Parties may grant approvals using these provisions.
    *   Until **1 September 2029**: Parties must accept approvals issued using the WAD 2100 provision.
    *   From **1 September 2029**: Parties may refuse approvals for vehicles with a WAD 2100 boundary.
*   **11.12.**: Parties may grant approvals to any preceding series.
*   **11.13.**: Parties shall continue to grant extensions to existing approvals under preceding series.

### 4. Communication Form (Annex 1)
*   **New items 16. to 16.3.**: Added to Part 2 for recording monitoring results:
    *   **16.1.**: Cowl monitoring area test points.
    *   **16.2.**: Test points for pedestrian/bicyclist head injury mitigation features (e.g., external airbags).
    *   **16.3.**: Manufacturer-requested test points for innovative solutions.

### 5. Test Procedures (Annex 5)
*   **Paragraphs 3.4.1. to 3.4.4.**: Amended details on marking the HIC1000 and HIC1700 zones on the bonnet top and windscreen test areas, including requirements for manufacturer drawings and surface area calculations.
*   **Paragraphs 4.1. to 4.3., 4.5., 5.1. to 5.3., 5.5.**: Amended test procedures for child and adult headform impactors, including minimum number of tests, test area boundaries, point selection, and impact tolerances. Clarifies that tests in the cowl monitoring area are at the discretion of the technical service and do not count towards the required nine tests per headform.
*   **New Paragraphs 4.8., 4.9., 5.8., 5.9.**: Introduce rules for test repetition in case of **atypical windscreen fracture** (at manufacturer's request or technical service's discretion) and provisions for **monitoring tests** on A-pillars/windscreen headers incorporating injury mitigation features or other innovative solutions. Results are recorded in the communication form but may not be required for approval recognition by some Parties.

### 6. Approval Mark (Annex 2)
*   Amended to show the example mark for the 03 series of amendments: `127R - 03185`.
