**Amendment Details**
This document is Amendment 2 to Revision 2 of Addendum 105 to Regulation No. 106. It is Supplement 12 to the original version of the Regulation.

**Amendment Content**
The amendment modifies **Annex 5, Table 7** of UN Regulation No. 106 by deleting specific rows for certain tyre size designations.

**Table 7 (1 of 4):**
Delete the complete row for tyre size designations:
*   AT21x7–10
*   AT22x9–8

**Table 7 (2 of 4):**
Delete the complete row for tyre size designations:
*   AT23x7–10
*   AT23x8–11
*   AT24x8–11
*   AT24x9–11
*   AT24x10–11
*   AT25x8–12
*   AT25x11–9
*   AT25x11–10
