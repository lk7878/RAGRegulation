**Corrigendum 2 to Regulation No. 108**

**Issued:** 24 June 2008
**Source:** E/ECE/324 Rev.2/Add.107/Corr.2, E/ECE/TRANS/505

This document is Corrigendum 2 to the original version of UNECE Regulation No. 108, concerning the approval for the production of retreaded pneumatic tyres for motor vehicles and their trailers. It was issued subject to Depositary Notification C.N.256.2008.TREATIES-1 dated 9 April 2008.

**Amendment Content:**

The corrigendum inserts a new paragraph **2.5.7.** into **Annex 7** of Regulation No. 108. The new text reads:

> "2.5.7. however, for tyres suitable for maximum speed of 300 km/h (speed symbol 'Y'), the duration of the test is 20 minutes at the initial test speed step and 10 minutes at the last speed step."
