# UN Regulation No. 69 (Revision 1)
**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of rear marking plates for slow-moving vehicles (by construction) and their trailers**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to rear marking plates for vehicles of category M, N, O and T and for mobile machinery, which, by construction, cannot move faster than 40 km/h.

## 2. Definitions
### 2.1 General
- **"SMV rear marking plate"**: a triangular plate with truncated corners with a characteristic pattern faced with retro-reflective and fluorescent material or devices (class 1); or with retro-reflective materials or devices only (class 2).
- **"Sample unit"**: a complete, finished SMV plate ready to be mounted on a vehicle and representative of current production.

### 2.2 Retro-reflection
- **"Retro-reflective material"**: a surface or device from which, when directionally irradiated, a relatively large portion of the incident radiation is retroreflected.
- **"Retro-reflective device"**: an assembly ready for use and comprising one or more retro-reflective optical units.

### 2.3 Geometric definitions
- Reference centre, Illumination axis, Observation axis, Observation angle (α), Observation half-plane, Reference axis, Entrance angle (β), First axis, First component of entrance angle (β₁), Second component of entrance angle (β₂), Second axis, Angle of rotation (ε).

### 2.4 Photometric terms
- **Coefficient of retro-reflection (R')**: expressed in candela per square metre per lux (cd . m⁻² . lx⁻¹).
- Angular diameter of the retro-reflective sample (η).
- Luminance factor.

### 2.5 Fluorescence
Definition of fluorescence and its effect on colour brightness.

### 2.6 Goniometer
Description of a goniometer for retro-reflection measurements.

### 2.7 Definition of "type"
SMV rear marking plates differ in type based on trade name/mark, characteristics of retro-reflective/fluorescent materials, or parts affecting retro-reflective properties.

## 3. Application for Approval
- Submitted by holder of trade name/mark or representative.
- Must include drawings, technical specifications of materials, and samples.
- Competent Authority verifies arrangements for conformity of production control.

## 4. Markings
- Must bear trade name/mark of applicant.
- If retro-reflective system is not for all angles of rotation, must have "TOP" marking.
- Markings must be on retro-reflective/fluorescent area or edge, visible when fitted, clearly legible and indelible.

## 5. Approval
- Granted if requirements met.
- Approval number assigned (first two digits indicate series of amendments). Symbol "RF" for class 1, "RR" for class 2.
- Notice communicated via form in Annex 2.
- Approved plates must bear international approval mark (circle with "E" and country number, plus approval number).
- Approval mark must be clearly legible and indelible (example in Annex 3).

## 6. General Specifications
- Must function satisfactorily in normal use, no detrimental defects.
- Components not easily dismantled.
- Means of attachment must allow stable/durable connection (e.g., screws, rivets).
- Outer surface easy to clean (not rough, no protuberances hindering cleaning).

## 7. Special Specification (Tests)
- Must satisfy conditions as to dimensions, shape, pattern, colorimetric, photometric, physical and mechanical requirements (Annexes 5-12).

## 8. Modifications and Extension of Approval
- Modifications must be notified to Administrative Department.
- Department may require further tests.
- Confirmation/refusal notified per paragraph 5.3.
- Extension assigned a series number and communicated.

## 9. Conformity of Production
- Procedures shall comply with Agreement Appendix 2.
- Manufactured to conform to approved type (meeting paragraphs 6 & 7).
- Minimum requirements in Annexes 13 & 14 must be complied with.
- Verification of control methods by approval authority at least once every two years.

## 10. Penalties for Non-conformity of Production
- Approval may be withdrawn if requirements not met or plate with approval mark does not conform.
- Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties via Annex 2 form.

## 11. Production Definitely Discontinued
- Holder must inform approval authority if manufacture ceases.
- Authority informs other Parties via Annex 2 form.

## 12. Transitional Provisions
- Relates to implementation of Supplement 2 to the 01 series of amendments.
- Specifies dates from which approvals must meet amended requirements, validity of existing approvals, and timelines for fitting plates meeting new requirements.

## 13. Names and Addresses
Parties shall communicate names/addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments to UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
1.  The CIE co-ordinate system
2.  Communication form (approval/refusal/extension/withdrawal/discontinuation)
3.  Arrangement of the approval mark
4.  Test procedure
5.  Specifications of shape and dimensions
6.  Colorimetric specifications
7.  Photometric specifications
8.  Resistance to external agents (weathering, corrosion, fuels, water, impact, cleaning)
9.  Resistance to heat
10. Rigidity of the plates
11. Stability in time of the optical properties
12. Example of rear marking plate (shape/dimensions)
13. Minimum requirements for conformity of production control procedures
14. Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector
15. Guidelines for installation on slow-moving vehicles (by construction) and their trailers (recommended for vehicles ≤ 30 km/h)
