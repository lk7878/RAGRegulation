# UN Regulation No. 41 - Revision 2 - Amendment 7 (Supplement 7 to 04 series)

**Noise emissions of motorcycles**

**Date of entry into force:** 15 October 2019

**Authentic and legally binding text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2019/3.

## Summary of Amendments

This document (Amendment 7) introduces modifications to UN Regulation No. 41 concerning the noise emissions of motorcycles. The key changes pertain to the Additional Sound Emission Provisions (ASEP), manufacturer declarations, conformity of production checks, and the associated test report forms.

## Detailed Amendments

### Paragraph 6.3. (Amended)
The paragraph is restructured into subparagraphs:
*   **6.3.1.** Prohibits manufacturers from intentionally altering or adjusting the vehicle, or introducing any device or procedure, **solely** for the purpose of passing noise emission tests, if such modifications are not operational during typical on-road use.
*   **6.3.2.** Requires the vehicle type to meet the requirements of **Annex 7**. For motorcycles with user-selectable software/programs affecting sound emission, **all modes** must comply with Annex 7, with testing based on the worst-case scenario.
*   **6.3.3.** Requires the manufacturer to provide a **statement** (according to Annex 8) in the type approval application, confirming compliance with paragraph 6.3.1.
*   **6.3.4.** Requires test results from paragraph 6.3.2. to be recorded in the test report and on the form in **Annex 1**.

### Paragraph 8.3. (Amended)
For **Conformity of Production (COP)**, the manufacturer must make a renewed declaration that the type still fulfills the requirements of paragraph 6.3.1. Measured sound levels according to Annex 7 must not exceed the limits given in Annex 7, paragraph 2.6., by more than **1.0 dB(A)**.

### Annex 1, Point 18 (Amended)
Point 18, "Additional sound emission provisions," is updated to include a structured table for recording ASEP test data. The table includes columns for:
*   Reference Point (i)
*   Reference Point (ii)
*   Additional operating condition 1
*   Additional operating condition 2

The data points to be recorded include:
*   Selected gear number
*   Vehicle speeds (at various measurement points: beginning of acceleration, vAA', vPP', vBB')
*   Pre-acceleration length
*   Engine speeds (nAA', nPP', nBB')
*   Wide open throttle test result (Lwot)
*   ASEP limit

Point 18.2. references the attached manufacturer's statement of compliance with paragraph 6.3.1.

### Annex 7, Footnote 1 to Paragraph 2.6. (Amended)
Clarifies the conditions for using test values (`L_wot(i)` and `n_wot(i)`) from Annex 3 tests during ASEP verification:
*   If Annex 3 and ASEP tests are performed on the **same vehicle in immediate sequence**, the Annex 3 values may be used, **if agreed by the Type Approval Authority**.
*   Otherwise, new measurements must be conducted as defined in Annex 3, paragraph 1., using the same gear (i) and pre-acceleration distance as during type approval.

### Annex 7, Paragraph 3.1. (Amended)
Specifies that the Type Approval Authority and technical service shall request tests to check compliance with the ASEP requirements (paragraph 2). To avoid undue workload, testing is restricted to:
1.  The two reference points defined in paragraph 3.2.
2.  **Two additional operating conditions** (other than the reference points) within the ASEP control range.

### Annex 8 (Amended - New Content)
Provides the template for the **"Statement of compliance with point 6.3.1. of the Additional Sound Emission Provisions (ASEP)"**.
The statement must be made by the manufacturer on company letterhead (max format A4) and include:
*   Manufacturer's name attesting compliance for the specified vehicle type.
*   A declaration made in good faith after appropriate evaluation.
*   Date.
*   Name and signature of the authorized representative.
