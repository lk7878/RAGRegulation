# Regulation No. 59
**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of replacement silencing systems**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation covers replacement silencing systems for vehicles of categories M and N.

## 2. Definitions
For the purpose of this Regulation:
- **"Silencing systems"** means a complete set of components necessary for limiting the noise produced by the engine of a motor vehicle and its exhaust.
- **"Silencing system component"** means one of the separate components which together form the silencing system (e.g. silencer proper, expansion chamber, resonator).
- **"Silencing system of different types"** means silencing systems which differ significantly in specified respects such as trade name, material characteristics, operating principles, assembly, or number of components.
- **"Replacement silencing system or components of said system"** means any part of the silencing system intended for use on a vehicle, other than a part of the type fitted to this vehicle when submitted for type approval pursuant to this Regulation.
- **"Design family of replacement silencing systems or replacement systems components"** means silencing systems or components thereof belonging to the same design family if all specified characteristics (e.g., gas flow through absorbing material, fibre type, binder, density, contact surface) are the same.
- **"Approval of a replacement silencing system or components of said system"** means the approval of the whole or part of a silencing system adaptable to one or several specified types of motor vehicles, as regards the limitation of their noise level.
- **"Vehicle type"** means a category of motor vehicles which do not differ significantly in specified respects such as body lines/materials, dimensions, engine type/characteristics, transmission, and exhaust/intake systems.

## 3. Application for approval
- Submitted by the manufacturer or accredited representative.
- Must be accompanied by documents in triplicate including descriptions of the vehicle type(s), the silencing system (with drawings and mounting instructions), and material specifications.
- The Technical Service may request samples of the system, the original system, and a representative vehicle or engine for testing.

## 4. Markings
- Each component (excluding tubes and mounting accessories) must bear the manufacturer's trade name/mark and commercial description.
- Markings must be clearly legible and indelible.

## 5. Approval
- Granted if the type meets the requirements of paragraph 6.
- An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments applicable at the time of approval.
- Notice of approval/refusal is communicated to applying Parties via a form per Annex 1.
- Approved components must bear an international approval mark (circle with "E", country code, regulation number "R", and approval number). Examples in Annex 2.

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. General specifications
- Systems must be designed for durability under normal conditions of use and resistant to corrosion.
- Systems must not be easily tamperable (e.g., removal of baffles). Manually adjustable multi-mode systems must meet requirements in all modes, with the highest noise level reported.

### 6.2. Specifications regarding noise levels
- **Conditions of measurement:** Tests must use "normal" tyres (as per UN R117), not "special use" or "snow" tyres.
- **Noise reduction performance:** Verified per methods in UN R51. For both moving and stationary vehicle tests, the noise level with the replacement system must not exceed the original system's type approval or measured value by more than a specified dB(A) limit (1 dB(A) for moving, 2 dB(A) for stationary). Specific conditions for back-to-back comparison are provided.

### 6.3. Measurement of the vehicle performances
- The replacement system must ensure vehicle performance comparable to the original.
- Verified by measuring back pressure on an engine dynamometer or a vehicle (road/roller). The back pressure with the replacement system must not exceed that of the original system by more than 25% under specified conditions (engine at speed corresponding to rated max power). Measuring points are defined in Annex 4.

### 6.4. Additional specifications regarding replacement silencing systems or components containing acoustically absorbing fibrous materials
- Fibrous materials may be used only if exhaust gas is not in contact with them, or if they belong to a proven durable design family. Otherwise, the system/component must undergo conditioning.
- **Conditioning methods:**
    1. **Continuous road operation for 10,000 km** (mix of urban and high-speed driving).
    2. **Conditioning on a test bench** (engine/vehicle on dynamometer over six six-hour periods with specific load/speed sequences).
    3. **Conditioning by pulsation** (using apparatus to interrupt exhaust flow for 2500 cycles under specified pressure and engine conditions).
- After conditioning, noise level is checked per paragraph 6.2.

## 7. Extension of approval
The manufacturer may request an extension of approval to other vehicle types. Procedure as per paragraph 3. Notice communicated per paragraph 5.3.

## 8. Modification of the type of silencing system
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further tests. Confirmation/refusal communicated per paragraph 5.3.

## 9. Conformity of production
Procedures must comply with the 1958 Agreement Appendix 2.
- Approved systems must conform to the approved type and satisfy requirements of paragraph 6 (with an additional 1 dB(A) margin for COP checks).
- The approval holder must ensure quality control, have necessary test equipment, record data, analyze results, and carry out at least the tests prescribed in Annex 5.
- The Type Approval Authority verifies control methods and conducts inspections (normally biennially). Sampling and testing procedures are detailed.

## 10. Penalties for non-conformity of production
Approval may be withdrawn if COP requirements are not met or tests are failed. Withdrawal must be notified to other applying Parties.

## 11. Production definitively discontinued
If manufacture ceases, the holder must inform the Type Approval Authority, which notifies other applying Parties.

## 12. Names and addresses of Technical Services responsible for conducting approval tests, and of Type Approval Authorities
Applying Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1:** Communication form for approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal.
- **Annex 2:** Examples of arrangement of the approval mark.
- **Annex 3:** Diagram and description of test apparatus for pulsation conditioning.
- **Annex 4:** Diagrams of measuring points for back pressure tests.
- **Annex 5:** Procedures for checks on conformity of production (sampling, testing, evaluation of results).
