# UNECE Regulation No. 83 - Revision 5 - Amendment 1

**Supplement 1 to the 07 series of amendments – Date of entry into force: 29 January 2016**

*This document is meant purely as documentation tool. The authentic and legal binding text is: ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2015/57 (as amended by paragraphs 63 and 64 of the report ECE/TRANS/WP.29/1116).*

## Summary of Key Amendments

This amendment introduces modifications to UNECE Regulation No. 83 concerning vehicle emissions and On-Board Diagnostics (OBD). Key changes include updates to In-Use Performance Ratio (IUPR) sampling requirements, revised type-approval transitional dates, clarifications on OBD monitoring and reporting, and alignment with updated ISO/SAE diagnostic communication standards.

## Detailed Amendments

### Main Text Amendments

*   **Paragraph 2.26.:** Deleted.
*   **Paragraphs 2.27. to 2.35. (former):** Renumbered as paragraphs 2.26. to 2.34.
*   **Footnote 3:** Text deleted.
*   **Paragraph 9.3.5.2. (IUPR Sampling):** Amended to specify:
    *   The number of sample lots is based on the number of vehicles in an OBD family approved with IUPR.
    *   For the first sampling period, all vehicle types in the family approved with IUPR are subject to sampling.
    *   For subsequent periods, only new or extended-approval vehicle types are subject.
    *   Minimum sample lot sizes: 6 vehicles for families with <5,000 registrations subject to sampling; 15 vehicles for others.
    *   Sample lots must represent sales patterns, including high-volume types (≥20% of family total).
    *   Small series productions (<1000 vehicles per OBD family) are exempt from minimum IUPR requirements and demonstration obligations.
*   **Paragraph 12.1.2.:** Deleted.
*   **Paragraph 12.2. (Type approvals):** Amended with new transitional provisions:
    *   **12.2.1.:** From the entry into force date (29 Jan 2016) for M1/N1-I vehicles and 1 Sep 2015 for N1-II/III & N2 vehicles, new type approvals must comply with Type I test limits and *Preliminary* OBD thresholds.
    *   **12.2.2.:** From 1 Sep 2015 (M1/N1-I) and 1 Sep 2016 (N1-II/III & N2), Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept approvals not granted under the 07 series amendments or not complying with Type I limits and *Preliminary* OBD thresholds.
    *   **12.2.3.:** From 1 Sep 2017 (M1/N1-I) and 1 Sep 2018 (N1-II/III & N2), new type approvals must comply with Type I test limits and *Final* OBD thresholds.
    *   **12.2.4.:** From 1 Sep 2018 (M1/N1-I) and 1 Sep 2019 (N1-II/III & N2), Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept approvals not granted under the 07 series amendments or not complying with Type I limits and *Final* OBD thresholds.

### Annex Amendments

**Annex 1:**
*   **Paragraph 3.2.12.2.7.6.3.:** Amended to require comprehensive documentation of OBD sensed components, fault detection strategies, MI activation, OBD output codes, and detailed explanations for data in diagnostic services $05 and $06.

**Annex 2, Appendix 1:**
*   **Paragraph 3.:** Amended with similar comprehensive documentation requirements as in Annex 1.

**Annex 11 (OBD):**
*   **Paragraph 2.:** Amended introductory text.
*   **Paragraph 2.10.:** Amended definition of a "driving cycle".
*   **New Paragraph 3.2.3.:** Allows malfunction identification outside a driving cycle (e.g., after engine shutdown).
*   **Paragraphs 3.3.4.9. and 3.3.4.10.:** Deleted.
*   **New Paragraphs 3.3.5.1. and 3.3.5.2.:** Specify monitoring requirements for total failure/removal of specific after-treatment devices (particulate trap, NOx system, DOC) on CI engines and for failures causing exceedance of OBD thresholds.
*   **Paragraph 3.8.1.:** Amended to allow erasure of fault codes/distance/freeze-frame if the fault is not re-registered in at least 40 engine warm-up cycles or 40 qualifying driving cycles.
*   **Paragraph 3.9.3.1.:** Amended regarding transmission of diagnostic signals using source addresses as per the referenced standard.
*   **New Paragraph 3.10.:** Adds provisions for vehicles with engine shut-off strategies, allowing autonomous restarts after a stall to be considered a new or continued driving cycle.

**Annex 11, Appendix 1:**
*   **Paragraph 1.:** Amended introduction to the OBD test procedure.
*   **New Paragraph 6.1.1.:** Type I test not required for demonstrating electrical failures (short/open circuit). Alternative demonstration methods allowed.
*   **New Paragraph 6.2.3.:** Use of additional/alternative preconditioning methods must be documented.
*   **Paragraph 6.3.1.5.:** Amended to specify electrical disconnection of the evaporative purge control device (if equipped and active on the selected fuel).
*   **Paragraphs 6.4.1.1. and 6.4.2.1.:** Amended test procedures for OBD demonstration, including provisions for bi-fuel vehicles and limits on simulated failures.
*   **Paragraphs 6.5.3. to 6.5.3.6.:** Amended to update referenced ISO/SAE diagnostic communication standards and specifications (e.g., ISO 15765-4:2011, ISO 15031 series, SAE J1979, etc.). Standards ISO 27145 and ISO 14229:2013 may be used as an option from 1 Jan 2019.
*   **New Paragraph 6.5.3.7.:** Requires manufacturers to make accessible (potentially on payment) necessary technical information for repair/maintenance to independent operators, unless protected by IP/secret know-how, which must not be improperly withheld.
*   **Paragraphs 7.6.1. to 7.6.2.1.:** Amended and expanded requirements for OBD system reporting of In-Use Performance Ratio (IUPR) data (numerators/denominators) for specific monitors (catalyst, O2 sensor, evaporative, EGR, VVT, secondary air, particulate filter, NOx system, boost pressure). Specifies reporting rules for components with multiple monitors and exempts continuously monitored short/open circuit failures from reporting. Defines "continuously" as monitoring always enabled with sampling ≥2Hz and conclusion within 15 seconds.
