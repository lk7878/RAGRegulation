**Amendment Details**
This document constitutes **Amendment 8** to **UN Regulation No. 74, Revision 2** (Addendum 73). It is also identified as **Supplement 13 to the 01 series of amendments**.
*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 30 September 2021
*   **Document Date:** 8 December 2021

**Content of Amendment**
The amendment modifies specific paragraphs within UN Regulation No. 74:

1.  **Paragraph 6.1.1.** is amended. The text specifies requirements for the number of lighting devices, referencing classes (e.g., A, B, BS, CS, DS, ES) from **UN Regulation No. 149**.

2.  **Paragraph 6.2.1.** and its associated footnote are amended. The text specifies requirements for the number of lighting devices, referencing classes (e.g., A, B, AS, BS, CS, DS, ES) from **UN Regulation No. 149**.
    *   The added footnote clarifies the use of specific headlamp types (Class A of UN R113 with LED modules, or Class AS of UN R149 with LED modules) only on vehicles with a maximum design speed not exceeding 25 km/h.

**Note on Agreement Title**
The parent agreement has undergone title changes:
*   Original (1958): Agreement concerning the Adoption of Uniform Conditions of Approval and Reciprocal Recognition of Approval for Motor Vehicle Equipment and Parts.
*   Revision 2 (1995): Agreement concerning the Adoption of Uniform Technical Prescriptions for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts which can be Fitted and/or be Used on Wheeled Vehicles and the Conditions for Reciprocal Recognition of Approvals Granted on the Basis of these Prescriptions.
