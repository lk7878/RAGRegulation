**Regulation No. 28 - Amendment 3**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 28 December 2000

This document constitutes **Amendment 3** to Regulation No. 28, which contains uniform provisions concerning the approval of audible warning devices (AWDs) and of motor vehicles with regard to their audible signals.

**Key Amendments:**

1.  **Paragraph 5.5.1., footnote 1/:** Amends the list of country codes assigned to Contracting Parties of the 1958 Agreement. Updates include codes for the Czech Republic, the Russian Federation, Croatia, Slovenia, Slovakia, Belarus, Estonia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Latvia, Bulgaria, Turkey, The former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia, the European Community (code 42), Japan, Australia, Ukraine, and South Africa. Specifies the procedure for assigning subsequent numbers to new contracting parties.

2.  **Paragraph 6.2.3.1.:** Amends the test voltage specification for AWDs supplied with direct current. The voltage measured at the terminal of the electric power source shall be `13/12` of the rated voltage.

3.  **Paragraph 6.2.5.:** Amends the specification for the resistance of connecting leads for AWDs supplied with direct current. The resistance (in ohms), including terminals and contacts, shall be as close as possible to `(0,10/12) x rated voltage` (in volts).

4.  **Paragraph 8.:** Replaces the entire paragraph with new **Conformity of Production** requirements for AWDs:
    *   Procedures must comply with Appendix 2 of the Revised 1958 Agreement (E/ECE/324-E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2).
    *   Approved AWDs must be manufactured to conform to the approved type (para. 6).
    *   The type approval authority may verify conformity control methods at production facilities at any time, with a normal frequency of once every two years.

5.  **Paragraph 16.:** Replaces the entire paragraph with new **Conformity of Production** requirements for vehicles:
    *   Procedures must comply with Appendix 2 of the Revised 1958 Agreement (E/ECE/324-E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2).
    *   Approved vehicles must be manufactured to conform to the approved type (para. 14).
    *   The type approval authority may verify conformity control methods at production facilities at any time, with a normal frequency of once every two years.

6.  **Annex 1, item 7:** Corrects the unit of measurement for rated operating pressure(s) to `kg/cm²`.
