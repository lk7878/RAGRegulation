**Amendment 5 to UN Regulation No. 128**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 8 October 2016

This amendment modifies Annex 1 of UN Regulation No. 128, which concerns the approval of light emitting diode (LED) light sources for use in approved lamp units on power-driven vehicles and their trailers.

**Key Changes:**

1.  **Updated Lists:**
    *   The list of categories of LED light sources and their corresponding sheet numbers is amended.
    *   The list of sheets for LED light sources and their sequence in the annex is amended.

2.  **Replacement and Insertion of Sheets:**
    *   Sheets LR3/1 to 5 are replaced by new sheets **L3/1 to 6**.
    *   Sheet LR4/1 is replaced by a new sheet **LR4/1**.
    *   Sheet LR4/4 is replaced by a new sheet **LR4/4**.
    *   New sheets **L5/1 to 6** are inserted after sheet LR4/5.

**Detailed Technical Revisions:**

The amendment provides detailed specifications for the following LED light source categories across the new and revised sheets:

*   **Categories LR3A, LR3B, LW3A, LW3B, LY3A, LY3B (Sheets L3/1 to L3/6):**
    *   **Sheet L3/1 & L3/2:** Defines main drawings, essential dimensional/electrical/photometric characteristics (including dimensions `a` to `h`, cap types, rated values, objective luminous flux values at 13.5V and 9V DC), and notes.
    *   **Sheet L3/3 & L3/4:** Specifies screen projection requirements using a box system to define the light emitting area (`f` dimension: 3.0 mm for LR3, 4.5 mm for LW3/LY3) and the proportion of total luminous flux emitted from areas A, B, and C.
    *   **Sheet L3/5 & L3/6:** Defines the normalized luminous intensity distribution test setup and provides tolerance bands for test point values (Tables 4a for LR3A/B and 4b for LW3A/B, LY3A/B).

*   **Categories LR4A and LR4B (Sheets LR4/1, LR4/4):**
    *   **Sheet LR4/1:** Provides the main drawing.
    *   **Sheet LR4/4:** Describes the setup for measuring the normalized luminous intensity distribution.

*   **Categories LR5A, LR5B, LW5A, LW5B, LY5A, LY5B (Sheets L5/1 to L5/6):**
    *   **Sheet L5/1 & L5/2:** Defines main drawings, essential dimensional/electrical/photometric characteristics (including dimensions `a` to `h`, cap types, rated values, objective luminous flux values at 13.5V and 9V DC), and notes.
    *   **Sheet L5/3 & L5/4:** Specifies screen projection requirements using a box system (`f` dimension: 4.5 mm) and the proportion of total luminous flux emitted from areas A, B, and C.
    *   **Sheet L5/5 & L5/6:** Defines the normalized luminous intensity distribution test setup and provides the tolerance band for test point values (Table 4, common for all LR5/LW5/LY5 categories).

**Note:** This document is a documentation tool. The authentic and legally binding text is: ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2016/25.
