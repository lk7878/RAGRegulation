**Amendment 3 to UN Regulation No. 140**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 11 January 2020

**Amendment Details:**

Paragraph 5.1. is amended to read:
> "5.1. To comply with this Regulation, vehicles shall be equipped with an ESC system that meets the functional requirements specified in paragraph 6. and the performance requirements in paragraph 7. under the test procedures specified in paragraph 9. and under the test conditions specified in paragraph 8. of this Regulation."
