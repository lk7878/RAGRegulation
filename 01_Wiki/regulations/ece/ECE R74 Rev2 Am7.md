**UN Regulation No. 74 - Revision 2 - Amendment 7 (02 series of amendments)**

**Date of entry into force:** 29 May 2020

**Authentic text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2019/79.

**Amendments:**

1.  **Paragraph 3.2.5.** amended to read: "A statement of the method used for the definition of the apparent surface (see paragraph 2.6.);"
2.  **New paragraph 3.2.6.** inserted: "At the discretion of the manufacturer, a statement indicating whether lamps approved for and equipped with LED substitute light sources are allowed to be installed on the vehicle or not and, if this is allowed, which lamps."
3.  **Paragraph 4.2.** amended regarding approval number assignment, specifying that the first two digits indicate the series of amendments incorporating the most recent major technical amendments at the time of approval issuance.
4.  **New paragraph 5.14.8.** inserted: "Direction indicator lamps4 (paragraph 6.8.)"
    *   **New footnote 4:** "Direction indicator lamps are mandatory on vehicles with a maximum design speed exceeding 25 km/h."
5.  **Paragraph 5.15.4.** amended to read: "Direction-indicator lamps5 (paragraph 6.8.)"
    *   **New footnote 5:** "Direction indicator lamps are optional on vehicles with a maximum design speed not exceeding 25 km/h (output of auxiliary propulsion is cut off at a vehicle speed ≤ 25 km/h)."
    *   Subsequent footnotes renumbered accordingly.
6.  **New paragraph 5.20.** inserted regarding the use of lamps with LED substitute light sources:
    *   Allowed only if the statement in paragraph 3.2.6. is present and positive.
    *   Verification at type approval and conformity of production: check for the presence of the marking on lamps related to the use of LED substitute light source(s).
7.  **New paragraphs 12.4. to 12.9.** inserted, detailing transitional provisions for the 02 series of amendments:
    *   **12.4:** From the entry into force date, Contracting Parties shall not refuse to grant/accept approvals under the 02 series.
    *   **12.5:** From 1 September 2023, Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept approvals to preceding series if first issued after this date.
    *   **12.6:** Contracting Parties starting to apply the Regulation after the entry into force of the latest series are not obliged to accept approvals under preceding series.
    *   **12.7:** Notwithstanding 12.5., Contracting Parties applying the 02 series shall continue to accept approvals to the preceding series (if first issued after 1 September 2023) for vehicles not affected by the 02 series changes.
    *   **12.8:** Until 1 September 2028, Contracting Parties applying the 02 series shall continue to accept approvals granted under the preceding series if first issued before 1 September 2023.
    *   **12.9:** Contracting Parties shall not refuse to grant approvals according to any preceding series.
8.  **Annex 1:** New item 5.12. and footnote 5 inserted in the communication form: "Lamps approved for and equipped with LED substitute light source(s) are allowed to be installed on this vehicle type: yes/no2, 5" (Footnote 5: "If yes, list the applicable lamps.")
9.  **Annex 2:** Amended to show examples of approval marks (Model A and Model B) for the 02 series of amendments.
