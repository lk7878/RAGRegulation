**Amendment Details**
*   This is **Amendment 1** to UN Regulation No. 135.
*   It is **Supplement 1** to the original version of the regulation.
*   **Date of entry into force:** 29 January 2016.
*   The authentic legal text is document **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2015/54**.

**Scope of Application (Amended Paragraph 1.1.)**
This Regulation applies to:
1.  **Category M1** vehicles with a gross vehicle mass of up to 3,500 kg.
2.  **Category N1** vehicles, **provided that** either of the following geometric criteria is met:
    *   The acute angle (α) between a horizontal plane through the front axle center and an angular transverse plane through the front axle center and the driver's R-point is **less than 22.0 degrees**.
    *   The ratio of the distance from the driver's R-point to the rear axle center (L101-L114) over the distance from the front axle center to the driver's R-point (L114) is **less than 1.30**.

**Reference**
*   The definition of vehicle categories refers to the **Consolidated Resolution on the Construction of Vehicles (R.E.3.)**, specifically document ECE/TRANS/WP.29/78/Rev.3, paragraph 2.
