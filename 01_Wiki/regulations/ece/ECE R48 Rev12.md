**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to the installation of lighting and light-signalling devices**

**1. Scope**
This Regulation applies to vehicles of categories M, N, and to their trailers (category O) with regard to the installation of lighting and light-signalling devices.

**2. Definitions**
For the purpose of this Regulation, key definitions include:
*   **Approval of a vehicle**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to the number and mode of installation of the lighting and light-signalling devices.
*   **Vehicle type with regard to the installation of lighting and light-signalling devices**: Vehicles which do not differ in essential respects such as dimensions, external shape, number and positioning of devices, headlamp-levelling system, and suspension system.
*   **Lamp**: A device designed to illuminate the road or to emit a light signal to other road users. Includes rear registration plate lamps and retro-reflectors.
*   **Lighting function**: Light emitted to illuminate the road and objects in the direction of vehicle movement.
*   **Light-signalling function**: Light emitted or reflected to give other road users visual information on the presence, identification and/or change of movement of the vehicle.
*   **Light source**: One or more elements for visible radiation (e.g., filament, gas-discharge, LED).
*   **Independent, Grouped, Combined, Reciprocally incorporated lamps**: Definitions based on separation of apparent surfaces, light sources, and lamp bodies.
*   **Driving-beam (main-beam) headlamp**: Lamp used to illuminate the road over a long distance ahead.
*   **Passing-beam (dipped-beam) headlamp**: Lamp used to illuminate the road ahead without causing undue dazzle to oncoming drivers.
*   **Direction-indicator lamp**: Lamp used to indicate an intended change of direction.
*   **Stop lamp**: Lamp used to indicate intentional retardation.
*   **Front/Rear position lamp**: Lamp used to indicate the presence and width of the vehicle from front/rear.
*   **Retro-reflector**: Device used to indicate presence by reflection of external light.
*   **Conspicuity marking**: Device to increase conspicuity by reflection of external light.
*   **Hazard warning signal**: Simultaneous operation of all direction-indicator lamps.
*   **Front/Rear fog lamp**: Lamp to improve illumination in fog or increase visibility in dense fog.
*   **Reversing lamp**: Lamp to illuminate the road to the rear and warn of reversing.
*   **Parking lamp**: Lamp to draw attention to a stationary vehicle in a built-up area.
*   **End-outline marker lamp**: Lamp fitted near the extreme outer edge and top to indicate overall width.
*   **Side marker lamp**: Lamp to indicate presence when viewed from the side.
*   **Daytime running lamp**: Forward-facing lamp to make the vehicle more visible during daytime.
*   **Cornering lamp**: Lamp to provide supplementary illumination near the forward corner towards which the vehicle is turning.
*   **Adaptive front lighting system (AFS)**: Lighting device providing beams with differing characteristics for automatic adaptation to varying conditions.
*   **Exterior courtesy lamp**: Lamp to assist entry/exit or loading operations.
*   **Manoeuvring lamp**: Lamp to provide supplementary illumination to the side during slow manoeuvres.
*   **Light emitting surface**: The surface as declared by the manufacturer.
*   **Illuminating surface**: The orthogonal projection as defined for lighting and light-signalling devices.
*   **Apparent surface**: The orthogonal projection for a defined direction of observation.
*   **Axis of reference (reference axis)**: The characteristic axis determined by the lamp manufacturer for photometric reference and installation.
*   **Centre of reference**: Intersection of the axis of reference with the exterior light-emitting surface.
*   **Angles of geometric visibility**: Angles determining the minimum solid angle field in which the lamp's apparent surface is visible.
*   **Extreme outer edge**: Plane parallel to the median longitudinal plane touching the lateral outer edge, disregarding certain projections.
*   **Overall width/length**: Distances between defined vertical planes.
*   **Single and multiple lamps**: Definitions for single lamps, two lamps, or an even number based on apparent surface configuration.
*   **Operating tell-tale / Closed-circuit tell-tale**: Signals indicating device operation status.
*   **Optional lamp**: Installation is at the manufacturer's discretion.
*   **Normal condition of use / Park condition of a vehicle**: Defined states for motor vehicles and trailers.
*   **Bend lighting**: Lighting function to provide enhanced illumination in bends.
*   **Pair / Matched pair**: Set of lamps of the same function on left and right sides.
*   **Emergency stop signal**: Signal indicating application of a high retardation force.
*   **Colour of light emitted**: Defined chromaticity coordinates for White, Selective-yellow, Amber, and Red.
*   **Night-time/Day-time Colour of light retro-reflected/reflected**: Defined chromaticity coordinates for various colours from retro-reflective devices.
*   **Rear-end collision alert signal (RECAS)**: Automatic signal from leading to following vehicle warning of imminent collision.
*   **Gonio(photo)meter system**: System used for photometric measurements.
*   **H plane**: Horizontal plane containing the centre of reference of the lamp.

**3. Application for approval**
The application, submitted by the manufacturer, must include documents describing the vehicle type, list of devices, layout drawings, and specific information if an AFS is fitted. A representative vehicle must be submitted for testing.

**4. Approval**
If requirements are met, approval is granted with an approval number (starting digits indicate amendment series). An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country number, plus regulation and approval number) must be affixed to conforming vehicles.

**5. General specifications**
*   Devices must be fitted to retain prescribed characteristics under normal conditions and vibrations.
*   Correct adjustment of illuminating lamps must be possible.
*   Light-signalling device reference axes must be parallel to the bearing plane, with tolerances.
*   Lamps constituting a pair must be fitted symmetrically and have substantially identical characteristics.
*   Grouped, combined, or reciprocally incorporated lamps are allowed provided all requirements are fulfilled. Stop and direction-indicator lamps cannot be reciprocally incorporated.
*   Height, width, and length measurements are defined based on apparent surfaces.
*   Photometric characteristics generally should not vary intentionally during activation, except for specific cases (e.g., flashing lamps, adaptive functions).
*   No confusing red light forward or white light rearward.
*   Electrical connections ensure simultaneous operation of certain lamps (e.g., position lamps) and prevent main/dipped-beam headlamps and front fog lamps from being on unless those lamps are also on. Dipped/main-beam headlamps or front fog lamps may substitute front position lamps under specific conditions.
*   Concealable lamps (main-beam, dipped-beam, front fog) are allowed with specific requirements for failure, control, and movement.
*   Prescribed colours for light emitted by each type of lamp.
*   Number of lamps as per individual specifications.
*   Lamps on movable components are allowed if requirements for position, visibility, and photometrics are met in all fixed positions.
*   Lamps must be operational; non-functional lamps are deemed not present.
*   Lamps with replaceable light sources (per Regulation No. 37) must allow replacement without expert assistance.
*   Temporary fail-safe replacement for rear position lamp function is allowed.
*   An AFS is considered equivalent to a pair of dipped-beam (and main-beam) headlamps.
*   Variable luminous intensity control is allowed for certain rear lamps under specified conditions.
*   For M and N category vehicles, electric power supply conditions for key devices must be demonstrated to comply with voltage limits.
*   General provisions for geometric visibility, including allowance for reduced vertical angle for lamps mounted low.
