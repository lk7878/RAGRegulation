**Regulation No. 134**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of motor vehicles and their components with regard to the safety-related performance of hydrogen-fuelled vehicles (HFCV)**

**Date of entry into force as an annex to the 1958 Agreement: 15 June 2015**

**Contents**

1.  Scope
2.  Definitions
3.  Application for approval
4.  Approval
5.  Part I – Specifications of the compressed hydrogen storage system
6.  Part II – Specifications of specific components for the compressed hydrogen storage system
7.  Part III – Specifications of a vehicle fuel system incorporating the compressed hydrogen storage system
8.  Modification of type and extension of approval
9.  Conformity of production
10. Penalties for non-conformity of production
11. Production definitively discontinued
12. Names and addresses of Technical Services responsible for conducting approval tests and of the Type Approval Authorities

**Annexes**
1.  Information documents and communication forms
2.  Arrangements of the approval marks
3.  Test procedures for the compressed hydrogen storage system
4.  Test procedures for specific components for the compressed hydrogen storage system
5.  Test procedures for vehicle fuel system incorporating the compressed hydrogen storage system

**1. Scope**
This Regulation applies to:
1.1. Part I - Compressed hydrogen storage systems for hydrogen-fuelled vehicles on their safety-related performance.
1.2. Part II - Specific components for compressed hydrogen storage systems for hydrogen-fuelled vehicles on their safety-related performance.
1.3. Part III - Hydrogen-fuelled vehicles of category M and N incorporating compressed hydrogen storage system on its safety-related performance.

*Note: This Regulation does not cover the electrical safety of electric power train, the material compatibility and hydrogen embrittlement of the vehicle fuel system, and the post crash fuel system integrity in the event of full width frontal impact and rear impact.*

**2. Definitions**
Key definitions include:
*   **Compressed hydrogen storage system (CHSS):** A system designed to store hydrogen fuel, composed of a pressurized container, pressure relief devices (PRDs) and shut off device(s).
*   **Hydrogen-fuelled vehicle:** Any motor vehicle that uses compressed gaseous hydrogen as a fuel to propel the vehicle.
*   **Nominal working pressure (NWP):** The gauge pressure that characterizes typical operation of a system. For compressed hydrogen gas containers, NWP is the settled pressure of compressed gas in fully fuelled container or storage system at a uniform temperature of 15 °C.
*   **Maximum fuelling pressure (MFP):** The maximum pressure applied to compressed system during fuelling (125% of NWP).
*   **Thermally-activated pressure relief device (TPRD):** A non-reclosing PRD activated by temperature to open and release hydrogen gas.
*   **Service life (of a compressed hydrogen container):** The time frame during which service (usage) is authorized (not more than 15 years).

**3. Application for approval**
*   **Part I:** Application for approval of a type of compressed hydrogen storage system, submitted by its manufacturer.
*   **Part II:** Application for approval of a type of specific component (TPRD, check valve, automatic shut-off valve), submitted by its manufacturer.
*   **Part III:** Application for approval of a vehicle type, submitted by the vehicle manufacturer.
*   Requires submission of representative samples and information documents (models in Annex 1).

**4. Approval**
*   Granting of type approval if requirements of respective Parts (I, II, III) are met.
*   Assignment of an approval number.
*   Affixing of an international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, Regulation number "134R", and approval number).

**5. Part I – Specifications of the compressed hydrogen storage system**
Specifies requirements for the CHSS, which includes the high-pressure container and primary closure devices (TPRD, check valve, automatic shut-off valve).
*   All new systems shall have an NWP of 70 MPa or less and a service life of 15 years or less.
*   **Performance Requirements:**
    *   5.1 Verification tests for baseline metrics (initial burst pressure, initial pressure cycle life).
    *   5.2 Verification test for performance durability (sequential hydraulic tests including proof pressure, drop, surface damage, chemical exposure & cycling, high temperature static pressure, extreme temperature cycling, residual proof pressure, and residual burst strength tests).
    *   5.3 Verification test for expected on-road performance (sequential pneumatic/hydraulic tests including proof pressure, ambient/extreme temperature gas cycling, static leak/permeation, residual proof pressure, and residual burst strength tests).
    *   5.4 Verification test for service terminating performance in fire (system must vent via TPRD without rupture).
    *   5.5 Requirements for primary closure devices (must be type-approved per Part II).
*   **Labelling:** Permanent label on each container with manufacturer info, serial number, date of manufacture, MFP, NWP, fuel type, date of removal from service, and number of cycles used in testing.

**6. Part II – Specifications of specific components for the compressed hydrogen storage system**
*   **6.1 TPRD requirements:** Must pass a series of performance tests detailed in Annex 4 (pressure cycling, accelerated life, temperature cycling, salt corrosion, vehicle environment, stress corrosion cracking, drop/vibration, leak, bench top activation, flow rate).
*   **6.2 Check valve and automatic shut-off valve requirements:** Must pass a series of performance tests detailed in Annex 4 (hydrostatic strength, leak, extreme temperature pressure cycling, salt corrosion, vehicle environment, atmospheric exposure, electrical tests, vibration, stress corrosion cracking, pre-cooled hydrogen exposure).
*   **6.3 Marking:** Each component must be marked with MFP and fuel type.

**7. Part III – Specifications of a vehicle fuel system incorporating the compressed hydrogen storage system**
Specifies requirements for the complete vehicle fuel system, which includes the CHSS (type-approved per Part I), piping, joints, and other hydrogen-containing components.
*   **7.1 In-use fuel system requirements:**
    *   Fuelling receptacle requirements (prevent reverse flow, labelling, mounting, location).
    *   Over-pressure protection for the low-pressure system.
    *   Hydrogen discharge systems (TPRD venting direction restrictions, vehicle exhaust hydrogen concentration limits).
    *   Protection against flammable conditions under single failure scenarios (leakage limits, warning signals, automatic shut-off).
    *   Fuel system leakage test.
    *   Tell-tale warning signal to the driver for hydrogen detection system malfunction or high concentration.
*   **7.2 Post-crash fuel system integrity:** Requirements after frontal (UN R12 or R94) and lateral (UN R95) impact tests, or alternative acceleration tests for vehicles where crash tests are not applicable.
    *   Fuel leakage limit (≤118 Nl/min average).
    *   Hydrogen concentration limit in enclosed spaces (≤4.0% by volume).
    *   Container must remain attached.
    *   Additional installation requirements for systems not subject to frontal/lateral impact tests (specified setback distances from vehicle edges).

**8. Modification of type and extension of approval**
Procedures for notifying the Type Approval Authority of modifications, which may be classified as a "revision" or an "extension" requiring further inspections/tests.

**9. Conformity of production**
Procedures to ensure production conforms to the approved type, including regular inspections and specific batch testing requirements for containers (proof pressure test, rupture test, ambient temperature pressure cycling test with defined sampling frequencies and relaxation provisions).

**10. Penalties for non-conformity of production**
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met.

**11. Production definitively discontinued**
Manufacturer must inform the approval authority if production of an approved type ceases.

**12. Names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities**
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN secretariat.

**Annexes (Summarized)**
*   **Annex 1:** Models for information documents and communication forms for approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal.
*   **Annex 2:** Arrangements of the approval marks.
*   **Annex 3:** Detailed test procedures for the compressed hydrogen storage system (baseline, durability, on-road performance, fire tests).
*   **Annex 4:** Detailed test procedures for specific components (TPRD, check valve, automatic shut-off valve).
*   **Annex 5:** Test procedures for the vehicle fuel system (post-crash integrity, hydrogen concentration measurement, leakage detection, etc.).
