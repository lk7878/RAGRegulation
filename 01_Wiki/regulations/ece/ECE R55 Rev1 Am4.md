# Regulation No. 55 - Amendment 4

**Supplement 4 to the 01 series of amendments**
**Date of entry into force: 8 October 2015**

This document contains amendments to **UN Regulation No. 55** (Uniform provisions concerning the approval of mechanical coupling components of combinations of vehicles), Revision 1.

## Amendments to the Regulation Text

### Paragraphs 2.6.3. to 2.6.3.2.
Amended to read:
> "**2.6.3. Class C Clevis type drawbar coupling**
> Drawbar couplings with a 50 mm diameter pin, with a jaw as well as an automatic closing and locking pin on the towing vehicle for connecting to the trailer by means of a drawbar eye — see Annex 5, paragraph 3.:
>
> **2.6.3.1. Class C50-1 to C50-7** Standard 50 mm pin diameter clevis type drawbar couplings.
>
> **2.6.3.2. Class C50-X** Non-standard 50 mm pin diameter clevis type drawbar couplings."

### Paragraph 12.1.
Amended to read:
> "**12.1.** Devices for remote indication and remote control are permitted only on automatic drawbar couplings and automatic fifth wheel couplings."

## Amendments to Annex 5

### Insertion of New Paragraph 1.4.
A new paragraph **1.4. Movable coupling devices (couplings that can be moved without separation)** is inserted:
> "A movable coupling device shall be designed for positive mechanical engagements in service position. In case of manual movement the actuating force shall not supersede 20 daN. The movement shall be limited by mechanical end stops."

### Renumbering
*   Paragraphs 1.4. to 1.6.2. (former) are renumbered as paragraphs **1.5. to 1.7.2**.
*   Paragraph 1.6.3. (former) is renumbered as paragraph **1.7.3**. Its Table 3 headline is amended to read **"Minimum characteristic values for standard flange type ball couplings"**. The word "maximum" is deleted twice in the legend of Table 3.
*   Paragraph 1.7. (former) is renumbered as paragraph **1.8**.

### Additions and Amendments to Section 3 (Drawbar Couplings)
*   A new paragraph **3.6. Opening devices** is added.
*   Paragraph 3.6. (former) is renumbered as paragraph **3.6.1**.
*   A new paragraph **3.6.2. Remote control** is added:
    > "For installations with remote control, paragraph 12.3.6. of Annex 5 applies."
*   Paragraph **3.7.4., Table 5**: The headline is amended to read **"Minimum characteristic values for standard drawbar couplings"**. The word "maximum" is deleted four times in the legend of the table.
*   Paragraph **3.7.5.** is deleted.

### Amendments to Section 4 (Drawbar Eyes)
*   Paragraph **4.3., Table 7**: The headline is amended to read **"Minimum characteristic values for standard drawbar eyes"**.
*   Paragraph **4.4.4., Table 9**: The headline is amended to read **"Minimum characteristic values for Class L toroidal drawbar eyes"**.

### Amendment to Section 10 (Hook Type Couplings)
*   Paragraph **10.1.1., Table 13**: The headline is amended to read **"Minimum characteristic values for Class K hook type couplings"**.

## Amendments to Annex 6

### Paragraph 3.5.3.
Amended to read:
> "**3.5.3. Static test on coupling locking device**
> With hook type couplings … static force of 0.6 D acting in the direction of opening. The test shall not cause the closure to open. The closure/locking device shall be functional after the test."
