**UN Regulation No. 150**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of retro-reflective devices and markings for power-driven vehicles and their trailers**

**Introduction**
This Regulation combines the provisions of individual UN Regulations Nos. 3, 27, 69, 70 and 104 into a single UN Regulation. The objective is to increase clarity, consolidate and streamline the complexity of requirements, and prepare for a future transition to performance-based requirements, without changing any of the detailed technical requirements already in force up to its date of entry into force.

**1. Scope**
This Regulation applies to retro-reflective devices as:
*   Retro-reflectors of the Classes IA, IB, IIIA, IIIB and IVA
*   Retro-reflective Markings of the Classes C, D, E and F
*   Retro-reflective Marking Plates for Heavy and Long Vehicles of the Classes 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5
*   Retro-reflective Marking Plates for Slow Moving Vehicles of the Classes 1 and 2
*   Advance Warning Triangles of Type 1 and 2

**2. Definitions**
*   **2.1.** Definitions from the latest series of amendments to UN Regulation No. 48 apply.
*   **2.1.1.** Defines "retro-reflective devices of different types" based on trade name/manufacturer, material characteristics, design features, etc.
*   **2.1.2.** Specifies simplified submission requirements for devices differing only by trade name/mark.
*   **2.2.** Defines a "type" of retro-reflective device or material.
*   **2.3.** Provides geometric and photometric definitions for the CIE-Goniometer System (e.g., illumination axis, observation angle, entrance angle, coefficient of retro-reflection R').

**3. Administrative provisions**
*   **3.1. Application for approval:** Specifies required documentation and samples for different device types (retro-reflectors, advance warning triangles, marking plates, marking materials).
*   **3.2. Approval:** Requires separate approval for each device type. Assigns an approval number. Lists device symbols (e.g., IA, IB, C, RF) and their corresponding change indexes (all 0 for the initial series of amendments 00).
*   **3.3. Approval mark:** Describes the approval marking (circle with 'E' and country number, approval number, device symbol, Regulation number and series) and the alternative Unique Identifier (UI) system linked to a UN secure internet database.
*   **3.4. Modifications and extension of approval:** Procedures for notifying modifications.
*   **3.5. Conformity of production:** Requires manufacturing to conform to the approved type. References minimum control procedures (Annex 2) and sampling requirements (Annex 3).
*   **3.6. Penalties for non-conformity:** Approval may be withdrawn.
*   **3.7. Production definitively discontinued:** Notification requirements.
*   **3.8. Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities:** Communication of responsible bodies to the UN.

**4. General requirements**
*   **4.1.** General construction and durability requirements for retro-reflective devices (collective term).
*   **4.2. Colorimetric test conditions:** Specifies test procedures for night-time colours (using CIE Illuminant A), day-time colours (using CIE Illuminant D65, 45/0 geometry), and fluorescent colours (with and without retro-reflection).
*   **4.3. Determination of the luminance factor:** Method for determining luminance factor β.

**5. Specific technical requirements**
*   **5.1. Retro-reflectors of Classes IA and IB:** Details requirements for dimensions/shape (Annex 5), photometry, colorimetry, and physical/mechanical tests. Specifies minimum CIL values (Table 3) for red, amber (x2.5), and colourless (x4) devices. Outlines chronological test order (Table 4).
*   **5.2. Retro-reflectors of Classes IIIA and IIIB:** Similar structure to 5.1, with specific CIL values (Table 5) for red devices. Chronological test order in Table 6.
*   **5.3. Retro-reflectors of Class IVA:** Requirements for wide-angle retro-reflectors. Specifies minimum CIL values (Table 7) for white, amber, and red devices. Chronological test order in Table 8.
*   **5.4. Retro-reflective markings of Class C:** Requirements for contour/strip marking materials. Specifies minimum coefficient of retro-reflection R' values (Table 9) for yellow, white, and red. Lists required resistance tests.
*   **5.5. Retro-reflective markings of Classes D and E:** Requirements for distinctive markings/graphics. Specifies **maximum** coefficient of retro-reflection R' values (Table 10) for any colour. May be of any colour.
*   **5.6. Retro-reflective markings of Class F and marking plates of Class 5:** Requirements for extremities marking. Specifies minimum R' values (Table 11) for white and red. Includes requirements for day-time colour and luminance factor.
*   **5.7. Retro-reflective marking plates of Classes 1, 2, 3 and 4:** Requirements for plates for heavy/long vehicles. Specifies minimum R' values (Table 12) for yellow and red. Includes requirements for day-time and fluorescent colours.
*   **5.8. Retro-reflective marking plates for slow moving vehicles (Class SMV):** Requirements for SMV plates. Specifies minimum R' values (Table 13) for the outer border and enclosed triangle. Composed of red retro-reflective and fluorescent (class 1) or retro-reflective only (class 2) materials.
*   **5.9. Advance warning triangles of type 1 and 2:** Requirements for dimensions, photometry, and colorimetry. Specifies minimum CIL values (Table 14). Defines colorimetric limits for retro-reflective device (night-time, Table 15) and fluorescent materials (daytime, Table 16). Requires a minimum luminance factor for fluorescent materials.

**6. Transitional provisions**
*   **6.1.** Contracting Parties shall continue to accept type approvals to preceding series of amendments if the device's change index is unaffected. Extensions to such approvals shall not be refused.

**Annexes (Listed in Contents)**
The Regulation includes 25 Annexes detailing communication forms, conformity of production procedures, test methods (e.g., photometry, heat resistance, water penetration, corrosion, weathering, impact), specifications for shape/dimensions, and examples of approval marks.
