**Regulation No. 111 - Amendment 1**

**Date of entry into force:** 4 April 2005

This document constitutes Amendment 1 to Addendum 110 (Regulation No. 111) of the UNECE 1958 Agreement (Revision 2). The regulation concerns uniform provisions for the approval of tank vehicles of categories N and O with regard to rollover stability.

**Amendments:**

1.  **New Definitions (Paragraphs 2.7., 2.7.1.):**
    *   **2.7. "Rollover threshold"** means the instant when all the wheels of one side of a vehicle have lost contact with the supporting surface (tilt table platform).
    *   **2.7.1.** The inclination angle of the tilt table surface is represented by the symbol "β".

2.  **Amendment to Paragraph 5.3.1.1.:**
    *   Amended to specify reaching a tilt table angle of β = 23°.

3.  **Amendments to Annex 1 (Communication Form):**
    *   **Item 3.2.:** Amended to read: "3.2. tank; make, model, effective volume: ………..."
    *   **Item 6.2.:** Amended to read: "6.2. mass of vehicle in running order: ……….... "
    *   **New Item 8.1.:** "8.1. Centre of gravity height of the vehicle in running order: ….………... "
    *   **New Item 11.5.:** "11.5. Stability enhancement engaged: ………. yes/not/not applicable 2/"

4.  **Amendments to Annex 3 (Test Procedure):**
    *   **Paragraph 7.:** Amended to introduce the test procedure section.
    *   **New Paragraphs 7.2. to 7.4.:** Introduce alternative test loading conditions and correction formulae for test results when using substitute fluids.
 *   **7.2.** Allows for further testing under alternative loading conditions if a vehicle fails the initial test, subject to authority agreement and safety considerations. If alternative conditions are not used, the initial test result stands.
 *   **7.2.1.** First alternative: Vehicle at maximum mass, tank fully or partially filled with its intended normal-use fluid.
 *   **7.2.2.** Second alternative: Vehicle overloaded beyond its normal maximum mass, tank fully filled with a substitute fluid.
 *   **7.2.3.** If the manufacturer disagrees with overloading for the additional test, the vehicle is considered as having failed.
 *   **7.3.** Provides formulae for correcting the recorded tilt table inclination angle (β) when tested with a substitute fluid, to derive the corrected angle (β_p) for the intended fluid. β_p must be ≥ the required minimum (β_c). Defines related variables (masses, densities, center of gravity heights, volume, track, suspension stiffness).
 *   **7.4.** Specifies correction using the formula in paragraph 7.3. if a test with a lower-density substitute fluid results in a total mass below the maximum permissible and the intended fluid has a higher density. Alternatively, the manufacturer may arrange testing with the intended fluid, considering safety risks.

5.  **Amendment to Annex 4 (Calculation of the Rollover Threshold):**
    *   **Paragraph 7.2.:** Amended regarding the calculation of kingpin effects for semi-trailers separated from tractors.
 *   Defines calculation of kingpin track width (T_K).
 *   Specifies calculation of kingpin roll stiffness (C_DRESK) using a reference load-dependent roll stiffness factor of 4 kN-m/rad: `C_DRESK = A_K * 4`, where A_K is the load on the kingpin.
