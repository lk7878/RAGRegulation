**UN Regulation No. 107 - Revision 8**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of category M2 or M3 vehicles with regard to their general construction**

**Scope**
This Regulation applies to every single-deck, double-deck, rigid or articulated vehicle of category M2 or M3, with specific exclusions (e.g., prisoner transport vehicles, ambulances, off-road vehicles, school buses) and special provisions for certain vehicle types (e.g., police vehicles, mobile libraries).

**Key Definitions**
*   **Vehicle Classes:** For vehicles with capacity >22 passengers (excluding driver): Class I (areas for standing passengers), Class II (principally seated, limited standing), Class III (exclusively seated). For vehicles with capacity ≤22 passengers: Class A (designed to carry standing passengers) and Class B (no provision for standing passengers).
*   **Articulated Vehicle:** A vehicle with two or more rigid, permanently connected sections with intercommunicating passenger compartments.
*   **Low Floor Vehicle:** A Class I, II, or A vehicle where at least 35% of the area for standing passengers is without steps and includes access to at least one service door.
*   **Bodywork:** A separate technical unit comprising all the special internal and external equipment of the vehicle.
*   **Separate Technical Unit:** A device intended to be part of a vehicle, which may be type-approved separately.
*   **Trolleybus:** An electrically driven vehicle from external overhead wires, including dual-mode or guided variants.
*   **Passenger with Reduced Mobility:** Includes disabled people, wheelchair users, elderly, pregnant women, people with luggage or children, etc.

**Approval Process**
*   **Application:** Submitted by the manufacturer or accredited representative, accompanied by the relevant model information document (Annex 1, Part 1).
*   **Approval Grant:** Granted if the vehicle, bodywork, or fire suppression system meets the requirements.
*   **Approval Mark:** An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, regulation number, approval number, and class symbol) must be affixed.
*   **Conformity of Production:** Manufacturing must conform to the approved type.

**General Requirements (Annex 3)**
All vehicles must comply with the requirements in Annex 3, covering:
*   **Masses and Dimensions:** Compliance with Annex 11. Calculation of passenger area (S0) and standing area (S1).
*   **Vehicle Marking:** The driver's area must display the maximum number of seated, standing passengers, and wheelchairs (if applicable).
*   **Stability:** Vehicles must not overturn on a surface tilted to 28 degrees from horizontal under specified loading conditions.
*   **Superstructure Strength:** Requirements for resistance in roll-over accidents.
*   **Exits:** Minimum number, dimensions, and operation requirements for service doors, emergency doors, emergency windows, and escape hatches.
*   **Interior Arrangement:** Requirements for steps, gangways, access passages, handrails, seats, priority seats, and lighting.
*   **Heating and Ventilation:** Provisions for adequate heating and ventilation systems.
*   **Fire Safety:** Requirements for materials, fire extinguishers, and optional fire suppression systems (detailed in Annex 13).
*   **Miscellaneous:** Provisions for luggage compartments, crew communication, boarding devices, visual contrast, and specific requirements for vehicles without roofs.

**Special Provisions**
*   **Accessibility (Annex 8):** Class I vehicles must be accessible for people with reduced mobility, including at least one wheelchair user. Requirements for ramps, lifts, kneeling systems, wheelchair spaces, and priority seating.
*   **Trolleybuses (Annex 12):** Additional safety prescriptions for electrical equipment and operation.
*   **Fire Suppression Systems (Annex 13):** Requirements for systems approved as a component or installed in a specific engine compartment.
*   **Bodywork as Separate Technical Unit (Annex 10):** Provisions for type-approval of bodywork separately from the chassis.

**Transitional Provisions**
Detailed timelines for the application of successive series of amendments (04 to 08 series), specifying when Contracting Parties must grant approvals only to newer amendments and when they may refuse approvals to older series.

**Annexes**
The Regulation contains detailed annexes:
*   **Annex 1:** Information documents and communication forms.
*   **Annex 2:** Arrangements of approval marks.
*   **Annex 3:** General requirements for all vehicles.
*   **Annex 4:** Explanatory diagrams.
*   **Annex 5:** Visual contrast requirements.
*   **Annex 6:** Guidelines for measuring door and ramp forces.
*   **Annex 7:** Alternative requirements for Class A and B vehicles.
*   **Annex 8:** Accessibility for passengers with reduced mobility.
*   **Annex 9:** (Reserved)
*   **Annex 10:** Type approval for separate technical unit (bodywork).
*   **Annex 11:** Masses and dimensions.
*   **Annex 12:** Additional safety prescriptions for trolleybuses.
*   **Annex 13:** Fire suppression systems.
