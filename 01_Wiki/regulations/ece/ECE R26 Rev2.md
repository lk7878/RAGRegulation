# Regulation No. 26: Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to their external projections

## 1. Scope and purpose
1.1. This Regulation applies to external projections of category M vehicles. It does not apply to exterior devices for indirect vision or to the ball of towing devices.
1.2. The purpose of this Regulation is to reduce the risk or seriousness of bodily injury to a person hit by the bodywork or brushing against it in the event of a collision. This is valid both when the vehicle is stationary and in motion.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **2.1. "Approval of a vehicle"**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to its external projections.
- **2.2. "Vehicle type"**: A category of motor vehicles which do not differ in essential respects such as shape or materials of the external surface.
- **2.3. "External surface"**: The outside of the vehicle including bonnet, luggage compartment lid, doors, wings, roof, lighting and light-signalling devices and visible strengthening components.
- **2.4. "Floor line"**: The line determined by the trace of contact points of a cone (half-angle 30°) moved around the laden vehicle, contacting the external surface as low as possible.
- **2.5. "Radius of curvature"**: The radius of the arc of a circle which comes closest to the rounded form of the component.
- **2.6. "Laden vehicle"**: Vehicle laden to the maximum permitted technical mass.
- **2.7. "Extreme outer edge"**: Defined planes parallel or perpendicular to the vehicle's longitudinal median plane, excluding specific projections like tyres, anti-skid devices, indirect vision devices, certain lamps, and parts on bumpers.
- **2.8. "Dimension of the projection"**: Determined by the method in Annex 3, paragraph 2.
- **2.9. "Nominal line of a panel"**: The line through the center of a sphere at its first and last contact with a component during measurement (Annex 3, 2.2).
- **2.10. "Aerial"**: Any device for transmitting/receiving electromagnetic signals.
- **2.11. "Bumper"**: Front or rear, lower, outer structure intended for low-speed collision protection.
- **2.12. "Bumper cover"**: Non-rigid outer surface of a bumper.

## 3. Application for approval
3.1. For vehicle type approval, submitted by manufacturer or representative, accompanied by photographs, drawings, and a representative vehicle sample.
3.2. For separate technical units (luggage racks, ski racks, aerials), submitted by manufacturer of vehicle or unit, accompanied by technical characteristics, assembly instructions, and a specimen.

## 4. Approval
4.1. For vehicles meeting requirements of paragraphs 5 and 6, an approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate amendment series). An international approval mark (circle with "E", country number, "R26", dash, approval number) must be affixed.
4.2. For separate technical units meeting requirements of paragraphs 6.16-6.18, a similar approval process and marking apply.

## 5. General specifications
5.1. Does not apply to parts: above 2m height, below floor line, or not contactable by a 100 mm sphere.
5.2. External surface shall not have pointed/sharp parts or projections likely to increase injury risk.
5.3. Shall not have parts likely to catch pedestrians, cyclists, or motorcyclists.
5.4. Protruding parts shall have radius of curvature ≥ 2.5 mm. Exceptions for parts protruding <5 mm (angles must be blunted) and parts protruding <1.5 mm.
5.5. Protruding parts made of material with hardness ≤60 Shore A may have radius <2.5 mm.
5.6. These general specs apply in addition to particular specs in paragraph 6, unless otherwise stated.

## 6. Particular specifications
Detailed requirements for specific components:
- **6.1. Ornaments**: Projecting >10 mm must retract/detach/bend under 10 daN force. Remaining projection ≤10 mm.
- **6.2. Headlights**: Visors/rims projection ≤30 mm from outermost transparent surface, radius ≥2.5 mm.
- **6.3. Grilles and gaps**: Exceptions to radius rules based on gap distance (≤40 mm, 25-40 mm, ≤25 mm). Junction edges blunted.
- **6.4. Windscreen wipers**: Specific requirements for protective casing, holder radius, and area. Wiper blades etc. must have no sharp angles.
- **6.5. Bumpers**: Ends turned inwards. Specific radius requirements (5 mm inward from contour line, 2.5 mm elsewhere) for rigid surfaces on contour line. Exceptions for parts projecting <5 mm and bumper covers.
- **6.6. Handles, hinges, etc.**: Projection limits: door/luggage handles ≤40 mm, others ≤30 mm. Specific requirements for rotating handles.
- **6.7. Wheels, etc.**: Exempt from radius rule. No pointed projections beyond wheel rim. Wheel discs may project ≤30 mm with radius ≥30 mm.
- **6.8. Sheet-metal edges**: Must be folded back or shielded. Exceptions for bonnet rear edge and luggage boot front edge.
- **6.9. Body-panels**: Folds may have radius <2.5 mm if ≥ H/10 (H is projection height per Annex 3).
- **6.10. Lateral air/rain deflectors**: Radius ≥1 mm on outward edges.
- **6.11. Jacking brackets and exhaust pipes**: Projection ≤10 mm beyond floor line vertical projection. Exhaust may project more if with rounded edges (radius ≥2.5 mm).
- **6.12. Air intake/outlet flaps**: Must meet general specs in all positions.
- **6.13. Roof**: Opening roofs considered closed. Convertibles examined with hood raised and lowered.
- **6.14. Windows**: Moving windows: no exposed edge facing forwards, no part beyond extreme outer edge.
- **6.15. Registration plate brackets**: Must meet radius rule if contactable by 100 mm sphere with plate fitted.
- **6.16. Luggage racks and ski racks**: Must be securely attached. Surfaces contactable by 165 mm sphere must have radius ≥2.5 mm (unless 6.3 applies). Fastening elements without tools project ≤40 mm.
- **6.17. Aerials**: If unattached end <2m from road, must be within 10 cm inside extreme outer edge. Must not protrude beyond extreme outer edge. Shafts may have radius <2.5 mm, but ends must have fixed capping radius ≥2.5 mm. Base projection ≤40 mm (special rules for breakaway and rear-mounted aerials).
- **6.18. Assembly instructions**: Approved separate technical units must be sold with instructions for compliant mounting.

## 7. Modification of a vehicle type
7.1. Modifications must be notified. Type Approval Authority decides on new approval, revision, or extension.
7.1.1. **Revision**: For minor changes unlikely to have adverse effect.
7.1.2. **Extension**: For changes requiring further tests, changes to communication document, or approval to later amendment series.

## 8. Conformity of production
8.1. Production must conform to approved type.
8.2.-8.4. Requirements for quality control procedures, access to equipment, record keeping, testing, and verification by Type Approval Authority.

## 9. Penalties for non-conformity of production
9.1. Approval may be withdrawn.
9.2. Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 10. Production definitively discontinued
Holder must inform Type Approval Authority, which notifies other Parties.

## 11. Names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Parties must communicate these to UN Secretary-General.

## 12. Transitional provisions
12.1.-12.11. Detailed timelines for application of the 02, 03, and 04 series of amendments regarding granting of approvals, validity of existing approvals, and acceptance of preceding series.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Communication form for vehicle type approval actions.
- **Annex 2**: Examples of approval mark arrangements.
- **Annex 3**: Methods for determining dimensions of projections and gaps (height of folds, projection of components, headlamp visors/rims, gaps/grilles).
- **Annex 4**: Communication form for separate technical unit approval actions.
