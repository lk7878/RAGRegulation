**UN Regulation No. 94 - Revision 4, Amendment 1**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to the protection of the occupants in the event of a frontal collision.**

*   **Document Reference:** This document (E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.93/Rev.4/Amend.1 / E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.1/Add.93/Rev.4/Amend.1) is meant purely as a documentation tool. The authentic and legally binding text is: **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2021/115**.
*   **Parent Agreement:** Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical United Nations Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts which can be Fitted and/or be Used on Wheeled Vehicles and the Conditions for Reciprocal Recognition of Approvals Granted on the Basis of these United Nations Regulations (Revision 3, including amendments in force as of 14 September 2017).
*   **Series Designation:** Supplement 1 to the 04 series of amendments.
*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 22 June 2022.
*   **Document Date:** 16 September 2022.

**Amendment Content:**

The amendment modifies paragraph 2.40. by renumbering an existing note as "Note 1" and inserting a new "Note 2". The new note 2 reads:
> "Note 2: For pulsating DC voltages (alternating voltages without change of polarity) the DC threshold shall be applied."
