# UN Regulation No. 5 - Sealed Beam Headlamps (SB)

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to sealed beam headlamps (SB) for vehicles of category T (as defined in Consolidated Resolution R.E.3).

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **"Sealed beam" headlamp unit (SB unit):** An integral, sealed headlamp unit comprising reflector, lens, and light source(s) that cannot be dismantled without destruction.
*   **Type differences:** SB units are of different types if they differ in trade name, optical characteristics, additional components, rated voltage/wattage, filament shape, beam kind (passing/driving/both), traffic handedness suitability, light colour, or lens/coating materials.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application must specify the beam type(s) and traffic system suitability. It must be accompanied by drawings, a technical description, and samples (5 white-light units, or 1 coloured-light + 5 corresponding white-light units). For plastic lenses, 13 lenses (or material samples) and a compatible reflector are required.

## 4. Markings
SB units must bear the applicant's trade name/mark, a space for the approval mark, and the rated voltage and wattage(s). Markings for traffic handedness ("R/D" for right-hand, "L/G" for left-hand) are required if applicable. All markings must be legible and indelible.

## 5. Approval
*   An approval number is assigned to each approved type.
*   An **approval mark** must be affixed, consisting of a circle containing the letter "E" and the distinguishing number of the approving country, followed by the approval number.
*   **Additional symbols** indicate:
    *   Traffic system: Arrow pointing right (right-hand only), left-right double-headed arrow (both systems).
    *   Beam function: "SC" (passing only), "SR" (driving only), "SCR" (both).
    *   Plastic lens: "PL".
    *   Non-simultaneous lighting of passing beam filament: An oblique stroke (`/`) after the passing beam symbol.
    *   Series of amendments (e.g., "03").
*   Examples of approval mark arrangements are provided in **Annex 4**.

## 6. General Specifications
SB units must retain their photometric characteristics under normal use and vibration. They must be adjustable on the vehicle unless adjustment is provided elsewhere. Terminals must be robust. Units adaptable for both right and left-hand traffic must have only two precise, non-intermediate setting positions.

## 7. Rated Values
*   Rated voltages: 6, 12, and 24 V.
*   Power consumption at test voltage must not exceed the marked rated wattage. Tolerances are specified (e.g., 0% over for listed wattages). Minimum illumination values must still be met.

## 8. Illumination
*   Photometric testing is performed on a screen 25 m in front of the unit.
*   **Passing Beam:** Must produce a sharp horizontal "cut-off". Specific minimum and maximum illumination values are prescribed for various points (B50L, 75R, 50R, 25L, 25R, zones I, III, IV) depending on traffic handedness.
*   **Driving Beam:** The point HV must be within the 90% isolux of maximum illumination (E_max ≥ 32 lux). Minimum illumination values are specified horizontally from HV.
*   Values are detailed in **Tables within the text**.

## 9. Colour
The light emitted shall be white.

## 10. Gauging of Discomfort
Discomfort caused by the passing beam shall be gauged (subject to a recommendation for Type Approval Authorities).

## 11. Conformity of Production
The holder of the approval must have effective quality control procedures. The Type Approval Authority verifies control methods and may conduct inspections and tests on samples.

## 12. Penalties for Non-Conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or if products bearing the mark do not conform to the approved type.

## 13. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Modifications to the approved type must be notified. The Authority may confirm approval or require further testing.

## 14. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Authority if production ceases.

## 15. Transitional Provisions
*   Approvals according to earlier series of amendments remain valid indefinitely.
*   Approvals for replacement headlamps may continue to be issued based on this and previous series.

## 16. Communication of Technical Services and Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate the details of their Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretary-General.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Special provisions for SB units for agricultural/forest tractors and slow-moving vehicles (diameter < 160 mm). Reduced illumination requirements and specific marking ("SM" in an inverted triangle).
*   **Annex 2:** Model communication form for approval, extension, refusal, withdrawal, or discontinuation.
*   **Annex 3:** Minimum requirements for conformity of production control procedures. Specifies acceptable tolerances for photometric values (generally ±20%) and test procedures for verification.
*   **Annex 4:** Examples of arrangements of approval marks for independent and grouped/combined lamps.
*   **Annex 5:** Tests for stability of photometric performance in operation. Includes tests on clean and artificially dirtied headlamps, and a test for change in vertical position of the cut-off line under heat (Δr ≤ 1.0 mrad criterion).
*   **Annex 6:** Requirements for lamps with plastic lenses. Includes tests for resistance to temperature changes, atmospheric/chemical agents, detergents/hydrocarbons, mechanical deterioration, and coating adherence. Includes test chronology in **Appendix 1**.
    *   **Appendix 2:** Method for measuring light diffusion and transmission.
    *   **Appendix 3:** Spray testing method for mechanical deterioration.
    *   **Appendix 4:** Adhesive tape adherence test method.
*   **Annex 7:** Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector. Provides a detailed sampling plan (first and repeated sampling) with decision criteria for contesting or confirming conformity based on measured photometric deviations.
