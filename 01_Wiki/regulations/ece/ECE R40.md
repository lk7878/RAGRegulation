# Regulation No. 40

**UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF MOTOR CYCLES EQUIPPED WITH A POSITIVE-IGNITION ENGINE WITH REGARD TO THE EMISSION OF GASEOUS POLLUTANTS BY THE ENGINE**

**Date of entry into force as an annex to the Agreement: 1 September 1979**

## CONTENTS
1. Scope
2. Definitions
3. Application for approval
4. Approval
5. Specifications and tests
6. Modification of the vehicle type
7. Extension of approval
8. Conformity of production
9. Penalties for non-conformity of production
10. Production definitely discontinued
11. Names and addresses of technical services responsible for conducting approval tests, and administrative departments

**Tables**
- Table I: Limits in terms of reference weight R for motor cycles with two-stroke engines
- Table II: Limits in terms of reference weight R for motor cycles with four-stroke engines

**Annexes**
- Annex 1: Essential characteristics of the engine and information concerning the conduct of tests
- Annex 2: Communication concerning the approval (or refusal or withdrawal of approval) of a vehicle type (motor cycle) with regard to the emission of gaseous pollutants by the engine, pursuant to Regulation No. 40
- Annex 3: Arrangements of approval marks
- Annex 4: Type-I test (Verifying the average emission of gaseous pollutants in a congested urban area)
- Annex 5: Type-II test (Verifying the emission of carbon monoxide at idling speed)
- Annex 6: Specifications of reference fuels
- Annex 7: Method of determining the power absorbed on the road by the dynamometric brake for motor cycles.

## 1. SCOPE
This Regulation applies to the emission of gaseous pollutants from positive-ignition engines of two-wheeled or three-wheeled motor cycles with an unladen weight of less than 400 kg having a maximum design speed exceeding 50 km/h and/or cylinder capacity exceeding 50 ccm.

## 2. DEFINITIONS
For the purposes of this Regulation,
- **2.1. "Approval of a vehicle"** means the approval of a vehicle type with regard to the limitation of the emission of gaseous pollutants from the engine;
- **2.2. "Vehicle type"** means a category of power-driven vehicles which do not differ in such essential respects as the equivalent inertia determined in relation to the reference weight and the engine and vehicle characteristics as defined in annexes;
- **2.3. "Reference weight"** means the weight of the vehicle in running order increased by a uniform figure of 75 kg. The weight of the vehicle in running order is its total unladen weight with all tanks full;
- **2.4. “Engine crank-case"** means the spaces in or external to an engine which are connected to the oil sump by internal or external ducts through which gases and vapours can escape;
- **2.5. "Gaseous pollutants"** mean carbon monoxide, hydrocarbons and nitrogen oxides, the last-named being expressed in nitrogen dioxide (NO₂) equivalent.

## 3. APPLICATION FOR APPROVAL
The application for approval of a vehicle type with regard to limitation of the emission of gaseous pollutants from its engine shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or by his duly accredited representative, accompanied by required documents and a representative vehicle for testing.

## 4. APPROVAL
If the vehicle type meets the requirements, approval shall be granted and an approval number assigned. An international approval mark shall be affixed to conforming vehicles.

## 5. SPECIFICATIONS AND TESTS
### 5.1. General
Components liable to affect emissions shall be designed to enable the vehicle to comply despite normal use and vibration.

### 5.2. Description of tests
The vehicle shall be subjected to Type-I and Type-II tests.
- **5.2.1. Type-I test:** Verifying the average emission of gaseous pollutants in a congested urban area. Conducted per Annex 4. Limits are given in Tables I and II.
- **5.2.1.2. Type-II test:** Verifying the emission of carbon monoxide at idling speed. Conducted per Annex 5. The carbon monoxide content shall not exceed 4.5 per cent by volume.

## 6. MODIFICATIONS OF THE VEHICLE TYPE
Any modification shall be notified to the approving authority, which may require further testing.

## 7. EXTENSION OF APPROVAL
Approval may be extended to vehicle types differing only in reference weight, overall transmission ratios, or from two-wheeled to three-wheeled vehicles under specified conditions.

## 8. CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Vehicles bearing the approval mark must conform to the approved type. Conformity is verified by testing samples from series production using statistical methods defined in the regulation.

## 9. PENALTIES FOR NON-CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Approval may be withdrawn if production does not comply.

## 10. PRODUCTION DEFINITELY DISCONTINUED
The approval holder must inform the authority if production ceases.

## 11. NAMES AND ADDRESSES OF TECHNICAL SERVICES
Parties shall communicate details of technical services and administrative departments to the UN Secretariat.

## TABLES

### Table I: Limits for Motor Cycles with Two-Stroke Engines
| Reference Weight (R) | Carbon Monoxide (CO) - Type Approval | Carbon Monoxide (CO) - Conformity of Production | Unburnt Hydrocarbons (HC) - Type Approval | Unburnt Hydrocarbons (HC) - Conformity of Production |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| R < 100 kg | 16 g/km | 20 g/km | 10 g/km | 13 g/km |
| 100 kg < R < 300 kg | 16 + 24 * (R-100)/200 g/km | 20 + 30 * (R-100)/200 g/km | 10 + 5 * (R-100)/200 g/km | 13 + 8 * (R-100)/200 g/km |
| R > 300 kg | 40 g/km | 50 g/km | 15 g/km | 21 g/km |

### Table II: Limits for Motor Cycles with Four-Stroke Engines
| Reference Weight (R) | Carbon Monoxide (CO) - Type Approval | Carbon Monoxide (CO) - Conformity of Production | Unburnt Hydrocarbons (HC) - Type Approval | Unburnt Hydrocarbons (HC) - Conformity of Production |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| R < 100 kg | 25 g/km | 30 g/km | 7 g/km | 10 g/km |

| R > 300 kg | 50 g/km | 60 g/km | 10 g/km | 14 g/km |

*Note: The measurement of the mass per km of nitrogen oxides is made for information only.*

## ANNEXES OVERVIEW
- **Annex 1:** Details the essential characteristics of the engine and information required for testing (e.g., engine description, fuel feed, ignition, exhaust system).
- **Annex 2:** Provides the form for communication of approval, refusal, withdrawal, or production discontinuation.
- **Annex 3:** Shows examples of arrangement of approval marks.
- **Annex 4:** Describes in detail the **Type-I test** procedure, including the operating cycle on a dynamometer bench, test equipment, preparation, and calculation methods for determining emitted masses of CO, HC, and NOx.
- **Annex 5:** Describes the **Type-II test** procedure for measuring carbon monoxide at engine idling speed.
- **Annex 6:** Specifies the properties of two reference fuels (No.1 and No.2) to be used for testing.
- **Annex 7:** Describes the method for determining the power absorbed on the road by the dynamometric brake, necessary for setting up the test bench.
