**Amendment 3 to UN Regulation No. 152**

**Document Reference:** E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.3/Add.151/Amend.3
**Date of Document:** 2 February 2021
**Date of Entry into Force:** 3 January 2021
**Authentic Legal Text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2020/98

This document constitutes Amendment 3 to UN Regulation No. 152, which contains uniform provisions concerning the approval of motor vehicles with regard to the Advanced Emergency Braking System (AEBS) for M and N category vehicles.

**Amendment Content:**

A new paragraph **6.10.** is inserted, titled **"Robustness of the system"**, with the following provisions:

*   **6.10.1.** Defines the test procedure for robustness. Each specified test scenario (defined by setup, vehicle speed, load condition, and category - Car-to-Car or Car-to-Pedestrian) must be performed twice. If one of the two runs fails, one repeat test is allowed. A scenario is considered passed if the performance requirements are met in two runs. The allowable failure rate within each category is capped at:
    *   (a) 10.0% of performed test runs for Car-to-Car tests.
    *   (b) 10.0% of performed test runs for Car-to-Pedestrian tests.

*   **6.10.2.** Requires that the root cause of any failed test run be analyzed jointly by the manufacturer and the Technical Service, with the analysis annexed to the test report. If the failure is not attributable to a deviation in the test setup, the Technical Service may conduct additional tests at other speeds within the relevant ranges specified in paragraphs 5.2.1.3., 5.2.1.4., 5.2.2.3., or 5.2.2.4.

*   **6.10.3.** Requires the manufacturer to demonstrate, via appropriate documentation during the assessment per **Annex 3**, that the AEBS is capable of reliably delivering the required performance.

**Context:**
This amendment is issued under the framework of the **Revised 1958 Agreement** concerning the adoption of harmonized UN Regulations for wheeled vehicles and reciprocal recognition of approvals.
