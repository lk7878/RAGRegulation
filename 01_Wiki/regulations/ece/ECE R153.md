# UN Regulation No. 153

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to vehicles of category M1 with a total permissible mass not exceeding 3,500 kg and to vehicles of category N1 with regard to fuel system integrity and safety of electric power train operating on high voltage in the event of a rear-end collision.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Vehicle type**: A category of vehicles which do not differ in essential aspects affecting the rear-end impact test results (e.g., dimensions, structure, tank properties, REESS location).
*   **Passenger compartment**: The space for occupant accommodation, bounded by the roof, floor, side walls, doors, glazing, bulkheads, and electrical protection barriers.
*   **High voltage**: > 60 V and ≤ 1,500 V DC or > 30 V and ≤ 1,000 V AC (rms).
*   **Rechargeable Electrical Energy Storage System (REESS)**: The rechargeable energy storage system that provides electric energy for propulsion.
*   **Electric power train**: The electrical circuit which includes the traction motor(s), and may also include the REESS, converters, wiring, and charging coupling system.
*   **Live parts**: Conductive parts intended to be electrically energized under normal conditions.
*   **Protection degree IPXXB**: Protection from contact with high voltage live parts, tested using a Jointed Test Finger.
*   **Working voltage**: The highest RMS voltage specified by the manufacturer that may occur between any conductive parts under normal operation or open circuit.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or their accredited representative in accordance with the 1958 Agreement procedures. The information document model is in Annex 1 – Appendix 1.

## 4. Approval
*   Approval is granted if the vehicle meets the requirements.
*   An approval number is assigned per Schedule 4 of the 1958 Agreement.
*   An international approval mark (a circle surrounding "E", country code, Regulation number "153R", and approval number) must be affixed to approved vehicles.

## 5. Requirements
Following the rear-end impact test, the vehicle must fulfill:
### 5.2.1 Fuel System Integrity
*   **Liquid fuel vehicles**: No more than slight leakage on collision. Continuous leakage rate shall not exceed 30 g/min.
*   **Compressed hydrogen vehicles**:
    *   Average hydrogen leakage rate shall not exceed 118 NL/min post-crash.
    *   Hydrogen/Helium concentration in passenger/luggage compartments shall not exceed 4.0%/3.0% for 60 minutes.
    *   Hydrogen storage containers must remain attached by at least one point.

### 5.2.2 Safety of Electric Power Train (High Voltage)
*   **Protection against electrical shock**: Post-impact, high voltage buses must meet at least one of four criteria:
    1.  **Absence of high voltage**: Voltages ≤ 30 VAC or 60 VDC within 60 seconds.
    2.  **Low electrical energy**: Total Energy on buses < 0.2 J.
    3.  **Physical protection**: Protection degree IPXXB is provided, and resistance between exposed parts and chassis is < 0.1 Ω.
    4.  **Isolation resistance**: Minimum value of 100 Ω/V (DC) or 500 Ω/V (AC) of working voltage between bus and electrical chassis.
*   **Electrolyte leakage**:
    *   **Aqueous electrolyte REESS**: No leakage into passenger compartment. ≤ 7% by volume (max 5.0 L) leaked outside.
    *   **Non-aqueous electrolyte REESS**: No liquid electrolyte leakage into passenger/luggage compartment or outside vehicle for 60 minutes.
*   **REESS retention**: REESS must remain attached by at least one anchorage and shall not enter the passenger compartment.

## 6. Test
Compliance is checked by the test methods set out in Annexes 3, 4, and 5.

## 7. Modification and Extension of Approval
Modifications to the approved vehicle type must be notified. The Type Approval Authority may designate it as a "revision" or an "extension" requiring further inspections/tests.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Procedures shall comply with Schedule 1 of the 1958 Agreement. Every produced vehicle must conform to the approved type.

## 9. Penalties for Non-conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority upon ceasing production.

## 11. Technical Services and Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties shall communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

---

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form and Information document for approval.
*   **Annex 2**: Examples of approval mark arrangements.
*   **Annex 3**: Procedure for rear-end impact test (rigid barrier impact at 50 km/h, 1100 kg mass; includes alternative offset deformable barrier test option).
*   **Annex 4**: Test conditions and procedures for post-crash hydrogen fuel system integrity (leak rate and concentration measurement).
*   **Annex 5**: Test procedures for vehicles equipped with electric power train (voltage, energy, physical protection IPXXB, isolation resistance, electrolyte leakage, and REESS retention assessment).
