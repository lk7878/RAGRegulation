# UN Regulation No. 124 (Rev.2/Add.123) - Uniform provisions concerning the approval of wheels for passenger cars and their trailers

## 1. Scope
This Regulation covers new replacement wheels designed for vehicles in categories M1, M1G, O1 and O2. It does not apply to original equipment (OE) wheels or vehicle manufacturer’s replacement wheels. It also does not apply to "Special wheels", which remain subject to national approval. The Regulation comprises requirements for wheel manufacture and installation.

## 2. Definitions
*   **Wheel**: A rotating load-carrying member between the tyre and the axle, usually consisting of a rim and a wheel disc (integral, permanently attached, or detachable).
*   **Wheel type**: Defined by manufacturer, size designation, materials, attachment holes, load capacity, inflation pressure, and production method.
*   **OE-wheels**: Wheels authorized by the vehicle manufacturer for initial fitment.
*   **Replacement wheels**: Intended to replace OE wheels during service life, including:
    *   Vehicle manufacturer's replacement wheels
    *   Identical replacement wheels
    *   Replica replacement wheels
    *   Pattern part replacement wheels
*   **Special wheels**: Wheels not meeting the criteria for OE or replacement wheels (e.g., different rim width/diameter).
*   **Inset**: Distance from the attachment face to the centre line of the rim.
*   **Dynamic radius**: Theoretical rolling circumference / 2π of the largest specified tyre.
*   **International Tyre and Rim Standards**: Documents from ISO, ETRTO, TRA, JATMA, TRAA, ALAPA, STRO.
*   **Technical crack**: Material separation >1 mm during dynamic test (excluding production defects).
*   **Wheel calliper**: The rotating profile shape formed by the internal wheel contour.
*   **Tyre size designation**: Shows nominal section width, aspect ratio, and rim diameter code.

## 3. Application for Approval
Submitted by the manufacturer with drawings, technical description, vehicle fitment documentation (Annex 10), and sample wheels or test reports.

## 4. Approval
Granted if requirements are met. An approval number is assigned. An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, Regulation number, and approval number) must be affixed to approved wheels.

## 5. Wheel Markings
Wheels must be permanently marked with:
*   Manufacturer name/trademark
*   Wheel/rim contour designation (according to an International Standard)
*   Wheel inset
*   Date of manufacture (month/year)
*   Part number

## 6. General Requirements
*   Rim contour must conform to specified International Standards and ensure correct tyre/valve fitting.
*   Materials must be analyzed per Annex 4.
*   **Identical replacement wheels** require no physical testing or vehicle fitment checks.
*   **Replica and pattern part replacement wheels** must pass specific tests depending on material (steel, aluminium alloy, magnesium alloy) and construction (one-piece, demountable rim), including:
    *   Corrosion test (Annex 5)
    *   Rotating bending test (Annex 6)
    *   Rolling test (Annex 7)
    *   Impact test (Annex 8)
    *   Alternating torque test (Annex 9, for demountable rim wheels)
*   Pattern part wheels must also guarantee proper vehicle fitment (same nominal dimensions, suitable for specified tyre sizes, pass checks in Annex 10).

## 7. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified to the Approval Authority, which may require further testing.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Procedures must comply with Appendix 2 of the 1958 Agreement. Verification by authorities at least once every two years.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or products do not conform.

## 10. Production Definitely Discontinued
Manufacturer must inform the approval authority.

## 11. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval actions.
*   **Annex 2**: Arrangement of the approval mark.
*   **Annex 3**: Arrangement of wheel markings.
*   **Annex 4**: Material test requirements (chemical analysis, mechanical properties).
*   **Annex 5**: Corrosion test (salt-spray test).
*   **Annex 6**: Rotating bending test (simulates lateral cornering forces).
*   **Annex 7**: Rolling test (simulates straight-ahead driving stress).
*   **Annex 8**: Impact test (evaluates strength against obstacle strikes). Includes Appendix for passenger car light alloy wheels.
*   **Annex 9**: Alternating torque test (simulates braking/acceleration torque).
*   **Annex 10**: Vehicle fitment checks and documentation (wheel calliper clearance, ventilation holes, wheel fixing, external projections).
