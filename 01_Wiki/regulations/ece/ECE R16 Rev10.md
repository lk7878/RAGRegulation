# UN Regulation No. 16 (Revision 10)

## 1. Scope
*(As detailed in the frontmatter)*

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **Safety-belt (seat-belt, belt)**: An arrangement of straps with a securing buckle, adjusting devices and attachments anchored to the vehicle interior, designed to diminish injury risk by limiting occupant mobility. Includes lap belts, diagonal belts, three-point belts, S-type belts, and harness belts.
- **Belt type**: Classification based on differences in rigid parts, strap material/geometry.
- **Retractor**: Device to accommodate the strap. Types include:
    - Non-locking retractor (type 1)
    - Manually unlocking retractor (type 2)
    - Automatically locking retractor (type 3)
    - Emergency locking retractor (type 4 & 4N)
- **Restraint system**: A system for a specific vehicle type consisting of a seat and a belt fixed to the vehicle.
- **ISOFIX**: A system for connecting child restraint systems to vehicles using two rigid anchorages.
- **ISOFIX child restraint system**: A child restraint system fulfilling UN R44 or R129, attached to an ISOFIX anchorages system.
- **i-Size seating position**: A seating position designed to accommodate i-Size child restraint systems as defined in UN R129.
- **Safety-belt reminder**: A system to alert the driver when occupants do not use the safety-belt, comprising detection and two levels of alert (first and second level warning).

## 3. Application for Approval
- Procedures and required documentation for approval of a **vehicle type** (para. 3.1) and a **safety-belt type** or **restraint system type** (para. 3.2).

## 4. Markings
Samples submitted for approval must be clearly marked with the manufacturer's name, initials, or trade mark.

## 5. Approval
- Granting of approval and assignment of an approval number for vehicle types (para. 5.2) and safety-belt/restraint system types (para. 5.3).
- Requirements for the international approval mark (E-mark).
- Additional symbols required on approved belts (e.g., "A" for three-point belt, "B" for lap belt, "S" for special-type belts, plus modifiers like "e" for energy absorber, "r" for retractor type).

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. General Specifications
Design and construction requirements to ensure satisfactory operation and reduced injury risk.

### 6.2. Rigid Parts
Requirements for buckles, adjusting devices, attachments, retractors, and pre-loading devices regarding strength, corrosion resistance, operation (e.g., buckle release force ≤ 6 daN), and durability.

### 6.3. Straps
Requirements for strap characteristics, width under load (≥46 mm at 980 daN), and breaking strength (≥1470 daN after room conditioning; ≥75% of that value after special conditioning).

### 6.4. Belt Assembly or Restraint System
- **Dynamic Test** (para. 6.4.1): Requirements to be met during the test, including:
    - No breakage or release of critical parts.
    - Forward displacement limits for the manikin (pelvis: 80-200 mm; chest: 100-300 mm; reduced for harness belts).
    - Special provisions for belts used with airbags (chest reference point speed ≤ 24 km/h if displacement exceeds limits).
    - Special assessment for restraint systems regarding contact with vehicle interior.
- **Strength after Abrasion Conditioning** (para. 6.4.2): Breaking strength must be ≥75% of unabraded strap average.

## 7. Tests
Detailed test procedures for:
- Corrosion test (para. 7.2)
- Micro-slip test (para. 7.3)
- Conditioning and breaking-strength test of straps (para. 7.4)
- Test of belt assembly components (buckle, attachments, adjusting devices) (para. 7.5)
- Additional tests for belts with retractors (durability, locking, dust resistance, retracting forces) (para. 7.6)
- **Dynamic test** of belt assembly or restraint system (para. 7.7). Can be performed using a deceleration or acceleration test device. Specifies mounting, manikin positioning, performance criteria (deceleration/acceleration pulse within specified corridor, velocity change).
- Buckle-opening test after dynamic test (para. 7.8)
- Additional tests on belts with pre-loading devices (para. 7.9)
- Test report requirements (para. 7.10)

## 8. Requirements Concerning Installation in the Vehicle
### 8.1. Safety-belt and Restraint Systems Equipment
- Mandatory fitting of safety-belts/restraint systems in specified vehicle categories and seating positions.
- Permitted belt types as specified in Annex 16.
- Requirements for vehicles fitted with frontal protection airbags (warning labels and information).

### 8.2. General Requirements
- Safety-belts, restraint systems, and child restraint systems must be fixed to conforming anchorages (UN R14 or R145).
- Installation must ensure satisfactory operation, minimize risk of strap slippage or deterioration, and ensure accessibility.

### 8.3. Special Requirements for Rigid Parts
- Buckles must be visible, reachable, and operable with one hand.
- Adjusting devices must be accessible and easy to use.
- Retractors must operate correctly.
- Information requirements for child transport and ISOFIX positions.
- Requirements for i-Size seating positions.

### 8.4. Safety-belt Reminder Equipment
- **Applicability**: Driver and same-row seats in M1 and N1 vehicles; all rear seats in M1 and N1 vehicles (with exemptions).
- **General Requirements**:
    - Visual warning (specific symbol or tell-tale).
    - Audible warning (sound or vocal information).
- **First Level Warning**: Visual warning activated for min. 30 sec (front) / 60 sec (rear) when ignition on and belt unfastened.
- **Second Level Warning**: Visual and audible warning activated for min. 30 sec when vehicle exceeds certain thresholds (distance >500m, speed >25 km/h, or time >60s) and belt is unfastened. Supersedes first level warning.

## Annexes (Listed in Contents)
The regulation includes 18 Annexes covering communication forms, approval mark arrangements, test apparatus diagrams, test procedures (e.g., for corrosion, dust, dynamic testing), descriptions of manikins and trolleys, instructions, and specific requirements for installation of safety-belts, restraint systems, ISOFIX, i-Size systems, and safety-belt reminder tests.
