# UN Regulation No. 161

**Uniform provisions concerning the protection of motor vehicles against unauthorized use and the approval of the device against unauthorized use (by mean of a locking system)**

**Document:** E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.3/Add.160
**Date:** 21 October 2021
**Date of entry into force as an annex to the 1958 Agreement:** 30 September 2021
**Authentic and legally binding text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2021/48

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to:
1.1. Approval of a vehicle of category M1 and N1 with regard to its devices to prevent unauthorized use.
1.2. The fitting of devices to vehicles of other categories is optional but any such device fitted is required to comply with all relevant provisions of this Regulation.
1.3. At the request of the manufacturer, Contracting Parties may grant approvals according to this Regulation to vehicles of other categories and devices for fitment to such vehicles.
1.4. This Regulation does not apply to radio transmission frequencies, whether or not related to the protection of vehicles against unauthorized use.

## 2. Definitions
2.1. "Component" means a device subject to the requirements of this Regulation and intended to be part of a vehicle, which may be type-approved independently of a vehicle where this Regulation makes express provisions for so doing.
2.2. "Separate technical unit" means a device subject to the requirements of this Regulation and intended to be part of a vehicle, which may be type-approved separately, but only in relation to one or more specified types of vehicle where this Regulation makes express provisions for so doing.
2.3. "Manufacturer" means the person or body who is responsible to the approval authority for all aspects of the type approval process and for ensuring conformity of production.
2.4. "Vehicle type" means a category of motor vehicles which do not differ in such essential respects as the manufacturer's type designation, the arrangement and design of the vehicle component(s) on which the device to prevent unauthorized use acts, and the type of device to prevent unauthorized use.
2.5. "Device to prevent unauthorized use" means a locking system designed to prevent unauthorized normal activation of the engine or other source of main engine power of the vehicle in combination with at least one system which: (a) locks the steering; or (b) locks the transmission; or (c) locks the gearshift control; or (d) locks brakes. In the case of a system which locks brakes, deactivation of the device shall not automatically release the brakes contrary to the driver's intention.
2.6. "Steering" means the steering control, the steering column and its accessory cladding, the steering shaft, the steering gearbox and all other components which directly affect the effectiveness of the device to prevent unauthorized use.
2.7. "Combination" means one of the specifically developed and constructed variations of a locking system which, when properly activated, permits operation of the locking system.
2.8. "Key" means any device designed and constructed to provide a method of operating a locking system which is designed and constructed to be operated only by that device.
2.9. "Rolling code" means an electronic code consisting of several elements the combination of which changes at random after each operation of the transmitting unit.

## 3. Application for approval
3.1. The application for approval of a vehicle or component type with regard to this Regulation shall be submitted by the manufacturer.
3.2. It shall be accompanied by an information document established in accordance with the model shown in Annex 1.
3.3. Vehicle(s) / component(s) representative of the type(s) to be approved shall be submitted to the technical service responsible for conducting the approval tests.

## 4. Approval
4.1. If the type submitted for approval meets the requirements, approval shall be granted.
4.2. An approval number shall be assigned to each type approved.
4.3. Notice of approval or of extension of approval shall be communicated to the Contracting Parties.
4.4. An international approval mark shall be affixed to every approved vehicle or component.
4.5. - 4.8. Details on the approval mark arrangement, legibility, and placement.

## 5. Approval of a vehicle of category M1 and N1 with regard to its devices to prevent unauthorized use
### 5.1. General specifications
5.1.1. The device must be deactivated to enable engine start and vehicle movement.
5.1.2. This requirement shall be met by the application of a single key.
5.1.3. Key removal is generally not permitted before the device is activated.
5.1.4. The device and its operated components must resist rapid, inconspicuous tampering with readily available tools.
5.1.5. Must be fitted as original equipment and resist dismantling when blocked, except with special tools.
5.1.6. Mechanical locking systems: at least 1,000 different key combinations.
5.1.7. Electrical/electronic locking systems: at least 50,000 variants, incorporating a rolling code and/or minimum scan time of ten days.
5.1.8. Applies 5.1.6 or 5.1.7 depending on device nature.
5.1.9. Key and lock shall not be visibly coded.
5.1.10. Lock design requirements (torque resistance, tumbler arrangements).
5.1.11. Devices must exclude risk of accidental operating failure while engine is running. Specific conditions for activation (e.g., engine stop condition, vehicle stationary or low speed).
5.1.12. Power assistance may be used only for activation/unlocking; operating position must be maintained without power supply.
5.1.13. Vehicle motive power cannot be activated until the device is deactivated.
5.1.14. Devices locking brakes are permitted only when brakes are held locked by a purely mechanical device (5.1.13 does not apply).
5.1.15. Driver warning feature activation requirements.

### 5.2. Particular specifications
#### 5.2.1. Devices acting on the steering
5.2.1.1. Must render steering inoperative; normal operation restored before engine start.
5.2.1.2. Cannot be prevented from functioning when set.
5.2.1.3. Must meet requirements after 2,500 locking cycles in each direction (wear test per Annex 4, Part 1).
5.2.1.4. In activated position, must satisfy one of three strength/slip/free-rotation criteria (static torque: 300 Nm, or yield/slip at ≥100 Nm, or free rotation with 200 Nm static blocking strength).
5.2.1.5. & 5.2.1.6. Key removal and failure mode provisions.

#### 5.2.2. Devices acting on the transmission or on brakes
5.2.2.1. Acting on transmission: must prevent rotation of driving wheels.
5.2.2.2. Acting on brakes: must brake at least one wheel on each side of at least one axle.
5.2.2.3. Cannot be prevented from functioning when set.
5.2.2.4. Must not block transmission/brakes inadvertently when key is in lock.
5.2.2.5. Must remain fully effective after 2,500 locking cycles wear.
5.2.2.6. Key removal provisions.
5.2.2.7. Transmission device strength: withstand 150% of maximum transmissible torque.
5.2.2.8. Brake device: capable of holding laden vehicle stationary on a 20% gradient.
5.2.2.9. Compliance with UN R13 or R13-H required.

#### 5.2.3. Devices acting on the gearshift control
5.2.3.1. Must prevent any change of gear.
5.2.3.2. - 5.2.3.4. Permissible locking positions for manual and automatic gearboxes.
5.2.3.5. Must remain fully effective after 2,500 locking cycles wear.

### 5.3. Electromechanical and electronic devices
Shall be submitted to the tests described in Annex 6.

## 6. Modification of the type and extension of approval
Procedures for revision and extension of approval, and communication thereof.

## 7. Conformity of production procedures
Procedures shall comply with the 1958 Agreement. Vehicles/components must conform to approved type. Tests carried out on a statistically controlled random basis. Verification of control methods by approval authority at least every two years.

## 8. Penalties for non-conformity of production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met. Notification required.

## 9. Production definitively discontinued
Holder of approval must inform authority upon ceasing manufacture. Authority informs other Contracting Parties.

## 10. Names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties shall communicate relevant details to the UN secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Information document model.
*   **Annex 2:** Communication form model (approval, extension, refusal, withdrawal, discontinuation).
*   **Annex 3:** Arrangements of approval marks.
*   **Annex 4:**
    *   **Part 1:** Wear producing test procedure for devices acting on the steering.
    *   **Part 2:** Test procedure for devices acting on the steering using a torque limiting device.
*   **Annex 5:** (Reserved)
*   **Annex 6:** Operation parameters and test conditions for devices (climatic conditions, degree of protection, weatherability, electrical conditions, and a series of operation tests including resistance to temperature/voltage, foreign body/water tightness, condensed water, reversed polarity, short-circuits, energy consumption, vibration, and electromagnetic compatibility).
*   **Annex 7:** Electromagnetic compatibility tests (immunity against conducted/radiated disturbances, electrostatic discharges, radiated emissions) referencing UN Regulation No. 10.
