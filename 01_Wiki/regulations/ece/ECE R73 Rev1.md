**Part I - Approval of vehicles with regard to their lateral protection devices (LPD)**

**12. Requirements**
*   **12.1. Position & Width:** LPD shall not increase overall vehicle width. Main outer surface ≤150 mm inboard from vehicle's outermost plane. Rearward end ≤30 mm inboard from outermost edge of rear tyres over at least the rearmost 250 mm.
*   **12.2. Surface:** Smooth and continuous. Overlaps must face rearwards/downwards. Gaps ≤25 mm longitudinally. Protrusions (e.g., bolt heads) ≤10 mm and smooth. Edges contacted by 100 mm sphere must have radius ≥2.5 mm.
*   **12.3. Construction:** May be continuous surface, horizontal rail(s), or combination. Rails ≤300 mm apart. Height: ≥50 mm for N2/O3; ≥100 mm and essentially flat for N3/O4.
*   **12.4. Forward Edge Position:**
    *   N2/N3: ≤300 mm rear of vertical plane tangential to forward wheel tyre.
    *   Drawbar trailer: ≤500 mm rear of above plane.
    *   Semi-trailer: ≤250 mm rear of support legs' median plane, and ≤2.7 m from kingpin centre.
    *   Central axle trailer: Forward of front axle centre, but not beyond bodywork front.
*   **12.4.2-12.4.4 Forward Edge Construction:** If in open space >25 mm, requires continuous vertical member with specified dimensions and inward turn/radius. Special provisions apply if edge falls within or near cab.
*   **12.5. Rearward Edge:** ≤300 mm forward of vertical plane tangential to rear wheel tyre.
*   **12.6.** Requirements for front and rear edges are independent. No LPD required between two steered axles if their centre lines ≤2100 mm apart.
*   **12.7. Overhang:** Front/rear overhang of LPD shall not exceed distance between links and ram centre measured during test (para 12.10).
*   **12.8. Lower Edge:** ≤550 mm above ground.
*   **12.9. Upper Edge:** ≤350 mm below vehicle structure cut by vertical plane tangential to tyre outer surface. Exceptions for load platform, high structures, container vehicles, and crane operator platforms.
*   **12.10. Strength & Rigidity:** Must withstand 1 kN static force applied via 220 mm (±10 mm) diameter ram. Deflection under load: ≤30 mm over rearmost 250 mm; ≤150 mm elsewhere. Compliance by calculation is acceptable if validated.
*   **12.11. Incorporated Components:** Permanently fixed components (spare wheels, tanks, etc.) may be incorporated if they meet dimensional requirements.
*   **12.12.** LPD may not be used for attachment of brake, air, or hydraulic pipes.
*   **12.13. Movable LPD:** If designed for multiple positions, must have secure locking in operating position. Force to change position ≤40 daN.

**13. Derogations**
*   **13.1.** Special provisions for extendible trailers, tank vehicles, vehicles with extendible legs, vehicles equipped for Ro/Ro transport, and vehicles fitted with cranes.
*   **13.2.** Vehicle sides designed to meet requirements may be regarded as replacing LPD.

**Part II - Approval of lateral protection devices (LPD)**

**14. Requirements**
*   **14.1. Surface:** Identical to 12.2.
*   **14.2. Construction:** Identical to 12.3.
*   **14.3. Forward Edge:** Must consist of a continuous vertical member over whole height, with specified dimensions and inward turn/radius (50 mm turn/radius for N2/O3; 100 mm for N3/O4).
*   **14.4. Strength & Rigidity:** Identical to 12.10.
*   **14.5. Incorporated Components:** Identical to 12.11.
*   **14.6. Movable LPD:** Identical to 12.13.

**Part III - Approval of a vehicle with regard to the installation of LPD of a type approved according to Part II**

**15. Requirements**
*   **15.1. Position & Width:** Identical to 12.1.
*   **15.2. Forward Edge Position & Construction:** Identical to 12.4.
*   **15.3. Rearward Edge:** Identical to 12.5.
*   **15.4.** Identical to 12.6.
*   **15.5. Overhang:** Shall not exceed distance measured during test per 14.4.
*   **15.6. Lower Edge:** Identical to 12.8.
*   **15.7. Upper Edge:** Identical to 12.9.
*   **15.8. Mounting:** Securely mounted, not liable to loosening from vibration.
*   **15.9. Incorporated Components:** Permanently fixed components may be incorporated if meeting dimensional requirements or approved per Part I.
*   **15.10.** Identical to 12.12.

**16. Derogations**
*   **16.1.** Special provisions similar to 13.1 for extendible trailers, tank vehicles, vehicles with extendible legs, vehicles equipped for Ro/Ro transport, and vehicles fitted with cranes.

**General Provisions (Applicable to all Parts)**
*   **3. Requirements:** Satisfied if vehicle equipped per Part I/III, or if vehicle sides/components inherently meet requirements.
*   **4. Application for Approval:** Specifies required documentation and submission of representative vehicle/LPD sample to technical service.
*   **5. Approval:** Procedures for granting approval, assigning approval numbers (first two digits indicate amendment series), communication forms, and approval mark affixing.
*   **6. Modification & Extension:** Procedure for notifying modifications and obtaining extension of approval.
*   **7. Conformity of Production:** Vehicles/LPD must conform to approved type.
*   **8. Penalties for Non-conformity:** Approval may be withdrawn for non-compliance.
*   **9. Production Discontinued:** Manufacturer must inform approval authority.
*   **10. Technical Services & Authorities:** Contracting Parties to communicate details.
*   **11. Transitional Provisions:** Relating to the 01 series of amendments.

**Annexes**
*   **Annex 1:** Communication forms for approval (Appendices 1-3 for Parts I-III).
*   **Annex 2:** Examples of approval marks.
*   **Annex 3:** Test conditions for LPD (on vehicle, representative part, or rigid bench) and vehicles (positioning, tyre pressure, suspension state).
