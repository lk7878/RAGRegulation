# UN Regulation No. 30 (Revision 3) - Pneumatic Tyres for Motor Vehicles and Their Trailers

## 1. Scope
This Regulation covers new pneumatic tyres designed primarily, but not only, for vehicles in categories M1, O1 and O2. It does not apply to tyres designed for:
- the equipment for vintage cars
- competitions.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **Type of pneumatic tyre**: A category of pneumatic tyres which do not differ in essential respects such as manufacturer, tyre-size designation, category of use, structure, speed category symbol, load-capacity index, and tyre cross-section.
- **Snow tyre**: A tyre whose tread pattern and structure are primarily designed to ensure in mud and fresh or melting snow a performance better than that of an ordinary (road-type) tyre.
- **Structure**: Technical characteristics of the tyre's carcass, including:
    - Diagonal or bias-ply
    - Bias-belted
    - Radial
    - Reinforced or Extra Load
    - Temporary use spare tyre
    - T-type temporary use spare tyre
    - Run flat tyre or Self supporting tyre
- **Speed category**: The maximum speed which the tyre can sustain, expressed by a speed category symbol (e.g., L=120 km/h, M=130 km/h, ..., Y=300 km/h).
- **Load-capacity index**: A number associated with the reference mass a tyre can carry.
- **Maximum load rating**: The maximum mass the tyre is rated to carry, with specific reductions for speeds above 210 km/h.
- **Run flat system or Extended mobility system**: An assembly of specified functionally dependent components, including a tyre, which together provide the specified performance granting the vehicle with the basic tyre functions, at least, at a speed of 80 km/h and a distance of 80 km when operating in flat tyre running mode.

## 3. Markings
Pneumatic tyres submitted for approval shall bear specified markings on the side walls, including:
- Trade name or mark
- Tyre-size designation
- Indication of structure (e.g., "R" for radial, "D" for diagonal, "B" for bias-belted, "F" for run flat)
- Speed category symbol
- Load-capacity index
- Inscription "M+S", "M.S" or "M&S" for snow tyres
- Word "TUBELESS" if designed for use without an inner tube
- Word "REINFORCED" or "EXTRA LOAD" if a reinforced tyre
- Date of manufacture (four digits: week and year)
- For temporary use spare tyres: "TEMPORARY USE ONLY"
- For T-type temporary use spare tyres: "INFLATE TO 420 kPa (60 psi)"
- For run flat or self-supporting tyres: a specified symbol.

Markings and the approval mark shall be moulded on or into the tyres, clearly legible, and situated in the lower area of the tyre on at least one side wall.

## 4. Application for Approval
The application shall be submitted by the holder of the trade name or mark and include details such as tyre-size designation, trade name, category of use, structure, speed category, load-capacity index, and overall dimensions. It shall be accompanied by sketches or photographs, a test report or samples, and evidence of conformity of production arrangements.

## 5. Approval
If the tyre meets the requirements, approval shall be granted with an approval number. Notice of approval, extension, refusal, withdrawal, or production discontinuation shall be communicated to the Parties to the 1958 Agreement. An international approval mark (a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the country number and the approval number) shall be affixed to conforming tyres.

## 6. Requirements
### 6.1. Dimensions of Tyres
- **Section width and outer diameter**: Calculated using specified formulae, with tolerances provided.
- **Method of measuring**: Described in Annex 6.
- **Tyre section-width specifications**: Overall width may exceed the calculated section width by specified percentages (e.g., 6% for diagonal tyres, 4% for radial/run flat tyres).
- **Tyre outer-diameter specifications**: Must be within calculated minimum and maximum values.

### 6.2. Load/Speed Performance Test
- The tyre shall undergo a load/speed performance test as per Annex 7.
- A tyre passes if it does not exhibit tread separation, ply separation, cord separation, chunking, or broken cords after the test.
- For run flat systems, an additional test in flat tyre running mode is required.
- The outer diameter after the test must not differ by more than ±3.5% from the diameter before the test.

### 6.3. Tread-Wear Indicators
- The tyre shall include at least six (or four for small rims) transverse rows of wear indicators in the principal grooves.
- Indicators must show when tread grooves are no longer more than 1.6 mm deep, with a tolerance of +0.60/-0.00 mm.

## 7. Modifications of the Type and Extension of Approval
Modifications shall be notified to the approving authority, which may require further testing. Changes to the tread pattern do not necessitate repeating tests. Extensions of approval shall be communicated.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Tyres shall be manufactured to conform to the approved type. The approving authority shall verify conformity control methods at least once every two years.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or if tyres fail tests. Withdrawal shall be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 10. Production Definitely Discontinued
The manufacturer shall inform the approving authority if production ceases, and this shall be communicated to other Parties.

## 11. Transitional Provisions
Contracting Parties shall not refuse extensions of approval to preceding series of amendments. Specific provisions relate to tread-wear indicators and approval series.

## 12. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments
Parties shall communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Administrative Departments to the UN Secretariat. They may designate manufacturers' laboratories as approved test laboratories.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval actions.
- **Annex 2**: Example of the arrangement of the approval mark.
- **Annex 3**: Arrangement of tyre markings.
- **Annex 4**: Load-capacity indices (table linking index numbers to masses in kg).
- **Annex 5**: Tyre size designation and dimensions (tables for various tyre series).
- **Annex 6**: Method of measuring pneumatic tyres.
- **Annex 7**: Procedure for load/speed performance tests, including preparation, test execution, and specific procedures for run flat systems and tyres for speeds over 300 km/h.
