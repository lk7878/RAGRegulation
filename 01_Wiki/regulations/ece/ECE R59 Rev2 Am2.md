# UN Regulation No. 59
**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of replacement silencing systems**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation covers exhaust replacement silencing systems for vehicles of categories M and N.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **"Silencing systems"**: A complete set of components necessary for limiting the sound produced by an engine of a motor vehicle, its intake and its exhaust.
- **"Exhaust silencing system"**: A complete set of components necessary for limiting the sound produced by the engine of a motor vehicle and its exhaust.
- **"Exhaust replacement silencing system or components of said system"**: Any part of the exhaust silencing system intended for use on a vehicle, other than a part of the type fitted to this vehicle when submitted for type approval pursuant to this Regulation.
- **"Approval of an exhaust replacement silencing system or components"**: The approval of the whole or part of an exhaust silencing system adaptable to one or several specified types of motor vehicles, as regards the limitation of their noise level.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application shall be submitted by the manufacturer or accredited representative, accompanied by required documents (description of vehicle type, drawings, information document, etc.). The Technical Service may request samples of the system, the original system, a representative vehicle, and/or a separate engine for testing.

## 4. Markings
Each component (excluding tubes and mounting accessories) must bear the trade name/mark and commercial description. Marking must also include the character "A", "B", or "C" indicating the phase of limit values (from UN R51) the system is intended for. Markings must be legible and indelible.

## 5. Approval
If the system meets the requirements, approval is granted. An approval number is assigned. Notice of approval/refusal is communicated to applying Parties. An international approval mark (circle with "E", country code, regulation number "R59", and approval number) must be affixed to every conforming component.

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. General Specifications
Systems must be designed for normal use conditions, resistant to corrosion, and constructed to prevent easy removal of silencing parts. Systems with manually adjustable modes must meet requirements in all modes.

### 6.2. Specifications Regarding Sound Levels
- **Measurement Conditions**: Tests must use "normal" tyres (as per UN R117), not "special use" or "snow" tyres.
- **Sound Reduction Performance**: Verified per methods in UN R51. Measured sound levels with the replacement system must not exceed the original system's levels by more than specified limits (e.g., 1 dB(A) for moving vehicle, 2 dB(A) for stationary).
- **Additional Sound Emission Provisions (ASEP)**: Must be fulfilled if the replacement system is for vehicles type-approved to UN R51 versions including ASEP. Specific testing and documentation requirements apply, especially for systems with adjustable modes, sound actuators, or variable geometries.

### 6.3. Measurement of Vehicle Performance
Vehicle performance with the replacement system must be comparable to the original. Verified by measuring back pressure, which must not exceed the original system's value by more than 25%.

### 6.4. Additional Specifications for Systems with Fibrous Materials
Fibrous materials may be used only if exhaust gas does not contact them, or if the system belongs to a proven design family not subject to deterioration. Otherwise, the system must undergo conditioning (e.g., 10,000 km road operation, test bench conditioning, or pulsation conditioning).

## 7. Extension of Approval
The manufacturer may request an extension of approval to other vehicle types. The procedure follows that of the initial application.

## 8. Modification of the Type
Any modification must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further tests.

## 9. Conformity of Production
Production must conform to approved types. The holder of approval must ensure quality control procedures, have necessary test equipment, record results, and conduct prescribed tests. The Type Approval Authority verifies conformity.

## 10. Information for Users and Technical Inspection
Each system must be accompanied by a document (paper or reference to website) containing specified information (type-approval number, marking, manufacturer details, intended vehicles, mounting instructions). See Annex 6.

## 11. Penalties for Non-Conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met.

## 12. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority if production ceases.

## 13. Technical Services and Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## 14. Transitional Provisions
- Upon entry into force of the 03 series, Contracting Parties shall not refuse approvals under it.
- 12 months after entry into force, approvals shall only be granted for types meeting the 03 series.
- 24 months after entry into force, extensions shall only be granted for types meeting the 03 series.
- Approvals to preceding series remain valid.

## Annexes
The Regulation includes detailed annexes covering:
- **Annex 1**: Approval communication form and information document template.
- **Annex 2**: Examples of approval mark arrangements.
- **Annex 3**: Diagram of test apparatus for pulsation conditioning.
- **Annex 4**: Diagrams for back pressure measuring points.
- **Annex 5**: Procedures for checks on conformity of production.
- **Annex 6**: Required information for users and technical inspection.
