# UN Regulation No. 127 (Revision 1, incorporating the 01 series of amendments)

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of motor vehicles with regard to their pedestrian safety performance**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to motor vehicles of categories M1 and N1.
However, vehicles of category N1 where the driver’s position "R-point" is either forward of the front axle or longitudinally rearwards of the front axle transverse centreline by a maximum of 1,100 mm, are exempted.
This Regulation does not apply to vehicles of category M1 above 2,500 kg maximum mass and which are derived from N1 category vehicles, and where the driver’s position "R-point" is either forward of the front axle or longitudinally rearwards of the front axle transverse centreline by a maximum of 1,100 mm.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions for the purpose of this Regulation include:
*   **Adult headform test area:** Area on the outer front structure bounded by WAD1700 or a line 82.5 mm rearward of the bonnet leading edge (whichever is more rearward), WAD2100 or a line 82.5 mm forward of the bonnet rear reference line (whichever is more forward), and 82.5 mm inside the side reference line.
*   **Assessment interval (AI):** For the flexible lower legform impactor, defined from first contact to the last zero crossing of femur and tibia bending moments after their first local maximum.
*   **Bonnet leading edge reference line:** Geometric trace of contact points between a 1000 mm straight edge (inclined 50° rearwards, lower end 600 mm above ground) and the front surface.
*   **Bonnet rear reference line:** Geometric trace of the most rearward points of contact between a 165 mm diameter sphere and the front structure while maintaining contact with the windscreen.
*   **Bonnet top test area:** Comprises the child and adult headform test areas.
*   **Bumper test area:** Frontal surface of the bumper limited by two longitudinal vertical planes intersecting points 66 mm inside the defined corners.
*   **Child headform test area:** Area on the outer front structure bounded by WAD1000 or a line 82.5 mm rearward of the bonnet leading edge (whichever is more rearward), WAD1700 or a line 82.5 mm forward of the bonnet rear reference line (whichever is more forward), and 82.5 mm inside the side reference line.
*   **Front structure:** All outer structures of the vehicle except the windscreen, windscreen header, A-pillars and structures rearward of these.
*   **Head Injury Criterion (HIC):** Calculated from accelerometer time histories. Formula: `HIC = { (1/(t2-t1)) * ∫(a)dt from t1 to t2 }^2.5 * (t2-t1)`, where `a` is resultant acceleration in g, and `t1`, `t2` are time instants (t2-t1 ≤ 15 ms) maximizing HIC.
*   **Lower bumper reference line:** Lower limit of significant pedestrian contact with the bumper, defined using a 700 mm straight edge inclined 25° forwards.
*   **Normal ride attitude:** Vehicle positioned with mass in running order, tyres at recommended pressure, front wheels straight, passenger mass on front seat, suspension set for 40 km/h.
*   **Upper bumper reference line:** Upper limit of significant pedestrian contact with the bumper, defined using a 700 mm straight edge inclined 20° rearwards.
*   **Vehicle type with regard to pedestrian protection:** Category of vehicles which, forward of the A-pillars, do not differ in essential respects such as structure, main dimensions, materials of outer surfaces, or component arrangement that could negatively affect impact test results.
*   **Wrap Around Distance (WAD):** Geometric trace on the vehicle's outer front surface described by a flexible tape held taut from ground level vertically below the bumper front face. Key WADs are 1000 mm (WAD1000), 1700 mm (WAD1700), and 2100 mm (WAD2100).

## 3. Application for Approval
The application, submitted by the manufacturer or authorized representative, must include an information document per Annex 1, Part 1, describing the vehicle type and providing dimensional drawings. A representative vehicle must be submitted for testing.

## 4. Approval
If the vehicle meets the requirements, approval is granted. An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate the series of amendments). An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country distinguishing number, plus "127R-approval number") must be affixed to conforming vehicles.

## 5. Specifications
### 5.1. Legform Test to Bumper
Requirements depend on lower bumper height at the test position:
*   **< 425 mm:** Apply flexible lower legform test (5.1.1).
*   **≥ 425 mm and < 500 mm:** Manufacturer may choose flexible lower legform (5.1.1) or upper legform test (5.1.2).
*   **≥ 500 mm:** Apply upper legform test (5.1.2).

**5.1.1. Flexible Lower Legform to Bumper (Test per Annex 5, para. 1):**
*   Max dynamic Medial Collateral Ligament (MCL) elongation ≤ 22 mm.
*   Max dynamic Anterior Cruciate Ligament (ACL) and Posterior Cruciate Ligament (PCL) elongation ≤ 13 mm.
*   Max dynamic tibia bending moment ≤ 340 Nm (with possible relaxation zone up to 380 Nm for nominated widths totalling max 264 mm).
*   Impactor must be certified per Annex 6, para. 1.

**5.1.2. Upper Legform to Bumper (Test per Annex 5, para. 2):**
*   Instantaneous sum of impact forces ≤ 7.5 kN.
*   Bending moment on impactor ≤ 510 Nm.
*   Impactor must be certified per Annex 6, para. 2.

### 5.2. Headform Tests
**5.2.1. Child and Adult Headform Tests (Test per Annex 5, paras. 3, 4, 5):**
*   HIC ≤ 1000 over two-thirds of the bonnet top test area.
*   HIC ≤ 1700 for the remaining one-third area.
*   If only a child headform test area exists, HIC ≤ 1000 over two-thirds, and ≤ 1700 for the remaining area.

**5.2.2. Child Headform Impact:**
*   HIC ≤ 1000 over a minimum of one-half of the child headform test area.
*   HIC ≤ 1700 for the remaining area.

**5.2.3.** Headform impactors must be certified per Annex 6, para. 3.

## 6. Modification of Vehicle Type and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may grant an extension or require further tests.

## 7. Conformity of Production
Vehicles must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. Conformity control methods are subject to verification by the Type Approval Authority at least once every two years.

## 8. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met.

## 9. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority if production of an approved type ceases.

## 10. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## 11. Transitional Provisions
*   From the entry into force of the 01 series of amendments, Contracting Parties shall not refuse approvals under this amended version.
*   From 1 September 2017, Contracting Parties shall grant approvals only if the vehicle meets the requirements of the 01 series of amendments.
*   Extensions of approvals granted under preceding amendments shall not be refused.
*   Approvals to preceding amendments shall continue to be accepted.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Information document and communication forms.
*   **Annex 2:** Arrangements of approval marks.
*   **Annex 3:** General test conditions (temperature, humidity, site, vehicle preparation).
*   **Annex 4:** Test impactor specifications for flexible lower legform, upper legform, and child/adult headforms.
*   **Annex 5:** Test procedures for flexible lower legform, upper legform, and headform impacts.
*   **Annex 6:** Certification procedures for all impactors (static and dynamic tests).
