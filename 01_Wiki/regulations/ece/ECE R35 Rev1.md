# UN Regulation No. 35 (Revision 1)
**UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF VEHICLES WITH REGARD TO THE ARRANGEMENT OF FOOT CONTROLS**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the arrangement and mode of operation of the foot controls of private (passenger) cars, whatever the position of the driver's seat.

## 2. Definitions
- **2.1. "approval of a vehicle"**: Approval of a vehicle type regarding foot controls.
- **2.2. "private (passenger) car"**: A motor vehicle designed to carry not more than nine persons.
- **2.3. "vehicle type"**: Vehicles which do not differ in structure/internal arrangements affecting foot control location/operation.
- **2.4. "accelerator pedal"**: Foot control for varying engine power output.
- **2.5. "service-brake pedal"**: Foot control for operating the service braking device.
- **2.6. "clutch pedal"**: Foot control for engaging/disengaging engine from transmission and road wheels.
- **2.7. "transverse plane"**: Plane perpendicular to the vehicle's median longitudinal plane.
- **2.8. "longitudinal plane"**: Plane parallel to the vehicle's median longitudinal plane.
- **2.9. "reference plane 'P'"**: A transverse plane defined by points related to the accelerator pedal and heel point.
- **2.10. "walls"**: Fixed structural members (e.g., transmission tunnel, wheel arches).

## 3. Application for Approval
Submitted by manufacturer or representative, accompanied by drawings and a representative vehicle.

## 4. Approval
- Granted if requirements met.
- An approval number is assigned.
- Communication via forms in Annex 1.
- An approval mark (circle with "E" and country number, plus regulation number) must be affixed.

## 5. Requirements (See Annex 4)
- **5.1.** Order from left to right: clutch pedal (if any); service-brake pedal; accelerator pedal.
- **5.2.** Left foot must have a rest position without being trapped.
- **5.3.** Full pedal travel without inadvertent operation of other controls.
- **5.4.** Distance between accelerator and brake pedal projections on plane "P": ≥100 mm and ≥50 mm.
- **5.5.** Distance between brake and clutch pedal projections on plane "P": ≥50 mm.
- **5.6.** Distance between clutch pedal projection and nearest wall on plane "P": ≥50 mm.
- **5.7.** Distances between brake pedal projection and walls on plane "P":
    - Three pedals: ≥130 mm (right), ≥160 mm (left).
    - Two pedals: ≥130 mm (right), ≥120 mm (left).

## 6. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified; approval may be extended or require further testing.

## 7. Conformity of Production
Vehicles bearing the approval mark must conform to the approved type.

## 8. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn for non-conformity.

## 9. Production Definitely Discontinued
Manufacturer must inform approval authority.

## 10. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments
Parties must communicate details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal.
- **Annex 2**: Examples of approval mark arrangements.
- **Annex 3**: **Procedure for determining the "H" point and the actual torso angle for seating positions in motor vehicles.** Includes purpose, definitions, requirements for data presentation and verification, and a detailed step-by-step procedure for measurement using the Three-dimensional "H" point machine (3-D H machine). Appendices describe the machine, the three-dimensional reference system, and a reference data form.
- **Annex 4**: Arrangement of foot controls (includes Figure 1 with dimensions for two-pedal automatic transmission setup).
