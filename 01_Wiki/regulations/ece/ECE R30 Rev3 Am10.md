**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of pneumatic tyres for motor vehicles and their trailers**

**Document Reference:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2021/75 (Authentic and legally binding text)
**Agreement:** Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical United Nations Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts...
**Regulation:** UN Regulation No. 30 (Addendum 29, Revision 3, Amendment 10)
**Supplement:** Supplement 24 to the 02 series of amendments
**Date of Entry into Force:** 7 January 2022

**Amendments to Annex 7:**

*   **Paragraph 3.1.14., symbol:** Replaced.
*   **Paragraph 3.2.:** Amended to specify inflating the tyre to 2.5 bar and conditioning the tyre-and-wheel assembly at a test room temperature of 38 ºC ± 3 ºC for not less than three hours.
*   **Paragraph 3.7.:** Amended to specify that during the test, the test room temperature must be maintained at 38°C ± 3°C. The temperature sensor shall be placed at a distance between 0.15 m and 1.00 m from the tyre sidewall.
*   **Paragraph 4.2.:** Amended to specify inflating the tyre to 2.5 bar and conditioning the tyre-and-wheel assembly at a test room temperature of 25 ºC ± 3 ºC for not less than three hours.
*   **Paragraph 4.7.:** Amended to specify that during the test, the test room temperature must be maintained at 25°C ± 3°C. The temperature sensor shall be placed at a distance between 0.15 m and 1.00 m from the tyre sidewall.
