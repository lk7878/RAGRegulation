# UN Regulation No. 29 (Revision 2)
**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to the protection of the occupants of the cab of a commercial vehicle**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to vehicles with separate driver’s cab of category N1 with regard to the protection of the occupants of the cab.

## 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation:
- **2.1. "Approval of a vehicle"**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to the protection of the occupants of the cab in the event of head-on impact or overturning.
- **2.2. "Vehicle type"**: A category of motor vehicle which does not differ in essential respects such as cab components or cab attachment to the chassis.
- **2.5. "Cab-over engine vehicle"**: A vehicle where more than half of the engine length is rearward of the foremost point of the windshield base and the steering wheel hub is in the forward quarter of the vehicle length.
- **2.8. "Test A"**: Frontal impact test to evaluate cab resistance in frontal impact.
- **2.9. "Test B"**: Impact test to the A-pillars to evaluate cab resistance in a 90° rollover accident with subsequent impact.
- **2.10. "Test C"**: Cab roof strength test to evaluate cab resistance in a 180° rollover accident.
- **2.11. "A-pillar"**: The foremost and outermost roof support.
- **2.12. "Windscreen"**: The frontal glazing situated between the A-pillars.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or representative, accompanied by triplicate drawings of the vehicle and cab structure. A model information document is provided in Annex 1, Part 1.

## 4. Approval
- **4.1.**: Approval is granted if the vehicle type meets the requirements of paragraph 5.
- **4.2.**: An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments at the time of approval.
- **4.4.**: An international approval mark must be affixed to each conforming vehicle, consisting of a circle with "E" and country number, followed by "29R", a dash, and the approval number.
- **4.8.**: Examples of approval mark arrangements are given in Annex 2.

## 5. Requirements
### 5.1. General Requirements
- **5.1.1.**: The cab shall be designed and attached to minimize injury risk to occupants in an accident.
- **5.1.2.**: **Vehicles of categories N1 and N2 (GVW ≤ 7.5 t)**: Subject to Tests A and C. A vehicle approved to UN R033 or UN R094 may be considered to satisfy Test A requirements.
- **5.1.3.**: **Vehicles of categories N3 and N2 (GVW > 7.5 t)**: Subject to Tests A, B, and C.
- **5.1.4.**: **Test A (frontal impact)** is only conducted on Cab-over-Engine vehicles.
- **5.1.5.**: One to three cabs may be used for compliance demonstration. Both phases of Test C (if applicable) must be on the same cab.
- **5.1.6.**: Tests may be waived if the manufacturer demonstrates via simulation/calculation that the cab will not deform dangerously into the survival space.

### 5.2. Survival Space Required After Test(s)
- **5.2.1.**: After each test, the cab must exhibit a survival space accommodating the manikin defined in Annex 3, Appendix 2, on the seat (in its median position) without contact with non-resilient parts (Shore-Hardness ≥ 50). Non-resilient parts movable with <100 N force are disregarded. As an alternative, a 50th percentile Hybrid II or III male dummy (as per UN R094) may be used.
- **5.2.2.**: This space must be verified for every manufacturer-provided seat.

### 5.3. Other Conditions
- **5.3.1.**: Cab securing components may distort or break, provided the cab remains attached to the chassis.
- **5.3.2.**: Doors shall not open during tests but are not required to open afterward.

## 6. Modification and Extension of Approval of the Vehicle Type
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing. Extensions are communicated to other Parties.

## 7. Conformity of Production
Production must conform to the approved type. The Competent Authority verifies conformity control methods at least once every two years.

## 8. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if production does not comply. Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 9. Production Definitely Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the granting authority upon ceasing production, which then notifies other Parties.

## 10. Transitional Provisions
Outlines the applicability dates for granting and recognizing approvals under the 02 and 03 series of amendments.

## 11. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: ECE Type-approval documentation (Part 1: Model information document; Part 2: Communication form).
- **Annex 2**: Arrangements of approval marks.
- **Annex 3**: Test procedure (including Appendices 1-2).
    - **Test A (Frontal Impact)**: Impactor mass ≥1500 kg, energy 29.4 kJ (N1, N2 ≤7.5t) or 55 kJ (N3, N2 >7.5t).
    - **Test B (Front Pillar Impact)**: Cylindrical impactor mass ≥1000 kg, energy 29.4 kJ.
    - **Test C (Roof Strength)**:
 - **Dynamic pre-load** (for N3, N2 >7.5t): Impactor mass ≥1500 kg, energy ≥17.6 kJ at 20° angle.
 - **Static load**: Corresponds to max authorized front axle mass (max 98 kN).
- **Annex 4**: Procedure for determining the "H" point and actual torso angle.
- **Annex 5**: Reference data concerning seating positions.
