**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of adaptive front-lighting systems (AFS) for motor vehicles**

**Addendum 122 – UN Regulation No. 123**
**Revision 2 – Amendment 7**
**Supplement 1 to the 02 series of amendments – Date of entry into force: 29 May 2020**

This document is meant purely as documentation tool. The authentic and legal binding text is: ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2019/92.

---

**Amendment Text:**

Add a new paragraph 5.16., to read:
"5.16. Instead of requirements of this Regulation, headlamps may conform with requirements of the latest version of the UN Regulation No. 149 as it relates to the adaptive forward-illumination systems."
