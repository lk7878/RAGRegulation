- **UN Regulation No. 16** (Revision 10, Amendment 3) establishes uniform technical provisions for the type approval of safety-belts, various restraint systems (including child restraint systems, ISOFIX, and i-Size), and for vehicles equipped with these systems and safety-belt reminders.
- The amendment entered into force on **22 June 2022** and was issued on 28 July 2022.
- A key technical insertion in this amendment is a new paragraph **3.1.2.7.**, requiring the "Description of the safety-belt reminder system."
- The legal basis is the **1958 Agreement** (as revised), facilitating the mutual recognition of type approvals among UNECE contracting parties.
- The definitive legal text is referenced as document **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2021/113**.
