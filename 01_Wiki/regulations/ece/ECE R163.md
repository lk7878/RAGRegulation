## Key Definitions (from Section 2)
*   **Vehicle alarm system (VAS):** A system intended for installation on (a) type(s) of vehicle(s), designed to indicate intrusion into or interference with the vehicle; these systems may provide additional protection against unauthorized use of the vehicle.
*   **Set:** The state of a VAS in which an alarm condition can be transmitted to warning devices.
*   **Unset:** The state of a VAS in which an alarm condition cannot be transmitted to warning devices.
*   **Immobilizer:** A device which is intended to prevent the vehicle being driven away powered by its own engine.
*   **Panic alarm:** A device which enables a person to use an alarm, installed on the vehicle, to summon assistance in an emergency.

## Part I - Approval of Vehicle Alarm Systems (Key Requirements)
*   **General Specs (Section 5):** Must provide a warning signal (audible, optionally optical/radio) upon intrusion/interference. Must not affect vehicle safety or performance. Must not activate inadvertently. Must be designed to resist tampering.
*   **Protection Range (6.1):** Must at least detect opening of doors, bonnet, and luggage compartment. Additional sensors (e.g., for passenger compartment, glass breakage, inclination) are allowed but must include measures against false alarms.
*   **Audible Alarm (6.2):** Signal must be clearly audible and distinct from other traffic sounds. Duration: 25-30 seconds. Must comply with sound level requirements (typically per UN R28, Part I, with a minimum of 100 dB(A) for separate devices).
*   **Optical Alarm (6.3, if fitted):** Flashing of direction indicators and/or interior lights. Duration: 25 seconds to 5 minutes. Trigger frequency: (2 ± 1) Hz.
*   **Setting/Unsetting (6.6):** Unsetting must be via a mechanical key (≥1000 variants), an electrical/electronic device (≥50,000 variants with rolling codes/min. 10-day scan time), or a device inside the protected compartment with entry/exit delay.
*   **Delays (6.7, 6.8):** Exit delay (if set from inside): 15-45 seconds. Entry delay (if unset from inside): 5-15 seconds before alarm activation.
*   **Power Supply (6.10):** From vehicle battery or a dedicated rechargeable battery.
*   **Test Conditions (Section 7):** Includes tests for temperature resistance (-40°C to +125°C depending on location), vibration, durability (300 alarm cycles), electromagnetic compatibility (Annex 7), resistance to false alarms (impact, voltage drop), and passenger compartment protection (Annex 8).

## Part II - Approval of a Vehicle with Regard to its Alarm System
*   Applies to Alarm Systems (AS) fitted as original equipment.
*   Requirements for protection range, audible/optical alarms, setting/unsetting, delays, etc., are largely analogous to those in Part I for VAS (Sections 10, 11).
*   Test conditions (Section 12) refer to procedures in Section 7, with exemptions for components already tested as part of the vehicle or previously documented.

## Approval Marking (Section 4)
*   An international approval mark must be affixed, consisting of a circle containing "E" and the country code, followed by "163R", a dash, and the approval number.
*   Examples are given in Annex 3.

## Conformity of Production & Penalties (Sections 15-17)
*   Production must conform to the approved type.
*   Approval can be withdrawn for non-conformity.
*   Authorities must be notified if production is discontinued.
