**Regulation No. 97 - Amendment 1**

This document constitutes **Amendment 1** to **Revision 1** of **Addendum 96** (Regulation No. 97) under the UNECE 1958 Agreement. It is identified as **Supplement 5 to the 01 series of amendments**.

**Key Provision:**
A new paragraph **1.4** is added to the regulation. It states:
> "1.4. The fitting of devices specified under Parts II and III to vehicles of categories other than M1, or N1 with a maximum mass exceeding 2 tonnes, is optional, but any such device fitted is required to comply with all relevant provisions of this Regulation. Vehicles approved in accordance with the provisions of Parts III or IV of Regulation No. 116 are deemed to comply with Parts II and III respectively of this Regulation."

**Context:**
*   **Parent Agreement:** The overarching framework is the "Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Uniform Technical Prescriptions for Wheeled Vehicles..." (Revision 2, including amendments effective 16 October 1995).
*   **Former Title of Agreement:** Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Uniform Conditions of Approval and Reciprocal Recognition of Approval for Motor Vehicle Equipment and Parts (Geneva, 20 March 1958).
*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 18 June 2007.
*   **Document Date:** 8 August 2007.
