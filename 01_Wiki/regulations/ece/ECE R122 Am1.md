# Amendment 1 to UNECE Regulation No. 122

This document is **Amendment 1** to **UNECE Regulation No. 122**, concerning the approval of vehicles of categories M, N and O with regard to their heating systems. It supplements the original version of the regulation.

## Key Information
- **Date of Entry into Force:** 22 July 2009
- **Document Reference:** E/ECE/324 Rev.2/Add.121/Amend.1 / E/ECE/TRANS/505
- **Issued:** 30 October 2009

## Content of the Amendment
This amendment modifies **Annex 9** of Regulation No. 122.

### Changes to Paragraph 2. (Definitions)
The definition paragraph is amended to specify that vehicle designations **EX/II, EX/III, AT, FL, OX and MEMU** are as defined in **Chapter 9.1 of the ADR** (European Agreement concerning the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road).
It also states that vehicles approved as complying with requirements for **EX/III** vehicles under this annex are deemed to comply with the requirements for **MEMU** vehicles.

### Changes to Paragraph 3.1. (General)
The scope of paragraph 3.1. is amended to explicitly include **MEMU** vehicles, reading: "**3.1. General (EX/II, EX/III, AT, FL, OT and MEMU vehicles)**".

### Changes to Paragraph 3.2. Title
The title of paragraph 3.2. is amended to read: "**3.2. EX/II, EX/III and MEMU vehicles**".

## Context
This amendment is part of the framework established by the **Revised Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Uniform Technical Prescriptions for Wheeled Vehicles** (Revision 2).
