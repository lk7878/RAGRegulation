**Amendment 1 to UN Regulation No. 59**

This document constitutes Amendment 1 to the original UN Regulation No. 59, which concerns the approval of replacement silencing systems. The amendment entered into force on 28 January 1990.

**Key Changes:**

1.  **Paragraph 3.3.3.2. (Noise Limit):** Amended to specify that the sound level measured in a stationary condition must not exceed the reference value by more than 3 dB(A).

2.  **Paragraph 6.4. (Conditioning Procedures):** Amended to introduce new conditioning procedures for the silencer. The text is revised to state that the silencer shall be submitted to conventional conditioning using one of the three installations and procedures described.

3.  **New Paragraphs 6.4.1. and 6.4.2.:** Introduce two new conditioning methods:
    *   **6.4.1. Continuous road operation for 10,000 km:** Specifies that about half the operation should be town driving and half long-distance high-speed runs. The program must include at least 10 breaks of at least three hours to allow for cooling and condensation effects.
    *   **6.4.2. Conditioning on a test bench:** Details a procedure conducted over six six-hour periods with breaks of at least 12 hours between each. Each six-hour period consists of two sets of a specific engine load and speed sequence cycle (idling, 1/4 load at 3/4 max speed, 1/2 load at 3/4 max speed, full load at 3/4 max speed, 1/2 load at max speed, and 1/4 load at max speed). Forced cooling simulating normal airflow is generally not permitted unless requested by the manufacturer to prevent exceeding temperatures recorded during maximum speed operation.

4.  **Renumbering:** Following the addition of the new paragraphs 6.4.1. and 6.4.2., the original paragraphs 6.4.1.-6.4.1.1. are to be renumbered as 6.4.3.-6.4.3.1., and paragraphs 6.4.2.-6.4.2.8. are to be renumbered as 6.4.3.2.-6.4.3.2.8. The title of the new paragraph 6.4.3. is "Conditioning by pulsation".
