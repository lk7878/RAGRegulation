**Amendments**

**Annex 10**

*   **Paragraph 1.1.**
    *   Specifies that vehicles of categories M2, M3, N, O2, O3, and O4 must meet all requirements of this annex. Any special device used must operate automatically.
    *   **Exception for ABS-equipped vehicles:** Vehicles equipped with an anti-lock braking system (ABS) that fulfill Annex 13 requirements are exempt from:
 *   (a) Adhesion utilization requirements (diagrams 1A, 1B, or 1C).
 *   (b) For towing vehicles and trailers with compressed air braking systems, unladen compatibility requirements (diagrams 2, 3, or 4). However, a braking rate must be developed between 20 kPa and 100 kPa (or equivalent digital demand) at the coupling head for all load conditions.
    *   **Note 1:** For trailers with electronically controlled brake force distribution, the annex requirements apply only when electrically connected to the towing vehicle via an ISO 7638:2003 connector.

*   **Paragraph 1.3.1.**
    *   **Type Approval Check (Laden Vehicles):**
 *   At least one axle must begin developing braking force when coupling head pressure is between 20 and 100 kPa (or equivalent).
 *   At least one axle in every other axle group must begin developing braking force at a pressure < 120 kPa (or equivalent).
    *   **Type Approval Check (Unladen Vehicles):**
 *   At least one axle must begin developing braking force when coupling head pressure is between 20 and 100 kPa (or equivalent).

*   **Paragraph 6. (Requirements in case of braking distribution system failure)**
    *   If requirements are met via a special device (e.g., mechanically controlled by suspension), or if such a device is equipped, the following must be possible upon its control failure:
 *   For power-driven vehicles: Stop the vehicle under conditions specified for secondary braking.
 *   For power-driven vehicles authorized to tow trailers with compressed-air brakes: Achieve a pressure at the control line coupling head within the range specified in paragraph 3.1.3. of this annex.
 *   For trailers: Attain a service braking performance of at least 30% of the prescribed performance.

*   **Paragraphs 7. and 7.1. (Markings)**
    *   Vehicles meeting annex requirements via a suspension-controlled mechanical device (or equipped with one) must be marked to show:
 *   The useful travel of the device between unladen and laden states.
 *   Any further information needed to check the device setting.

**Annex 13**

*   **Paragraph 1.1.**
    *   This annex defines the required braking performance for road vehicles equipped with an anti-lock braking system(s).
