# UN Regulation No. 138 (Revision 2, Addendum 137)

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of Quiet Road Transport Vehicles with regard to their reduced audibility (QRTV)**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to electrified vehicles of categories M and N which can travel in the normal mode, in reverse or at least one forward drive gear, without an internal combustion engine operating, in respect to their audibility.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Approval of a vehicle**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to sound.
*   **Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System (AVAS)**: A component or set of components installed in vehicles with the primary purpose to fulfil the requirements of this Regulation.
*   **Vehicle type**: A category of motor vehicles which does not differ essentially in specified respects affecting sound emission.
*   **Frequency Shift**: The variation of the frequency content of the AVAS sound as a function of the vehicle speed.
*   **Electrified vehicle**: A vehicle with a powertrain containing at least one electric motor or electric motor-generator. Subcategories include Pure Electric Vehicle (PEV), Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV), Fuel Cell Vehicle (FCV), and Fuel Cell Hybrid Vehicle (FCHV).
*   **Mass in running order**: Defined mass including driver, fuel, standard equipment, etc.
*   **Pause function**: A mechanism to halt temporarily the operation of an AVAS.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application shall be submitted by the manufacturer or accredited representative, accompanied by required documents describing the vehicle type, engine(s), and AVAS components (if applicable).

## 4. Markings
Components of the AVAS (if applicable) shall bear the trade name/mark of the manufacturer and a designated identification number, clearly legible and indelible.

## 5. Approval
*   Approval is granted if the vehicle type meets specified requirements.
*   An approval number is assigned.
*   An international approval mark (a circle surrounding "E" followed by the country distinguishing number, the Regulation number "138R", and the approval number) must be affixed to every conforming vehicle.

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. General Specifications
The vehicle must fulfil the acoustic requirements.

### 6.2. Acoustics Characteristics
The sound emitted is measured per **Annex 3**. The operational speed range is >0 km/h up to and including 20 km/h.

#### 6.2.1. Constant Speed Tests (10 km/h and 20 km/h)
The vehicle must emit a sound that:
(a) Has a minimum overall sound pressure level (see Table 2).
(b) Has at least two of the specified one-third octave bands (at least one ≤1600 Hz).
(c) Meets minimum sound pressure levels in the chosen bands (see Table 2).

#### 6.2.2. Reversing Test
The vehicle must emit a sound with a minimum overall sound pressure level (see Table 2, column 5).

#### 6.2.3. Frequency Shift
At least one tone within the specified frequency range must vary proportionally with speed by an average of at least **0.8% per 1 km/h** in the speed range from 5 km/h to 20 km/h when driving forward.

#### 6.2.4. – 6.2.6. Other Provisions
Covers stationary sound, driver-selectable sounds (must comply), and an optional pause function for AVAS with specific conditions and warnings.

#### 6.2.7. Maximum Sound Level for AVAS
A vehicle equipped with an AVAS shall not emit an overall sound level of more than **75 dB(A)** when driving forward (measured at 2m).

## 7. Modification and Extension of Approval
Any modification must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Production must conform to the approved type and satisfy the requirements.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the authority if production ceases.

## 11. Transitional Provisions
Until 30 June 2019, ISO 10844:1994 could be used as an alternative to ISO 10844:2014 for test track compliance.

## 12. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form and Addendum (Technical Information document) for approval notifications.
*   **Annex 2**: Examples of arrangements of the approval mark.
*   **Annex 3**: **Methods and instruments for measuring the sound made by motor vehicles**. Contains detailed specifications for:
    *   Instrumentation (sound level meters, calibrators, speed measurement).
    *   Test site (outdoor per ISO 10844:2014, or qualified indoor hemi-anechoic/anechoic chamber).
    *   Meteorological conditions and background noise requirements/corrections.
    *   Test procedures for vehicle sound level (constant speed, reversing) including microphone positions, vehicle conditions, and data compilation.
    *   Test procedures for frequency shift, with five possible test methods (A-E).
    *   Appendix with figures and flowcharts illustrating test setups and procedures.
