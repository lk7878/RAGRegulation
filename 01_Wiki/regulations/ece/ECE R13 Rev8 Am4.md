**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles of categories M, N and O with regard to braking**

**Document Information**
*   **Document Reference:** E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.12/Rev.8/Amend.4 / E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.1/Add.12/Rev.8/Amend.4
*   **Authentic Legal Text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2016/49
*   **Date of Document:** 22 February 2017
*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 9 February 2017
*   **Regulatory Context:** Supplement 14 to the 11 series of amendments to Regulation No. 13 (Revision 8, Amendment 4), under the 1958 Agreement.

**Amendment Details**

This document contains specific amendments to Annex 12 and Appendix 4 of Regulation No. 13.

**Annex 12 Amendments:**

1.  **Paragraph 2.2.18.** is amended to define `s'` (effective/useful travel of control in millimetres) as determined by paragraph 10.4. of the annex.
2.  **Paragraph 10.4.2.3.** is amended to specify the calculation for hydraulic braking systems: `s’ = s – s”`.

**Appendix 4 Amendments:**

1.  **Paragraph 5.8.3.** is amended to provide a formula for `s'` and stipulates it shall not be greater than the travel of the master cylinder actuator as specified in paragraph 8.2. of Appendix 2.
2.  **Paragraph 5.7.6.** is amended to define the braking torque when a trailer moves rearward (including rolling resistance) and sets a maximum limit (`shall not be greater than: 0.08•g•G •R`).
3.  **Paragraph 5.8.6.** is amended with identical content to paragraph 5.7.6., defining the braking torque when a trailer moves rearward (including rolling resistance) with the same maximum limit.
