# UN Regulation No. 145

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to:
(a) Vehicles of category M1 with regard to their ISOFIX anchorage systems and their ISOFIX top tether anchorages intended for child restraint systems. Other categories of vehicles fitted with ISOFIX anchorages must also comply.
(b) Vehicles of any category with regard to their i-Size seating positions, if any are defined by the vehicle manufacturer.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Approval of a vehicle**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to ISOFIX anchorage systems, ISOFIX top tether anchorages, and i-Size seating positions.
*   **ISOFIX**: A system for connecting child restraint systems (CRS) to vehicles using two rigid vehicle anchorages, two corresponding rigid attachments on the CRS, and a means to limit pitch rotation.
*   **ISOFIX anchorage system**: A system of two ISOFIX low anchorages designed for attaching an ISOFIX CRS in conjunction with an anti-rotation device.
*   **ISOFIX top tether anchorage**: A feature (e.g., a bar) located in a defined zone, designed to accept a top tether strap connector and transfer restraint force.
*   **i-Size seating position**: A seating position, if defined by the manufacturer, designed to accommodate i-Size CRS and fulfilling this Regulation's requirements.
*   **Static force application device (SFAD)**: A test fixture used to verify the strength of ISOFIX anchorages and vehicle floor (for i-Size).
*   **Vehicle floor contact surface**: The area of the vehicle floor that intersects with the support leg foot assessment volume and is designed to withstand support leg forces from an i-Size CRS.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application, submitted by the manufacturer or representative, must include drawings, material specifications, technical descriptions of the anchorages and, if applicable, the vehicle floor contact surface. Representative vehicle parts may be submitted for testing.

## 4. Approval
*   Approval is granted if the vehicle meets the Regulation's requirements.
*   An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments applicable at the time of approval.
*   An international approval mark (a circle containing "E", the country code, and the Regulation number) must be affixed to conforming vehicles.
*   Communication of approval, extension, refusal, or withdrawal is done via a form conforming to Annex 1.

## 5. Specifications
### 5.1. General Specifications
*   ISOFIX anchorages, top tether anchorages, and i-Size vehicle floor contact surfaces must be designed to enable the vehicle to comply with this Regulation under normal use.
*   They are designed for ISOFIX CRS of mass groups 0, 0+, 1 (UN R44) and for i-Size CRS of integral class (UN R129).

### 5.2. ISOFIX Anchorage Systems: Design and Positioning
*   Each ISOFIX low anchorage is a 6 mm ± 0.1 mm diameter rigid horizontal bar, with a minimum effective length of 25 mm.
*   The system must be located at least 120 mm behind the design H-point.
*   It must be possible to attach the child restraint fixture "ISO/F2" or "ISO/F2X" (as defined in UN R16). i-Size positions must also accommodate "ISO/R2" with support leg volume and "ISO/B2".
*   The fixture's bottom surface must have specific pitch, roll, and yaw angle limits when installed.
*   Anchorages must be permanently in position or storable (requirements apply when deployed).
*   Each low anchorage bar or guidance device must be visible from a specified angle or be permanently marked with the ISOFIX symbol (Annex 4, Figure 12) or the word "ISOFIX".

### 5.3. ISOFIX Top Tether Anchorages: Design and Positioning
*   The anchorage point must be located within a defined zone relative to the seating position, using either a template based on SAE J826 (method 1, Annex 4 Figures 6-10) or relative to the "ISO/F2" fixture (method 2, Annex 4 Figure 11).
*   It may be located outside the zone if a routing device is used that ensures equivalent function and strength.
*   It must permit attachment of a standard ISOFIX top tether hook (Annex 4, Figure 3) and allow latching/unlatching.
*   Measures must be taken to prevent misuse of other anchorages in the zone (e.g., design all as top tether anchorages, mark only top tether anchorages, or mark others as not for ISOFIX use).
*   Covers over top tether anchorages must be identifiable (e.g., with symbol from Annex 4, Figure 13) and removable without tools.

### 5.4. i-Size Seating Position Requirements
*   Must be permanently marked adjacent to the ISOFIX low anchorages with the i-Size symbol (a square containing a pictogram, Annex 5, Figure 4).
*   **Geometrical requirements**: The vehicle floor must intersect the entire support leg foot assessment volume (defined in Annex 5, Figures 1 & 2), ensuring a proper support leg contact area.
*   **Vehicle floor strength**: The entire vehicle floor contact surface must be of sufficient strength to withstand the loads specified in the test (paragraph 6.2.4.5).

### 5.5. Minimum Number of ISOFIX Positions
*   Category M1 vehicles must have at least two ISOFIX positions complying with this Regulation.
*   At least two positions must have both ISOFIX anchorages and a top tether anchorage.
*   At least one system must be in the second seat row.
*   Exceptions and reductions are specified for vehicles with single seat rows, specific performance cars (high power/mass ratio), vehicles with integrated child restraints, convertibles, ambulances/hearses, etc.
*   Mandatory ISOFIX positions may be replaced by i-Size seating positions.

## 6. Tests
### 6.1. Static Test Requirements
*   Tests are performed using a Static Force Application Device (SFAD, Annex 4, Figures 1 & 2).
*   The strength of ISOFIX anchorage systems, ISOFIX top tether anchorages, and i-Size vehicle floor contact surfaces is verified.
*   All ISOFIX/i-Size positions in a seat row that can be used simultaneously must be tested simultaneously.
*   Tests can be on a complete vehicle or representative parts.

### 6.2. Forces, Directions, and Excursion Limits
*   **Forward direction test**: A force of 8 kN ± 0.25 kN is applied at 0° ± 5° (initially 10°±5° above horizontal). Horizontal longitudinal excursion of point X on the SFAD must not exceed 125 mm.
*   **Oblique direction test**: A force of 5 kN ± 0.25 kN is applied at 75° ± 5° to either side of forward. Excursion in the force direction must not exceed 125 mm.
*   **Test with top tether**: A pre-load is applied. With the 8 kN forward force, horizontal excursion of point X must not exceed 125 mm.
*   **Test for i-Size seating positions**: A modified SFAD with a support leg test probe (SFAD_SL, Annex 5, Figure 3) is used. With the 8 kN forward force, horizontal excursion of point X must not exceed 125 mm. The vehicle floor contact surface must withstand the load.
*   **Seat inertia forces**: An additional test is required if loads are transferred into a seat assembly. A force equal to 20 times the mass of relevant seat parts is applied forward. No breakage is allowed, and excursion limits must be met. Exemption exists if the seat is already approved for adult safety-belt anchorage loads (UN R14).

## 7. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Modifications to the approved vehicle type must be notified. The Type Approval Authority may require further testing. Extensions of approval are communicated to other parties.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Vehicles bearing the approval mark must conform to the approved type. Conformity is verified through random checks and, if necessary, tests.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if production does not conform or fails checks. Withdrawal must be notified to other contracting parties.

## 10. Production Definitely Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the granting authority upon ceasing production, who then notifies other parties.

## 11. Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval notifications.
*   **Annex 2**: Examples of the arrangement of the approval mark.
*   **Annex 3**: Procedure for determining the "H" point and actual torso angle (references R.E.3).
*   **Annex 4**: Detailed specifications and figures for ISOFIX anchorages systems and top tether anchorages (including SFAD design, zone definitions, symbols).
*   **Annex 5**: Detailed specifications and figures for i-Size seating positions (including support leg foot assessment volume, vehicle floor contact surface, test device, symbol).
