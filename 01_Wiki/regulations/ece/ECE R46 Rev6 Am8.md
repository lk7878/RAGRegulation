# UN Regulation No. 46 - Amendment 8 (05 series of amendments)

**Document:** E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.45/Rev.6/Amend.8 / E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.1/Add.45/Rev.6/Amend.8
**Date of Entry into Force:** 8 October 2022
**Issued:** 14 November 2022
**Authentic Legal Text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2022/52

This document constitutes **Amendment 8** to the **05 series of amendments** of **UN Regulation No. 46 (Revision 6)**.

## Summary of Key Amendments

This amendment introduces modifications and additions to the uniform provisions for the approval of indirect vision devices (e.g., mirrors, camera-monitor systems) and the approval of motor vehicles regarding the installation of such devices.

## Detailed Amendments

### 1. New Paragraph 6.1.1.9. (Mirror Height Exemption)
*   The requirements of paragraph 6.1.1.3. do not apply to mirrors if their lower edge is mounted **not less than 2 m above the ground** when the vehicle is loaded to its maximum technical permissible mass.

### 2. Amendment to Paragraph 6.2.1.3. (CMS Electromagnetic Compatibility)
*   The effectiveness of CMS (Classes I-VI) must not be adversely affected by magnetic or electrical fields.
*   Compliance must be demonstrated by meeting the technical requirements and transitional provisions of **UN Regulation No. 10, 04 series of amendments or later**.

### 3. Amendments to Paragraphs 6.2.2.1.1. to 6.2.2.1.5. (CMS External Projections and Radius of Curvature)
*   **6.2.2.1.1.:** Specifies radius of curvature requirements for parts in potential static contact with a sphere (165 mm diameter for interior CMS parts, 100 mm for exterior CMS parts). The radius "c" must be not less than 2.5 mm.
*   **6.2.2.1.2.:** Exempts parts protruding less than 5 mm, provided outward-facing angles are blunted. Parts protruding less than 1.5 mm are considered safe. Defines a method for determining projection dimensions.
*   **New 6.2.2.1.2.1.:** Method for determining projection dimension on a convex surface.
*   **New 6.2.2.1.2.2.:** Method for determining projection dimension on non-convex surfaces using a 100 mm diameter sphere (see Figure 1).
*   **6.2.2.1.3.:** Exempts edges of small fixing holes/recesses (<12 mm diameter/longest diagonal) from radius requirements if blunted.
*   **New 6.2.2.1.4.:** For soft parts (Shore A hardness <60) on a rigid support, radius requirements apply only to the support.
*   **New 6.2.2.1.5.:** The radius requirements of 6.2.2.1.1. do not apply to CMS if their lower edge is mounted **not less than 2 m above the ground** under maximum load conditions.

### 4. Amendment to Paragraph 6.3.1.1. (Test Exemption for High-Mounted Devices)
*   The impact test (6.3.2.) is not required for Class II-VI exterior indirect vision devices where **no part is less than 2 m from the ground** under maximum load, regardless of adjustment.
*   This derogation also applies to attachments (plates, arms, etc.) less than 2 m high if they do not project beyond the vehicle's overall width measured at a specific transverse plane.
*   A mounting description must be provided.
*   If this derogation is used, the arm must be indelibly marked with the symbol "**△ 2m**" and the type approval certificate must be endorsed accordingly.

### 5. Amendment to Paragraph 16.1.1. (CMS Intended Use, Activation, and Deactivation)
*   The intended use must be in the operator's manual. Activation/deactivation procedures for Class II and III CMS must allow safe vehicle use.
*   CMS must activate when the vehicle is opened (e.g., door unlock).
*   After engine switch-off, the system must remain operational for at least **T1 = 120 s**. For a subsequent period **T2 = (420 - T1) seconds**, it must be reactivatable to provide the required field of vision within 1 second by manoeuvring any front door. After T2, it must be reactivatable within 7 seconds.
*   Alternative concepts providing at least the same safety level may be demonstrated to the Technical Service and Approval Authority via a safety concept per Annex 12, paragraph 2.

### 6. New Paragraphs 22.18. to 22.25. (Transitional Provisions)
*   **22.18.:** From the entry into force of the 05 series, Contracting Parties shall not refuse approvals under this amended version.
*   **22.19.:** From **1 September 2024**, Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept approvals to preceding series first issued **after** this date.
*   **22.20.:** Until **1 September 2026**, Contracting Parties shall accept approvals to preceding series first issued **before 1 September 2024**.
*   **22.21.:** From **1 September 2026**, Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept approvals to preceding series.
*   **22.22.:** Notwithstanding 22.21., approvals for indirect vision devices **other than CMS** issued to preceding series shall continue to be accepted.
*   **22.23.:** Notwithstanding 22.21., approvals for vehicles and CMS **not affected by the 05 series changes** issued to preceding series shall continue to be accepted.
*   **22.24.:** Contracting Parties may grant approvals according to any preceding series.
*   **22.25.:** Contracting Parties shall continue to grant extensions of existing approvals to any preceding series.

### 7. Amendment to Annex 5 (Approval Mark Arrangement)
*   Updates the description and example of the approval mark for an indirect vision device.
*   The example shows a Class II main rear-view device approved in the Netherlands (E4) under approval number 052439, indicating the 05 series was included at the time of approval.
*   Specifies placement rules for the approval number and additional symbols relative to the circle and "E".
