# Regulation No. 43

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of safety glazing materials and their installation on vehicles**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to:
(a) Safety glazing materials intended for installation as windscreens or other panes, or as partitioning, on vehicles of category L with bodywork, M, N, O, and T;
(b) Vehicles of categories M, N and O with regard to the installation of these materials;

In both cases, to the exclusion of:
(i) Glazing for lighting and light-signalling devices, instrument panels, special bullet-proof glazing and double-windows;
(ii) Small plastic glazing that is not required for either the driver's forward or rearward field of vision.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **2.1. "Toughened-glass"**: Single layer of glass subjected to special treatment to increase mechanical strength and condition fragmentation.
*   **2.2. "Laminated-glass"**: Two or more layers of glass held together by plastic interlayer(s). Can be ordinary or treated.
*   **2.5. "Glass-plastics"**: Glazing comprising one layer of glass and one or more layers of plastic, with a plastic surface facing inward.
*   **2.6. "Plastic glazing"**: Material containing organic polymeric substances. Subcategories include rigid, flexible, laminated-rigid plastic panes and windscreens.
*   **2.13. "Windscreen"**: Glazing in front of the driver for viewing the road ahead.
*   **2.21. "Type of safety glazing material"**: Glazing not exhibiting essential differences in principal and secondary characteristics.
*   **2.37. "Type of vehicle"** (regarding installation): Vehicles of the same category not differing in manufacturer, type designation, and essential construction/design aspects.

## 3. Application for approval
*   **3.1.-3.3.**: Application for glazing type approval by manufacturer. Requires technical description, diagrams/drawings (including windscreen positioning data), and submission of test pieces.
*   **3.4.-3.6.**: Application for vehicle type approval regarding glazing installation by vehicle manufacturer. Requires drawings showing glazing position and technical details of glazing materials, plus submission of a representative vehicle.

## 4. Markings
Every piece of safety glazing material must bear a clear, indelible trade name/mark of the manufacturer and the ECE Regulation No. 43 number allocated to the prime manufacturer.

## 5. Approval
*   **5.1.-5.7.**: **Component (Glazing) Approval**: Granted if samples meet requirements. An approval number is assigned. An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, regulation number "43R", and approval number) must be affixed. Additional symbols denote glazing type (e.g., I for toughened glass windscreen, V for light transmittance <70%, VIII for rigid plastic glazing with application code /A, /B, /C).
*   **5.8.-5.15.**: **Vehicle Approval**: Granted if vehicle meets installation requirements of Annex 24. An international approval mark must be affixed to the vehicle.

## 6. General requirements
Glazing materials must minimize injury risk upon shattering, be resistant to normal traffic incidents, environmental conditions, and abrasion. They must be sufficiently transparent, not cause noticeable distortion, and allow adequate visibility for safe stopping if the windscreen shatters.

## 7. Particular requirements
All safety glazing types must comply with specific requirements detailed in respective annexes (e.g., Annex 4 for toughened-glass windscreens, Annex 14 for rigid plastic panes).

## 8. Tests
Prescribed tests include:
*   **8.1.1. Fragmentation test**: To verify fragments minimize injury risk and check residual visibility (for windscreens).
*   **8.1.2. Mechanical strength tests**: Ball-impact test (227g and 2260g) and Headform test.
*   **8.1.3. Test of resistance to the environment**: Abrasion, high temperature, radiation, humidity, temperature changes, simulated weathering, and cross-cut tests.
*   **8.1.4. Optical qualities**: Light transmission, optical distortion, and secondary-image-separation tests.
*   **8.1.5. Burning-behaviour (fire-resistance) test**.
*   **8.1.6. Test of resistance to chemicals**.
*   **8.1.7. Flexibility and fold test** (for plastic glazing).
*   **8.2.**: Tables specify which tests apply to different glazing material types (glass-based and plastic-based).

## 9. Modification or extension of approval of a type of safety glazing material
Modifications or additions (e.g., new windscreen to a group) must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing.

## 10. Conformity of production
Production must conform to the Agreement's procedures, including checks per Annex 23. Normal inspection frequency is once per year.

## 11. Penalties for non-conformity of production
Approval may be withdrawn for non-conformity, with notification to other Contracting Parties.

## 12. Transitional provisions
Outlines timelines for the application of amendments (e.g., Supplement 12, 01 series of amendments) and the validity of approvals granted under previous series.

## 13. Production definitively discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority upon ceasing production of an approved type.

## 14. Names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat. Technical Services must comply with harmonized standards (ISO/IEC Guide 25).

## Annexes (List)
The regulation contains numerous annexes detailing communication forms, approval mark arrangements, test procedures, and specific requirements for each glazing type.
*   **Annex 1 & Appendices 1-13**: Communication forms and details for each glazing type.
*   **Annex 1A**: Communication form for vehicle approval.
*   **Annex 2 & 2A**: Examples of approval marks for components and vehicles.
*   **Annex 3**: General test conditions (fragmentation, ball-impact, headform, abrasion, etc.).
*   **Annexes 4-12, 14-19**: Particular requirements and tests for specific glazing types (e.g., toughened-glass windscreens, laminated-glass panes, rigid plastic panes).
*   **Annexes 20-24**: Procedures for measurement, determining test areas, H-point, conformity of production checks, and installation provisions.
