**Amendment Details**

This document constitutes **Amendment 2** to **Revision 3** of **UN Regulation No. 112**. It is an addendum to the 1958 Agreement concerning the adoption of uniform technical prescriptions for wheeled vehicles.

**Technical Amendment**

This amendment modifies **Annex 7, Paragraph 4** of Regulation No. 112, which deals with the "Change of the vertical position of the cut-off line" for headlamps under thermal influence.

The revised text specifies the test procedure and acceptance criteria for the vertical displacement (Δr) of the headlamp's cut-off line after being subjected to heat cycles. The procedure involves testing samples from two groups (Sample A and Sample B) under defined conditions.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1.  A headlamp is acceptable if Δr does not exceed **1.5 mrad upwards** and does not exceed **2.5 mrad downwards**.
2.  If the displacement from the first headlamp tested (from Sample A) is between 1.5-2.0 mrad upwards or 2.5-3.0 mrad downwards, the second headlamp from Sample A must also be tested. The **mean of the absolute values** from both samples must then meet the original criteria (≤1.5 mrad up, ≤2.5 mrad down).
3.  If the criteria are not met with Sample A, both headlamps from Sample B are tested. **Each** of them must individually meet the original displacement limits.
