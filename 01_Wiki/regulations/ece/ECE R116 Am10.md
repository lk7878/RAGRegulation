**Amendment 10 to UN Regulation No. 116**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 8 October 2022.

**Amended Paragraphs:**

1.  **Paragraph 5.3.1.1.** is amended to read:
    > "5.3.1.1. A device to prevent unauthorized use acting on the steering shall render the steering inoperative. Before the engine as a motive power can be started, the normal steering operation must be restored."

2.  **Paragraph 8.3.1.1.** is amended to read:
    > "8.3.1.1. An immobilizer shall be designed so as to prevent the operation of the vehicle under its motive power by at least one of the following means: …"
