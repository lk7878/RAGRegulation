**Amendment 2 to Regulation No. 28**

**Date of entry into force:** 8 January 1991

**Incorporates:**
*   Supplement 2 to the original version (00) of this Regulation.
*   Corrigendum 1 to Supplement 2 to 00 (referenced in depositary notification C.N.95.1992.TREATIES-10 of 16 June 1992).

**Amendments:**

**Paragraph 1 – SCOPE**
Amended to read:
"1. SCOPE
This Regulation applies to:
1.1. audible warning devices (AWD) supplied with direct or alternating current or compressed air, which are intended for fitting to motor vehicles of categories L3 to 5, M and N, excluding mopeds (categories L1 and L2).
1.2. the audible signals of motor vehicles listed in 1.1.

*Note 1:* An AWD consisting of several sound outlets activated by a single power unit shall be regarded as an AWD.
*Note 2:* As defined in the Consolidated Resolution (R.E.3) (TRANS/SC1/WP29/78).
*Note 3:* An AWD consisting of several units, each emitting a sound signal and operated simultaneously by the actuation of a single control shall be regarded as an audible warning system."

**Paragraph 2 – DEFINITIONS**
*   Amends the term "... audible warning devices (AWD) of ...".
*   Adds a new paragraph 2.3: "type of electrical supply (direct or alternating current);" and renumbers subsequent paragraphs.
*   Adds a new paragraph 2.10:
    "2.10. The AWD is principally intended for:
    2.10.1. motor cycles of a power less than or equal to 7 kW (class I)
    2.10.2. vehicles of categories M and N and motor cycles of a power greater than 7 kW (class II)"

**Paragraph 3 – APPLICATION FOR APPROVAL**
Adds a new paragraph 3.4:
"3.4. The competent authority shall verify the existence of satisfactory arrangements for ensuring effective control of the conformity of production before type approval is granted."

**Paragraph 5 – APPROVAL**
*   Paragraph 5.3 amended to read: "... rated voltage, rated frequency or frequencies ..."
*   Paragraph 5.4 amended to read: "Notice of approval or refusal or extension or withdrawal of approval or production definitely discontinued of a type ..."
*   Adds a new paragraph 5.5.3: "an additional symbol in the form of a figure in Roman numerals, showing the class to which the AWD belongs."
*   Paragraph 5.6 amended to read: "The approval mark and the additional symbol must be clearly legible and indelible."

**Paragraph 6 – REQUIREMENTS**
*   **Paragraph 6.1.1:** Adds: "For warning devices supplied with alternating current, this requirement shall apply only at constant generator speed, within the range specified in paragraph 6.2.3.2."
*   **Paragraph 6.2.3 – Test conditions:** Amended to specify voltage supply conditions for both direct and alternating current AWDs. For AC AWDs, acoustic characteristics must be recorded at generator speeds of 50%, 75%, and 100% of the maximum continuous operation speed. Endurance test speed is selected by the manufacturer from this range.
*   **Paragraph 6.2.4:** Amended regarding the use of a rectified current source for testing DC AWDs.
*   **Paragraph 6.2.5:** Amended for AWDs supplied with direct current.
*   **Paragraph 6.2.7 – Sound-pressure level limits:** Amended with specific limits for two classes of AWDs:
    *   (a) 115 dB(A) for AWDs principally for motorcycles with power ≤ 7 kW.
    *   (b) 118 dB(A) for AWDs principally for categories M, N vehicles and motorcycles with power > 7 kW.
    *   **Paragraph 6.2.7.1:** Adds frequency band (1,800 to 3,550 Hz) sound-pressure level requirements:
 *   (a) ≥ 95 dB(A) for class (a) AWDs.
 *   (b) ≥ 105 dB(A) for class (b) AWDs.
    *   **Paragraph 6.2.7.2:** AWDs meeting the (b) characteristics may be used on vehicles mentioned in (a).
*   **Paragraph 6.2.8:** Amended to specify voltage/speed variation ranges for testing (115%-95% of rated voltage for DC; 50%-100% of max generator speed for AC).
*   **Paragraph 6.3.1 – Endurance test:** Specifies different operation cycles for the two classes:
    *   10,000 times for AWDs principally for motorcycles with power ≤ 7 kW.
    *   50,000 times for AWDs principally for categories M, N vehicles and motorcycles with power > 7 kW.
    Each cycle: 1 second on, 4 seconds off. Test conducted with an air current of ~10 m/sec.
*   **Paragraph 6.3.4:** Amended regarding adjustment of the AWD during and after the endurance test.

**Paragraph 7 – MODIFICATIONS OF THE TYPE OF AWD AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL**
*   Title amended to include "AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL".
*   Adds a new paragraph 7.3: "The competent authority issuing the extension of approval shall assign a series number to each communication form drawn up for such an extension."

**Paragraph 8 – CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION**
Comprehensively amended to detail requirements for the approval holder and the competent authority, including:
*   8.1: Requirement for manufactured AWDs to conform to the approved type.
*   8.2: Requirement for suitable production controls.
*   8.3: Specific obligations of the approval holder (quality control procedures, access to equipment, record-keeping, analysis, sufficient checks, and corrective actions for non-conformity).
*   8.4: Authority of the competent authority to verify control methods, inspect records, take samples for testing, and carry out prescribed tests. Specifies a normal inspection frequency of once per year and actions for non-conformity.

**Paragraph 13 – APPROVAL**
*   Paragraph 13.3 amended to include "extension or withdrawal of approval or production definitely discontinued".
*   Adds a new paragraph 13.9 (mirroring 3.4 for vehicles): "The competent authority shall verify the existence of satisfactory arrangements for ensuring effective control of the conformity of production before type approval is granted."

**Paragraph 14 – REQUIREMENTS FOR VEHICLES**
*   **Paragraph 14.1:** Adds a transitional provision: "Audible warning devices of Class II approved under this Regulation in its original form, and therefore not bearing the symbol II in their approval mark, may continue to be fitted to vehicle types submitted for approval pursuant to this Regulation."
*   **Paragraph 14.4:** Amended regarding test surface and, for DC devices, engine status.
*   **Paragraph 14.8 – Sound-pressure level for vehicle audible signals:** Amended with specific limits:
    *   (a) 83 to 112 dB(A) for motorcycles with power ≤ 7 kW.
    *   (b) 93 to 112 dB(A) for vehicles of categories M and N and motorcycles with power > 7 kW.

**Paragraph 15 – MODIFICATIONS OF VEHICLE TYPE AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL**
*   Title amended.
*   Adds a new paragraph 15.3 (mirroring 7.3): "The competent authority issuing the extension of approval shall assign a series number to each communication form drawn up for such an extension."

**Paragraph 16 – CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION (FOR VEHICLES)**
Comprehensively amended with requirements parallel to those in Paragraph 8, but specific to vehicle production, covering:
*   Conformity to the approved type (16.1).
*   Appropriate production checks (16.2).
*   Obligations of the approval holder regarding quality control, sufficient checks on AWD number/type, and corrective actions (16.3).
*   Authority of the competent authority to verify control methods and carry out random checks (16.4).
*   Actions required if unsatisfactory results are found (16.5).

**Annexes 1, 2 and 3**
*   **Annexes 1 & 2 (Communication forms):** Under "Communication concerning" insert "extension of approval." Add "Extension No." on line with "Approval No." Amend the last paragraph regarding the annexed list of documents.
*   **Annex 3 (Approval mark):** Inserts symbol "I" above the circle and amends text to read "... this AWD of Class I has ...".
