# UN Regulation No. 96 (Revision 3 - Amendment 2)

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to all engines falling within the categories set out in paragraph 1.1 which are installed in or are intended to be installed in category T vehicles and non-road mobile machinery.

### 1.1 Engine Categories
For the purposes of this Regulation, the following engine categories, divided into the sub-categories set out in Appendix 1, apply:
*   **Category NRE**: Engines for category T vehicles and non-road mobile machinery intended and suited to move, or to be moved, by road or otherwise, and are not included in any other category.
*   **Category NRG**: Engines having a reference power > 560 kW, exclusively for use in generating sets.
*   **Category NRSh**: Hand-held Spark Ignition (SI) engines having a reference power < 19 kW, exclusively for use in hand-held machinery.
*   **Category NRS**: SI engines for category T vehicles and non-road mobile machinery having a reference power < 56 kW and not included in category NRSh.
*   **Category SMB**: SI engines exclusively for use in snowmobiles.
*   **Category ATS**: SI engines exclusively for use in All-terrain vehicles (ATVs) and Side-by-Side (SbS) that are either category T vehicles or non-road mobile machinery.

## 2. Definitions and Abbreviations
Provides comprehensive definitions for terms used throughout the Regulation, including but not limited to:
*   Adjustable parameter, Auxiliary emission control strategy (AECS), Base emission control strategy (BECS)
*   Compression ignition (CI) engine, Constant-speed engine, Variable-speed engine
*   Defeat strategy, Deterioration factors, Dual-fuel engine
*   Emission control system, Emission durability period (EDP), Engine family, Engine type
*   Gaseous pollutants, Particulate matter (PM), Particle number (PN)
*   Non-road mobile machinery, Original equipment manufacturer (OEM), Parent engine
*   Regeneration, Spark ignition (SI) engine, Tampering, Type approval

## 3. Application for Approval
Specifies the procedure and documentation required from the manufacturer or accredited representative for applying for approval of an engine type or engine family.

## 4. Approval
Outlines the conditions for granting approval, the format of the approval mark, and the requirements for marking engines.

## 5. Requirements and Tests
### 5.1 General
Engines must be designed to comply with emission limits throughout the emission durability period under normal conditions of use.
*   **5.1.1** Final emission test results must not exceed the exhaust emission limits in Appendix 2.
*   **5.1.2** Final results are calculated by applying crankcase emissions, adjustment factors for infrequent regeneration, and deterioration factors.
*   **5.1.3** Testing is carried out using specified reference fuels (diesel, petrol, natural gas/bio methane, LPG, ethanol, etc.).
*   **5.4** The use of defeat strategies is prohibited.
*   **5.5** Engines must be designed to prevent tampering as far as possible.
*   **5.6** Specifies requirements for testing within a defined "control area" around the non-road steady-state test cycle for electronically controlled NRE and NRG engines.
*   **5.7** Crankcase emissions must be added to exhaust emissions if discharged to the atmosphere.

## 6. Installation on the Vehicle
*   **6.1** Manufacturers must provide OEMs and end-users with correct installation and usage information.
*   **6.2** OEMs must install engines according to manufacturer instructions. If modifications adversely affect emissions, the OEM is considered the manufacturer for regulatory purposes.

## 7. Engine Family and Engine Type
Defines parameters for grouping engines into families and selecting a parent engine for testing, as detailed in Annex 10.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Describes the quality management and audit procedures to ensure ongoing production conformity, including initial assessment and product conformity arrangements. Details test procedures for verifying conformity in cases of non-compliance.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or engines fail production conformity tests.

## 10. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Procedures for revising or extending an approval when changes are made to the engine type or family.

## 11. Production Definitively Discontinued
Manufacturer must inform the approval authority if production of an approved type ceases.

## 12. Transitional Provisions
Specifies dates from which the 05 series of amendments apply for type-approval and placing on the market for each engine category and sub-category (see Tables 1-6 in the text).

## 13. Names and Addresses
Contracting Parties must communicate details of their Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## Appendices
*   **Appendix 1**: Definition of engine sub-categories.
*   **Appendix 2**: Exhaust emission limits (Stage V) for all engine categories (NRE, NRG, NRSh, NRS, SMB, ATS).
*   **Appendix 3**: Emission durability periods (EDP in hours) for all engine categories.
*   **Appendix 4**: Requirements for engines using specified fuels, fuel mixtures, or emulsions (e.g., standard/fuel-specific ranges, universal/restricted fuel ranges for gaseous fuels).

## Annexes (Referenced in Text)
The Regulation includes numerous Annexes detailing technical procedures and requirements:
*   **Annex 1**: Templates for information folder and document.
*   **Annex 2**: Communication forms.
*   **Annex 3**: Arrangements of approval marks.
*   **Annex 4**: Test procedure.
*   **Annex 5**: Method for data evaluation and calculation.
*   **Annex 6**: Technical characteristics of reference fuels.
*   **Annex 7**: Technical requirements for dual-fuel engines.
*   **Annex 8**: Methodology for adapting test results to include deterioration factors.
*   **Annex 9**: Requirements regarding emission control strategies.
*   **Annex 10**: Parameters for defining engine types and families.
