# UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF REAR MARKING PLATES FOR HEAVY AND LONG VEHICLES

**Regulation No. 70**
**Revision 1**

## 1. SCOPE
This Regulation applies to rear marking plates for:
1.1. articulated class II and III vehicles of category M;
1.2. vehicles of category N3, except tractors for semi-trailers;
1.3. vehicles of category O1, O2 and O3 exceeding 8.0 m in length;
1.4. vehicles of category O4.

## 2. DEFINITIONS
### 2.1. General Definitions
- **"Rear marking plate"**: a plate faced with retro-reflective and fluorescent material or devices intended to increase the visibility and permit easy identification of heavy and long vehicles.
- **"Sample unit"**: a complete, finished marking plate ready to be mounted on a vehicle and representative of current production.
- **Classes of rear marking plates**:
    - **Class 1**: Rear marking plates for heavy motor vehicles (trucks and tractors) with red fluorescent and yellow retro-reflective alternative stripes.
    - **Class 2**: Rear marking plates for long vehicles (trailers and semi-trailers) with red fluorescent border and yellow retro-reflective centre.
    - **Class 3**: Rear marking plates for heavy motor vehicles (trucks and tractors) with red retro-reflective and yellow retro-reflective alternative stripes.
    - **Class 4**: Rear marking plates for long vehicles (trailers and semi-trailers) with red retro-reflective border and yellow retro-reflective centre.

### 2.2. Retro-reflection Definitions
- **"Retro-reflection"**: Reflection in which radiation is returned in directions close to the direction from which it came.
- **"Retro-reflective material"**: a surface or device from which, when directionally irradiated, a relatively large portion of the incident radiation is retro-reflected.
- **"Retro-reflecting device"**: an assembly ready for use and comprising one or more retro-reflecting optical units.

### 2.3. Geometric Definitions
- **"Reference centre"**: a point on or near a retro-reflective area designated as the centre for specifying performance.
- **"Illumination axis"**: a line from the reference centre to the light source.
- **"Observation axis"**: a line from the reference centre to the photometer head.
- **"Observation angle (α)"**: the angle between the illumination axis and the observation axis.
- **"Entrance angle (β)"**: the angle from the illumination axis to the reference axis, characterized by two components, β1 and β2.
- **"Angle of rotation (ε)"**: angle through which the sample is turned about its mean vertical axis.

### 2.4. Photometric Definitions
- **"Coefficient of retro-reflection (R')"**: the quotient of the coefficient of luminous intensity R of a plane retro-reflecting surface by its area A. Expressed in candelas per lux per m² (cd·lx⁻¹·m⁻²).
- **"Luminance factor"**: the ratio of the luminance of the body considered to the luminance of a perfect diffuser under identical conditions.

### 2.5. Fluorescence
The phenomenon where certain substances emit radiations of longer wavelength when exposed to ultraviolet or blue radiations.

### 2.7. Definition of "type"
Rear marking plates differ in type based on:
- Trade name or mark.
- Characteristics of the retro-reflective material.
- Characteristics of the fluorescent material.
- Parts affecting the properties of the retro-reflective material or devices.
*(Differences in shape and dimensions do not constitute a different type.)*

## 3. APPLICATION FOR APPROVAL
The application, submitted by the holder of the trade name or mark, must include:
- Detailed drawings.
- Technical specifications of retro-reflective and fluorescent materials.
- Samples as specified in Annex 4.

## 4. MARKINGS
Every plate must bear:
- The trade name or mark of the applicant.
- The word "TOP" (if the retro-reflective system is not omni-rotational).
Markings must be legible, indelible, and visible when fitted.

## 5. APPROVAL
### 5.2. Approval Number
An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments. The symbol above the circle indicates the class: "RF" for classes 1/2 (retro-reflective/fluorescent), "RR" for classes 3/4 (retro-reflective only).

### 5.4. Approval Mark
Every approved plate must bear an international approval mark consisting of:
- A circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the approving country.
- The approval number.

## 6. GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Must function satisfactorily in normal use.
- Components must not be easily dismantled.
- Means of attachment must guarantee a stable and durable connection.
- Outer surface must be easy to clean.

## 7. SPECIAL SPECIFICATIONS (TESTS)
Must satisfy requirements for shape, stripe slope, colorimetric, photometric, physical, and mechanical properties as set forth in Annexes 5 to 12.

## 8. MODIFICATIONS AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL
Any modification must be notified. The approving authority may require further tests.

## 9. CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Production must conform to the approved type. Minimum control procedures are set in Annex 13.

## 10. PENALTIES FOR NON-CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met.

## 11. PRODUCTION DEFINITELY DISCONTINUED
The approval holder must inform the authority if manufacture ceases.

## 12. REMARK CONCERNING DIMENSIONS
Parties may restrict the total summarized length of plates installed on vehicles within the prescribed range.

## 13. TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
Outlines the timeline for applying amendments from Supplement 3 to the 01 series of amendments.

## 14. NAMES AND ADDRESSES
Parties must communicate the names and addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments to the UN Secretariat.
