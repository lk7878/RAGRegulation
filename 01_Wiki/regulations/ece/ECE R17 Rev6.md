**1. Scope**
This Regulation applies to:
(a) Vehicles of categories M and N1 with regard to the strength of seats and their anchorages and with regard to their head restraints;
(b) Vehicles of categories M2 and M3 with regard to seats not covered by UN Regulation No. 80, in respect of the strength of seats and their anchorages, and in respect of their head restraints;
(c) Vehicles of category M1 with regard to the design of the rear parts of seat backs and the design of devices intended to protect the occupants from the danger resulting from the displacement of luggage in a frontal impact.
It does not apply to vehicles with regard to side-facing or rearward-facing seats, or to any head restraint fitted to these seats.

**2. Definitions**
Key definitions include:
- **Approval of a vehicle**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to the strength of the seats and their anchorages, the design of the rear parts of the seat-backs and the characteristics of their head restraints.
- **Vehicle type**: A category of motor vehicles which do not differ in essential respects such as structure, dimensions, materials of seats and anchorages, and head restraint characteristics.
- **Seat**: A structure intended to seat one person. Classified as forward-facing, rearward-facing, or side-facing based on orientation.
- **Bench seat**: A structure intended to seat more than one adult person.
- **Anchorage**: The system by which the seat assembly is secured to the vehicle structure.
- **Adjustment system**: Device for adjusting the seat to suit occupant morphology (longitudinal, vertical, angular displacement).
- **Displacement system**: Device to displace/rotate the seat for easy access to space behind.
- **Locking system**: Device ensuring the seat and its parts are maintained in the position of use.
- **Head restraint**: Device to limit rearward displacement of an occupant's head relative to the torso to reduce cervical injury risk. Types: integrated, detachable, separate.
- **R point**: The seating reference point.
- **Reference line**: A defined line on a manikin.
- **Partitioning system**: Parts or devices (e.g., netting) intended to protect occupants from displaced luggage.

**3. Application for Approval**
- Submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or accredited representative.
- Requires detailed documentation (descriptions, drawings) of the vehicle type, seats, anchorages, adjustment/displacement/locking systems, and head restraints (if applicable).
- Submission of a representative vehicle or necessary parts, and additional sets of seats and anchorages (and head restraints if applicable) to the Technical Service.

**4. Approval**
- Granted if the vehicle meets relevant requirements.
- An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate the series of amendments).
- An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country number, plus regulation number and approval details) must be affixed to conforming vehicles.
- Specific marking ("RA") if seats are fitted/capable of being fitted with head restraints.
- Examples of approval mark arrangements are in Annex 2.

**5. Requirements**
- **5.1 General requirements**: Prohibits side-facing seats in certain vehicle categories (with exceptions for emergency/service vehicles).
- **5.2 General requirements for M1 vehicles**: Covers automatic locking systems, placement of unlocking controls, energy dissipation and surface characteristics of rear seat parts, strength of seat frames/anchorages during tests, and functionality of displacement systems post-test.
- **5.3 General specifications for N1, N2, N3 and M2, M3 seats (not covered by R80)**: Requires firm attachment, automatic locking for sliding seats and adjustable seat-backs, and automatic locking for folding/tip-forward seats (with exceptions).
- **5.4 Mounting of head restraints**: Mandates head restraints on every outboard front seat in M1 vehicles. Specifies requirements for M2 and N1 vehicles.
- **5.5 Special requirements for seats with head restraints**: Head restraints must not be an additional danger, must be padded to prevent direct contact with structure, must pass energy absorption test, and must be securely attached.
- **5.6 Height of head restraints**: Specifies minimum height measurements (800 mm for front seats, 750 mm for others), requirements for adjustable head restraints, and permitted reductions for clearance.
- **5.7 Seats capable of being fitted with head restraints**: Requirements for energy dissipation and minimum height of the mounting device must be verified.
- **5.8 Gap between seat-back and head restraint**: Limits maximum gap.
- **5.9 & 5.10 Gaps in head restraints**: Permits gaps exceeding 60 mm under specific conditions and after additional testing.
- **5.11 Width of head restraint**: Must provide appropriate head support, covering at least 85 mm to each side of the seat's median plane.
- **5.12 Backward displacement**: The maximum backward displacement (X) permitted by the head restraint must be less than 102 mm.
- **5.13 Strength**: Head restraint and anchorage must withstand specified load without breakage.
- **5.14 Adjustability**: Head restraint must not be raised beyond maximum operational height except by deliberate user action.
- **5.15 Seat-back strength**: If no breakage occurs during head restraint strength test, seat-back strength requirements are deemed met.
- **5.16 Protection from displaced luggage**: Specifies strength requirements for seat-backs/head restraints and partitioning systems forming the forward boundary of the luggage compartment. Includes test criteria limiting forward displacement during a simulated frontal impact.

**6. Tests**
- **6.1 General specifications**: Seat-back locked at specified inclination; testing of symmetrical seats; head restraints in most unfavourable position; folding seats in use position.
- **6.2 Test of strength of the seat-back and its adjustment systems**: Application of a 53 daNm rearward moment to the upper seat-back frame.
- **6.3 Test of strength of seat anchorage and adjustment/locking/displacement systems**: Application of a longitudinal deceleration/acceleration (≥20g for 30 ms) simulating frontal and rear collisions. Specifies seat positions for testing. Allows alternative complete vehicle barrier collision test.
- **6.4 Test of head restraint performance**: Procedure involving application of forces/moments to determine backward displacement (X) and check effectiveness under increased load.
- **6.5 Determination of head restraint height**: Measurement from R point to tangent at top of head restraint.
- **6.6 Determination of head restraint width**: Measurement in a specified plane.
- **6.7 Determination of gap distance "a"**: Measurement using a 165 mm sphere.
- **6.8 Tests for checking energy dissipation**: Defines areas (1, 2, 3) on seat-backs and head restraints for impact testing with a pendulum apparatus.
- **6.9 Equivalent test methods**: Other methods may be used if equivalence is proved.

**7. Conformity of Production**
- Vehicles must be manufactured to conform to the approved type.
- Type Approval Authority may verify control methods and carry out random checks.

**8. Penalties for Non-conformity**
- Approval may be withdrawn if production does not comply or vehicles fail checks.
- Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

**9. Modifications and Extension of Approval**
- Modifications to seats/anchorages/head restraints must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further assessment.
- Extension of approval requires a new series number and notification.

**10. Production Definitively Discontinued**
- Manufacturer must inform approval authority, which notifies other Parties.

**11. Instruction for Use**
- For seats with adjustable head restraints, manufacturers must provide instructions for operation, adjustment, locking, and removal.

**12. Technical Services and Approval Authorities**
- Contracting Parties must communicate details of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

**13. Transitional Provisions**
- Details the phased implementation and acceptance of approvals under different amendment series (06, 07, 08, 09), including key dates up to September 2022.

**Annexes**
- **Annex 1**: Model communication form for approval notifications.
- **Annex 2**: Arrangements of the approval mark (models A, B, C, D).
- **Annex 3**: Procedure for determining the "H" point and actual torso angle (references R.E.3).
- **Annex 4**: Determination of the height and width of head restraints (figures).
- **Annex 5**: Details of lines and measurements taken during tests (figure).
- **Annex 6**: Test procedure for checking energy dissipation (pendulum impact test).
- **Annex 7**: Method for testing the strength of seat anchorages and their systems (inertia test and barrier collision test).
- **Annex 8**: Determination of dimension "a" of head restraint gaps (figures).
- **Annex 9**: Test procedure for devices protecting against displacement of luggage (test blocks, preparation, dynamic test).
    - **Appendix**: Corridor of sled's deceleration/acceleration vs. time for frontal impact simulation.
