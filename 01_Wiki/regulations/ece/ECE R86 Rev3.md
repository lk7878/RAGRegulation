# UN Regulation No. 86 (Revision 3)

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of agricultural or forestry vehicles with regard to the installation of lighting and light-signalling devices**

This document incorporates all valid text up to the **01 series of amendments** (Date of entry into force: 22 June 2017).

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to vehicles of category T, R and S, with regard to the installation of lighting and light-signalling devices.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Vehicle type with regard to the installation of lighting and light-signalling devices**
*   **Lighting function** and **Light-signalling function**
*   **Lamp** and various lamp types (Independent, Grouped, Combined, Reciprocally incorporated, Concealable, etc.)
*   Specific lamp functions (e.g., Main-beam headlamp, Dipped-beam headlamp, Direction-indicator lamp, Stop lamp, etc.)
*   **Illuminating surface**, **Apparent surface**, **Light-emitting surface**
*   **Axis of reference**, **Center of reference**, **Angles of geometric visibility**
*   **Single lamp**, **Two lamps**
*   **Optional lamp**
*   **Operating tell-tale**, **Circuit-closed tell-tale**
*   **Signalling devices other than lamps** (e.g., Slow-Moving Vehicles (SMV) rear marking plate, Conspicuity marking, Signalling panel/foil)

## 3. Application for Approval
Submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or accredited representative, accompanied by required documents and particulars (description, list of devices, diagrams, drawings).

## 4. Approval
*   Granting of approval if requirements are met.
*   Assignment of an approval number (first two digits indicate series of amendments).
*   Requirements for the international approval mark (circle with "E", country number, regulation number "86R", approval number).
*   Mark to be placed close to or on the vehicle data plate.

## 5. General Specifications
*   **Fitting and Stability:** Devices must retain characteristics under normal use and vibration.
*   **Socket Outlet:** Vehicles with connections for trailed vehicles/machines must have specified ISO socket outlets.
*   **Adjustability:** Correct adjustment of headlamps must be easily carried out.
*   **Orientation:** Reference axis of lamps must be parallel to bearing plane (±3° tolerance).
*   **Pairs of Lamps:** Must be mounted symmetrically, have same color, and substantially identical photometric characteristics.
*   **Grouped/Combined/Reciprocally Incorporated Lamps:** Permitted if all requirements (color, position, etc.) are fulfilled. Specific rules for stop and direction-indicator lamps.
*   **Height and Width Measurements:** Defined from the apparent surface.
*   **Photometric Characteristics:** Shall not be intentionally varied during activation (with exceptions for flashing lamps, ambient light adaptation, etc.).
*   **Color Confusion Prevention:** No red light visible forward, no white light visible rearward (with exceptions). Verification zones defined.
*   **Electrical Connections:**
    *   Front/rear position lamps, end-outline marker lamps, side-marker lamps, and rear registration plate lamp must switch ON/OFF simultaneously (with exceptions).
    *   Main-beam, dipped-beam, and front fog lamps cannot be switched on unless the lamps in the previous point are also on (with exceptions for luminous warnings).
    *   Dipped-beam/main-beam/front fog lamps may substitute front position lamps under specific conditions.
*   **Tell-tales:** Circuit-closed tell-tale function may be fulfilled by operating tell-tale.
*   **Colors of Light Emitted:** Specified for each lamp type (e.g., White for headlamps, Amber for direction-indicators, Red for stop lamps).
*   **Concealable Lamps:** Only main-beam, dipped-beam, and front fog lamps may be concealable. Specific requirements for failure modes, controls, and operation temperature range.
*   **Lamps of Variable Position:** All lamps may vary position except main-beam, dipped-beam, and at least one pair of rear reflectors, provided they remain attached and can be locked automatically.
*   **Geometric Visibility:** No obstacles inside the angles of geometric visibility.
*   **Number of Lamps:** As per individual specifications.
*   **Non-operational Lamps:** A lamp is deemed not present if it cannot be made to operate by installing only a light source and/or fuse (except retro-reflectors).

## 6. Individual Specifications
Detailed requirements for each type of lighting and light-signalling device, including:
*   **Presence** (Mandatory/Optional/Prohibited)
*   **Number**
*   **Arrangement**
*   **Position** (Width, Height, Length)
*   **Geometric Visibility** (Horizontal and Vertical angles)
*   **Orientation**
*   **Electrical Connections**
*   **Tell-tale** (Mandatory/Optional)
*   **Other Requirements**

The specifications cover the following devices:
6.1. Main-beam headlamps
6.2. Dipped-beam headlamps
6.3. Front fog lamps
6.4. Reversing lamp(s)
6.5. Direction-indicator lamps
6.6. Hazard warning signal
6.7. Stop lamps
6.8. Front position lamps
6.9. Rear position lamps
6.10. Rear fog-lamps
6.11. Parking lamps
6.12. End-outline marker lamps
6.13. Work lamp(s)
6.14. Rear retro-reflectors, non-triangular
6.15. Side retro-reflectors, non-triangular
6.16. Rear registration plate illuminating device
6.17. Front retro-reflectors, non-triangular
6.18. Side-marker lamps
6.19. Daytime running lamp
6.20. Cornering lamp
6.21. Conspicuity markings
6.22. SMV rear marking plate
6.23. Exterior Courtesy Lamp
6.24. Manoeuvring lamps
6.25. Rear retro-reflectors, triangular
6.26. Signalling panel and signalling foil

## 7. Modification and Extension of Approval
Procedures for communicating modifications and obtaining extensions of approval.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Vehicles bearing the approval mark must conform to the approved type.

## 9. Penalties for Non-conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
Manufacturer must inform the Type Approval Authority if production ceases.

## 11. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
To be communicated to the UN Secretariat.

## 12. Transitional Provisions
Relating to the entry into force of the 01 series of amendments.

## Annexes
1.  **Communication:** Model for approval communication form.
2.  **Examples of arrangements of approval marks.**
3.  **The relevant parts of Annex 3 of UN Regulation No. 48 apply:** Illustrations for illuminating surface, light-emitting surface, apparent surface, etc.
4.  **Visibility of lamps:** Diagrams for verifying no red light forward and no white light rearward.
5.  **Direction-indicator lamps – Geometric visibility:** Diagrams for Arrangements A, B, C, D.
6.  **Signalling panels and signalling foils:** Specifications for dimensions, arrangement, number, colors, and photometric requirements.
