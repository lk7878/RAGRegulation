# Regulation No. 137

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to vehicles of category M1 with a maximum permissible mass below 3.5 tonnes; other vehicles may be approved at the request of the manufacturer.

## 2. Definitions
For the purpose of this Regulation:
*   **2.1. "Protective system"**: Interior fittings and devices intended to restrain the occupants and contribute towards ensuring compliance with the requirements.
*   **2.2. "Type of protective system"**: A category of protective devices which do not differ in essential respects (technology, geometry, constituent materials).
*   **2.3. "Vehicle width"**: Defined distance between two parallel planes touching the vehicle on either side, excluding certain external devices.
*   **2.4. "Vehicle type"**: A category of power-driven vehicles which do not differ in essential respects that could negatively affect the frontal impact test results (e.g., dimensions, structure, mass, engine siting, REESS location).
*   **2.5. "Passenger compartment"**:
    *   **2.5.1. With regard to occupant protection**: Space bounded by the roof, floor, side walls, doors, outside glazing, front bulkhead, and the plane of the rear compartment bulkhead or rear-seat back support.
    *   **2.5.2. For electric safety assessment**: Space bounded by the roof, floor, side walls, doors, outside glazing, front and rear bulkheads/gates, and electrical protection barriers/enclosures.
*   **2.6. "R point"**: Manufacturer-defined reference point for each seat.
*   **2.7. "H point"**: Reference point determined for each seat by the testing service according to Annex 6.
*   **2.8. "Unladen kerb mass"**: Mass of the vehicle in running order, unoccupied and unladen but complete with standard equipment (fuel, coolant, tools, spare wheel).
*   **2.9. "Airbag"**: Device installed to supplement safety belts, which automatically deploys in a severe impact to limit injury.
*   **2.10. "Passenger airbag"**: Airbag assembly intended to protect occupant(s) in seats other than the driver's.
*   **2.11. "High voltage"**: > 60 V and ≤ 1,500 V DC or > 30 V and ≤ 1,000 V AC rms.
*   **2.12. "Rechargeable Electrical Energy Storage System (REESS)"**: System providing electrical energy for propulsion.
*   **2.13. "Electrical protection barrier"**: Part providing protection against direct contact with high voltage live parts.
*   **2.14. "Electrical power train"**: Electrical circuit including traction motor(s), and may include REESS, energy conversion system, converters, wiring, and charging coupling system.
*   **2.15. "Live parts"**: Conductive part(s) intended to be electrically energized in normal use.
*   **2.16. "Exposed conductive part"**: Conductive part that can be touched (per IPXXB) and becomes energized under isolation failure.
*   **2.17. "Direct contact"**: Contact of persons with high voltage live parts.
*   **2.18. "Indirect contact"**: Contact of persons with exposed conductive parts.
*   **2.19. "Protection IPXXB"**: Protection from contact with high voltage live parts provided by a barrier or enclosure, tested with a Jointed Test Finger.
*   **2.20. "Working voltage"**: Highest rms voltage specified by the manufacturer that may occur between any conductive parts in open circuit or normal operation.
*   **2.21. "Coupling system for charging the REESS"**: Electrical circuit used for charging the REESS from an external power supply.
*   **2.22. "Electrical chassis"**: Set of conductive parts electrically linked together, whose potential is taken as reference.
*   **2.23. "Electrical circuit"**: Assembly of connected high voltage live parts designed to be energized in normal operation.
*   **2.24. "Electrical energy conversion system"**: System (e.g., fuel cell) that generates electrical energy for propulsion.
*   **2.25. "Electronic converter"**: Device for controlling and/or converting electrical power for propulsion.
*   **2.26. "Enclosure"**: Part enclosing internal units and providing protection against direct contact.
*   **2.27. "High voltage bus"**: Electrical circuit, including the REESS charging coupling system, that operates on high voltage.
*   **2.28. "Solid insulator"**: Insulating coating (e.g., on wiring, connectors, varnish) to prevent direct contact with high voltage live parts.
*   **2.29. "Automatic disconnect"**: Device that, when triggered, galvanically separates energy sources from the rest of the high voltage circuit.
*   **2.30. "Open type traction battery"**: Battery requiring liquid and generating hydrogen gas released to the atmosphere.
*   **2.31. "Automatically activated door locking system"**: System that locks doors automatically at a pre-set speed or condition.

## 3. Application for Approval
*   Submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or accredited representative.
*   Requires detailed documentation: vehicle description, drawings, mass, passenger compartment details, protective system description, electrical power train description.
*   A representative vehicle must be submitted to the Technical Service for testing.

## 4. Approval
*   Granted if the vehicle type meets the Regulation's requirements.
*   An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate the amendment series).
*   An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, plus regulation and approval number) must be affixed to conforming vehicles.
*   Examples of approval marks are given in Annex 2.

## 5. Specifications
### 5.1. General Specifications
*   "H" point determined per Annex 6.
*   Belts must meet Regulation No. 16; anchorage points must meet Regulation No. 14.

### 5.2. Specifications for the Restraint System Test (Full Width Rigid Barrier Test)
Vehicle tested per Annex 3. Test is satisfactory if all conditions (5.2.1 to 5.2.8) are met.
*   **5.2.1. Performance Criteria (Annex 4 & 8)**:
    *   **Hybrid III 50th percentile male (driver)**:
 *   Head Performance Criterion (HPC) ≤ 1000; resultant head acceleration ≤ 80 g for >3 ms.
 *   Neck: Axial tensile force ≤ 3.3 kN; Shear force ≤ 3.1 kN; Bending moment (extension) ≤ 57 Nm.
 *   Thorax Compression Criterion (ThCC) ≤ 42 mm.
 *   Viscous Criterion (V*C) ≤ 1.0 m/s.
 *   Femur Force Criterion (FFC) ≤ 9.07 kN.
    *   **Hybrid III 5th percentile female (passenger)**:
 *   Head Performance Criterion (HPC) ≤ 1000; resultant head acceleration ≤ 80 g for >3 ms.
 *   Neck: Axial tensile force ≤ 2.9 kN; Shear force ≤ 2.7 kN; Bending moment (extension) ≤ 57 Nm.
 *   Thorax Compression Criterion (ThCC) ≤ 34 mm.
 *   Viscous Criterion (V*C) ≤ 1.0 m/s.
 *   Femur Force Criterion (FFC) ≤ 7 kN.
*   **5.2.2. Steering Wheel Displacement**: Residual displacement ≤ 80 mm upward, ≤ 100 mm rearward.
*   **5.2.3. Doors**: No door shall open during the test.
*   **5.2.4. Doors After Impact**: Side doors shall be unlocked.
*   **5.2.5. Post-Impact Accessibility**: Must be possible (without tools, except to support dummy weight) to:
    *   Open at least one door per row of seats (or move seats/backrests for evacuation).
    *   Release dummies from restraint system (max 60 N force on release).
    *   Remove dummies without seat adjustment.
*   **5.2.6./5.2.7. Fuel Leakage**: Only slight leakage allowed; continuous leakage ≤ 30 g/min.
*   **5.2.8. Electrical Safety (for vehicles with electric power train)**:
    *   **5.2.8.1. Protection Against Electrical Shock**: After impact, at least one of four criteria must be met:
 1.  **Absence of high voltage**: Voltages Vb, V1, V2 ≤ 30 VAC or 60 VDC.
 2.  **Low electrical energy**: Total Energy (TE) on high voltage buses < 2.0 J.
 3.  **Physical protection**: Protection IPXXB provided; resistance between exposed conductive parts and electrical chassis < 0.1 Ω.
 4.  **Isolation resistance**: Minimum value of 100 Ω/V (DC buses) or 500 Ω/V (AC buses) of working voltage.
    *   **5.2.8.2. Electrolyte Spillage**: Within 30 min after impact, no electrolyte shall spill into passenger compartment. Max 7% spillage from REESS (max 5.0 L for open type batteries outside compartment).
    *   **5.2.8.3. REESS Retention**: REESS inside compartment must remain in place. REESS outside compartment must not enter passenger compartment.

## 6. Instructions for Users of Vehicles Equipped with Airbags
*   Vehicles must carry information indicating airbag equipment.
*   Driver airbag: "AIRBAG" inscription on steering wheel.
*   Passenger airbag: Warning label about hazard of rearward-facing child restraints on seats with active airbags. Label specifications (pictograms, size ≥120x60 mm) and placement (e.g., sun visor) are detailed.
*   Owner's manual must contain specific warning text in relevant languages.

## 7. Modification and Extension of Approval of the Vehicle Type
*   Modifications affecting structure, seats, interior, or energy-absorption capability must be notified.
*   Type Approval Authority may require further tests (e.g., full test per Annex 3, simplified test per Annex 7).
*   Extension of approval requires a new series number.

## 8. Conformity of Production
*   Every approved vehicle must conform to the approved type.
*   Holder of approval must ensure tests are carried out.
*   Type Approval Authority verifies control methods at least once every two years.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
*   Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met.
*   Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
*   Holder must inform the Type Approval Authority, which notifies other Parties.

## 11. Transitional Provisions
*   From entry into force of 01 series amendments (17 Dec 2016), approvals must be to the amended Regulation.
*   Approvals to the original Regulation may continue to be granted/extended until 1 Sep 2020.
*   After 1 Sep 2020, Parties are not obliged to accept new approvals to the original form, but must continue to accept those granted before that date.

## 12. Names and Addresses
Contracting Parties must communicate details of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval/refusal/withdrawal.
*   **Annex 2**: Examples of approval marks.
*   **Annex 3**: Test procedure (Full Width Rigid Barrier test). Details vehicle preparation, dummy installation (HIII 50th male driver, HIII 5th female passenger), propulsion, test speed (50 km/h), measurements.
*   **Annex 4**: Performance criteria calculation methods (HPC, neck, thorax, femur).
*   **Annex 5**: Arrangement and installation of dummies and adjustment of restraint systems.
*   **Annex 6**: Procedure for determining "H" point and torso angle (references RE.3).
*   **Annex 7**: Test procedure with trolley (simplified test).
*   **Annex 8**: Technique of measurement: Instrumentation specifications (data channels, calibration, filtering).
*   **Annex 9**: Test procedures for electrical safety (protection against shock, electrolyte spillage, REESS retention). Includes measurement methods for voltage, energy, isolation resistance, and physical protection check with jointed test finger.
