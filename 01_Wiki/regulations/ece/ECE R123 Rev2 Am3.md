**Regulation No. 123 - Revision 2 - Amendment 3**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of adaptive front-lighting systems (AFS) for motor vehicles**

*   **Document Status:** This document is meant purely as documentation tool. The authentic and legally binding text is: **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2015/32**.
*   **Entry into Force:** 8 October 2015.
*   **Issued:** 9 November 2015.

**Amendments:**

This document constitutes **Amendment 3** to **Revision 2** of UNECE Regulation No. 123 (Addendum 122). It is identified as **Supplement 7 to the 01 series of amendments**.

**Detailed Amendments:**

1.  **Annex 4, Paragraph 1.2.1. - Preparation of the test sample**
    *   The text is amended, including the insertion of a new footnote 6.
    *   **1.2.1.1. Test mixture for glass lenses:** Specifies the composition of the water and polluting agent mixture:
 *   9 parts by weight silica sand (0-100 µm)
 *   1 part by weight vegetable carbon dust from beech wood (0-100 µm)
 *   0.2 parts by weight NaCMC5
 *   5 parts by weight sodium chloride (99% pure)
 *   An appropriate quantity of distilled water (conductivity < 1 µS/m).
    *   **1.2.1.2. Test mixture for plastic lenses:** Specifies the composition:
 *   9 parts by weight silica sand (0-100 µm)
 *   1 part by weight vegetable carbon dust from beech wood (0-100 µm)
 *   0.2 parts by weight NaCMC5
 *   5 parts by weight sodium chloride (99% pure)
 *   13 parts by weight distilled water (conductivity < 1 µS/m)
 *   2 + 1 parts by weight of surface-actant. **Footnote 6:** The tolerance on quantity is due to the necessity of obtaining dirt that correctly spreads out on all types of plastic lens.
    *   Paragraph 1.2.1.3. is deleted.
    *   Former paragraphs 1.2.1.4. and 1.2.1.5. are renumbered as 1.2.1.3. and 1.2.1.4.

2.  **Annex 11, Paragraph 4.2., Table UV**
    *   The table is amended to read as follows, providing spectral weighting factors S(λ) for ultraviolet radiation exposure limits according to "IRPA/INIRC Guidelines". The wavelengths (in nanometres) are representative; other values should be interpolated.

3.  **Annex 11, Paragraph 5.2.**
    *   The paragraph is amended to specify requirements for submitting and testing LED modules:
 *   One module of each type shall be submitted with its light source control gear (if applicable) and sufficient instructions.
 *   Suitable thermal management (e.g., heat sink) may be provided to simulate conditions similar to the corresponding headlamp application.
 *   Before testing, the LED module shall be aged for at least seventy-two hours under the same conditions as in the corresponding headlamp application.
 *   If using an integrating sphere, it shall have a minimum diameter of one meter, and at least ten times the maximum dimension of the LED module, whichever is largest. Flux measurements can also be performed using a goniophotometer. Prescriptions from CIE Publication 84 - 1989 regarding room temperature, positioning, etc., shall be considered.
 *   The LED module shall be burned in for approximately one hour in the closed sphere or goniophotometer.
 *   Flux shall be measured after stability has occurred, as explained in paragraph 4.3.1.2. of the annex.
