# UN Regulation No. 57 - Headlamps for Motorcycles

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the approval of headlamps using filament lamps and incorporating lenses of glass or plastic material, which are provided for the equipment of motor cycles and vehicles treated as such.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include "Lens", "Coating", and criteria for headlamps of different "types" (based on trade name, markings, optical characteristics, etc.).

## 3. Application for Approval of a Headlamp
The application must be submitted by the trade name owner or representative, accompanied by drawings, technical description, samples (headlamps with colourless lenses, and lenses or material samples for plastic), and test reports for materials.

## 4. Markings
Headlamps must bear clear, legible, and indelible inscriptions including the trade name/mark, exterior marking (e.g., MB), filament lamp category indication (S1, S2, S1/S2), and for plastic lenses, the letters "PL". Spaces must be reserved for the approval mark.

## 5. Approval
If samples satisfy requirements, approval is granted with an approval number indicating the series of amendments. Notices are communicated to applying Parties. Approved headlamps must bear an international approval mark (circle with "E" and country number, plus approval number). The approval mark arrangement is shown in Annex 2.

## 6. General Specifications
Headlamps must be designed to withstand vibrations and maintain performance. They must have an adjustment device (with exceptions), ensure correct filament lamp positioning, and maintain photometric performance in use (Annex 4). Plastic lenses require additional tests (Annex 6).

## 7. Special Specifications
*   The lens position must be locked.
*   Photometric measurements use a standard screen (Annex 3) and standard filament lamps (Regulation No. 37).
*   The passing beam must have a sharp, horizontal cut-off.
*   The beam pattern must not have detrimental lateral variations.
*   Illumination is measured with a specified photoelement.

## 8. Provisions Concerning Coloured Lenses and Filters
Approval can be for colourless or selective yellow light. The colorimetric characteristics and minimum transmission factor (≥ 0.78) for selective yellow are specified. Filters must be non-removable.

## 9. Transitional Provisions
Outlines the application timeline for the 01 series of amendments, including dates after which only amended versions can be approved and potential prohibitions on fitting non-compliant devices.

## 10. Conformity of Production
Approved headlamps must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. The holder must have quality control procedures, test equipment, record results, and carry out at least the tests in Annex 5. Competent authorities may verify control methods.

## 11. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or marked headlamps do not conform. Withdrawals must be notified to other Parties.

## 12. Modification and Extension of Approval of a Type of Headlamp
Modifications must be notified. Approval may be confirmed, require further tests, or be refused. Extensions are assigned a series number and communicated.

## 13. Production Definitely Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the authority if production ceases, who then notifies other Parties.

## 14. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments
Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Model communication form for approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal/discontinuation.
*   **Annex 2:** Examples of approval mark arrangements.
*   **Annex 3:** Photometric tests, including aiming procedures, illumination requirements for driving and passing beams, and a diagram of the measuring screen.
*   **Annex 4:** Tests for stability of photometric performance in operation (clean and dirty conditions) and test for change in vertical position of the cut-off line under heat.
*   **Annex 5:** Minimum requirements for conformity of production control procedures, including test tolerances and sampling criteria.
*   **Annex 6:** Requirements for lamps with plastic lenses, including tests for resistance to temperature changes, atmospheric/chemical agents, detergents/hydrocarbons, mechanical deterioration, and coating adherence. Includes appendices on test chronology, light diffusion/transmission measurement, spray testing, and adhesive tape testing.
*   **Annex 7:** Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector, defining procedures and acceptance/ rejection criteria for verifying conformity of production.
