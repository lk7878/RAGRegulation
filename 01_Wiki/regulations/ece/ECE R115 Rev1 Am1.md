**Amendment Details**

This document is **Amendment 1** to **Revision 1** of **UNECE Regulation No. 115**. The full title is: "Uniform provisions concerning the approval of: I. Specific LPG (liquefied petroleum gases) retrofit systems to be installed in motor vehicles for the use of LPG in their propulsion system II. Specific CNG (compressed natural gas) retrofit systems to be installed in motor vehicles for the use of CNG in their propulsion system".

**Date of Entry into Force:** 10 June 2014.

**Technical Amendments**

The amendment modifies calculation formulas in Annexes 6A and 6B of the regulation:

1.  **Annex 6A, Paragraph 2 (LPG energy ratio):**
    *   Amends the formula for calculating the LPG energy ratio (`G_LPG`).
    *   Specifies that the fuel consumption value (`FC_norm`) is to be calculated in accordance with paragraph 1.4.3.(b) of Annex 6 to **Regulation No. 101**.
    *   Clarifies that if applicable, the correction factor `cf` in the `FC_norm` calculation shall use the H/C ratio of the gaseous fuel.
    *   Defines the density `d` for LPG as 0.538 kg/liter.

2.  **Annex 6B, Paragraph 2 (CNG energy ratio):**
    *   Amends the formula for calculating the CNG energy ratio (`G_CNG`).
    *   Specifies that the fuel consumption value (`FC_norm`) is to be calculated in accordance with paragraph 1.4.3.(c) of Annex 6 to **Regulation No. 101**.
    *   Defines the density `d` for CNG as 0.654 kg/m³.
    *   Introduces a correction factor `cf` with defined values:
 *   `cf = 1` for G20 reference fuel.
 *   `cf = 0.78` for G25 reference fuel.
