## Key Definitions (Extract)
-   **Approval of an engine (engine family):** Approval with regard to the level of emission of gaseous and particulate pollutants, smoke and the OBD system.
-   **Approval of a vehicle:** Approval of vehicle type with regard to the level of emission of gaseous and particulate pollutants and smoke by its engine, the OBD system, and the engine installation.
-   **Base Emission Strategy (BES):** Emission strategy active throughout the speed and load operating range unless an Auxiliary Emission Strategy (AES) is activated.
-   **Auxiliary Emission Strategy (AES):** Emission strategy that becomes active and replaces/modifies a BES for a specific purpose and in response to specific conditions.
-   **Defeat strategy:** An emission strategy that does not meet the performance requirements for a BES/AES and is prohibited.
-   **Useful life:** The relevant period of distance and/or time over which compliance with emission limits must be assured (e.g., 160,000 km/5 years for M1, N1, M2; 300,000 km/6 years for N2, N3 ≤16t, M3 Class I/II/A/B≤7.5t; 700,000 km/7 years for N3>16t, M3 Class III/B>7.5t).

## Main Requirements
-   **Approval:** Manufacturers must demonstrate compliance through tests and requirements specified in Paragraph 5 and Annexes 4, 6, 7, 9A, 9B, 9C, 10, 11, and 12.
-   **Fuel Approval:** Requirements for universal fuel range approval and restricted fuel range approval (for natural gas/LPG positive ignition engines) are detailed, including testing on reference fuels.
-   **OBD System:** All engine systems and vehicles must be equipped with an OBD system complying with Annex 9A, including in-use performance requirements.
-   **Replacement Pollution Control Devices:** Must be type-approved as separate technical units (requirements in Annex 13).
-   **Approval Marks:** Specific marking requirements including circle with "E", country code, regulation number "R", approval number, and additional characters to distinguish fuel type (e.g., D for diesel, P for petrol, H/L/HL for natural gas range).
-   **Conformity of Production:** Procedures defined in Appendices 1-3, based on statistical sampling.
-   **In-Service Conformity:** Requirements and procedures for checking conformity throughout the vehicle's normal life under normal conditions of use (detailed in Annex 8).
-   **Off-Cycle Emissions:** Requirements to limit emissions under all conditions, including PEMS demonstration tests (Annex 10).
-   **NOx Control Measures:** Requirements to ensure correct operation, including at low temperatures (Annex 11).
-   **Electronic System Security:** Requirements to prevent tampering (Annex 9B).

## Transitional Provisions (06 Series of Amendments)
-   New type approvals must comply with the 06 series of amendments from its entry into force (27 January 2013).
-   Phased introduction of specific OBD monitoring and NOx control requirements between 2013 and 2015.
-   Type approvals under the 05 series ceased to be valid from 1 January 2014.
