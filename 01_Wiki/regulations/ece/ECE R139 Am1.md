# UN Regulation No. 139 - Brake Assist Systems (BAS) - Amendment 1

**Document Reference:** E/ECE/324/Rev.2/Add.138/Amend.1 − E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2/Add.138/Amend.1
**Authentic Legal Text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2018/60
**Date of Entry into Force:** 29 December 2018

## Scope
This amendment to UN Regulation No. 139 establishes uniform provisions concerning the approval of passenger cars with regard to Brake Assist Systems (BAS).

## Key Amendment
The amendment modifies paragraph 5.1. The revised text requires:
*   Vehicles to be equipped with a brake assist system meeting the functional requirements specified in paragraph 6.
*   Compliance to be demonstrated by meeting the provisions of paragraphs 8 or 9 under the test requirements of paragraph 7.
*   In addition to the requirements of this regulation, vehicles must also be equipped with ABS in accordance with the technical requirements of **UN Regulation No. 13-H**.
