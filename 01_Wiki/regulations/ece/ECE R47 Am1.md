**Amendment 1 to Regulation No. 47**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 12 June 2007

This document constitutes **Amendment 1** to **UNECE Regulation No. 47**, titled "Uniform provisions concerning the approval of mopeds equipped with a positive-ignition engine with regard to the emission of gaseous pollutants by the engine".

**Key Amendments:**

1.  **Paragraph 1. (Scope):** Amended to read:
    > "1.1. This Regulation applies to the emission of gaseous pollutants from positive-ignition engines of vehicles of categories L1 and L2. 1/"
    > *Footnote 1/ refers to the vehicle category definitions in Annex 7 to the Consolidated Resolution on the Construction of Vehicles (R.E.3).*

2.  **Paragraph 4.4.1. (Footnote Renumbering and Update):** The reference and text of footnote 1/ are renumbered as footnote 2/ and its content is updated to the latest list of country codes assigned under the 1958 Agreement. This list includes codes for contracting parties (e.g., 1 for Germany, 43 for Japan, 51 for the Republic of Korea) and specifies the procedure for assigning new numbers.

3.  **Paragraph 8.3.2. (Footnote Renumbering):** The reference to footnote 1/ in this paragraph is renumbered as footnote 3/.
