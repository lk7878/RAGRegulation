# UN Regulation No. 58 - Corrigendum 1 to Amendment 1 to Revision 2

**Corrigendum 1 to Amendment 1 to Revision 2 of the Regulation - Date of entry into force: 26 July 2012**

This document contains corrections to **Revision 2 – Amendment 1** of **Addendum 57: Regulation No. 58**.

## Scope
Uniform provisions concerning the approval of:
I. Rear underrun protective devices (RUPDs)
II. Vehicles with regard to the installation of an RUPD of an approved type
III. Vehicles with regard to their rear underrun protection (RUP)

## Corrections

### Paragraph 2.3.
Corrected text:
> "2.3. Any vehicle in one of the categories M1, M2, M3, N1, O1 or O2 shall be deemed …
> Where there is more than one rear axle, the width to be considered is that of the widest.
> The requirements of paragraphs 2.3.(b) and 2.3.(c) above shall be satisfied at least on a line:
> (a) At a distance of not more than 450 mm from the rear extremity of the vehicle;
> (b) That may have interruptions totalling not more than 200 mm."

### Paragraph 24.1.
Corrected text:
> "24.1. If the vehicle submitted for approval pursuant to this Regulation meets the requirements of paragraph 2.3.(b) or paragraph 2.3.(c) or paragraph 25 and has been tested following the conditions set out in paragraph 2.2., approval of that vehicle type shall be granted."

### Paragraph 31.5.
Corrected text:
> "31.5. As from 48 months …
> (a) … meets the requirements of paragraph 2.3.(b) or paragraph 2.3.(c) or Part III of this Regulation as amended by the 02 series of amendments;
> (b) … the requirements of paragraph 2.3.(b) or paragraph 2.3.(c) or Part III of this Regulation as amended by the 02 series of amendments.
> …."

### Annex 3
Corrected text:
> "Annex 3
> ….
> of a type of a vehicle with regard to its rear underrun protection (RUP) pursuant to paragraph 2.3.(b) / paragraph 2.3.(c) / Part III2 of Regulation No. 58.
> ___
> 2 Strike out what does not apply."
