# UN Regulation No. 54 (Revision 3)

## 1. Scope
This Regulation covers new pneumatic tyres designed primarily for vehicles of categories M2, M3, N, O3 and O4. However, it does not apply to tyre types identified by speed category symbols corresponding to speeds below 80 km/h.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **Type of pneumatic tyre**: A category of pneumatic tyres which do not differ in essential respects such as manufacturer, tyre-size designation, category of use, structure, speed category, load-capacity indices, and cross-section.
- **Categories of use**: Normal tyre, Snow tyre, Special use tyre (including Professional off-road tyre).
- **Structure**: Diagonal (bias-ply) or Radial.
- **Tyre-size designation**: Includes nominal section width, nominal aspect ratio (or nominal outer diameter), and a conventional number "d" denoting the nominal rim diameter.
- **Load-capacity index**: One or two numbers indicating the load the tyre can carry in single or dual operation.
- **Speed category**: A symbol indicating the speed at which the tyre can carry the load indicated by the associated load-capacity index (e.g., F=80 km/h, G=90 km/h, up to H=210 km/h).
- **Tyre Class**:
    - **Class C2**: Load capacity index in single formation ≤ 121 and speed category symbol ≥ "N".
    - **Class C3**: Load capacity index in single formation ≥ 122; OR load capacity index ≤ 121 and speed category symbol ≤ "M".

## 3. Markings
Tyres submitted for approval must display specified markings on the sidewall(s), including:
- Manufacturer's name or trade mark.
- Tyre-size designation.
- Structure indication (e.g., "R" for radial).
- Speed-category symbol(s).
- "M+S", "M.S" or "M&S" for snow tyres.
- Load-capacity indices.
- "TUBELESS" if applicable.
- Date of manufacture (four digits: week and year).
- Regroovable symbol ("Ω") or word if applicable.
- "PSI" index for test inflation pressure.
- Tyre to rim fitment configuration symbol (if applicable, for tyres approved after 1 March 2004).
- "MPT" (or "ML") and/or "POR" for special use tyres.
- Suffixes "C", "LT", or "CP" after rim diameter marking under specified conditions.
- "FRT" for free-rolling tyres designed for trailer axles.
Markings and the approval mark must be moulded, clearly legible, and indelible, located on at least one lower sidewall (with exceptions for specific fitment configurations).

## 4. Application for Approval
Submitted by the manufacturer or representative, specifying details such as tyre-size designation, manufacturer, category of use, structure, speed category, load-capacity indices, dimensions, and test parameters. Must be accompanied by sketches/photographs and either a test report or samples.

## 5. Approval
Granted if requirements are met. An approval number is assigned. Notice is communicated via a form (Annex 1). An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country number, plus approval number) must be affixed to conforming tyres. Provisions for subsequent retreading in accordance with Regulation No. 109 are included.

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. Dimensions of Tyres
- **Section width**: Calculated by formula `S = S1 + K(A - A1)`, with specific values for different tyre types and fitment configurations.
- **Outer diameter**: Calculated by formula `D = d + 2H`, with specific values for listed tyre sizes and fitment configurations.
- **Method of measuring**: Described in Annex 6.
- **Tolerances**: Specified for overall width and outer diameter relative to calculated values, varying by tyre structure, category of use, and size.

### 6.2. Load/Speed Endurance Test
- Each tyre type must undergo at least one test per procedure in Annex 7.
- Post-test criteria: No tread, ply, cord separation, chunking, or broken cords. Outer diameter change ≤ 3.5%.
- Additional tests required for additional load/speed combinations (per paragraph 6.2.5.).
- Specific test programmes for tyres with speed category symbol Q and above.

### 6.3. Tread Pattern
- **Special use tyre**: Block tread pattern with specified minimum tread depth and void-to-fill ratio (≥11 mm and ≥35% for C2; ≥16 mm and ≥35% for C3).
- **Professional off-road tyre**: Additional requirements including maximum speed rating (≤Q for C2, ≤K for C3).

## 7. Modification and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified. Tread pattern modification does not necessitate retesting. Confirmation/refusal of approval is communicated.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Manufactured tyres must conform to the approved type. Verification of control methods by the Type Approval Authority at least once every two years per production facility.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if production non-conformity is found or if series tyres fail tests. Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority upon ceasing manufacture, who then notifies other Parties.

## 11. Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Parties must communicate details of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat. Use of manufacturers' laboratories is permitted under certain conditions.

## Annexes
1.  **Communication Form**: Model for approval/refusal/withdrawal notifications.
2.  **Arrangement of Approval Mark**: Example and specifications.
3.  **Arrangement of Tyre Markings**: Examples and minimum height requirements.
4.  **List of Load-Capacity Indices**: Table mapping indices (60-200) to maximum mass in kg.
5.  **Tyre-Size Designation and Dimensions**:
    - Part I: European tyres (Tables A-D for various rim types and applications).
    - Part II: United States tyres (Tables A-E for various vehicle types and services, with specific tolerances).
6.  **Method of Measuring Pneumatic Tyres**: Procedure for measuring overall width and outer diameter.
7.  **Procedure for Load/Speed Endurance Tests**:
    - Preparation and test procedure.
    - Specific programme for tyres with speed category symbol Q and above.
    - Appendix 1: Endurance-test programme table (load and speed steps for different load indices and speed categories).
    - Appendix 2: Relation between "PSI" pressure index and pressure units (bar, kPa).
8.  **Variation of Load Capacity with Speed**: Table showing permissible load variation percentages for different speeds, load indices, and speed categories.
9.  **Communication - Upgrade of Service Description for Retreading**: Form for authorizing retreading of tyres to a higher service description under Regulation No. 109.
