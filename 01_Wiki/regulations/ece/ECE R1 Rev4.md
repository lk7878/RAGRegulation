# UN Regulation No. 1 (Revision 4, Amendment 2) - Provisions concerning the approval of motor vehicle headlamps emitting an asymmetrical passing beam or a driving beam or both and equipped with filament lamps

## 2.2. Test results
### 2.2.1.
The result expressed in milliradians (mrad) shall be considered as acceptable when the absolute value Δ r1= r3- r60 recorded on the headlamp is not more than 1.0 mrad (A r1 ≤ 1.0 mrad).

### 2.2.2.
However, if this value is more than 1.0 mrad but not more than 1.5 mrad(1.0 mrad < △ r≤ 1.5 mrad) a second headlamp shall be tested as described in paragraph 2.1 of this annex after being subjected three consecutive times to the cycle as described below, in order to stabilize the position of mechanical parts of the headlamp on a base representative of the correct installation on the vehicle:
- Operation of the passing lamp for one hour(the voltage shall be adjusted as specified in paragraph 1.1.1.2.above).
- Period of rest for one hour.

The headlamp type shall be considered as acceptable if the mean value of the absolute values Δ r measured on the first sample and Δ r measured on the second sample is not more than 1.0 mrad (△ r1+△ rⅡ)≤ 1.0 mrad.

## 3. CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
One of the sampled headlamps shall be tested according to the procedure described in paragraph 2.1. above after being subjected three consecutive times to the cycle described in paragraph 2.2.2. above. The headlamp shall be considered as acceptable if a r does not exceed 1.5 mrad.

If this value exceeds 1.5 mrad, but is not more than 2.0 mrad, a second headlamp shall be subjected to the test after which the mean of the absolute values recorded on both samples shall not exceed 1.5 mrad.

## Annex 5 - EXAMPLES OF ARRANGEMENTS OF APPROVAL MARKS
(See paragraph 4 of this Regulation)

The headlamp bearing the approval marking shown above is a headlamp meeting the requirements of this Regulation in respect of both the driving beam and the passing beam, and which is designed for right-hand traffic only.

**Note:**
The approval number and the additional symbols shall be placed close to the circle and either above or below the letter "E", or to the right or left of that letter. The digits of the approval number shall be on the same side of the letter "E" and face the same direction.
The use of Roman numerals as approval numbers should be avoided so as to prevent any confusion with other symbols.

## Annex 7 - REQUIREMENTS FOR LAMPS INCORPORATING LENSES OF PLASTIC MATERIAL - TESTING OF LENS OR MATERIAL SAMPLES AND OF COMPLETE LAMPS
### 1. GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS
Specifies requirements for samples of plastic lenses/material and complete lamps supplied for approval.

### 2. TESTS
Describes a series of tests for plastic lenses/materials and complete lamps:
- **2.1 Resistance to temperature changes:** Cycle test with photometric measurement before/after. Variation ≤ 10%.
- **2.2 Resistance to atmospheric and chemical agents:** Exposure to radiation and chemical mixture. Requirements for surface integrity and limits on mean transmission variation (Δtm ≤ 0.020) and diffusion variation (Δdm ≤ 0.020).
- **2.3 Resistance to detergents and hydrocarbons:** Immersion and rubbing tests. Limit on mean transmission variation (Δtm ≤ 0.010).
- **2.4 Resistance to mechanical deterioration:** Uniform abrasion test (spray method). Limits on mean transmission (Δtm < 0.100) and diffusion (Δdm ≤ 0.050) variation.
- **2.5 Test of adherence of coatings:** Grid-cut and tape pull test. Impairment area ≤ 15% of gridded surface.
- **2.6 Tests of the complete lamp:** Mechanical deterioration and coating adherence tests on complete lamp samples, with specific photometric tolerance limits (e.g., not exceeding max values by >30% at certain points).

### 3. VERIFICATION OF THE CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Criteria for series production conformity regarding lens materials and photometric performance after testing.

### Appendices to Annex 7:
- **Appendix 1:** Chronological order of approval tests for plastic materials and complete lamps.
- **Appendix 2:** Method of measurement of the diffusion and transmission of light, including equipment diagram and measurement procedure.
- **Appendix 3:** Spray testing method for mechanical deterioration, specifying equipment (spray gun, test mixture) and procedure until a reference diffusion change (Δd = 0.0250 ± 0.0025) is achieved.
- **Appendix 4:** Adhesive tape adherence test method to determine linear force of adhesion under standard conditions.
