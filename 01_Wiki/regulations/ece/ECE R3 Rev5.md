# UN Regulation No. 3 - Retro-reflecting Devices

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to retro-reflecting devices for vehicles of categories L, M, N, O, and T.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **Retro-reflection**: Reflection in directions close to the direction from which the light came.
- **Retro-reflecting optical unit**: A combination of optical components producing retro-reflection.
- **Retro-reflecting device**: An assembly ready for use comprising one or more retro-reflecting optical units.
- **Coefficient of luminous intensity (CIL)**: Quotient of the luminous intensity reflected in the direction considered, divided by the illumination of the retro-reflecting device.
- **Classes**: Devices are divided into three classes according to photometric characteristics: **Class IA or IB**, **Class IIIA or IIIB**, and **Class IVA**. Classes IB and IIIB are devices combined with other non-watertight signal lamps integrated into the vehicle body.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application must be submitted by the holder of the trade name/mark and include drawings, technical descriptions, and samples as specified.

## 4. Markings
Every device must bear:
- The trade name or mark.
- The word "TOP" if necessary to determine the angle of rotation.
- For devices with reduced light distribution, a specific vertical arrow symbol.
- A space for the approval mark.

## 5. Approval
- Approval is granted if samples meet requirements.
- An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments.
- An international approval mark (circle with "E", country number, approval number, and class symbol) must be affixed.
- Specific rules apply for grouped, combined, or reciprocally incorporated lamps.

## 6. General Specifications
Devices must be well-constructed, durable, not easily dismantled, easy to clean, and have no accessible inner surface in normal use. Class IVA devices must have stable fixation means.

## 7. Special Specifications (Tests)
Devices must satisfy requirements for dimensions, shape, colorimetry, photometry, and physical/mechanical properties as per Annexes 5-11 and 13-16. Test procedures differ by class.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Production must conform to the approved type. Verification involves control procedures (Annex 17) and sampling by an inspector (Annex 18). Type Approval Authorities verify control methods at least once every two years.

## 9. Penalties for Non-conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or if marked devices do not conform to the approved type.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority upon ceasing manufacture.

## 11. Technical Services and Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities.

## 12. Transitional Provisions
- As from 24 months after UN R150 enters into force, Contracting Parties shall cease granting new approvals to this Regulation.
- Extensions of approval for this and previous series of amendments shall not be refused.
- Approvals for replacement devices for vehicles in use may continue to be granted.

## Annexes (Summary)
- **Annex 1**: Symbols and units.
- **Annex 2**: Communication form for approval notifications.
- **Annex 3**: Examples of approval marks.
- **Annex 4**: Test procedure for Class IA and IIIA devices.
- **Annex 5**: Specifications of shape and dimensions for all classes.
- **Annex 6**: Colorimetric specifications.
- **Annex 7**: Photometric specifications and CIL values tables for all classes.
- **Annex 8**: Resistance to external agents (water, dirt, corrosion, fuels, oils, abrasion).
- **Annex 9**: Stability in time of optical properties.
- **Annex 10**: Resistance to heat.
- **Annex 11**: Colour-fastness.
- **Annex 12**: Chronological order of tests for Classes IA/IIIA.
- **Annex 13**: Resistance to impact for Class IVA.
- **Annex 14**: Test procedure for Class IVA.
- **Annex 15**: Chronological order of tests for Class IVA.
- **Annex 16**: Test procedure for Classes IB and IIIB devices.
- **Annex 17**: Minimum requirements for conformity of production control.
- **Annex 18**: Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector.
