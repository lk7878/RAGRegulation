# Regulation No. 31 - Halogen Sealed-Beam Headlamps (HSB)

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to headlamps for vehicles of categories M, N, and T.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Halogen sealed-beam headlamp unit (HSB unit):** An indivisible headlamp unit with integral reflector, optical system, and halogen light source(s). Categories: 1 (driving beam only), 21 (passing beam only), 22 (both beams).
*   **Lens:** The outermost light-transmitting component.
*   **Coating:** Product(s) applied to the lens outer face.
*   **HSB units of different types:** Differ in trade name, optical characteristics, components altering optical effects, rated voltage, filament shape, beam kind, or lens/coating materials.
*   **Colour of the light emitted:** As defined in Regulation No. 48.

## 3. Application for Approval
Submitted by the trade name owner. Must specify beam type(s) and traffic handedness. Requires drawings, technical description, and samples (5 for uncoloured light, specific combinations for coloured light). For plastic lenses, 13 lenses (or material samples) and a compatible reflector are required.

## 4. Markings
Units must bear the applicant's trade name/mark, a space for the approval mark, and the rated voltage/wattage.

## 5. Approval
*   Granted if samples meet requirements.
*   An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate the latest amendment series, e.g., 03).
*   An approval mark must be affixed, consisting of:
    *   A circle with "E" and country code.
    *   The approval number.
    *   Additional symbols indicating traffic handedness (arrow), beam type (HSC, HSR, HSCR), maximum luminous intensity reference mark, and "PL" for plastic lenses.

## 6. General Specifications
*   Must withstand normal use and vibrations.
*   Must have an adjustment device (with exceptions).
*   Terminals must be robust and fixed.
*   Electrical connections must conform to Annex 3.
*   Complementary tests for photometric stability (Annex 6) and plastic lens tests (Annex 7) are required.

## 7. Rated and Test Values
*   Rated voltage: 12 V.
*   Maximum wattage at 13.2 V: 75 W (driving beam), 68 W (passing beam).

## 8. Illumination
Measured on a vertical screen 25 m forward.

### 8.2 Passing Beam Requirements
*   Must produce a sharp horizontal "cut-off".
*   Specific illumination values are required at designated points (B 50 L, 75 L, 75 R, 50 R, 50 V, 25 L, 25 R) and within zones I, III, and IV, with different limits for right-hand and left-hand traffic units.

### 8.3 Driving Beam Requirements
*   Maximum illumination (E_M) must be between 48 lux and 240 lux.
*   For combined beams, E_M must not exceed 16 times the illumination at point 75 R (or 75 L).
*   Minimum illumination levels are specified horizontally from point HV.

## 9. Colour
HSB units shall emit white light.

## 10. Gauging of Discomfort
The discomfort caused by the passing beam shall be gauged.

## 11. Conformity of Production
*   Products must conform to the approved type.
*   The approval holder must have quality control procedures, test equipment, and record results.
*   The Type Approval Authority conducts inspections and may test samples.

## 12. Penalties for Non-Conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or if marked units do not conform.

## 13. Modification and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified. The Authority may confirm, require further tests, or refuse. Extensions are communicated to other Parties.

## 14. Production Definitively Discontinued
The holder must inform the Authority, which notifies other Parties.

## 15. Transitional Provisions
*   Approvals according to earlier series of amendments remain valid indefinitely.
*   Replacements for vehicles in use may continue to be approved to this Regulation.

## 16. Names and Addresses
Parties must communicate the details of their Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretary-General.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Communication form model.
*   **Annex 2:** Examples of approval mark arrangements.
*   **Annex 3:** Electrical connection diagrams for HSB unit categories.
*   **Annex 4:** Measuring screen diagrams for right-hand and left-hand traffic.
*   **Annex 5:** Minimum requirements for conformity of production control procedures, including test tolerances.
*   **Annex 6:** Tests for stability of photometric performance (clean and dirty conditions) and change in cut-off line under heat.
*   **Annex 7:** Requirements and tests for lamps with plastic lenses (resistance to temperature, chemicals, detergents, abrasion, coating adherence).
*   **Annex 8:** Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector, defining acceptance/contesting criteria.
