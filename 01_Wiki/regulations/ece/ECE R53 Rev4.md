**1. Scope**
This Regulation applies to vehicles of category L3 with regard to the installation of lighting and light-signalling devices.

**2. Definitions**
For the purpose of this Regulation:
- **2.1. "Approval of a vehicle"**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to the number and mode of installation of the lighting and light-signalling devices.
- **2.2. "Vehicle type"**: A category of vehicles which do not differ in essential respects such as dimensions, external shape, and the number and position of devices.
- **2.3. "Transverse plane"**: A vertical plane perpendicular to the median longitudinal plane of the vehicle.
- **2.4. "Unladen vehicle"**: Vehicle without driver or passenger, unladen, with full fuel tank and normal complement of tools.
- **2.5. "Lamp"**: Device designed to illuminate the road or emit a light signal. Includes rear registration plate lamps and retro-reflectors.
    - **2.5.1. "Equivalent lamps"**: Lamps with the same function, authorized in the country of registration, possibly with different characteristics.
    - **2.5.2. "Independent lamp"**: Devices with separate apparent surfaces, light sources, and lamp bodies.
    - **2.5.3. "Grouped lamps"**: Devices with separate apparent surfaces and light sources, but a common lamp body.
    - **2.5.4. "Combined"**: Devices with separate apparent surfaces, but a common light source and lamp body.
    - **2.5.5. "Reciprocally incorporated"**: Devices with separate light sources or a single source operating under different conditions, with totally or partially common apparent surfaces and a common lamp body.
    - **2.5.6. "Driving-beam (main-beam) headlamp"**: Lamp used to illuminate the road over a long distance ahead.
    - **2.5.7. "Passing-beam (dipped-beam) headlamp"**: Lamp used to illuminate the road ahead without dazzling oncoming drivers.
 - **2.5.7.1. "Principal passing-beam (principal dipped beam)"**: Dipped beam produced without the contribution of infrared (IR) emitters and/or additional light sources for bend lighting.
    - **2.5.8. "Direction indicator lamp"**: Lamp used to indicate intent to change direction.
    - **2.5.9. "Stop lamp"**: Lamp used to indicate application of the service brake.
    - **2.5.10. "Rear-registration-plate illuminating device"**: Device used to illuminate the space for the rear registration plate.
    - **2.5.11. "Front position lamp"**: Lamp indicating the presence of the vehicle when viewed from the front.
    - **2.5.12. "Rear position lamp"**: Lamp indicating the presence of the vehicle when viewed from the rear.
    - **2.5.13. "Retro-reflector"**: Device indicating presence by reflecting light from an external source.
    - **2.5.14. "Hazard warning signal"**: Simultaneous operation of all direction indicator lamps.
    - **2.5.15. "Front fog lamp"**: Lamp to improve road illumination in fog, snow, rain, or dust.
    - **2.5.16. "Rear fog lamp"**: Lamp to make the vehicle more visible from the rear in dense fog.
    - **2.5.17. "Daytime running lamp"**: Forward-facing lamp to make the vehicle more visible during daytime.
    - **2.5.18. "Interdependent lamp system"**: Assembly of two or three interdependent lamps providing the same function.
 - **2.5.18.1. "Interdependent lamp marked 'Y'"**: Device operating as part of an interdependent lamp system.
    - **2.5.19. "Lamps marked 'D'"**: Independent lamps approved as separate devices, allowed to be used independently or in an assembly of two as a "single lamp".
- **2.6. "Light-emitting surface"**: All or part of the exterior surface of the transparent material as declared by the manufacturer.
- **2.7. "Illuminating surface"**:
    - **2.7.1. "Illuminating surface of a lighting device"** (headlamps, fog lamps): Orthogonal projection of the full aperture of the reflector (or projection lens) on a transverse plane.
    - **2.7.2. "Illuminating surface of a light-signalling device"** (except retro-reflectors): Orthogonal projection bounded by screens allowing only 98% of total luminous intensity.
    - **2.7.3. "Illuminating surface of a retro-reflector"**: Orthogonal projection delimited by planes continuous to the outermost parts of the optical system.
- **2.8. "Apparent surface"**: For a defined direction of observation, the orthogonal projection of either the boundary of the illuminating surface or the light-emitting surface.
- **2.9. "Axis of reference" ("reference axis")**: Characteristic axis determined by the lamp manufacturer for photometric measurements and installation.
- **2.10. "Centre of reference"**: Intersection of the axis of reference with the exterior light-emitting surface.
- **2.11. "Angles of geometric visibility"**: Angles determining the minimum solid angle field in which the apparent surface of the lamp shall be visible.
- **2.12. "Extreme outer edge"**: Plane parallel to the median longitudinal plane touching the lateral extremity of the vehicle, disregarding projections of mirrors, direction indicators, position lamps, and retro-reflectors.
- **2.13. "Over-all width"**: Distance between the two vertical planes defined in paragraph 2.12.
- **2.14. "A single lamp"** means:
    - (a) A device with one function, one or more light sources, and one apparent surface.
    - (b) Any assembly of two lamps marked "D".
    - (c) Any assembly of two independent retro-reflectors approved separately.
    - (d) Any interdependent lamp system composed of two or three interdependent lamps marked "Y".
- **2.15. "Distance between two lamps"**: Shortest distance between the two apparent surfaces in the direction of the reference axis.
- **2.16. "Operating tell-tale"**: Visual or auditory signal indicating a device is switched on and operating correctly.
- **2.17. "Circuit-closed tell-tale"**: Visual signal indicating a device is switched on, but not necessarily operating correctly.
- **2.18. "Optional lamp"**: Lamp whose installation is at the manufacturer's discretion.
- **2.19. "Ground"**: Substantially horizontal surface on which the vehicle stands.
- **2.20. "Device"**: Component or combination of components used to perform one or several functions.
- **2.21. "Colour of the light emitted"**: As defined in UN Regulation No. 48 and its amendments in force at the time of type approval application.
- **2.22. "Gross vehicle mass" or "maximum mass"**: Technically permissible maximum laden mass declared by the manufacturer.
- **2.23. "Laden"**: Loaded to attain the gross vehicle mass.
- **2.24. "Horizontal inclination"**: Angle between the beam pattern when the motorcycle is set as specified and when it is banked.
- **2.25. "Horizontal inclination adjustment system (HIAS)"**: Device that adjusts the horizontal inclination of the headlamp towards zero.
- **2.26. "Bank angle"**: Angle made with the vertical by the vertical longitudinal median plane when the motorcycle is rotated about its longitudinal axis.
- **2.27. "HIAS signal"**: Any control signal, additional control input, or control output to/from the system.
- **2.28. "HIAS signal generator"**: Device reproducing one or more HIAS signals for system test.
- **2.29. "HIAS test angle"**: Angle δ created by the headlamp cut-off line and HH line.
- **2.30. "Bend lighting"**: Lighting function to provide enhanced illumination in bends.
- **2.31. "H plane"**: Horizontal plane containing the centre of reference of the lamp.
- **2.32. "Sequential activation"**: Electrical connection where individual light sources of a lamp are activated in a predetermined sequence.
- **2.33. "Emergency stop signal"**: Signal to indicate to other road users that a high retardation force has been applied.

**3. Application for approval**
- **3.1.** Submitted by vehicle manufacturer or accredited representative.
- **3.2.** Accompanied by documents in triplicate: description of vehicle type, list of lighting/light-signalling devices, layout drawing of installation, and if necessary, layout drawing of each lamp.
- **3.3.** An unladen vehicle representative of the type, fitted with complete equipment, shall be submitted.

**4. Approval**
- **4.1.** Granted if vehicle meets requirements for all devices specified.
- **4.2.** Approval number assigned. First two digits indicate series of amendments.
- **4.3.** Notice communicated via form in Annex 1.
- **4.4.** International approval mark affixed: circle with "E" and country number, followed by "53R" and approval number.
- **4.5.** If vehicle conforms to other Regulations, additional symbols placed in vertical columns.
- **4.6.** Mark shall be legible and indelible.
- **4.7.** Mark placed close to or on vehicle data plate.
- **4.8.** Examples in Annex 2.

**5. General specifications**
- **5.1.** Devices fitted to retain prescribed characteristics under normal use and vibration.
- **5.2.** Illuminating lamps installed for easy orientation adjustment.
- **5.3.** Reference axis of light-signalling devices parallel to bearing plane; tolerance ±3°. For side retro-reflectors, perpendicular to median longitudinal plane; for others, parallel.
- **5.4.** Height and orientation verified with vehicle unladen on flat surface, median plane vertical, handlebars straight, tyre pressures as prescribed.
- **5.5.** In absence of specific instructions:
    - **5.5.1.** Single lamps mounted with centre of reference in median longitudinal plane.
    - **5.5.2.** Lamps constituting a pair: mounted symmetrically, symmetrical to each other, same colorimetric requirements, identical photometric characteristics, come on/off simultaneously.
- **5.6.** Grouped, combined, reciprocally incorporated or single lamps:
    - **5.6.1.** Allowed provided all requirements (colour, position, etc.) are fulfilled.
    - **5.6.2.** Single lamps:
 - **5.6.2.1.** Composed of distinct parts: projection area ≥60% of smallest circumscribing quadrilateral OR distance between facing edges ≤75 mm.
 - **5.6.2.2.** Composed of two lamps marked "D" or two independent retro-reflectors: projection area ≥60% OR distance ≤75 mm.
 - **5.6.2.3.** Interdependent lamp system: must fulfil 5.6.2.1.
- **5.7.** Height measured from highest/lowest point of apparent surface. For passing-beam, minimum height from lowest point of effective outlet.
- **5.8.** Only direction indicators, hazard warning, and emergency stop signal may flash.
    - **5.8.1.** Photometric characteristics of direction indicators may be varied by sequential activation.
- **5.9.** No red light visible towards front, no white light visible towards rear. Verified as per Annex 4.
- **5.10.** Electrical connections: front position lamp (or passing-beam if no front position), rear position lamp, and rear-registration-plate illuminating device switch on/off simultaneously.
    - **5.10.1.** In interdependent lamp system, all light sources switch on/off simultaneously.
- **5.11.** Driving-beam, passing-beam, and fog lamp cannot be switched on unless lamps in 5.10 are on. Exception for luminous warnings.
    - **5.11.1.** Daytime running lamp (if installed) automatically ON when engine running, OFF when headlamp on. If no DRL, headlamp automatically on when engine running.
- **5.12.** Tell-tale lamps:
    - **5.12.1.** Readily visible to driver.
    - **5.12.2.** "Circuit-closed" tell-tale may be replaced by "operating" tell-tale.
- **5.13.** Colours of lights:
    - Driving-beam headlamp: white
    - Passing-beam headlamp: white
    - Direction-indicator lamp: amber
    - Stop lamp: red
    - Rear-registration plate lamp: white
    - Front position lamp: white or amber
    - Rear position lamp: red
    - Rear retro-reflector, non-triangular: red
    - Side retro-reflector, non-triangular: amber (front), amber or red (rear)
    - Vehicle-hazard warning signal: amber
    - Front fog lamp: white or selective yellow
    - Rear fog lamp: red
    - Daytime running lamp: white
    - Emergency stop signal: amber or red
- **5.14.** Mandatory devices: driving-beam headlamp, passing-beam headlamp, direction indicators, stop lamp (S1 or UN R50), rear-registration-plate illuminating device, front position lamp, rear position lamp, rear retro-reflector (non-triangular), side retro-reflector (non-triangular).
- **5.15.** Optional devices: hazard warning signal, front fog lamp, rear fog lamp, daytime running lamp, stop lamp (S3), emergency stop signal.
- **5.16.** Fitting in conformity with paragraph 6.
- **5.17.** Fitting of other devices prohibited for type approval.
- **5.18.** Devices type-approved for M1 and N1 vehicles may also be fitted to motorcycles.
- **5.19.** Rear position lamps, direction-indicators, and retro-reflectors on movable components only if requirements met at all fixed positions.
- **5.20.** General provisions on geometric visibility:
    - **5.20.1.** No obstacle inside angles of geometric visibility.
    - **5.20.2.** If measurements closer, shift direction parallel.
    - **5.20.3.** If part hidden, prove remaining part conforms to photometric values.
    - **5.20.4.** Vertical angle below horizontal may be reduced to 5° if lamp <750 mm above ground.
    - **5.20.5.** For interdependent lamp system, requirements fulfilled when all lamps operated together.

**6. Individual specifications**
- **6.1. Driving-beam headlamp**
    - **6.1.1. Number**: One or two, depending on cylinder capacity and approved type (UN R113, 112, 1, 8, 20, 57, 72, 98).
    - **6.1.3. Position**: Width, length, height (500-1300 mm), distance between lamps ≤200 mm.
    - **6.1.4. Geometric visibility**: Within divergent space of ≥5°.
    - **6.1.5. Orientation**: Forwards, may move with steering. HIAS may be installed.
    - **6.1.6. Electrical connections**: Passing-beam may remain illuminated with driving-beam.
    - **6.1.7. Tell-tales**: Mandatory non-flashing blue "circuit-closed" tell-tale. Mandatory flashing amber "HIAS failure" tell-tale.
    - **6.1.8. Other requirements**: Aggregate max intensity ≤430,000 cd. In HIAS failure, possible to deactivate HIAS and re-position beam.
- **6.2. Passing-beam headlamp**
    - **6.2.1. Number**: One or two, depending on cylinder capacity and approved type (UN R113, 112, 1, 8, 20, 57, 72, 98).
    - **6.2.3. Position**: Width, height (500-1200 mm), length, distance between lamps ≤200 mm.
    - **6.2.4. Geometric visibility**: α=15° up, 10° down; β=45° left/right (single) or 45° out, 10° in (pair).
    - **6.2.5. Orientation**: Forwards, may move with steering. Vertical inclination -0.5% to -2.5%. For light source >2000 lumens, automatic levelling device may be used. HIAS may be installed, shall not adjust more than bank angle. Bend lighting only with principal passing-beam.
    - **6.2.6. Electrical connections**: Control for passing-beam switches off driving-beam. Bend lighting connected to activate only with principal passing-beam and based on bank angle.
    - **6.2.7. Tell-tales**: Optional non-flashing green "circuit-closed". Mandatory flashing amber "HIAS failure". Bend lighting switches OFF automatically on control system failure.
    - **6.2.8. Other requirements**: In HIAS failure, possible to deactivate HIAS and re-position beam.
- **6.3. Direction indicator lamp**
    - **6.3.1. Number**: Two per side.
    - **6.3.2. Arrangement**: Two front (cat.1/UN R6 or 11/UN R50), two rear (cat.2/UN R6 or 12/UN R50).
    - **6.3.3. Position**: Width: front min 240 mm between, outside headlamp plane, min separation based on intensity; rear min 180 mm between. Height: 350-1200 mm. Length: rear not >300 mm from rearmost limit.
    - **6.3.4. Geometric visibility**: Horizontal: 20° in, 80° out. Vertical: 15° up/down (may be 5° down if <750 mm).
    - **6.3.5. Orientation**: Front may move with steering.
    - **6.3.6. Electrical connections**: Switch on independently, all on one side by one control.
    - **6.3.7.** May not be reciprocally incorporated except with amber front position lamp.
    - **6.3.8. "Operating" tell-tale**: Mandatory flashing green (or auditory).
    - **6.3.9. Other requirements**: Flashing frequency 90±30/min. May flash synchronously or alternately. Light appears within 1s, first extinction within 1.5s. On failure of one, others continue.
- **6.4. Stop lamp**
    - **6.4.1. Number**: One or two (S1/UN R7 or UN R50). Optional one (S3/UN R7).
    - **6.4.3. Position**: Height: S1/R50: 250-1500 mm; S3: ≥850 mm and above S1/R50. Length: at rear.
    - **6.4.4. Geometric visibility**: S1/R50: Horizontal: 45° L/R (single) or 45° out, 10° in (pair). Vertical: 15° up/down (may be 5° down if <750 mm). S3: Horizontal: 10° L/R. Vertical: 10° up, 5° down.
    - **6.4.5. Orientation**: Rearwards.
    - **6.4.6. Electrical connections**: All light up simultaneously on brake application.
    - **6.4.7. "Tell-tale"**: Optional non-flashing warning light for malfunction.
- **6.5. Rear-registration-plate illuminating device**
    - **6.5.1. Number**: One (cat.2/UN R50), may consist of several components.
    - **6.5.6. Tell-tale**: Optional, function performed by position lamp tell-tale.
    - **6.5.7. Other requirements**: Photometric characteristics may be modified when combined/reciprocally incorporated with stop or rear fog lamp.
- **6.6. Front position lamp**
    - **6.6.1. Number**: One or two (white) or two (amber, one per side).
    - **6.6.3. Position**: Width, height (350-1200 mm), length at front.
    - **6.6.4. Geometric visibility**: Horizontal: 80° L/R (single) or 80° out, 20° in (pair). Vertical: 15° up/down (may be 5° down if <750 mm).
    - **6.6.5. Orientation**: Forwards, may move with steering.
    - **6.6.6. "Circuit-closed" tell-tale**: Mandatory non-flashing green.
    - **6.6.7. Other requirements**: If reciprocally incorporated with front direction indicator, position lamp switched off when indicator flashing.
- **6.7. Rear position lamp**
    - **6.7.1. Number**: One or two.
    - **6.7.3. Position**: Height: 250-1500 mm, length at rear.
    - **6.7.4. Geometric visibility**: Horizontal: 80° L/R (single) or 80° out, 45° in (pair). Vertical: 15° up/down (may be 5° down if <750 mm).
    - **6.7.5. Orientation**: Rearwards.
    - **6.7.6. "Circuit-closed" tell-tale**: Optional, function by front position lamp tell-tale.
    - **6.7.7. Other requirements**: If reciprocally incorporated with direction indicator, may be switched OFF during activation.
- **6.8. Rear retro-reflector, non-triangular**
    - **6.8.1. Number**: One or two.
    - **6.8.3. Position**: Height: 250-900 mm.
    - **6.8.4. Geometric visibility**: Horizontal: 30° L/R (single) or 30° out, 10° in (pair). Vertical: 15° up/down (may be 5° down if <750 mm).
    - **6.8.5. Orientation**: Rearwards.
- **6.9. Vehicle-hazard warning signal**
    - **6.9.1.** Given by simultaneous operation of direction indicators.
    - **6.9.2. Electrical connections**: Separate control, may activate automatically after collision or emergency stop signal deactivation.
    - **6.9.3. "Circuit-closed" tell-tale**: Mandatory flashing red or simultaneous operation of direction indicator tell-tale.
    - **6.9.4. Other requirements**: Flashing 90±30/min. Light appears within 1s, first extinction within 1.5s.
- **6.10. Front fog lamp**
    - **6.10.1. Number**: One or two.
    - **6.10.3. Position**: Width: single centre or ≤250 mm from median plane; pair symmetrical. Height: ≥250 mm, not higher than passing-beam. Length at front.
    - **6.10.4. Geometric visibility**: α=5° up/down; β=45° L/R (single, except off-centre inward 10°); β=45° out, 10° in (pair).
    - **6.10.5. Orientation**: Forwards, may move with steering.
    - **6.10.6.** May not be combined with any other front lamp.
    - **6.10.7. "Circuit-closed" tell-tale**: Optional non-flashing green.
    - **6.10.9. Electrical connections**: May be switched on/off independently of headlamps.
- **6.11. Rear fog lamp**
    - **6.11.1. Number**: One or two.
    - **6.11.3. Position**: Height: 250-900 mm, length at rear, distance from stop lamp ≥100 mm.
    - **6.11.4. Geometric visibility**: α=5° up/down; β=25° L/R (single) or 25° out, 10° in (pair).
    - **6.11.5. Orientation**: Rearwards.
    - **6.11.6. Electrical connections**: Can light only when driving-beam, passing-beam, or front fog lamp on. May be switched off independently of front fog lamp. May remain on until position lamps off.
    - **6.11.7. "Circuit-closed" tell-tale**: Mandatory non-flashing amber.
- **6.12. Side retro-reflector, non-triangular**
    - **6.12.1. Number per side**: One or two.
    - **6.12.3. Position**: On side, height 300-900 mm, length not masked by clothes.
    - **6.12.4. Geometric visibility**: β=30° front/rear; α=15° up/down (may be 5° down if <750 mm).
    - **6.12.5. Orientation**: Perpendicular to median plane, directed outwards. Front may move with steering.
- **6.13. Daytime running lamp**
    - **6.13.1. Presence**: Optional.
    - **6.13.2. Number**: One or two (UN R87).
    - **6.13.4. Position**: Width: single centre or ≤250 mm from median plane; pair symmetrical, distance ≤420 mm (exceptions). Height: 250-1500 mm. Length at front.
    - **6.13.5. Geometric visibility**: Horizontal: out 20°, in 10°. Vertical: up 10°, down 10°.
    - **6.13.6. Orientation**: Forwards, may move with steering.
    - **6.13.7. Electrical connections**: Switches OFF automatically when headlamps ON (except for warnings). Rear position lamp ON when DRL ON. Front position and plate lamp may be ON. If ≤40 mm from front indicator, may switch OFF or reduce intensity during indicator activation. If reciprocally incorporated with indicator, switched OFF during activation.
    - **6.13.8. Tell-tale**: Optional closed-circuit green.
    - **6.13.9. Other requirements**: DRL symbol (ISO 2575) may be used.
- **6.14. Emergency stop signal**
    - **6.14.1. Presence**: Optional. Given by simultaneous operation of all stop or direction indicator lamps.
    - **6.14.2.-6.14.6.** As specified for direction indicators or stop lamps.
    - **6.14.7. Electrical connections**: All lamps flash in phase at 4.0±1.0 Hz (filament: 4.0 +0.0/-1.0 Hz). Operates independently. Activated automatically only when speed >50 km/h and emergency braking logic signal (UN R78) provided. Deactivated automatically if signal no longer provided or hazard warning activated.
    - **6.14.8. Tell-tale**: Optional.

**7. Modifications**
- **7.1.** Notified to Type Approval Authority, which may require further test.
- **7.2.** Confirmation/refusal communicated.
- **7.3.** Extension series number assigned.

**8. Conformity of production**
- **8.1.** Vehicles manufactured to conform to approved type.
- **8.2.** Minimum requirements in Annex 5 complied with.
- **8.3.** Type Approval Authority may verify control methods annually.

**9. Penalties for non-conformity**
- **9.1.** Approval may be withdrawn.
- **9.2.** Withdrawal notified.

**10. Production definitively discontinued**
Holder informs Type Approval Authority, which notifies other Parties.

**11. Transitional provisions**
- **11.1.-11.3.** Regarding Supplement 10 to 01 series.
- **11.4.-11.6.** Regarding 02 series of amendments.

**12. Names and addresses**
Contracting Parties communicate names/addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to UN Secretary-General.

**Annexes**
- **Annex 1**: Communication form.
- **Annex 2**: Arrangement of approval marks.
- **Annex 3**: Lamp surfaces, axis, centre, angles of geometric visibility.
- **Annex 4**: Forward visibility of red lights, rearward visibility of white lights.
- **Annex 5**: Control of conformity of production (tests for position, visibility, alignment, connections, intensities, presence/colour).
- **Annex 6**: Explanation of "horizontal inclination", "bank angle", and angle "δ".
