**Corrigendum Details**

This document is **Corrigendum 3** to **UN Regulation No. 66, Revision 1** (Uniform provisions concerning the approval of large passenger vehicles with regard to the strength of their superstructure).

**Issued:** 13 June 2007.

**Correction:**
The corrigendum specifies a single textual correction in the regulation's annexes:
*   **Location:** Annex 3, page 24, Paragraph 2.1.
*   **Correction:** Replace "rigidly held 200 mm above" with "rigidly held 100 mm above".

**Context:**
This corrigendum is issued under the framework of the **Revised 1958 Agreement** (Revision 2, including amendments effective 16 October 1995) concerning the adoption of uniform technical prescriptions for wheeled vehicles and reciprocal recognition of approvals. It also references a prior corrigendum (Corrigendum 2 to the 01 series of amendments).
