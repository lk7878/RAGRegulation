# UN Regulation No. 92 (Revision 1 - Amendment 2)

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of non-original replacement exhaust silencing systems (NORESS) for vehicles of categories L1, L2, L3, L4 and L5 with regard to sound emission**

**Date of entry into force:** 10 October 2017

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to non-original replacement exhaust silencing systems for vehicles of categories L1, L2, L3, L4 and L5.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
- **2.1. "Non-original replacement exhaust silencing system (NORESS)"**: A system of a type different from that fitted to the vehicle on approval or extension of approval, used only as a replacement.
- **2.5. "Approval of a NORESS or component(s) thereof"**: Approval of the whole or part of a silencing system adaptable to specified vehicle types regarding sound level limitation.
- **2.6. "Vehicle type"**: Vehicles which do not differ in essential respects such as engine type, drive train, and exhaust silencing system arrangement.

## 3. Application for approval
- Submitted by the manufacturer or accredited representative.
- Requires documents describing the vehicle type(s), the NORESS (with drawings and assembly instructions), and specifications.
- The Technical Service may request samples of the NORESS, the original system, and a representative test vehicle.

## 4. Markings
- Each NORESS component (excluding pipes/fittings) must bear the manufacturer's trade name/mark and commercial designation.
- Markings must be legible, indelible, and visible when fitted.
- Packaging or a label must provide manufacturer details and a list of intended vehicle models.
- Detailed mounting and handling instructions must be provided.

## 5. Approval
- Granted if the NORESS meets the specifications in paragraph 6.
- An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments applicable at the time of approval.
- An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, regulation number "92R", and approval number) must be affixed to approved NORESS.

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. General specifications
The silencer must be designed and constructed to:
- Ensure compliance under normal use and vibration.
- Display reasonable corrosion resistance.
- Not reduce original ground clearance or create safety hazards (sharp edges, high surface temperatures, interference with other parts).
- Be tamper-resistant while allowing for defined maintenance.

### 6.2. Specifications regarding sound levels
- Acoustic efficiency verified using methods in UN Regulations Nos. 9, 41, or 63.
- When fitted to the test vehicle, sound levels must not exceed those measured with the original silencing system.

### 6.3. Additional requirements
- **6.3.1. Tampering protection**: Construction must not permit easy removal of key silencing parts (e.g., baffles). If removable, attachment must cause permanent damage if removed.
- **6.3.2. Multi-mode NORESS**: Must meet all requirements in all operating modes. Reported sound levels are from the mode with highest levels.
- **6.3.3. Prohibition of defeat devices**: No intentional alteration or device solely for passing tests that is not operational during typical on-road use.
- **6.3.4. Additional sound emission provisions (ASEP)**: If designed for vehicles type-approved under the 04 series of amendments to UN R41, the NORESS must also fulfill ASEP requirements. A statement of compliance (Annex 4) is required.

### 6.4. Measurement of vehicle performance
- The NORESS must ensure vehicle performance is comparable to the original system.
- Maximum power and corresponding engine speed with the NORESS must not exceed values with the original system by more than ±5%.
- Test methods: engine dynamometer or vehicle roller dynamometer.

### 6.5. Additional provisions regarding fibrous materials
- Use of fibrous absorbent material is permitted only if requirements in Annex 3 are met.

### 6.6. Evaluation of pollutant emissions
- The test vehicle fitted with the NORESS must fulfill the pollution requirements according to its type approval.

## 7. Modification and extension of approval
- Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further tests.
- Extension of approval to other vehicle types follows the procedure in paragraph 3.

## 8. Conformity of production
- Procedures must comply with the 1958 Agreement, Appendix 2.
- Approved NORESS must be manufactured to conform to the approved type.
- The approval holder must carry out at least the tests prescribed in paragraph 6.
- Verification of conformity control by the authority at least once every two years.

## 9. Penalties for non-conformity of production
- Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met or if tests are failed.

## 10. Production definitively discontinued
- The approval holder must inform the authority if production of an approved type ceases.

## 11. Names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
- Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval (Parts A for vehicles under UN R41 04 series, B for vehicles under UN R9 or R63).
- **Annex 2**: Example of the approval mark.
- **Annex 3**: Requirements for fibrous absorbent materials used in NORESS (e.g., asbestos-free, retention tests, conditioning).
- **Annex 4**: Statement of Compliance with the Additional Sound Emission Provisions (ASEP).
