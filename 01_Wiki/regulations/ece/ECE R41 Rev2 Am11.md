**Amendment Details**

This document is **Amendment 11** to **Revision 2** of **Addendum 40 (UN Regulation No. 41)**. It is also designated as **Supplement 10 to the 04 series of amendments**.

**Key Amendments**

1.  **Paragraph 5.5.2.** is amended to specify the format for the approval marking: the Regulation number, followed by the letter "R", a dash, and the approval number, placed to the right of the prescribed circle.
2.  **Paragraph 5.6.** is amended regarding the marking for motorcycles approved under multiple UN Regulations. If a motorcycle conforms to a type approved under other Regulations, the main symbol need not be repeated. Instead, the numbers and symbols of all applicable Regulations granted by the same approving country are to be placed in vertical columns to the right of the main symbol.
3.  **Annex 2**:
    *   The introductory phrase for **Model A** (marking) is amended to read: "(See paragraph 5.5. of this Regulation)".
    *   The introductory phrase for **Model B** (marking) is amended to read: "(See paragraph 5.6. of this Regulation)".
