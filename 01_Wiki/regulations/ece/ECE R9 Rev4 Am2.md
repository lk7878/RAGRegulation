**UN Regulation No. 9 - Revision 4 - Amendment 2**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of category L2, L4 and L5 vehicles with regard to sound emission**

*   **Authentic and legally binding text:** `ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2022/3`.
*   **Date of entry into force:** 8 October 2022 (Supplement 2 to the 08 series of amendments).

**Transitional Provisions (Paragraph 11.)**

1.  From the entry into force date of the 08 series of amendments, Contracting Parties shall not refuse to grant or accept type approvals under this amended Regulation.
2.  From 1 September 2021, Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept new UN type approvals to the preceding series of amendments.
3.  Contracting Parties may grant type approvals according to any preceding series of amendments, but the test track specification may conform to ISO 10844:2014.
4.  Contracting Parties shall continue to grant extensions of existing approvals to any preceding series of amendments, with the test track optionally conforming to ISO 10844:2014.
5.  Until 1 September 2024, Contracting Parties shall accept UN type approvals to the preceding series of amendments, first issued before 1 September 2021.
6.  From 1 September 2024, Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept UN type approvals issued to the preceding series of amendments.
7.  Contracting Parties who start applying this Regulation after the most recent amendments' entry into force are not obliged to accept UN type approvals granted under preceding series.
8.  Notwithstanding point 6, Contracting Parties shall continue to accept UN type approvals issued under preceding series for vehicles/systems not affected by the 08 series amendments.

**Amendments to Annex 3 (Test Site and Conditions)**

*   **Paragraph 2.1.1. (Test site):** The surface of the test track shall conform to ISO 10844:2014.

**Amendments to Annex 5 (Test Track Layout)**

*   **Title:** Changed to "Test track layout".
*   **Deleted Elements:** Footnotes 1, 2, and 3; Paragraphs 1, 2 (and sub-paragraphs 2.1-2.5), 3 (and its extensive sub-structure 3.1, 3.2, etc.), Figure 2, Table 1, and Paragraphs 4 (4.1-4.3), 5 (5.1-5.3), 6 (6.1, 6.1.1-6.1.6, 6.1.6.1-6.1.6.7, 6.2) are all deleted.
*   **Figure 1:** Amended to show the "Test track layout with dimensions in meters". The key indicates the minimum area covered with the test road surface and microphone positions at a height of 1.2m. The source is cited as "ISO 10844:1994, with modifications."
