**Addendum 111 – UN Regulation No. 112**
**Revision 3 – Amendment 7**
**Supplement 1 to the 02 series of amendments – Date of entry into force: 29 May 2020**

This document constitutes Amendment 7 to Revision 3 of UN Regulation No. 112.

**Amendment Content:**
Add a new paragraph 5.12., to read:
"5.12. Instead of requirements of this Regulation, headlamps may conform with requirements of the latest version of UN Regulation No. 149 as it relates to headlamps emitting an asymmetric passing beam."
