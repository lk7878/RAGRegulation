# UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF HEADLAMPS FOR MOPEDS EMITTING A DRIVING BEAM AND A PASSING BEAM

**Regulation No. 76**

**Date of entry into force as an annex to the Agreement: 1 July 1988**

4.  **Markings**

## REGULATION TEXT

### 1. SCOPE
This regulation applies to the approval of headlamps using filament lamps, producing a driving beam and a passing beam, which are provided for the equipment of mopeds and vehicles treated as such.

### 2. DEFINITION OF "TYPE"
Headlamps of different "types" means headlamps which differ in such essential respects as:
*   2.1 The trade name or mark;
*   2.2 The characteristics of the optical system;
*   2.3 The inclusion or the suppression of components capable of altering the optical effects by reflection, refraction or absorption.
*   2.4 A change in the colour of the beam emitted by a headlamp whose other characteristics are not changed does not constitute a change of headlamp type.

### 3. APPLICATION FOR APPROVAL
*   3.1 The application for approval shall be submitted by the holder of the trade name or mark or by his duly accredited representative.
*   3.2 Every application shall be accompanied by:
    *   3.2.1 Drawings in triplicate, sufficiently detailed to permit identification of the type.
    *   3.2.2 A brief technical description stating, in particular, the category or categories of the filament lamp provided.
    *   3.2.3 Two devices with colourless lenses.

### 4. MARKINGS
*   4.1 Headlamps submitted for approval shall clearly, legibly and indelibly bear the following inscriptions:
    *   4.1.1 The trade name or mark of the applicant;
    *   4.1.2 The indication of the category of the provided filament lamp.
*   4.2 Moreover, they shall include on the lens and on the main body a space of sufficient size to accommodate the approval marking.

### 5. APPROVAL
*   5.1 If the two samples of a headlamp type submitted meet the requirements of this Regulation, approval shall be granted.
*   5.2 An approval number shall be assigned to each type approved.
*   5.3 Notice of approval or extension or refusal of approval shall be communicated to the Parties to the Agreement.
*   5.4 Every approved headlamp shall bear an international approval mark consisting of:
    *   5.4.1.1 A circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the country which has granted approval;
    *   5.4.1.2 The letter R, the Regulation number and an approval number.
*   5.6 Annex 2 gives an example of the arrangement of the approval mark.

### 6. GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS
*   6.1 Each device shall conform to the specifications of paragraph 7.
*   6.2 Headlamps shall be so designed and constructed that, in normal use, despite vibrations, their satisfactory operation continues to be ensured.
*   6.3 The parts intended for holding the filament lamp shall be so made that the filament lamp can be mounted in no other position than the correct one.

### 7. SPECIAL SPECIFICATIONS
*   7.1 The correct position of the lens in relation to the optical system shall be unequivocally marked and be locked against rotation in service.
*   7.2 For photometric measurement, a measuring screen as per annex 3 and a standard filament lamp according to Regulation No. 37 shall be used.
*   7.3 The passing beam shall have a sharp, substantially horizontal cut-off.
*   7.4 The beam pattern shall not present any lateral variations detrimental to good visibility.
*   7.5 Illumination shall be measured by a photo-receptor with a useful area within a 65 mm square.

### 8. PROVISIONS CONCERNING COLOURED LENSES AND FILTERS
*   8.1 Approval may be obtained for headlamps emitting either colourless or selective-yellow lights. Colorimetric characteristics for selective-yellow are specified (dominant wavelength 575-585 nm, purity factor 0.90-0.98, transmission factor ≥ 0.78).
*   8.2 The filter must be part of the headlamp and attached so it cannot be removed by the user.
*   8.3 Contracting Parties may prohibit headlamps emitting uncoloured or selective-yellow light on vehicles they register.

### 9. CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Every headlamp bearing an approval mark shall conform to the approved type. For samples from series production, measured light intensities shall be at least 80% of the specified minimum and not exceed 120% of the specified maximum values in annex 3.

### 10. PENALTIES FOR NON-CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
*   10.1 Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met.
*   10.2 Parties withdrawing an approval shall notify other Contracting Parties.

### 11. MODIFICATIONS OF A TYPE OF HEADLAMP AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL
*   11.1 Every modification shall be notified to the approving authority.
*   11.2 Confirmation or refusal of approval shall be communicated to other Parties.
*   11.3 The authority issuing an extension shall assign a series number and inform other Parties.

### 12. PRODUCTION DEFINITELY DISCONTINUED
If manufacture of an approved headlamp ceases, the approval holder shall inform the granting authority, which shall then notify other Parties.

### 13. NAMES AND ADDRESSES OF TECHNICAL SERVICES AND ADMINISTRATIVE DEPARTMENTS
Parties shall communicate to the UN Secretariat the names and addresses of their technical services and administrative departments.

---

**Notes:**
1.  As defined in the 1968 Vienna Convention on road traffic.
2.  If manufacturing with coloured lenses, two samples of coloured lenses shall be submitted for colour testing.
3.  If the lens cannot be separated from the main body, a space on the lens suffices.
4.  If different headlamp types share an identical lens or reflector, they may bear multiple approval marks if the specific type's number is unambiguous.
5.  Lists country codes (e.g., 1 for Germany, 2 for France... 22 for USSR). Subsequent numbers assigned chronologically upon ratification/accession.
