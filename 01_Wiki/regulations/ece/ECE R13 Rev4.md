**UN Regulation No. 13-H (Revision 4)**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of passenger cars with regard to braking**

**Contents**
1. Scope
2. Definitions
3. Application for approval
4. Approval
5. Specifications
6. Tests
7. Modification of vehicle type or braking system and extension of approval
8. Conformity of production
9. Penalties for non-conformity of production
10. Production definitively discontinued
11. Names and addresses of Technical Services responsible for conducting approval tests, and of Type Approval Authorities
12. Transitional provisions

**Annexes**
1. Communication (including Appendix - List of vehicle data for the purpose of UN Regulation No. 90 approvals)
2. Arrangements of approval marks
3. Braking tests and performance of braking systems (including Appendix - Procedure for monitoring the state of battery charge)
4. Provisions relating to energy sources and energy storage devices (energy accumulators)
5. Distribution of braking among the axles of vehicles (including Appendix 1 - Wheel-lock sequence test procedure, Appendix 2 - Torque wheel test procedure)
6. Test requirements for vehicles fitted with anti-lock systems (including Appendix 1 - Symbols and definitions, Appendix 2 - Utilisation of adhesion, Appendix 3 - Performance on differing adhesion surfaces, Appendix 4 - Method of selection of the low adhesion surface)
7. Inertia dynamometer test method for brake linings
8. Special requirements to be applied to the safety aspects of complex electronic vehicle control systems

**Key Provisions:**

*   **Scope (1):** Applies to braking of vehicles of categories M1 and N1. Does not cover vehicles with design speed ≤ 25 km/h, vehicles fitted for invalid drivers, or approval of ESC/BAS systems.
*   **Definitions (2):** Detailed definitions for vehicle type, braking equipment, control, transmission, brake, progressive braking, laden vehicle, maximum mass, electric regenerative braking (Categories A & B), automatically commanded braking, selective braking, braking signal, emergency braking signal, etc.
*   **Specifications (5):**
    *   **General (5.1):** Braking equipment must be durable, resistant to corrosion/aging, asbestos-free, and immune to adverse effects from magnetic/electrical fields (referencing UN R10). Defines functions of service, secondary, and parking braking systems. Requires application of Annex 8 for complex electronic systems.
    *   **Characteristics (5.2):** Extensive requirements covering independence of controls, failure warnings (red/yellow signals), distribution of braking force, wear adjustment and indication, fluid identification, warning devices for stored energy, special provisions for electric regenerative braking systems, electric control transmissions, and generation of braking/emergency braking signals.
*   **Tests (6):** Braking tests and required performance are described in Annex 3.
*   **Transitional Provisions (12):** From 1 September 2018, Contracting Parties shall grant new approvals only to the 01 series of amendments, but continue to accept approvals to the 00 series.

**Annex 3 - Braking Tests and Performance:**
*   Defines test procedures (Type-0 ordinary performance, Type-I fade/recovery) and performance requirements for service, secondary, and parking braking systems under various conditions (laden/unladen, engine connected/disconnected).
*   Includes formulas for stopping distance and mean fully developed deceleration.
*   Specific provisions for vehicles with electric regenerative braking systems.

**Annex 5 - Braking Distribution:**
*   Requires that the adhesion utilization curve of the rear axle must not be above that of the front axle for braking rates between 0.15 and 0.8.
*   Describes test procedures (wheel-lock sequence, torque wheel) to verify compliance.

**Annex 6 - Anti-lock Systems:**
*   Specifies test requirements for vehicles fitted with anti-lock braking systems (ABS).
