# Regulation No. 141

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to their Tyre Pressure Monitoring Systems (TPMS)**

*Addendum 140 to the Agreement concerning the Adoption of Uniform Technical Prescriptions for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts which can be Fitted and/or be Used on Wheeled Vehicles and the Conditions for Reciprocal Recognition of Approvals Granted on the Basis of these Prescriptions (Revision 2).*

**Date of entry into force as an annex to the 1958 Agreement:** 22 January 2017

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the approval of vehicles of category M1 up to a maximum mass of 3,500 kg and N1 when equipped with a tyre pressure monitoring system, except for vehicles fitted with dual wheels on an axle.

## 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation:
*   **"Approval of a vehicle"** means the approval of a vehicle type with regard to its tyre pressure monitoring system.
*   **"Vehicle type"** means vehicles which do not differ significantly in essential aspects such as manufacturer's trade name, features influencing TPMS performance, or TPMS design.
*   **"Wheel"** means a complete wheel consisting of a rim and a wheel disc.
*   **"Tyre"** means a pneumatic tyre.
*   **"Maximum mass"** means the maximum technically permissible mass stated by the manufacturer.
*   **"Maximum axle load"** means the maximum total vertical force on one axle as indicated by the manufacturer.
*   **"Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS)"** means a system fitted on a vehicle, able to evaluate tyre inflation pressure or its variation over time and transmit corresponding information to the user while the vehicle is running.
*   **"Cold tyre inflation pressure"** means the tyre pressure at ambient temperature, without pressure build-up from usage.
*   **"Recommended cold inflation pressure (P_rec)"** means the pressure recommended by the vehicle manufacturer for each tyre position.
*   **"In service operating pressure (P_warm)"** means the inflation pressure elevated from P_rec by temperature effects during usage.
*   **"Test Pressure (P_test)"** means the actual pressure of the tyre(s) selected after deflation during the test procedure.

## 3. Application for approval
The application shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or accredited representative, accompanied by a description of the vehicle type (as per Annex 1) and a representative vehicle sample.

## 4. Approval
If the vehicle meets the requirements, approval shall be granted and an approval number assigned. An international approval mark must be affixed to approved vehicles.

## 5. Specifications and tests
### 5.1. General
*   Applies to M1 (≤3500 kg) and N1 vehicles with single tyres on all axles.
*   TPMS effectiveness must not be adversely affected by magnetic or electrical fields (comply with Regulation No. 10).
*   System must operate from 40 km/h or below up to the vehicle's maximum design speed.
*   Vehicle must fulfil puncture, diffusion, and malfunction tests specified in Annex 3.

### 5.2. Tyre pressure detection for incident-related pressure loss
The TPMS must illuminate the warning signal within **10 minutes** of cumulative driving after the in-service pressure in one tyre is reduced by **20%** or reaches a minimum of **150 kPa**, whichever is higher.

### 5.3. Detection for a tyre pressure level significantly below the recommended pressure
The TPMS must illuminate the warning signal within **60 minutes** of cumulative driving after the in-service pressure in up to four tyres is reduced by **20%** or reaches a minimum of **150 kPa**, whichever is higher.

### 5.4. Malfunction detection
The TPMS must illuminate the warning signal within **10 minutes** after a malfunction affecting signal generation/transmission occurs.

### 5.5. Warning indication
*   Must be an optical warning signal conforming to Regulation No. 121.
*   Must be visible in daylight and verifiable from the driver's seat.
*   Must activate for a bulb check with ignition in "on" position.
*   Malfunction indication may be the same as under-inflation signal; if so, it must flash initially then remain continuously illuminated.
*   The tell-tale may flash to indicate TPMS reset status.

## 6. Supplementary information
The owner's manual (or a prominent display if no manual) must contain information about the TPMS, an image of the warning symbol, and corrective actions for low pressure warnings.

## 7. Modifications and extension of approval of the vehicle type
Modifications to the approved vehicle type must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may grant an extension or require further tests.

## 8. Conformity of production
Conformity of production procedures must comply with Appendix 2 of the 1958 Agreement. The Type Approval Authority must verify production at least once per year.

## 9. Penalties for non-conformity of production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met.

## 10. Production definitively discontinued
The approval holder must inform the authority if production ceases.

## 11. Names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Communication form for approval, extension, refusal, or withdrawal.
*   **Annex 2:** Examples of approval marks.
*   **Annex 3:** **Test requirements for Tyre Pressure Monitoring Systems (TPMS)** - Details test conditions, procedures for puncture, diffusion, and malfunction tests, including speed ranges, ambient conditions, deflation protocols, and warning signal verification.
