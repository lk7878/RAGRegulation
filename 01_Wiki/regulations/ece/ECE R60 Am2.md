**Regulation No. 60 - Amendment 2**

**UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF TWO-WHEELED MOTOR CYCLES AND MOPEDS WITH REGARD TO DRIVER-OPERATED CONTROLS INCLUDING THE IDENTIFICATION OF CONTROLS, TELL-TALES AND INDICATORS**

**Supplement 2 to the original version of the Regulation - Date of entry into force: 12 August 2004**

**Amendment Details:**

*   **Paragraph 6.2.2.2.1.** is amended to read:
    > "6.2.2.2.1. Vehicles equipped with riding pedals must, and vehicles equipped with a platform or with footrests integrated into a platform may, conform to the requirement.
    > Position of control: on handlebars: left side forward
    > Type of control: hand lever."

**Context:**
This document is Amendment 2 to Addendum 59 of Revision 1 of the UNECE 1958 Agreement (E/ECE/324 Rev.1/Add.59/Amend.2). The Agreement concerns the adoption of uniform technical prescriptions for wheeled vehicles, equipment and parts and the reciprocal recognition of approvals.
