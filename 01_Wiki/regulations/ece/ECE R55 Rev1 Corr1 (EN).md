# UN Regulation No. 55 - Corrigendum 1 (Revision 1)

**Document:** E/ECE/324 Rev.1/Add.54/Rev.1/Corr.1 / E/ECE/TRANS/505
**Date:** 5 July 2002

This document is **Corrigendum 1** to the 01 series of amendments of **UN Regulation No. 55, Revision 1**. It corrects errors in the base regulation and its amendments.

## Scope
This corrigendum provides corrections to the uniform technical prescriptions for the approval of mechanical coupling components used in combinations of vehicles.

## Key Corrections

### Textual Corrections in Main Body
*   **Paragraph 2.6.3.1.:** Corrected to read: “2.6.3.1. Class C50-1 to 50-7 Standard .....”
*   **Paragraph 2.6.4.3.:** Corrected to read: “2.6.4.3. Class D50-C and 50-D Standard .....”
*   **Paragraph 2.12.:** Corrected definition of `D_c` (D value in kN for centre axle trailers).

### Corrections in Annexes
*   **Annex 1, Item 9.3.:** Corrects the text for specifying trailer mass and permissible static mass on the coupling ball.
*   **Annex 2, Item 6.:** Corrects the text for specifying trailer mass and permissible static mass on the coupling ball.
*   **Annex 4 (Examples below Table 1):** Corrects the term “load” to “mass” (twice) and the unit “kgf” to “kg” (twice).
*   **Annex 5:**
    *   **Paragraph 1.6.3. (text below Table 3):** Corrects the definition of `S` to “Maximum static mass, (kg)”.
    *   **Paragraph 3.3.:** Corrects text regarding approval limits for pivoting jaws (up to 50 kg bearing mass and 5 kN V value).
    *   **Paragraph 8.1., Figure 18:** Corrects a dimension from "30 +0/-1.5" to "35 +0/-1.5".
*   **Annex 6, Paragraph 3.2.3.:** Corrects an equation to read: `F_a = g(C + S/1,000) kN`.
*   **Annex 7:**
    *   **Paragraph 1.4.1.:** Corrects text related to height measurement when bearing mass exceeds 50 kg.
    *   **Appendix 1, Heading:** Corrected to “LOADING CONDITIONS FOR THE MEASUREMENT OF COUPLING BALL HEIGHT”.

## Context
This corrigendum was issued following depositary notification C.N.602.2002.TREATIES-1 dated 13 June 2002. It amends the **01 series of amendments** to Regulation No. 55 (Revision 1).

**Parent Agreement:** The 1958 Agreement (Revision 2, as amended October 1995) concerning the adoption of uniform technical prescriptions for wheeled vehicles and reciprocal recognition of approvals.
