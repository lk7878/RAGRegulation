**Amendment 1 to UN Regulation No. 148**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 29 May 2020

This document constitutes Amendment 1 to UN Regulation No. 148, which contains uniform provisions concerning the approval of light-signalling devices (lamps) for power-driven vehicles and their trailers.

**Key Amendments:**

1.  **Definitions (Paragraph 2):**
    *   **2.1:** Clarifies that definitions from the latest series of amendments to UN Regulation No. 48 apply, unless otherwise specified in this Regulation or in the pertinent installation regulations (UN Regulations Nos. 53, 74, 86).
    *   **2.2 ("Lamps of different types"):** Specifies that the use of LED substitute light source(s) does not constitute a change of type, but paragraph 4.7.7 applies.

2.  **Application for Approval (Paragraph 3):**
    *   **3.1.2.2 (Technical Description):** Requires the technical description to specify, among other things:
 *   The prescribed filament light source category (from UN Regulation No. 37).
 *   The prescribed LED light source category (from UN Regulation No. 128).
 *   The light source module specific identification code.
 *   If applicable, that the lamp is also approved with LED substitute light source(s) per UN Regulation No. 128.
 *   For certain stop lamps mounted inside the vehicle, the optical properties of the rear window(s).
    *   **3.3.4.1.1 (Markings - Visibility):** Requires the approval marking/Unique Identifier and the prescribed category of LED substitute light source(s) (if any) to be visible when the lamp is fitted or when relevant vehicle parts (hood, boot lid, door) are opened.
    *   **3.3.4.3 (Markings - Content):** Requires lamps (except those with non-replaceable light sources) to bear a marking indicating the prescribed light source category/categories and/or the light source module specific identification code. If approved for LED substitute light sources, those categories must also be marked.
    *   **3.5.1.1.1 (New - Conformity of Production for DRL):** Specifies that for a daytime running lamp (DRL) with a maximum luminous intensity not exceeding 700 cd (as identified in Annex 1), the 700 cd value shall be used for conformity of production procedures.
    *   **3.5.4 (New - Conformity of Production Exemption):** States that testing with LED substitute light sources is exempted from conformity of production control.

3.  **General Specifications (Paragraph 4):**
    *   **4.3.1.4 (Light Source Holder):** Specifies that the light source holder must conform to IEC 60061, applying the holder data sheet relevant to the prescribed light source category. Alternatively, if an LED substitute light source category is prescribed, the relevant holder data sheet for that category applies.
    *   **4.7.7 (New - Testing with LED Substitute Light Sources):** Requires that if a lamp is also approved with LED substitute light source(s) at the applicant's discretion, all photometric and colorimetric measurements must be repeated using those prescribed LED substitute light source(s).

4.  **Annex 1 (Communication):**
    *   **New Item 9.1.5:** Adds a field to indicate whether a DRL's maximum luminous intensity does not exceed 700 cd (yes/no).
    *   **Item 9.2:** Amends the information sheet to include fields indicating whether the lamp is approved for LED substitute light source(s) (yes/no) and, if yes, the category of those light sources.

5.  **Annex 2 (Minimum Angles of Geometric Visibility):**
    *   **Table A2-1, line "Rear position pair (MR)":** Amends the minimum vertical angles to `15° / 10°` and `15° / 5°`.
