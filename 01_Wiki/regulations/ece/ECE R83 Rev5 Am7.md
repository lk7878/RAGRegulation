**UN Regulation No. 83, Revision 5, Amendment 7**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to the emission of pollutants according to engine fuel requirements**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 29 December 2018

**Amendment Details:**

*   **Paragraph 7.1.1.2.** is amended to read:
    > "7.1.1.2. For category N vehicles, the approval shall be extended to vehicles with a lower reference mass, only if the emissions of the vehicle already approved are within the limits prescribed for the vehicle for which extension of the approval is requested."

**Authentic Legal Text:** The authentic and legally binding text of this amendment is document **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2018/49**.

**Context:** This document (E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.82/Rev.5/Amend.7) is an amendment to Addendum 82 (UN Regulation No. 83) under the framework of the United Nations Agreement concerning the adoption of harmonized technical regulations for wheeled vehicles.
