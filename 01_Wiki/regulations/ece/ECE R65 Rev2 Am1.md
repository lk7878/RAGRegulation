**Amendment 1 to Regulation No. 65 (Revision 2)**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 18 November 2012

This document constitutes **Amendment 1** to **UNECE Regulation No. 65, Revision 2**, concerning the approval of special warning lamps for power-driven vehicles and their trailers.

**Key Amendments:**

1.  **Definitions (Paragraph 1):**
    *   **1.1.1:** Revised the definition of "Rotating or stationary flashing lamp" to specify it emits light intermittently all around its vertical axis (Category T or HT).
    *   **New 1.1.4:** Added definition for "Half bar" – a special warning lamp emitting light intermittently from 135° left to 135° right relative to its horizontal reference axis, intended for mounting to the front or rear of the vehicle.
    *   **1.2.6 & 1.2.7:** Revised to specify requirements for the "colour and/or type of the light source" and the "colour and construction of the light source module".
    *   **1.8:** Revised the definition of the "Reference axis" to include provisions for half bars (Category HT), specifying a horizontal axis parallel to the vehicle's median longitudinal plane. The manufacturer must indicate the lamp's position relative to this axis.

2.  **Approval Requirements and Extensions (Paragraph 2):**
    *   **2.2.6:** Revised to require two samples of the outer lens for approval, provided the lamp's construction (excluding lens color) remains unchanged.
    *   **New 2.2.7:** Allows for approval extensions to lamps of another color where the only difference is the outer lens color. In such cases, only photometric and colorimetric tests are required. This extension does not apply to approvals for lamps with light sources of different colors.

3.  **Markings and Device Composition (Paragraphs 4 & 5):**
    *   **4.4.1.3:** Revised marking requirements to include "HT" for half bars, followed by "A" (amber), "B" (blue), or "R" (red) according to the unit's color.
    *   **5.7:** Revised to state that a rotating or flashing special warning lamp of Category T or HT may consist of multiple optical systems, provided the requirements of Annex 5, paragraph 8 are met. The manufacturer must supply mounting information.

4.  **Annex Updates:**
    *   **Annex 1, Item 1:** Updated the list of applicable lamp types to include "half bar".
    *   **Annex 2:** Added new examples (h) and (i) for approval marks specific to half bars, illustrating markings for blue (HTB 1) and amber (HTA 2) lamps approved in Germany.
    *   **Annex 5, Paragraph 7.2:** Revised the table of effective luminous intensity (Jₑ) requirements. Key changes include:
 *   Explicitly applying the requirements to both **Category T** and **Category HT** lamps.
 *   Specifying different horizontal angle criteria:
 *   For **Category T**: 360° around the reference axis.
 *   For **Category HT**: a minimum of ±135° relative to its horizontal reference axis.
 *   Updated minimum intensity values for various vertical angles (±4°, ±8°) for day and night conditions, differentiated by color (blue, amber, red).
 *   Maintained maximum intensity limits within specified angular areas.

**Note:** This amendment integrates the "half bar" as a new category (HT) of special warning lamp into Regulation No. 65, defining its characteristics, approval process, markings, and photometric requirements.
