**Amendment 2 to UN Regulation No. 139**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 11 January 2020

**Scope:** This amendment provides uniform technical provisions for the approval of passenger cars with regard to Brake Assist Systems (BAS).

**Key Amendment:**

Paragraph 5.1. is amended to read:

> "5.1. To comply with this Regulation, vehicles shall be equipped with a brake assist system meeting the functional requirements specified in paragraph 6. of this Regulation. Compliance with these requirements shall be demonstrated by meeting the provisions of paragraphs 8. or 9. of this Regulation under the test requirements specified in paragraph 7. of this Regulation. In addition to the requirements of this Regulation, vehicles equipped with a brake assist system shall also be equipped with ABS in accordance with technical requirements of UN Regulation 13-H."
