**Regulation No. 66**

**UNIFORM TECHNICAL PRESCRIPTIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF LARGE PASSENGER VEHICLES WITH REGARD TO THE STRENGTH OF THEIR SUPERSTRUCTURE**

**1. SCOPE**
This Regulation applies to single-deck rigid or articulated vehicles designed and constructed for the carriage of more than 22 passengers, whether seated or standing, in addition to the driver and crew.

**2. TERMS AND DEFINITIONS**
For the purposes of this Regulation, the following terms and definitions are used:
*   **2.1. Units of measurement:** Specifies units for dimensions, mass, force, moment, energy, and gravitational constant.
*   **2.2. "Vehicle":** A bus or coach designed for passenger transportation.
*   **2.3. "Vehicle type":** A category of vehicles with the same design, dimensions, and construction.
*   **2.4. "Family of vehicle types":** Vehicle types covered by the approval of the worst case.
*   **2.5. "Worst case":** The vehicle type least likely to withstand the regulation's requirements, defined by structural strength, reference energy, and residual space.
*   **2.6. "Approval of a vehicle type":** The official process of checking and testing a vehicle type against the regulation.
*   **2.7. "Extension of approval":** The process of approving a modified vehicle type based on an earlier approved type.
*   **2.8. "Articulated vehicle":** A vehicle with two or more rigid, permanently connected sections with intercommunicating passenger compartments.
*   **2.9. "Passenger compartment":** Space for passengers, excluding fixed appliances.
*   **2.10. "Driver's compartment":** Space for the driver's exclusive use.
*   **2.11. "Occupant restraint":** Any device connecting an occupant to their seat during a rollover.
*   **2.12. "Vertical longitudinal central plane" (VLCP):** The vertical plane through the mid-points of the front and rear axle tracks.
*   **2.13. "Residual space":** A space to be preserved in the compartments to provide better survival possibility in a rollover.
*   **2.14. "Unladen kerb mass" (Mk):** Mass of the vehicle in running order, unoccupied and unladen, with specified additions (driver mass, 90% fuel, etc.).
*   **2.15. "Total occupant mass" (Mm):** Combined mass of occupants in seats fitted with restraints.
*   **2.16. "Total effective vehicle mass" (Mt):** Mk combined with a portion (k=0.5) of Mm considered rigidly attached.
*   **2.17. "Individual occupant mass" (Mmi):** Mass of an individual occupant (68 kg).
*   **2.18. "Reference energy" (ER):** The potential energy of the vehicle type at the starting, unstable position of the rollover process.
*   **2.19. "Rollover test on a complete vehicle":** A test on a full-scale vehicle to prove superstructure strength.
*   **2.20. "Tilting bench":** A device (tilting platform, ditch, concrete surface) for rollover testing.
*   **2.21. "Tilting platform":** A rigid plane that can rotate to tilt a vehicle or body section.
*   **2.22. "Body work":** The complete structure of the vehicle in running order.
*   **2.23. "Superstructure":** The load-bearing components of the bodywork that contribute to strength, energy absorption, and preserve the residual space.
*   **2.24. "Bay":** A structural section of the superstructure forming a closed loop between two planes perpendicular to the VLCP, containing pillars, side walls, roof, and floor sections.
*   **2.25. "Body section":** A structural unit representing part of the superstructure for testing, containing at least two bays.
*   **2.26. "Original body section":** A body section with bays in the exact form and position as in the actual vehicle.
*   **2.27. "Artificial body section":** A body section built from bays not in the same position as in the actual vehicle, but structurally equivalent.
*   **2.28. "Rigid part":** A structural part with no significant deformation/energy absorption during the test.
*   **2.29. "Plastic zone" (PZ):** A limited part of the superstructure where large plastic deformations, distortion, and energy absorption are concentrated.
*   **2.30. "Plastic hinge" (PH):** A simple plastic zone on a rod-like element.
*   **2.31. "Cantrail":** The longitudinal structural part above the side windows, hitting the ground first in a rollover.
*   **2.32. "Waistrail":** The longitudinal structural part below the side windows.

**3. APPLICATION FOR APPROVAL**
*   **3.1.** Submitted by the manufacturer or representative to the Administrative Department.
*   **3.2.** Requires accompanying documents and particulars including:
    *   Vehicle identification data, layout drawings, masses, axle loads, centre of gravity positions (measured per Annex 3).
    *   Reference energy calculation (ER = M * g * h1).
    *   Drawings/description of superstructure (per Annex 4) and residual space.
    *   Documentation specific to the chosen approval test method (Annexes 5-9).
*   **3.3.** A complete vehicle may be requested for verification of data.
*   **3.4.** Appropriate test pieces may be requested based on the chosen test method.

**4. APPROVAL**
*   **4.1.** Granted if requirements of paragraph 5 are met.
*   **4.2.** An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments.
*   **4.3.** Notice of approval/refusal/extension communicated to Parties via a form (Annex 1).
*   **4.4.-4.7.** Specifies the international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, regulation number "R66", approval number), its placement (near data plate), and requirements for legibility/indelibility. Example in Annex 2.

**5. GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS AND REQUIREMENTS**
*   **5.1. Requirements:** The superstructure must have sufficient strength to ensure the residual space remains unharmed during and after the rollover test. This means:
    *   **5.1.1.** No part outside the residual space at the start shall intrude into it. Parts originally inside are ignored.
    *   **5.1.2.** No part of the residual space shall project outside the contour of the deformed structure.
*   **5.2. Residual space:** Defined by creating a vertical transverse plane with a specified periphery and moving it through the vehicle length along lines through defined "SR points" on seats. Dimensions and rules for foremost/rearmost positions are given.
*   **5.3. Specification of rollover test on a complete vehicle as the basic approval method:** Describes the lateral tilting test where a vehicle is tilted to unstable equilibrium and tips over into an 800mm deep concrete ditch. Detailed in Annex 5.
*   **5.4. Specifications of equivalent approval tests:** Instead of the basic test, one of the following may be chosen at the manufacturer's discretion:
    *   5.4.1. Rollover test on body sections (Annex 6).
    *   5.4.2. Quasi-static loading test of body sections (Annex 7).
    *   5.4.3. Quasi-static calculation based on component tests (Annex 8).
    *   5.4.4. Computer simulation of the rollover test (Annex 9).
*   **5.5. Testing of articulated buses:** Each rigid section must comply. Sections may be tested separately or in combination.
*   **5.6. Direction of rollover test:** Conducted on the side more dangerous to the residual space, considering lateral eccentricity of the centre of gravity, asymmetry of residual space, and asymmetrical constructional features.

**6. MODIFICATION AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL OF A VEHICLE TYPE**
*   **6.1.** Any modification must be advised. The Administrative Department may agree, require further tests, or refuse extension.
*   **6.2.** Decisions based on three worst-case criteria: structural (is superstructure changed/stronger?), energy (is reference energy same/smaller?), residual space (is new space within the approved one?).
*   **6.3.** Rules for granting extension based on favorable/unfavorable changes to the three criteria.
*   **6.4.** Confirmation/refusal communicated per paragraph 4.3.
*   **6.5.** A series number is assigned for each extension communication.

**7. CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION**
*   **7.1.** Procedure shall comply with the Agreement.
*   **7.2.** Every approved vehicle must conform to the approved type.
*   **7.3.** Normal inspection frequency is once every two years, which may be increased if non-conformity is found.

**8. PENALTIES FOR NON-CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION**
*   **8.1.** Approval may be withdrawn if requirements of paragraph 7 are not met.
*   **8.2.** Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

**9. PRODUCTION DEFINITELY DISCONTINUED**
*   The approval holder must inform the Administrative Department if production ceases, who then notifies other Parties.

**10. TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS**
*   Relates to the entry into force of the 01 series of amendments, specifying dates after which new approvals must meet the amended regulation and conditions for existing approvals.

**11. NAMES AND ADDRESSES OF TECHNICAL SERVICES...**
*   Parties shall communicate the names and addresses of technical services and Administrative Departments to the UN secretariat.
