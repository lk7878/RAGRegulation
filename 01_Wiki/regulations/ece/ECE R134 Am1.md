***

This document is Amendment 1 to Addendum 133 of UNECE Regulation No. 134. It is a supplement to the original version of the regulation.

**Authentic/Legal Text:** The authentic and legally binding text is document ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2015/53.

**Amendment Details:**
*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 20 January 2016
*   **Document Date:** 5 February 2016

**Specific Amendment:**
The amendment modifies paragraph **7.1.1.2.** regarding the fuelling receptacle label. The revised text requires a label to be affixed close to the fuelling receptacle (e.g., inside a refilling hatch) showing the following information:
*   Fuel type (e.g., "CHG" for gaseous hydrogen)
*   MFP
*   NWP
*   Date of removal from service of containers.
