**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of front position lamps, rear position lamps, stop lamps, direction indicators and rear-registration-plate illuminating devices for vehicles of category L**

**Document Information**
*   **Agreement:** Concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical United Nations Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts...
*   **Agreement Revision:** 3 (including amendments in force on 14 September 2017)
*   **Regulation:** Addendum 49 – UN Regulation No. 50
*   **Regulation Version:** Revision 3 - Amendment 4
*   **Supplement:** 20 to the original version of the Regulation
*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 10 February 2018
*   **Document Date:** 26 April 2018
*   **Authentic Texts:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2017/80 and ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2017/81 (as amended by paragraph 76 of report ECE/TRANS/WP.29/1131).

**Amendments**

1.  **Paragraph 6.4.1.**
    *   **Amended Text:** "The device shall only be equipped with light source(s) approved according to UN Regulation No. 37 and/or UN Regulation No. 128, provided that no restriction on the use is made in UN Regulation No. 37 and its series of amendments in force at the time of application for type approval or in UN Regulation No. 128 and its series of amendments in force at the time of application for type approval."

2.  **Paragraph 6.8.**
    *   **Amended Text:** Specifies conditions under which the flash for direction indicator lamps of categories 11, 11a, 11b, 11c, or 12 may be produced by sequential activation of light sources. Conditions include:
 *   (a) Each activated light source remains lit until the end of the ON cycle.
 *   (b) Activation sequence produces a uniform progressive signal from the inboard to the outboard edge of the light emitting surface.
 *   (c) The signal must be continuous with no vertical oscillations. The distance between adjacent/tangential distinct parts of the light emitting surface shall not exceed 50mm (measured perpendicularly to the reference axis), overriding values in Regulation No. 53, paragraph 5.6.2. These interruptions shall not create vertical overlap and shall not be used for other functions.
 *   (d) The sequence variation must finish no more than 200 ms after the beginning of the ON cycle.
 *   (e) The orthogonal projection of the light emitting surfaces must be circumscribed by a rectangle on a plane normal to the reference axis, with its longer sides parallel to the H-plane. The ratio of horizontal to vertical sides must not be less than 1.7.
 *   Compliance with these conditions must be verified in flashing mode.

3.  **New Paragraphs 14.3. to 14.5.**
    *   **14.3.:** From the official entry into force date of Supplement 20, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall not refuse to grant approval under the Regulation as amended by this Supplement.
    *   **14.4.:** From 1 September 2018, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall grant approvals only if the type of direction indicator to be approved meets the requirements of the Regulation as amended by Supplement 20.
    *   **14.5.:** Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall not refuse to grant extensions of approval granted prior to the entry into force date of Supplement 20.
