**Amendment Details**

This document is **Amendment 1** to **Revision 6** of **UNECE Regulation No. 46**. It is identified as **Supplement 3 to the 04 series of amendments**.

*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 8 October 2016
*   **Promulgation/Publication Date:** 28 October 2016
*   **Authentic Legal Text:** The binding text is document `ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2016/9`. This PDF serves as a documentation tool.

**Regulation Context**
This regulation falls under the **Revised 1958 Agreement** concerning the adoption of uniform technical prescriptions for wheeled vehicles and reciprocal recognition of approvals.

**Amendment Content**

The amendment modifies paragraph **15.2.1.2.** of Regulation No. 46.

The revised text reads:
> "15.2.1.2. The provisions of this Regulation do not apply to the surveillance mirrors defined in paragraph 2.1.1.3. Nevertheless, the exterior surveillance mirrors shall be mounted at least 2 m above the ground when the vehicle is under a load corresponding to its maximum technical permissible mass or shall be fully integrated in a housing including Class II or III mirror(s) which is (are) type approved to this Regulation."
