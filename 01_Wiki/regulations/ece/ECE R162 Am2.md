**Amendment Details**
*   **Base Regulation:** UN Regulation No. 162 (Uniform technical prescriptions concerning approval of immobilizers and approval of a vehicle with regard to its immobilizer)
*   **Amendment Number:** 2
*   **Supplement:** 2 to the original version of the Regulation
*   **Authentic Legal Text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2022/30

**Content Summary**
This document is Amendment 2 to UN Regulation No. 162. It amends specific technical requirements for vehicle immobilizers.

**Key Amendment**
*   **Paragraph 5.2.1.1.** is amended to specify that an immobilizer must be designed to prevent vehicle operation by at least one defined means.

**Contextual Note**
This amendment is issued under the framework of the **Revised 1958 Agreement** (Revision 3, as of September 2017), which governs the adoption of harmonized UN Regulations for wheeled vehicles and mutual recognition of approvals.
