# Regulation No. 55: Uniform Provisions Concerning the Approval of Mechanical Coupling Components of Combinations of Vehicles

## 1. Scope
1.1. This Regulation lays down the requirements which mechanical coupling devices and components shall meet in order to be regarded internationally as being mutually compatible.
1.2. This Regulation applies to devices and components intended for:
    1.2.1. motor vehicles and trailers intended to form a combination of vehicles;
    1.2.2. motor vehicles and trailers intended to form articulated vehicles, where the vertical load imposed on the motor vehicle by the trailer does not exceed 200 kN.
1.3. This Regulation applies to:
    1.3.1. standard devices and components;
    1.3.2. non-standard devices and components;
    1.3.3. non-standard miscellaneous devices and components.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Mechanical coupling devices and components:** All items on the frame, bodywork, and chassis used to connect vehicles.
*   **Automatic coupling:** Coupling achieved by reversing the towing vehicle against the trailer to engage, lock, and indicate proper engagement automatically.
*   **Standard mechanical coupling devices and components:** Conform to standard dimensions and characteristic values in this Regulation and are interchangeable within their class.
*   **Non-standard mechanical coupling devices and components:** Do not fully conform to standard dimensions but can connect to standard devices in the relevant class.
*   **Non-standard miscellaneous mechanical coupling devices and components:** Do not conform to standard dimensions and cannot connect to standard devices (e.g., for special heavy transport).
*   **Characteristic values (D, Dc, S, V, U):** Theoretical reference values for horizontal and vertical forces used as the basis for testing.
*   **Centre axle trailer:** A trailer with a drawbar fixed in the vertical plane, axle(s) close to its centre of gravity, and vertical load on the coupling ≤ 10% of trailer mass or 1000 kg (whichever is lesser).
*   **Positive mechanical engagement:** Design ensuring the device will not open or disengage under normal or test forces.

## 3. Application for Approval of a Mechanical Coupling Device or Component
3.1. Submitted by the holder of the trade name/mark or accredited representative.
3.2. Requires detailed information including drawings, characteristic values, technical description, restrictions, and samples.

## 4. General Requirements for Mechanical Coupling Devices or Components
4.1. Must conform to dimensional and strength specifications in Annexes 5 and 6. No detrimental cracks, fractures, or excessive permanent distortion after testing.
4.2. Critical parts must be made of steel (or equivalent material approved by authorities).
4.3. Must be safe to operate; coupling/uncoupling possible by one person without tools. For trailers >3.5 tonnes, only automatic coupling devices are allowed (except Class T).
4.4. Must function satisfactorily and retain characteristics with correct maintenance.
4.5. Must have positive mechanical engagement and be locked in the closed position. Spring forces may only be used to close the device and prevent vibration-induced disengagement.
4.6. Must be accompanied by installation and operating instructions.
4.7. For certain after-market Class A or S devices for trailers ≤3.5 tonnes, height and installation features must be verified by authorities.
4.8. For heavy-duty/non-standard miscellaneous devices (Class S, T), requirements for the closest standard/non-standard device apply.

## 5. Application for Approval of a Vehicle Fitted with a Mechanical Coupling Device or Component
5.1. Vehicle manufacturers must make necessary information available to enable coupling manufacturers to design suitable devices.
5.2. Submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or accredited representative.
5.3. Requires detailed vehicle description, coupling information, drawings, characteristic values, and a representative vehicle sample.

## 6. General Requirements for Vehicles Fitted with a Mechanical Coupling Device or Component
6.1. The fitted device/component must be approved per paragraphs 3, 4, and Annexes 5, 6.
6.2. Installation must meet Annex 7 requirements.
6.3. Operating instructions must be provided with the vehicle.

## 7. Markings
7.1. Must bear the trade name/mark of the manufacturer/supplier/applicant.
7.2. Must have space for the approval mark (Annex 3).
7.3. Must be marked with the coupling class and relevant characteristic values (Annex 4).
7.4. A maximum of two alternative characteristic values may be marked.
7.5. Any usage restrictions must be marked.
7.6. All markings must be permanent and legible when installed.

## 8. Approval
8.1. Granted if samples meet Regulation requirements and conformity of production is satisfactory.
8.2. An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate amendment series).
8.3. Notice of approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal is communicated to Contracting Parties.
8.4. An approval mark must be affixed to every approved device/component.
8.5. The approval mark is a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the country distinguishing number and the approval number (see Annex 3).

## 9. Modifications and Extension of Approval
9.1. Modifications must be notified to the granting authority, which may require further testing.
9.2. Confirmation or refusal of approval for modifications is communicated.
9.3. Extensions of approval are assigned a series number and communicated.

## 10. Conformity of Production Procedures
10.1. The approval holder must ensure test results are recorded and documents are available (max 10 years after production ends).
10.2. The granting authority may verify conformity control methods at production facilities (normal frequency: once every two years).

## 11. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
11.1. Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or if a marked device does not conform to the approved type.
11.2. Withdrawal of approval must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 12. Production Definitely Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the granting authority if production ceases. The authority then notifies other Contracting Parties.

## 13. Transitional Provisions
Certain Contracting Parties (EC Member States at the time) declared they would only be bound by the Agreement's obligations for devices/components intended for vehicles other than category M1.

## 14. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments
Contracting Parties must communicate the names and addresses of responsible technical services and administrative departments to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes Overview
*   **Annex 1 & 2:** Communication forms for approval actions.
*   **Annex 3:** Example of the approval mark arrangement.
*   **Annex 4:** Examples of how to mark characteristic values on devices.
*   **Annex 5:** Detailed requirements for specific coupling classes (A to L, S, T), including dimensions, articulation, locking mechanisms, and characteristic values.
*   **Annex 6:** Testing procedures (dynamic endurance tests, static tests) for all coupling classes.
*   **Annex 7:** Installation requirements and special provisions.
*   **Appendix 1:** Loading conditions for measuring tow ball height on M1 vehicles.
