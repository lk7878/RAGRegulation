**Amendment 2 to UN Regulation No. 135 (Pole Side Impact Performance)**

This document constitutes Amendment 2 to UN Regulation No. 135. The authentic and legally binding text is referenced as `ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2019/110`.

**Amendment Details:**
*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 29 May 2020.
*   **Documentation Date:** 1 July 2020.

**Specific Amendment:**

Paragraph 5.4.2.1 is amended to read as follows:

> **5.4.2.1.** The door shall remain latched;
>
> This requirement is deemed to be fulfilled:
>
> (a) If it is clearly visible, that the door lock is latched; or
>
> (b) If the door does not open under a static tractive force of at least 400 N in the y-direction applied to the door, according to the Figure below, as close as possible to the window sill and to the edge of the door opposite to the hinged side, except to the door handle itself.
>
> **Figure**
> (A diagram is referenced here showing a force vector of 400N applied in the Y-direction, with angles of 90° +/- 5° relative to the X and Z axes.)
