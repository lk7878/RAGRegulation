**UN Regulation No. 122 (Amendment 6)**

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles of categories M, N and O with regard to their heating systems**

**Document Reference:** E/ECE/324/Rev.2/Add.121/Amend.6 / E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2/Add.121/Amend.6

**Date of Entry into Force:** 25 September 2020
**Document Date:** 2 November 2020

**Amendment Details:**

The amendment modifies paragraph 6.2.1. and its associated table. The updated text specifies requirements for heat pump heating systems across vehicle categories M, N, and O, indicating that they are subject to the provisions of Annexes 4, 5, 6, and 8 (covering air quality, temperature, exhaust, and LPG safety). A note (Note 2) is referenced for heat pump systems in category M vehicles.

**Authentic Legal Text:** The authentic and legally binding text is published as document ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2020/23.

**Context:** This amendment is part of the framework established under the "Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical United Nations Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts..." (Revision 3).
