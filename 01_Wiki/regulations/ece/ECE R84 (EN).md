# UN Regulation No. 84

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the measurement of the fuel consumption indicated by the manufacturer, from all internal combustion engined vehicles of category M1 and of category N1, having a maximum total mass less than 2 tonnes.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Approval of a vehicle**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to the measurement of the fuel consumption.
*   **Vehicle type**: A category of power-driven vehicles which do not differ in such essential respects as: body, engine, transmission, tyres and unladen mass.
*   **Unladen mass**: The mass of the vehicle in running order without crew, passengers or load, but with the fuel tank full and the usual set of tools and spare wheel on board, where applicable.
*   **Reference mass**: The "unladen mass" of the vehicle increased by a uniform figure of 100 kg.
*   **Maximum mass**: The technically permissible maximum mass declared by the vehicle manufacturer.
*   **Cold start device**: A device which enriches the air/fuel mixture of the engine temporarily, to assist starting.
*   **Starting aid**: A device which assists engine starting without enrichment of the air fuel mixture, e.g. glow plug, changed injection timing, etc.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application for approval of a vehicle type with regard to the measurement of the fuel consumption indicated by the manufacturer shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or by his duly accredited representative. It must be accompanied by specified documents (description of engine type, basic features of the vehicle) and a representative vehicle shall be submitted for testing.

## 4. Approval
*   Approval is granted if the fuel consumption of the vehicle type has been measured according to the conditions specified in the Regulation.
*   An approval number is assigned.
*   Notice of approval, extension, or refusal is communicated to the Parties to the 1958 Agreement.
*   An international approval mark must be affixed to every conforming vehicle.

## 5. Specifications and Tests
*   **General**: Components liable to affect fuel consumption must be designed to enable the vehicle to comply with the Regulation under normal use.
*   **Description of tests**: The vehicle shall be subjected to tests under the following driving conditions:
    1.  Cycle simulating urban driving.
    2.  Constant speed test at 90 km/h.
    3.  Constant speed test at 120 km/h (not required if the vehicle's maximum design speed is less than 130 km/h).
*   Results are expressed in litres/100 km rounded to 0.1.
*   **Fuel**: Must be the appropriate CEC reference fuel.
*   **Interpretation of results**: Manufacturer's indicated values are accepted if they do not differ by more than +4% from the values measured by the technical service. If the difference is greater than 4%, the value recorded by the technical service is adopted.

## 6. Modification and Extension of Approval of the Vehicle Type
Any modification must be notified. The administrative department may consider it insignificant or require further tests. Confirmation or extension of approval must be communicated.

## 7. Conditions of Extension of the Type Approval for Vehicle Type
Describes the conditions under which further fuel consumption tests are required for vehicle variants differing from a basic model (e.g., modifications affecting emissions, outer shape, mass, transmission, engine, etc.). Also covers the interpretation of results from further tests and the selection of basic reference models.

## 8. Conformity of Production
*   Approved vehicles must be manufactured to conform to the approved type.
*   The holder of the approval must ensure effective quality control procedures, have necessary checking equipment, record test data, and carry out prescribed tests.
*   Competent authorities may verify production methods at any time, including inspections at least every two years.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met. Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 10. Production Definitely Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the granting authority if production of an approved type ceases. The authority then notifies other Parties.

## 11. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments
Parties must communicate the names and addresses of responsible technical services and administrative departments to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Essential characteristics of the engine and information concerning the conduct of tests.
*   **Annex 2**: Communication form concerning approval, refusal, extension, withdrawal, or production discontinuation.
*   **Annex 3**: Arrangements of approval marks.
*   **Annex 4**: ECE method of measuring fuel consumption. Details test conditions, measurement procedures for urban cycle and constant speeds (90 & 120 km/h) on track or chassis dynamometer, and presentation of results.
*   **Annex 5**: Tests on the chassis dynamometer - urban cycle tests. Describes the operating cycle, test vehicle and equipment requirements, and test procedure. Includes appendices on the cycle breakdown, dynamometer characteristics, determination of total road load power and dynamometer calibration, and verification of non-mechanical inertias.
*   **Annex 6**: Checks of conformity of production. Specifies sampling, test procedures, and acceptance criteria (measured consumption must not differ by more than 10% from the approved figure).

## Notes
1.  Vehicle categories are described in the Consolidated Resolution on the Construction of Vehicles (R.E.3).
2.  Lists distinguishing numbers for countries party to the 1958 Agreement (e.g., 1 for Germany, 2 for France).
3.  The 120 km/h test is not done if the vehicle's maximum design speed is less than 130 km/h.
4.  CEC: European Coordinating Council for the development of performance tests for lubricants and engine fuels.
