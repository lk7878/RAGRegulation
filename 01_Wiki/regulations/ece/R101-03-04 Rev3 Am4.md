# Regulation No. 101, Revision 3, Amendment 4

**Document Reference:** E/ECE/324/Rev.2/Add.100/Rev.3/Amend.4, E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2/Add.100/Rev.3/Amend.4
**Supplement:** 5 to the 01 series of amendments
**Authentic Text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2015/58

## 1. Scope
This regulation establishes uniform provisions concerning the approval of:
*   Passenger cars powered by an internal combustion engine only, or by a hybrid electric power train, with regard to the measurement of carbon dioxide (CO₂) emissions, fuel consumption, and/or electric energy consumption and electric range.
*   Categories M and N vehicles powered by an electric power train only, with regard to the measurement of electric energy consumption and electric range.

## 2. Transitional Provisions (Paragraphs 13. to 13.4.)
**13.1.** From the official date of entry into force of Supplement 4 to the 01 series of amendments to Regulation No. 101, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall not refuse to grant or accept type approvals under the Regulation as amended by that Supplement.

**13.2.** Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall not refuse to grant extensions of type approvals for vehicle types that have been issued according to any previous version of this Regulation.

**13.3.** Contracting Parties may continue to grant type approvals to vehicle types complying with the requirements of any previous version of this Regulation, provided the vehicles are approved or intended to be approved to any series of amendments **preceding the 07 series of amendments to Regulation No. 83**.

**13.4.** After the entry into force of Supplement 4 to the 01 series of amendments, Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept, for national or regional type approval, a vehicle type approved to any previous version of this Regulation, **unless** they accept vehicles approved to any series of amendments **preceding the 07 series of amendments to Regulation No. 83**.
