# UN Regulation No. 108 - Amendment 2

**Document Reference:** E/ECE/324 Rev.2/Add.107/Amend.2 / E/ECE/TRANS/505
**Date:** 12 July 2005
**Addendum:** 107
**Amendment:** 2
**Supplement:** 2 to the original version of the Regulation
**Date of Entry into Force:** 23 June 2005

## Scope
This document constitutes Amendment 2 to UN Regulation No. 108, concerning the approval for the production of retreaded pneumatic tyres for motor vehicles and their trailers.

## Key Amendments

### 1. Definitions and Markings (Paragraph 2.21.)
*   **Paragraph 2.21.2.** is amended to specify that the size designation includes the nominal outer diameter expressed in mm, depending on the tyre design type.
*   **New Paragraph 2.21.4.** is inserted, requiring an indication of the tyre-to-rim fitment configuration when it differs from the standard configuration.

### 2. New Definition (Paragraph 2.23.)
*   **New Paragraph 2.23.1.** defines "Tyre to rim fitment configuration" as the type of rim to which the tyre is designed to be fitted. For non-standard rims, this is identified by a symbol on the tyre (e.g., "CT", "TR", "TD", or "A").

### 3. Transitional Provision (Paragraph 3.2.9.)
*   **New Paragraph 3.2.9.** establishes that, no later than two years after this amendment's entry into force, tyres manufactured using the "bead to bead" process or any process renewing the sidewall material must place the identification mentioned in paragraph 2.21.4. immediately after the rim diameter marking.

### 4. Marking Arrangement (Annex 3)
Annex 3 is amended to detail the arrangement of retread tyre markings, including specific examples:
*   General example of marking arrangement.
*   Specific example for tyres with a tyre-to-rim fitment configuration "A": `185-560 R 400A`
    *   `185`: Nominal section width (mm)
    *   `560`: Outer diameter (mm)
    *   `R`: Tyre structure code
    *   `400`: Nominal rim diameter (mm)
    *   `A`: Tyre-to-rim fitment configuration
*   Rules for the positioning and order of:
    *   The size designation (e.g., `185/70 R 14` or `185-560 R 400A`).
    *   The service description (load index and speed symbol), which must follow the size designation immediately.
    *   Other specified symbols and words.
