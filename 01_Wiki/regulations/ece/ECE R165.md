# UN Regulation No. 165

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of audible reverse warning devices and of motor vehicles with regard to their audible reverse warning signals**

**Date of entry into force as an annex to the 1958 Agreement:** 19 January 2023

## 1. Scope
1.1. This Regulation applies to:
    1.1.1. **PART I:** Approval of audible reverse warning devices which are intended for fitting to motor vehicles of categories M1 (M1 > 3500 kg), N2, N3 and M3.
    1.1.2. **PART II:** Approval of motor vehicles listed in 1.1.1. with regard to fitting of audible reverse warning devices automatically activated when reverse gear is selected and the propulsion system is on.

## 2. Definitions
For the purpose of this Regulation:
2.1. **"Audible reverse warning device"** means a device, emitting an acoustic signal to the outside of a vehicle while reversing, which is intended to give audible warning of the presence of a vehicle, with the primary purpose to fulfil the requirements of this Regulation.
    2.1.1. **"Non-self-adjusting audible reverse warning device"** means a device which gives audible reverse warning sound independent of "Ambient noise" levels.
    2.1.2. **"Self-adjusting audible reverse warning device"** means a device which automatically adjusts its sound level, throughout a defined range, in order to maintain a sound level differential between the sound output of the device and the "Ambient noise" measured by itself.
    2.1.3. **"Stepwise self-adjusting audible reverse warning device"** means a device which automatically adjusts to a fixed sound level (covering Low level, Normal level or High level), depending on the "Ambient noise" measured by itself.
    2.1.4. **"Multiple audible reverse warning system"** means a combination of "Non-self-adjusting audible reverse warning devices" capable of functioning independently when applying "Low level", "Normal level", "High level".
2.2. **"Low level" mode** means the emitted sound level of the "Audible reverse warning device" which is to aid sufficient safety of vulnerable road users during quiet times and/or quiet areas.
2.3. **"Normal level" mode** means the emitted sound level of the "Audible reverse warning device" which is to aid the sufficient safety of vulnerable road users during normal traffic hours and areas not covered by 2.2. and 2.4.
2.4. **"High level" mode** means the emitted sound level of the "Audible reverse warning device" which is to aid sufficient safety of vulnerable road users and when "Normal level" is deemed insufficient for safety, during times and/or areas not covered by 2.2 and 2.3 (e.g. industrial or road construction sites).
2.5. **Basic designation for "Non-self-adjusting audible reverse warning device"**
    2.5.1. **Class L:** Able to emit sound levels of "Low level" only.
    2.5.2. **Class N:** Able to emit sound levels of "Normal level" only.
    2.5.3. **Class H:** Able to emit sound levels of "High level" only.
    2.5.4. **Class I:** Able to emit sound levels of "Low level" and "Normal level".
    2.5.5. **Class II:** Able to emit sound levels of "Normal level" and "High level".
    2.5.6. **Class III:** Able to emit sound levels of "Low level", "Normal level" and "High level".
2.6. **Basic designation for "Multiple audible reverse warning system"**
    2.6.1. **Class M-I:** Able to emit sound levels of "Low level" and "Normal level".
    2.6.2. **Class M-II:** Able to emit sound levels of "Normal level" and "High level".
    2.6.3. **Class M-III:** Able to emit sound levels of "Low level", "Normal level" and "High level".
2.7. **Basic designation for audible reverse warning sound**
    2.7.1. **"Tonal sound"** means a sound, which contains basically a single frequency, commonly in the frequency range from 500 Hz to 4000 Hz.
    2.7.2. **"Broadband sound"** means a sound, which contains a large number of single frequency components, continuously distributed over a required frequency range covering at least 1000 Hz to 4000 Hz.
    2.7.3. **"One-third octave band sound"** means a sound which has its main energy and nearly constant power spectral density in 1 of 7 one-third octave frequency bands (Center frequency: 800, 1000, 1250, 1600, 2000, 2500 or 3150 Hz).
2.8. **Background and ambient noise**
    2.8.1. **"Background noise"** is any disturbing sound other than the sound signal of the audible reverse warning device at test conditions in this regulation.
    2.8.2. **"Ambient noise"** is any disturbing sound other than the sound signal of the audible reverse warning device, around the audible reverse warning device and the vehicle.
2.9. **"Reference sound"** is the sound, other than the "Background noise" or the warning sound, that is applied at the test situation, and to which the self-adjusting and stepwise self-adjusting audible reverse warning device will adapt.
2.10. **"Type of audible reverse warning device"** means audible reverse warning devices not differing essentially from each other with respect to trade name/mark, basic designation (principle of device and principle of sound).
2.11. **Table 1. Symbols and Abbreviations** (see OCR content for details).

## I. Part I: Audible reverse warning device
### 3. Application for approval
3.1. Submitted by manufacturer or accredited representative.
3.2. Accompanied by technical information document (Annex 1A model).
3.3. Accompanied by two samples.
3.4. Type Approval Authority verifies conformity of production arrangements.

### 4. Markings
4.1. Device shall bear trade name/mark of manufacturer, model commercial name/number, and approval mark.
4.2. Approval mark according to paragraph 5.4.
4.3. All markings clearly legible and indelible.

### 5. Approval
5.1. Granted if samples conform to specifications.
5.2. Approval number assigned.
5.3. Communication via form in Annex 1A.
5.4. International approval mark affixed.
5.5. Examples in Annex 2.
5.6. Mark arrangements verified.

### 6. Specifications
6.1. **General specifications**
    6.1.1. Device emits acoustic signal with repeatable pattern (24-120 cycles/min).
    6.1.2. Must pass acoustic, mechanical, endurance, and environmental tests.
    6.1.3. Alternative sounds selectable by driver must comply.
6.2. **Measuring instruments**
    6.2.1. Acoustic measurements per IEC 61672-1:2013 Class 1.
    6.2.2. Other instrumentation accuracy specified (voltage, resistance, distance, time, meteorological).
6.3. **Measurement of sound characteristics of "Non-self-adjusting audible reverse warning device"**
    6.3.1. Test environment: anechoic chamber, semi-anechoic, or open site per ISO 10844:2014.
    6.3.2. Microphone height 1.20 m ± 0.05 m, distance 1.00 m ± 0.05 m from sound outlet.
    6.3.3. Device rigidly mounted.
    6.3.4. Supply voltage: DC at 13/12 rated voltage ±0.7 V; AC per generator specifications.
    6.3.5.-6.3.6. Additional electrical specifications.
    6.3.7. **Sound pressure level limits:**
 * Low level: 62 dB(A) to <77 dB(A)
 * Normal level: 77 dB(A) to ≤92 dB(A)
 * High level: 97 dB(A) to ≤112 dB(A)
 Offset between Low and Normal level ≥5 dB.
    6.3.8. Specifications must be met after endurance test with voltage/speed variations.
    6.3.9. Start-up time ≤ two cycles.
    6.3.10. Rated frequency tolerance ≤10%.
6.4. **Measurement of sound characteristics of "Self-adjusting" and "Stepwise self-adjusting" devices**
    6.4.1. Test environment similar to 6.3.1.
    6.4.2. Setup includes loudspeaker for reference sound.
    6.4.3. Device rigidly mounted.
    6.4.4.-6.4.6. Supply voltage and electrical specifications.
    6.4.7. Reference sound (pink noise) at three levels: 45 ±2, 60 ±2, 80 ±2 dB(A).
    6.4.8. **"Self-adjusting device" output limits per reference sound level:**
 * Ref level 1 (45 dB(A)): 63-74 dB(A)
 * Ref level 2 (60 dB(A)): 78-89 dB(A)
 * Ref level 3 (80 dB(A)): 98-109 dB(A)
    6.4.9. **"Stepwise self-adjusting device" output levels and ambient noise mapping table** (see OCR for details).
    6.4.10. Specifications must be met after endurance test.
    6.4.11. Start-up time ≤ two cycles.
    6.4.12. Frequency range 1k-3.5k Hz, stability ≤10% variation.
6.5. **Endurance test**
    6.5.1. Supplied at rated voltage.
    6.5.2. Heat dispersal ensured.
    6.5.3. Temperature cycling tests (-30°C to +70°C).
    6.5.4. Durability: 50 hours operation at 25°C ±11°C.
    6.5.5. Vibration endurance (2000 cycles/min, 2 mm amplitude, x,y,z directions).
    6.5.6. Acoustic performance checked after complete procedure.
6.6. **Resistance against dust and water:** IP54 protection per IEC 60529.

### 7. Modification and extension of approval
7.1. Modifications notified to Type Approval Authority.
7.2. Communication of confirmation/refusal.
7.3. Extension series number assigned.

### 8. Conformity of production
8.1. Devices manufactured to conform to approved type.
8.2. Verification by authority at least once every two years.

### 9. Penalties for non-conformity of production
9.1. Approval may be withdrawn.
9.2. Notification of withdrawal to other Parties.

### 10. Production definitively discontinued
10.1. Holder informs authority; authority notifies other Parties.

## II. Part II: Audible reverse warning signals for motor vehicles
### 11. Definitions relevant for Part II
11.1. **"Approval of the motor vehicle"** means approval of a vehicle type with regard to its audible reverse warning signal.
11.2. **"Vehicle type"** defined by bodywork affecting sound, number/type/position of device(s).
11.3. **"Pause function"** means a mechanism to halt temporarily the operation of a reverse warning device.

### 12. Application for approval
12.1. Submitted by vehicle manufacturer or representative.
12.2. Accompanied by technical information document (Annex 1B model).
12.3.-12.4. List and drawings of non-component-type-approved devices.
12.5. Representative vehicle submitted.
12.6. Conformity of production arrangements verified.

### 13. Approval
13.1. Granted if vehicle meets requirements.
13.2. Approval number assigned.
13.3. Communication via form in Annex 1B.
13.4. International approval mark affixed.
13.5.-13.9. Marking details and verification.

### 14. Specifications
14.1. **General specifications**
    14.1.1. Device automatically activated when reverse gear selected and propulsion system on.
    14.1.2. Designed to withstand vibration.
    14.1.3. Resistant to corrosion.
    14.1.4. Default mode on restart is "Normal level" for applicable devices.
    14.1.5. Alternative sounds selectable by driver must be compliant.
14.2. **Specifications regarding sound levels**
    14.2.1. Sound measured per methods in 14.4 or 14.5.
    14.2.2. **Limit values:**
 14.2.2.1. **"Non-self-adjusting" or "Multiple system":**
 * Low level: 45 dB(A) to <60 dB(A)
 * Normal level: 60 dB(A) to ≤75 dB(A)
 * High level: 80 dB(A) to ≤95 dB(A)
 Offset Low/Normal ≥5 dB.
 14.2.2.2. **"Self-adjusting device":** Output +5 dB to +8 dB above "Ambient noise" (range 45-95 dB(A)).
 14.2.2.3. **"Stepwise self-adjusting device":** Same limits as 14.2.2.1.
    14.2.3. Values entered in test report.
14.3. **Pause function**
    14.3.1. Allowed only if vehicle equipped with non-audible safety/rear visibility system per UN R158. Disabled when towing unequipped category O vehicles.
    14.3.2. Operable by driver in normal seating position.
    14.3.3. Activation clearly indicated to driver.
    14.3.4. Deactivated on vehicle restart.
    14.3.5. Owner's manual must warn of increased risks.
14.4. **Measurement on stationary vehicle for "Non-self-adjusting" and "Multiple system"**
    14.4.1. Vehicle specifications (endurance test, device location, test voltage).
    14.4.2. Instruments per 6.2.
    14.4.3. Measurement at 7.00 m ± 0.10 m rear of vehicle.
    14.4.4. Microphone in mean longitudinal plane.
    14.4.5. Background noise correction procedure with criteria table.
    14.4.6. Maximum sound level sought between 0.5-1.5 m height.
14.5. **Measurement on stationary vehicle for "Stepwise self-adjusting" or "Self-adjusting" device**
    14.5.1. Vehicle specifications similar to 14.4.1.
    14.5.2. Instruments per 6.2.
    14.5.3. Measurement at 7.00 m ± 0.10 m rear.
    14.5.4. Microphone placement.
    14.5.5. Background noise correction procedure with criteria table.
    14.5.6. **Specific for "Self-adjusting device":** Verification of adjustment function using reference sound.
    14.5.7. **Specific for "Stepwise self-adjusting device":** Verification of output levels per ambient noise.
14.6. **General:** Contracting Parties declare which Classes they mandate if vehicles not equipped with self-adjusting devices.

### 15. Modification and extension of approval of vehicle type
15.1. Modifications notified.
15.2. Communication of confirmation/refusal.
15.3. Extension series number assigned.

### 16. Conformity of production
16.1. Vehicles manufactured to conform.
16.2. Verification at least once every two years.

### 17. Penalties for non-conformity of production
17.1. Approval may be withdrawn.
17.2. Notification of withdrawal.

### 18. Production definitively discontinued
18.1. Holder informs authority; authority notifies other Parties.

### 19. Names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
19.1. Communicated to UN Secretariat.
