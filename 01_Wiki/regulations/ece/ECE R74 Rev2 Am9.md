**Amendment Details**

This document constitutes **Amendment 9** to **Revision 2** of **UN Regulation No. 74**. It is **Supplement 14** to the **01 series of amendments**. The amendment entered into force on **4 January 2023**.

**Authentic Text**
The authentic and legally binding text is referenced as: **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2022/101**.

**Technical Amendments**

The amendment modifies specific paragraphs within UN Regulation No. 74:

1.  **Paragraph 6.1.7. "Electrical connections"** is amended.
    *   The passing-beam(s) may remain switched ON with the driving-beam(s).
    *   For vehicles fitted with secondary driving-beam(s) approved under **UN Regulations Nos. 113 or 149**, at least one of the following lamps must remain ON with the secondary driving beam(s):
 *   Passing-beam(s); or
 *   A primary driving beam approved according to UN Regulations Nos. 113 or 149; or
 *   A driving-beam of Class A or B approved according to the 01 and subsequent series of amendments to UN Regulation No. 149.

2.  **Paragraph 6.2.1. "Number" and its associated footnote** are amended.
    *   The list of applicable headlamp classes is updated to include:
 *   Classes A, B, AS*, BS, CS, DS, or ES of UN Regulation No. 149.
 *   Classes C or V of the 01 and subsequent series of amendments to UN Regulation No. 149.
    *   The footnote clarifies that headlamps of **Class A (UN R113) with LED modules** or **Class AS (UN R149) with LED modules** are permitted only on vehicles with a maximum design speed not exceeding **25 km/h**.

**Governing Agreement**
This regulation is adopted under the *Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical United Nations Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts...* (Revision 3, including amendments effective 14 September 2017).
