# Regulation No. 11

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to door latches and door retention components**

*This document is meant purely as documentation tool. The authentic and legal binding text is: ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2014/71*

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to vehicles of categories M and N with respect to latches and door retention components such as hinges and other supporting means on doors, which can be used for the entry or exit of the occupants and/or can present the risk of occupants being thrown from a vehicle as a result of impact.

## 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation:
*   **2.1 "Approval of a vehicle"** means the approval of a vehicle type with regard to door latches and door retention components.
*   **2.2. "Vehicle type"** means a category of motor vehicles which do not differ in such essential respects as: designation, type of latch, type of door retention component, the way in which they are fitted, and type of sliding doors.
*   **2.3. "Auxiliary door latch"** is a latch equipped with a fully latched position with or without a secondary latch position, and fitted to a door or door system equipped with a primary door latch system.
*   **2.4. "Auxiliary door latch system"** consists, at a minimum, of an auxiliary door latch and a striker.
*   **2.5. "Back door"** is a door or door system on the back end of a motor vehicle through which passengers can gain ingress or egress (including ejection), or through which cargo can be loaded or unloaded. It does not include trunk lids or doors/windows composed entirely of glazing material with latches/hinges attached directly to the glazing.
*   **2.6. "Body member"** is that portion of the hinge normally affixed to the body structure.
*   **2.7. Additional protections**
    *   **2.7.1. "Child safety lock system"** is a locking device which can be engaged and released independently of other locking devices and which, when engaged, prevents operation of the interior door handle or other release device.
    *   **2.7.2. "Full locking system"** is a system that renders the interior door release handle or any other door interior latch release control of any of the vehicles doors inoperable other than by operation of the systems locks.
*   **2.8. "Doors"** means hinged or sliding doors which lead directly into a compartment that contains one or more seating positions and which are not folding doors, roll-up doors and doors that are designed to be easily attached to or removed from motor vehicles manufactured for operation without doors.
*   **2.9. "Door closure warning system"** is a system that will activate a visual signal located where it can be clearly seen by the driver when a door latch system is not in its fully latched position and while the vehicle ignition is activated.
*   **2.10. "Door Hinge System"** is one or more hinges used to support a door.
*   **2.11. "Door latch system"** consists, at a minimum, of a latch and a striker.
*   **2.12. "Door member"** is that portion of the hinge normally affixed to the door structure and constituting the swinging member.
*   **2.13. "Door system"** is the door, latch, striker, hinges, sliding track combinations and other door retention components on a door and its surrounding doorframe. The door system of a double door includes both doors.
*   **2.14. "Double door"** is a system of two doors where the front door or wing door opens first and connects to the rear door or bolted door, which opens second.
*   **2.15. "Fork-bolt"** is the part of the latch that engages and retains the striker when in a latched position.
*   **2.16. "Fork-bolt opening direction"** is the direction opposite to that in which the striker enters the latch to engage the fork-bolt.
*   **2.17. "Fully latched position"** is the coupling condition of the latch that retains the door in a completely closed position.
*   **2.18. "Hinge"** is a device used to position the door relative to the body structure and control the path of the door swing for passenger ingress and egress.
*   **2.19. "Hinge pin"** is that portion of the hinge normally interconnecting the body and door members and establishing the swing axis.
*   **2.20. "Latch"** is a device employed to maintain the door in a closed position relative to the vehicle body with provisions for deliberate release (or operation).
*   **2.21. "Primary door latch"** is a latch equipped with both a fully latched position and a secondary latched position and is designated as a "primary door latch" by the manufacturer.
*   **2.22. "Primary door latch system"** consists, at a minimum, of a primary door latch and a striker.
*   **2.23. "Secondary latched position"** refers to the coupling condition of the latch that retains the door in a partially closed position.
*   **2.24. "Side front door"** is a door that, in a side view, has 50 per cent or more of its opening area forward of the rearmost point on the driver's seat back.
*   **2.25. "Side rear door"** is a door that, in a side view, has 50 per cent or more of its opening area to the rear of the rearmost point on the driver's seat back.
*   **2.26. "Striker"** is a device with which the latch engages to maintain the door in the fully latched or secondary latched position.
*   **2.27. "Trunk lid"** is a movable body panel that provides access from outside the vehicle to a space wholly partitioned from the occupant compartment by a permanently attached partition or fixed or fold-down seat back.

## 3. Application for approval
The application shall be submitted by the manufacturer or accredited representative, accompanied by drawings, technical descriptions, and samples of latches and retention components. A representative vehicle shall be submitted for testing.

## 4. Approval
If the vehicle type meets the requirements, approval shall be granted and an approval number assigned. An international approval mark shall be affixed to conforming vehicles.

## 5. General requirements
*   **5.1.** Applies to all side and back doors and door components in scope, except for folding doors, roll-up doors, detachable doors, and emergency egress doors.
*   **5.2. Door latches**
    *   **5.2.1.** Each hinged door system shall have at least one primary door latch system.
    *   **5.2.2.** Each sliding door system shall have either a primary door latch system, or a door latch system with a fully latched position **and** a door closure warning system.

## 6. Performance requirements
### 6.1. Hinged doors
*   **6.1.1. Load test one:** Primary/auxiliary latch systems in fully latched position shall not separate under 11,000 N load perpendicular to latch face. Primary latch in secondary position shall not separate under 4,500 N.
*   **6.1.2. Load test two:** Primary/auxiliary latch systems in fully latched position shall not separate under 9,000 N load in fork-bolt opening direction. Primary latch in secondary position shall not separate under 4,500 N.
*   **6.1.3. Load test three (vertical doors):** Primary latch shall not disengage from fully latched position under 9,000 N vertical load.
*   **6.1.4. Inertial load:** Primary/auxiliary latch systems shall not disengage from fully latched position when subjected to a 30 g inertial load in specified vehicle directions (longitudinal, transverse, and for back doors, vertical). Can be demonstrated by dynamic test or calculation.
*   **6.1.5. Door hinges:** Each hinge system shall support the door and not separate under specified loads (11,000 N longitudinal, 9,000 N transverse, and for vertical doors, 9,000 N vertical).

### 6.2. Sliding side doors
*   **6.2.1. Load test one:** At least one door latch system in fully latched position shall not separate under 11,000 N load perpendicular to latch face. Primary latch in secondary position shall not separate under 4,500 N.
*   **6.2.2. Load test two:** At least one door latch system in fully latched position shall not separate under 9,000 N load in fork-bolt opening direction. Primary latch in secondary position shall not separate under 4,500 N.
*   **6.2.3. Inertial load:** Door latch system shall not disengage from fully latched position when subjected to a 30 g inertial load in longitudinal and transverse directions. Can be demonstrated by dynamic test or calculation.
*   **6.2.4. Door system:** Track/slide combination shall not separate from door frame when a total transverse force of 18,000 N is applied to the door.

### 6.3. Door locks
*   **6.3.1.** Each door shall have at least one locking device preventing exterior handle operation, with interior operating means.
*   **6.3.2. Rear side doors:** Each shall have a locking device preventing interior handle operation, requiring separate actions to unlock and operate (e.g., child safety lock).
*   **6.3.3. Back doors:** If equipped with interior handle, shall have an interior locking device preventing its operation, requiring separate actions to unlock and operate.

## 7. Test procedures
*   **7.1. Hinged doors:** Load tests per Annex 3, inertial tests per Annex 4, hinge tests per Annex 5.
*   **7.2. Sliding side doors:** Load tests per Annex 3, inertial tests per Annex 4, full door system test per Annex 6.

## 8. Modification and extension of approval of the vehicle type
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing.

## 9. Conformity of production
Vehicles bearing the approval mark shall conform to the approved type. Random checks shall be conducted.

## 10. Penalties for non-conformity of production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met.

## 11. Production definitively discontinued
The approval holder shall inform the Type Approval Authority if production ceases.

## 12. Names and addresses of Technical Services responsible for conducting approval tests and of Type Approval Authority
Contracting Parties shall communicate relevant information to the UN Secretariat.

## 13. Transitional provisions
Specifies dates for application of the 03 and 04 series of amendments regarding granting of approvals and first registration of vehicles.
