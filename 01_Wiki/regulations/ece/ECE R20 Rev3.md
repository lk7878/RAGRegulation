**UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF MOTOR VEHICLE HEADLAMPS EMITTING AN ASYMMETRICAL PASSING BEAM OR A DRIVING BEAM OR BOTH AND EQUIPPED WITH HALOGEN FILAMENT LAMPS (H4 LAMPS)**

**CONTENTS**

**A. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS**
1. **Scope**: This Regulation applies to motor vehicle headlamps which may incorporate lenses of glass or plastic material.
2. **Definitions**: Defines terms such as "Lens", "Coating", and criteria for headlamps of different "types" (trade name, optical characteristics, traffic direction suitability, beam kind, lens/coating materials).
3. **Markings**: Headlamps must bear the applicant's trade name/mark and have space for the approval mark. Headlamps for both right/left-hand traffic must be marked "R/D" and "L/G".
4. **Approval**: Grants approval if samples satisfy provisions. Describes the composition and arrangement of the international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, approval number, and additional symbols for beam type, traffic direction, plastic lens "PL", maximum luminous intensity reference). Includes rules for grouped/combined/reciprocally incorporated lamps.

**B. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS FOR HEADLAMPS**
5. **General specifications**: Headlamps must retain photometric characteristics under vibration, have an adjustment device (with exceptions), secure filament lamp fixation, and allow only two distinct settings for right/left-hand traffic. Complementary tests for photometric stability and plastic lenses are required.
6. **Illumination**:
    *   **General**: Illumination is measured on a vertical screen 25m away using a standard 12V H4 filament lamp.
    *   **Passing Beam**: Must produce a sharp "cut-off" with specific geometric limits. Prescribes aiming procedure and minimum/maximum illumination values (in lux) at specified points (e.g., 75R, 50L, 50V, 25L, 25R) and zones (I, III, IV) on the screen for right-hand and left-hand traffic.
    *   **Driving Beam**: Prescribes aiming and requirements, including that point HV must be within the 80% isolux of maximum illumination (E_M). E_M must be between 48 and 240 lux. Provides formula for calculating and rounding the maximum intensity reference mark (I'_M).
    *   **Adjustable Reflectors**: Requirements apply for each declared mounting position, with verification procedures including tests after vertical adjustment.
7. **Provisions concerning coloured lenses and filters**: Specifies colorimetric coordinates and transmission factor (≥0.78) for selective-yellow filters/lenses. Filters must be non-removable by the user.
8. **Gauging of discomfort**: Discomfort caused by the passing beam shall be gauged (subject of a future recommendation).
9. **Standard (reference) headlamp**: Defines specifications for a reference headlamp (min. 160mm diameter, illumination within 90%-120% of prescribed limits) for testing purposes.
10. **Observation concerning colour**: Notes that Contracting Parties may prohibit white or selective-yellow headlamps on vehicles they register.

**C. FURTHER ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS**
11. **Modification of the headlamp type and extension of approval**: Procedures for notifying modifications and extending approvals.
12. **Conformity of production**: Holder must ensure effective quality control, have test equipment, record data, analyze results, and carry out prescribed tests. Competent Authority may verify control methods and conduct inspections.
13. **Penalties for non-conformity of production**: Approval may be withdrawn for non-compliance, with notification to other Parties.
14. **Production definitely discontinued**: Manufacturer must inform approval authority upon ceasing production.
15. **Names and addresses of Technical Services and Administrative Departments**: Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.
16. **Transitional provisions**:
    *   ECE approvals under this Regulation ceased 6 months after the entry into force of Regulation No. 112.
    *   Existing approvals and extensions remain valid indefinitely.
    *   Approvals for replacement headlamps for vehicles in use may continue to be issued.
    *   Fitting of headlamps approved to this Regulation on new vehicles, vehicle types, or vehicles in use remains permitted as specified.

**ANNEXES**
*   **Annex 1**: Model communication form for approval/refusal/extension/withdrawal.
*   **Annex 2**: Examples of arrangements of approval marks for various headlamp types (independent, grouped, combined).
*   **Annex 3**: Measuring screen diagrams and dimensions for right-hand and left-hand traffic.
*   **Annex 4**: Tests for stability of photometric performance in operation (clean and dirty headlamp tests, test for change in vertical position of cut-off line under heat).
*   **Annex 5**: Minimum requirements for conformity of production control procedures (general tolerances, manufacturer's verification tests, sampling criteria).
*   **Annex 6**: Requirements for lamps incorporating lenses of plastic material - testing of lens/material samples and of complete lamps (resistance to temperature, atmospheric/chemical agents, detergents/hydrocarbons, mechanical deterioration, coating adherence).
*   **Annex 7**: Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector (sampling plan for production conformity verification, including first and repeated sampling procedures).
