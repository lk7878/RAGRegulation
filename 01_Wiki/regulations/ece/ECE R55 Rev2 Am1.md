**UN Regulation No. 55 - Revision 2 - Amendment 1 (Supplement 5 to the 01 series of amendments)**

**Date of entry into force:** 8 October 2016

This document constitutes Amendment 1 to Revision 2 of UN Regulation No. 55. The following are the amendments to the uniform provisions concerning the approval of mechanical coupling components of combinations of vehicles.

**Amendments:**

*   **Paragraph 2.5.** is amended to clarify the definition of "Non-standard miscellaneous mechanical coupling devices and components".
*   **Paragraph 2.6.12.** (Class S) is amended.
*   A new **paragraph 2.6.14.** is added, defining **Class W** couplings (Non-standard miscellaneous, automatic drawbar coupling clevis type with integrated automated electric and pneumatic connectors).
*   **Paragraph 2.9.** (Remote indicators) is amended.
*   **Paragraph 3.2.8.** is amended regarding submission of installation data for device/component designed for a specific vehicle type.
*   **Paragraph 4.7.** is amended regarding verification of installation features for certain after-market Class A, K, or S devices/components.
*   **Paragraph 5.1.** is amended regarding the availability of installation information from vehicle manufacturers or approval authorities to applicants.
*   **Paragraph 5.3.** and its subparagraphs are amended. Paragraphs 5.3.2. and 5.3.2.1. are deleted.
*   **Paragraph 13.** is replaced with new text concerning the application of the Regulation by EU Member States and transitional provisions for Supplement 5.
*   **Annex 1, Items 10. and 11.** are amended regarding instructions and information for attachment.
*   **Annex 2, Items 8. and 9.** are similarly amended.
*   A new **Appendix 1 to Annex 2** is inserted, providing a detailed list of installation data required from the vehicle manufacturer for a mechanical coupling device or component designed for a specific vehicle type.
*   **Annex 4, Table 1** is amended to include marking requirements for Class S and other coupling classes.
*   **Annex 5, Paragraph 1.2.** is amended regarding towing bracket dimensions.
*   **Annex 5:** A new **paragraph 12.** is inserted, specifying requirements for **Class W** drawbar type couplings (automated connection/disconnection, articulation angles, remote control/indication). Subsequent paragraphs are renumbered.
*   **Annex 6:**
    *   **Paragraph 3.1.3.** is amended regarding fixing point specifications.
    *   **Paragraph 3.4.2.** is amended regarding testing of Class L toroidal eyes.
    *   **Paragraph 3.5.2.** is amended regarding dynamic testing of hook type couplings. Paragraph 3.5.2.4 is deleted.
    *   **Paragraph 3.6.3.** is amended regarding verification of resistance to bending for steered axles.
*   **Annex 7:**
    *   **Paragraph 1.1.** is amended regarding attachment, height, clearance, and coupling/uncoupling angles for coupling balls, hook couplings, and towing brackets on M1, M2 (<3.5t), and N1 vehicles. Includes provisions for non-demountable equipment and registration plate visibility.
    *   **Paragraph 1.2.** is amended regarding attachment of coupling heads or toroidal drawbar eyes on trailers.
    *   **Paragraph 1.3.4.** is amended regarding minimum angles for coupling and uncoupling drawbar eyes and Class K hook type couplings for vehicles >3.5t.
