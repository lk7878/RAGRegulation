# Regulation No. 103 (Revision 1)
**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of replacement pollution control devices for power-driven vehicles**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to the type-approval, as separate technical units, of pollution control devices to be fitted in one or more types of motor vehicles of categories covered by the scope of the related version of Regulation No. 83 as replacement parts.
Catalytic converters and particulate filters are considered pollution control devices for this Regulation.

## 2. Definitions
- **2.1. "Original pollution control device"**: A device or assembly covered by the vehicle type approval, indicated in Annex 2 of Regulation No. 83.
- **2.2. "Replacement pollution control device"**: A device or assembly for which approval can be obtained under this Regulation, other than the original.
- **2.3. "Original replacement pollution control device"**: A device or assembly indicated in Annex 2 of Regulation No. 83 but offered as a separate technical unit by the vehicle type-approval holder.
- **2.4. "Type of pollution control device"**: Catalytic converters and particulate filters not differing in specified essential aspects (substrate, material, volume, catalyst content, dimensions, etc.).
- **2.5. "Vehicle type"**: As defined in Regulation No. 83.
- **2.6. "Approval of a replacement pollution control device"**: Approval regarding pollutant emissions, noise, vehicle performance, and OBD compatibility.
- **2.7. "Deteriorated replacement pollution control device"**: A device aged or artificially deteriorated to meet specific requirements in Regulation No. 83.
- **2.8. "Periodically regenerating system"**: A device requiring regeneration within less than 4,000 km of normal operation.

## 3. Application for Approval
- **3.1.** Submitted by manufacturer or authorized representative.
- **3.2.** Requires documents in triplicate: drawings, vehicle type description, device position drawings, approval mark location, OBD compatibility indication, and information document model.
- **3.3.** Requires provision to technical service: approved test vehicle(s) with new original device, a sample of the replacement device, and an additional deteriorated sample for OBD-equipped vehicles.

## 4. Approval
- **4.1.** Granted if requirements in paragraph 5 are met.
- **4.2.** Original replacement devices listed in Regulation No. 83 Annex 2 do not need approval if they meet marking and documentation requirements.
- **4.3.** An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the amendment series.
- **4.4.** Approval may be granted without verification if the device type is listed in Regulation No. 83 Annex 2.
- **4.5.** Approval/refusal communicated via form in Annex 1.
- **4.6.-4.9.** Specifies the international approval mark (circle with "E" and country code, regulation number "103R", and approval number), its placement, and examples in Annex 2.

## 5. Requirements
### 5.1. General Requirements
- Device must enable vehicle compliance with original regulations, be mounted in the exact original position, include equivalent thermal protections, and be durable.

### 5.2. Requirements Regarding Emissions
- Test vehicle(s) with the replacement device undergo a Type I test per Regulation No. 83.
- Procedure involves comparison with the original device: establishing a baseline with the original device, then testing with the replacement device.
- Emissions (M) with the replacement device must satisfy: `M ≤ 0.85S + 0.4G` and `M ≤ G`, where S is the mean with the original device and G is the applicable limit value adjusted for deterioration.

### 5.3. Requirements Regarding Noise and Vehicle Performance
- Must satisfy Regulation No. 59. As an alternative, maximum absorbed power measured with the replacement device must not be more than 5% lower than with the original device.

### 5.4. Requirements Regarding Durability
- Must comply with Regulation No. 83 Type V test or apply specified deterioration factors based on engine category (see table in text).

### 5.5. Requirements Regarding OBD Compatibility (if applicable)
- Required only if the original device was monitored by OBD.
- Compatibility demonstrated per procedures in Regulation No. 83, Annex 11, Appendix 1.
- Specific rules for preconditioning, system status verification, malfunction indicator activation, and adjustment of OBD threshold limits.

### 5.6. Requirements for Replacement Periodically Regenerating Systems
- Subject to specific tests per Regulation No. 83 Annex 13.
- Other requirements (emissions evaluation, noise/performance, durability, OBD) apply analogously.

## 6. Modification of Type and Extension of Approval
- Modifications must be notified. Authority may accept or require further testing.
- Confirmation/refusal communicated per paragraph 4.5.

## 7. Conformity of Production
- Devices must conform to the approved type and meet requirements.
- Approval authority may carry out checks/tests.

## 8. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
- Approval may be withdrawn for non-conformity, with notification to other parties.

## 9. Production Definitively Discontinued
- Holder must inform approval authority, which notifies other parties.

## 10. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Approval Authorities
- Contracting Parties communicate details to UN Secretariat.

## 11. Documentation
- Each device must be marked with manufacturer's name/trademark and accompanied by information on applicable vehicles (including OBD suitability) and installation instructions.

## Appendices and Annexes
- **Appendix 1**: Information document template for type approval application.
- **Annex 1**: Model communication form for approval actions.
- **Annex 2**: Examples of approval mark arrangements.
