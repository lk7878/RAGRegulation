# UN Regulation No. 33 (Revision 1)
**UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF VEHICLES WITH REGARD TO THE BEHAVIOUR OF THE STRUCTURE OF THE IMPACTED VEHICLE IN A HEAD-ON COLLISION**

Incorporating Corrigendum 1 to the original text of the Regulation, the amended procedure for determining the "H" point and the actual torso angle, and general updating of administrative provisions.

## 1. SCOPE
This regulation applies to the behaviour of the structure of the passenger compartment of private (passenger) cars (other than forward-control vehicles) when subjected to a head-on collision.

## 2. DEFINITIONS
*   **Vehicle type:** A category of power-driven vehicles which do not differ in essential respects affecting the impact test results (e.g., dimensions, forward structure, passenger compartment lines, engine siting).
*   **Forward-control vehicle:** A vehicle where the centre of the steering wheel is in the forward quarter of the vehicle's total length.
*   **Passenger compartment:** The space for occupant accommodation, bounded by the roof, floor, side walls, doors, outside glazing, front bulkhead, and the plane of the rear compartment bulkhead or the rear seat back support.
*   **Unladen kerb weight:** The weight of the vehicle in running order, unoccupied and unladen but complete with fuel, coolant, lubricant, tools, and a standard spare wheel.

## 3. APPLICATION FOR APPROVAL
Submitted by the manufacturer or representative, including detailed vehicle description, drawings, and particulars of unladen kerb weight. A representative vehicle must be submitted for testing.

## 4. APPROVAL
*   Granted if the vehicle meets requirements.
*   An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments applicable at the time of approval.
*   An international approval mark (a circle with "E" and country code, plus "33R-XXXXXX") must be affixed to conforming vehicles.
*   Examples of the approval mark arrangement are provided in Annex 2.

## 5. REQUIREMENTS
After a frontal barrier impact test at 48.3 km/h with an unladen vehicle (no manikin), the passenger compartment must satisfy the following:
*   **Seat/Instrument Panel Clearance:** ≥ 450 mm between the "R" point plane and the rearmost instrument panel projection over a specified width (para 5.2).
*   **Footwell Length:** ≥ 650 mm from the front of the compartment to the "R" point plane along a specified line (para 5.3).
*   **Footwell Width:** ≥ 250 mm for each front seat (para 5.4).
*   **Headroom:** The vertical distance from floor to roof through the "R" point shall not be reduced by more than 10% (para 5.5).
*   **Measurement:** Distances may be measured with a 10 daN compression force (para 5.6).
*   **Injury Risk:** No rigid component in the compartment shall constitute a risk of serious injury (para 5.7).
*   **Doors:** Side doors shall not open due to the impact (para 5.8).
*   **Egress:** After impact, sufficient doors must be openable without tools to allow all occupants to exit (except for vehicles without a rigid roof) (para 5.9).

## 6. TESTS
Compliance with requirements is checked by the methods in Annexes 3 ("H" point determination) and 4 (frontal impact test).

## 7. MODIFICATIONS AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL
Modifications must be notified. The authority may require further testing. Extensions of approval are communicated to other parties.

## 8. CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Vehicles bearing the approval mark must conform to the approved type. Random checks on production vehicles are conducted, primarily through measurements, but may include impact tests if necessary.

## 9. PENALTIES FOR NON-CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Approval may be withdrawn if production does not conform or if vehicles fail tests. Withdrawal must be communicated to other parties.

## 10. PRODUCTION DEFINITELY DISCONTINUED
The approval holder must inform the granting authority if production ceases. The authority informs other parties.

## 11. NAMES AND ADDRESSES
Parties must communicate the details of their technical services and administrative departments to the UN Secretariat.

## ANNEX 3 - PROCEDURE FOR DETERMINING THE "H" POINT AND ACTUAL TORSO ANGLE
*   **Purpose:** To establish the "H" point location and actual torso angle for seating positions and verify them against manufacturer design specifications ("R" point and design torso angle).
*   **Tolerances:** The measured "H" point must lie within a 50mm x 50mm square centered on the "R" point. The actual torso angle must be within 5° of the design torso angle. If not, multiple measurements are taken, and averages may be used.
*   **Procedure:** Details the installation and use of the Three-Dimensional "H" Point Machine (3-D H machine) in the vehicle seat under specified conditions (vehicle attitude, seat adjustment, temperature, etc.).
*   **Appendices:**
    *   Appendix 1: Description and diagrams of the 3-D H machine.
    *   Appendix 2: Description of the three-dimensional reference system.
    *   Appendix 3: Form for listing reference data (R point coordinates, design torso angle, seat adjustment specs).
