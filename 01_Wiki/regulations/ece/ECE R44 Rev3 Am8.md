**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of restraining devices for child occupants of power-driven vehicles ("Child Restraint Systems")**

*   **Regulation:** UN Regulation No. 44
*   **Revision:** 3
*   **Amendment:** 8
*   **Supplement:** 15 to the 04 series of amendments
*   **Date of entry into force:** 28 May 2019
*   **Publication date:** 24 June 2019
*   **Authentic and legally binding text:** ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2018/131

**Amendment Content**

This document constitutes Amendment 8 to Revision 3 of UN Regulation No. 44.

The amendment modifies paragraph 4.4., which specifies requirements for a warning label on rearward-facing child restraint systems.

**Key Changes to Paragraph 4.4.:**
*   **Label Location & Surface:** The label must be permanently attached on the visible inner surface (including the inside of a side wing near the child's head) in the approximate area where the child's head rests.
*   **Label Dimensions:** Minimum overall dimensions of 120 mm x 60 mm, or an equivalent area.
*   **Layout Flexibility:** The label layout may differ from the provided example, but the content must meet the precise prescriptions.
*   **Additional Information:** No other information is permitted on the label unless placed outside a clearly marked rectangle of at least the required minimum dimensions. An exception is made for a part number, barcode, or similar identification mark not exceeding 8 mm x 35 mm (or equivalent area).
*   **Pictogram Integrity:** No deviations in the shape and orientation of the prescribed pictograms are permitted. Customized appearances of the pictogram images are prohibited, except for the "hand with pointing index finger" and the "open face booklet with letter 'i'" pictograms, provided they remain clearly recognizable.
*   **Production Tolerances:** Small irregularities in line thickness, label imprinting, and other relevant production tolerances are acceptable.

**Referenced Figures:**
*   **Figure A:** Warning label (layout example).
*   **Figure B:** Specifies the use of the ISO 2575:2004 - Z.01 pictogram, with a minimum outer diameter of 38 mm.
*   **Figure C:** Specifies the use of a pictogram depicting airbag deployment danger, measuring 40 mm in width and 28 mm in height (or proportionally larger).
