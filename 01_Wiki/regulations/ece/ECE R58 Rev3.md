# UN Regulation No. 58 (Revision 3)

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of:**
I. Rear underrun protective devices (RUPDs)
II. Vehicles with regard to the installation of an RUPD of an approved type
III. Vehicles with regard to their rear underrun protection (RUP)

## 1. Scope
### 1.1. This Regulation applies to:
1.1.1. **PART I:** The RUPDs which are intended to be fitted to vehicles of categories M, N and O1.
1.1.2. **PART II:** The installation on vehicles of categories M, N and O1 of RUPDs which have been type approved to Part I of this Regulation.
1.1.3. **PART III:** Vehicles of categories M, N and O1 equipped with an RUPD which has not been separately approved according to Part I of this Regulation or so designed and/or equipped that its component parts can be regarded as totally or partially fulfilling the function of the RUPD.
1.1.4. Vehicles of categories M1, M2, M3, N1, O1 and O2 on grounds of rear underrun protection.

### 1.2. This Regulation does not apply to:
1.2.1. Tractive units for articulated vehicles.
1.2.2. Trailers specially designed and constructed for the carriage of very long loads of indivisible length, such as timber, steel bars, etc.

### 1.3. Exemptions
Vehicles where any RUPD (e.g., fixed, removable, foldable, adjustable, etc.) is incompatible with their use on traffic roads may be partly or fully exempted from this Regulation, subject to the decision of the Type Approval Authority.

## 2. General Requirements
### 2.1. All vehicles shall be so constructed and/or equipped as to offer effective protection over their whole width against under-running of vehicles mentioned in paragraph 1. of this Regulation in the event of rear collision with vehicles of category M1 and N1.
### 2.2. The vehicle shall be tested under the conditions as laid down in paragraph 2. of Annex 5.
### 2.3. Compliance Conditions
Any vehicle in one of the categories M1, M2, M3, N1, O1 or O2 shall be deemed to satisfy the condition set out above:
(a) If it satisfies the same conditions as set out in Part II or Part III, or
(b) If the ground clearance of the rear part of the unladen vehicle does not exceed 550 mm over a width which is not shorter than that of the rear axle by more than 100 mm on either side (excluding any tyre bulging close to the ground), or
(c) If, in case of vehicles of categories O1 and O2 where the tyres project for more than half of their width outside the bodywork (excluding the wheel guards) or outside the chassis in the absence of bodywork, the ground clearance of the rear part of the unladen vehicle does not exceed 550 mm over a width which is not less than 100 mm deducted from the distance measured between the innermost points of the tyres (excluding any tyre bulging close to the ground), on either side.

Where there is more than one rear axle, the width to be considered is that of the widest.
The requirements of paragraphs 2.3.(b) and 2.3.(c) above shall be satisfied at least on a line:
(a) At a distance of not more than 450 mm from the rear extremity of the vehicle;
(b) That may have interruptions totalling not more than 200 mm.

### 2.4. Off-road Vehicles (Category G)
Any vehicle of category G will be deemed to satisfy the condition for the ground clearance set out above, if the departure angle (ISO 612:1978) does not exceed:
(a) 10° for vehicles of categories M1G and N1G;
(b) 20° for vehicles of categories M2G and N2G; and
(c) 25° for vehicles of categories M3G and N3G;
over a width which is not shorter than that of the rear axle by more than 100 mm on either side (excluding any bulging of the tyres close to the ground).

Where there is more than one rear axle, the width to be considered is that of the widest. The requirements of subparagraphs (a) to (c) of this paragraph shall be satisfied at least on a line:
(a) At a distance of not more than 450 mm from the rear extremity of the vehicle;
(b) That may have interruptions summing up in not more than 200 mm.

## 3. Definitions Common to Parts I, II and III
### 3.1. For the purpose of this Regulation:
3.1.1. **"Unladen mass"** means the mass of the vehicle in running order, unoccupied and unladen but complete with fuel, coolant, lubricant, tools and a spare wheel (if provided as standard equipment by the vehicle manufacturer).
3.1.2. **"Maximum mass"** means the mass stated by the vehicle manufacturer to be technically permissible (this mass may be higher than the "permissible maximum mass" laid down by the national administration).
3.1.3. An **"RUPD"** normally consists of a cross-member and links to the chassis side members or other structural members of the vehicle.
3.1.4. **"Separate cab"** means a cab attached to the vehicle's frame by specific links and which has no common part with the cargo area.

## Part I. Approval of RUPDs
### 4. Definitions
4.1.1. **"Approval of an RUPD"** means the approval of such a type of RUPD with respect to the requirements laid down in paragraph 7. below.
4.1.2. **"Type of RUPD"** means RUPDs which do not differ with respect to the essential characteristics such as shape, dimensions, attachment, materials and the markings cited in paragraph 5.2.2. below.

### 5. Application for Approval
5.1. The application for approval of an RUPD shall be submitted by the manufacturer of the RUPD or by his duly accredited representative.
5.2. Required documentation and a sample RUPD must be submitted.
5.3. An RUPD representative of the type to be approved shall be submitted to the Technical Service for testing.
5.4. The Type Approval Authority shall verify arrangements for conformity of production control.

### 6. Approval
6.1. Approval is granted if the RUPD meets the requirements of paragraph 7.
6.2. An approval number is assigned. The first two digits indicate the series of amendments (e.g., 03 for the 03 series).
6.3. Notice of approval, extension, or refusal is communicated to Contracting Parties via a form in Annex 1.
6.4. An international approval mark must be affixed to every approved RUPD.
6.5. The approval mark shall be clearly legible and indelible.
6.6. Examples of approval mark arrangements are given in Annex 4.

### 7. Requirements
7.1. **Cross-member dimensions:** The cross-member shall have a section height of at least 120 mm (100 mm for certain vehicle categories). Lateral extremities must be rounded with a radius of at least 2.5 mm.
7.2. **Adjustable RUPDs:** If designed for multiple positions, there must be a secure method to lock it in the service position. Operator force to change position must not exceed 40 daN. A label (min. 60 x 120 mm) must inform the operator of the standard protective position.
7.3. **Resistance to forces:** The RUPD must offer adequate resistance to longitudinal forces, demonstrated via the test procedure in Annex 5. Maximum horizontal deflection during/after test must be recorded.
7.4. **Vehicles with platform lifts:** Special requirements apply if the RUPD is interrupted for the lift mechanism, including maximum lateral clearance (2.5 cm) and minimum effective surface area for individual elements.

### 8. Conformity of Production
Procedures must comply with the 1958 Agreement. Every approved RUPD must conform to the approved type. The Type Approval Authority may verify control methods periodically.

### 9. Penalties for Non-conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or if the device fails the test in Annex 5. Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

### 10. Modification and Extension of Approval
Any modification must be notified. The Type Approval Authority may require further testing. Extensions are assigned a series number and communicated.

### 11. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority if production ceases. The Authority notifies other Contracting Parties.

### 12. Names and Addresses
Contracting Parties must communicate the names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## Part II. Approval of a Vehicle with Regard to the Installation of an RUPD of an Approved Type
### 13. Definitions
13.1.1. **"Approval of a vehicle"** means the approval of a vehicle type with regard to the installation of an RUPD of an approved type according to Part I.
13.1.2. **"Vehicle type"** is defined based on essential aspects like rear axle width, vehicle rear structure, and the approved RUPDs fitted.

### 14. Application for Approval
14.1. Submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or representative.
14.2. Requires drawings, vehicle maximum mass, list of RUPDs, and possibly type approval communication forms for the RUPDs.
14.3. A representative vehicle fitted with the approved RUPD must be submitted for testing.
14.4. Conformity of production arrangements must be verified.

### 15. Approval
15.1. Granted if the vehicle is fitted with an approved RUPD and meets the requirements of paragraph 16, and has been tested per paragraph 2.2.
15.2. An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate amendment series).
15.3. Notice is communicated via a form in Annex 2.
15.4. An international approval mark must be affixed to the vehicle.
15.5.-15.8. Details on the placement and combination of approval marks.

### 16. Requirements for Installation of an Approved RUPD
16.1. **Ground clearance (heavy vehicles):** For categories N2 (MaxM >8t), N3, O3, O4, ground clearance under the device (unladen) must not exceed 450 mm (for certain suspensions) or 500 mm/8° departure angle. Height of test force application points is also regulated.
16.2. **Ground clearance (lighter vehicles):** For categories M, N1, N2 (MaxM ≤8t), O1, O2, ground clearance must not exceed 550 mm. Height of test force application points must not exceed 600 mm.
16.3. **Width:** The RUPD width must not exceed the rear axle width by more than 100 mm on either side, with specific provisions for bodywork and vehicles with protruding tyres.
16.4. **Horizontal distance from rear:** Maximum distance from the rear of the RUPD cross-member to the vehicle's rear extremity is specified (e.g., 400 mm minus deformation for lighter vehicles, 300 mm before test for heavier vehicles with platform lifts). Non-structural protrusions are excluded.
16.5. **Post-test ground clearance:** For heavy vehicles, after test forces are applied, ground clearance increase is limited to 60 mm (max 600 mm for vehicles with 8° departure angle).
16.6. **Vehicle maximum mass** must not exceed the value indicated on the RUPD's type approval form.
16.7. **Label for adjustable RUPDs** must be placed visibly at the driver's place or near the RUPD.

### 17. Conformity of Production
Every approved vehicle must conform to the approved type. Authority may verify control methods.

### 18. Penalties for Non-conformity
Approval may be withdrawn. Withdrawal must be notified.

### 19. Modification and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified. Authority may require further testing. Extensions are communicated.

### 20. Production Definitively Discontinued
Manufacturer must inform the Authority. Authority notifies other Parties.

### 21. Names and Addresses
Contracting Parties must communicate details of Technical Services and Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## Part III. Approval of a Vehicle with Regard to its Rear Underrun Protection (RUP)
### 22. Definitions
22.1.1. **"Approval of a vehicle"** means the approval of a vehicle type with regard to its RUP.
22.1.2. **"Vehicle type"** is defined based on essential aspects affecting the requirements.
22.1.3. **"Rear underrun protection (RUP)"** means either a special RUPD or bodywork/chassis parts that fulfill the RUPD function.

### 23. Application for Approval
23.1. Submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or representative.
23.2. Requires detailed description, drawings, maximum mass, and description of the RUP.
23.3. A representative vehicle must be submitted for testing.
23.4. Conformity of production arrangements must be verified.

### 24. Approval
24.1. Granted if the vehicle meets the requirements of paragraph 2.3.(b), 2.3.(c), or paragraph 25, and has been tested per paragraph 2.2.
24.2. An approval number is assigned (first two digits indicate amendment series).
24.3. Notice is communicated via a form in Annex 3.
24.4. An international approval mark must be affixed to the vehicle.
24.5.-24.8. Details on the placement and combination of approval marks.

### 25. Requirements for RUP
25.1. **Ground clearance (heavy vehicles):** Similar to Part II, paragraph 16.1.
25.2. **Ground clearance (lighter vehicles):** Similar to Part II, paragraph 16.2.
25.3. **Horizontal distance from rear:** Similar to Part II, paragraph 16.4, with specific distances for different vehicle categories and conditions (before/during test).
25.4. **Width:** Similar to Part II, paragraph 16.3.
25.5. **Cross-member dimensions:** Same as Part I, paragraph 7.1.
25.6. **Adjustable RUP:** Similar to Part I, paragraph 7.2, requiring a secure locking method, operator force limit (40 daN), and a label (min. 60 x 120 mm) placed near the RUP.
25.7. **Resistance to forces:** The RUP must offer adequate resistance. After test forces, the horizontal distance to the vehicle rear must not exceed 400 mm at any test point. Post-test ground clearance increase is limited for heavy vehicles.
25.8. **Practical test vs. calculation:** A practical test may be waived if calculation shows compliance with Annex 5, paragraph 3.
25.9. **Vehicles with platform lifts:** Special requirements similar to Part I, paragraph 7.4.

### 26. Conformity of Production
Every approved vehicle must conform to the approved type. Authority may verify control methods.

### 27. Penalties for Non-conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or if the vehicle fails the test. Withdrawal must be notified.

### 28. Modification and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified. Authority may require further testing. Extensions are communicated.

### 29. Production Definitively Discontinued
Manufacturer must inform the Authority. Authority notifies other Parties.

### 30. Names and Addresses
Contracting Parties must communicate details of Technical Services and Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## Part IV. Transitional Provisions
### 31. Transitional Provisions
31.1. From the entry into force of the 03 series of amendments, Contracting Parties shall not refuse approvals under the 03 series.
31.2. Until 1 September 2019, Contracting Parties shall not refuse approvals under the 02 series of amendments.
31.3. From 1 September 2019, approvals shall only be granted if the type meets the requirements of the 03 series.
31.4. From 1 September 2021, Contracting Parties are not obliged to accept vehicles/components not approved under the 03 series for national/regional type approval.
31.5. Until 1 September 2021, extensions for approvals under the 02 series shall not be refused. Specific conditions apply beyond this date.
31.6. Contracting Parties applying the Regulation after the most recent amendment's entry into force are not obliged to accept approvals under preceding series.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Communication form for RUPD approval (Part I).
*   **Annex 2:** Communication form for vehicle approval regarding installation of an approved RUPD (Part II).
*   **Annex 3:** Communication form for vehicle approval regarding its RUP (Part III).
*   **Annex 4:** Examples of approval mark arrangements.
*   **Annex 5:** Test conditions and procedures.
    *   **1. Test conditions for RUPDs:** Can be tested on a vehicle or a representative chassis part. Simulation by calculation is allowed under validation conditions.
    *   **2. Test conditions for vehicles:** Specifies vehicle state during test (level surface, tyre pressure, suspension condition, restraint method).
    *   **3. Test procedure:** Specifies application of horizontal forces via a test mandrel.
 *   **3.1.1.** A force (180 kN or 85% of force from vehicle max mass, whichever is lesser) applied to two symmetric points.
 *   **3.1.2.** A force (100 kN or 50% of force from vehicle max mass, whichever is lesser) applied to two points near the wheel edges and a third central point. Reduced forces apply for certain N2 vehicles.
 *   **3.1.3.** Force levels can be reduced to 80% for specific vehicles listed in Annex 6.
 *   **3.2.** Defines replacement force application points if the intended point is within an interruption area (e.g., for platform lifts).
*   **Annex 6:** Lists specific vehicle types (vehicles with tipping bodies, vehicles fitted with a platform lift at the rear).
*   **Annex 7:** Table summarizing key requirements (section height, ground clearance, horizontal distance, test force) for different vehicle categories.
