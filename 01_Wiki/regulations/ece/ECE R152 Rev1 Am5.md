**Document Information**
*   This document is **Amendment 5** to **Revision 1** of **UN Regulation No. 152**.
*   It is an addendum to the Agreement concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical UN Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles.
*   The authentic legal text is referenced as **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2022/19** (as amended).
*   **Date of Entry into Force:** 8 October 2022.

**Summary of Amendments**
This amendment introduces technical modifications and clarifications to UN R152 regarding Advanced Emergency Braking Systems (AEBS). Key changes include:

1.  **Revised Definitions (Paragraphs 2.12., 2.13.):**
    *   Clarifies the definition of a "dry road affording good adhesion" and its required Peak Braking Coefficient (PBC).
    *   Specifies methods and reference standards (ASTM E1136-19, ASTM F2493-20, UN R13-H Appendix 2) for measuring the sufficient nominal PBC.

2.  **New Definition (Paragraph 2.17.):**
    *   Introduces a formal definition and calculation formula for **"the mean fully developed deceleration (dₘ)"**.

3.  **Updated Performance Requirements (Paragraphs 5.2.1.4., 5.2.2.4.):**
    *   Refines the conditions under which the AEBS must achieve specified speed reductions to avoid or mitigate collisions.
    *   **For vehicle targets (5.2.1.4):** Specifies conditions such as road adhesion, mass conditions, lateral displacement limits, ambient illumination (≥1000 Lux), weather, and driving path.
    *   **For pedestrian targets (5.2.2.4):** Specifies conditions including pedestrian movement, situational clarity, road adhesion, mass conditions, impact point displacement, ambient illumination (≥2000 Lux), weather, and driving path.

4.  **Revised Test Procedures (Paragraphs 6.1.1., 6.3.1.):**
    *   **Test Surface (6.1.1.):** Requires tests on a flat, dry concrete/asphalt road with good adhesion, allowing a consistent slope between 0% and 1%.
    *   **Test Target (6.3.1.):** Specifies that the target for vehicle detection tests must be a regular production passenger car (M1 category) or a "soft target" representative of a passenger vehicle according to **ISO 19206-3:2021**. The reference point is the most rearward point on the vehicle's centerline.
