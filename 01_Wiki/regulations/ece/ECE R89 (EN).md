**Regulation Structure and Scope**

This Regulation (UN R89) is divided into three distinct but related parts concerning the limitation of maximum speed for heavy vehicles:

*   **Part I**: Approval of vehicles with regard to limitation of their maximum speed (for vehicles equipped with an integrated speed limiting function not separately approved as an SLD).
*   **Part II**: Approval of vehicles with regard to the installation of a speed limitation device (SLD) of a type approved under Part III.
*   **Part III**: Approval of speed limitation devices (SLD) intended for fitting to vehicles.

The Regulation applies to vehicles of categories M3, N2, and N3, as defined in the Consolidated Resolution on the Construction of Vehicles (R.E.3). Its purpose is to limit the maximum road speed of these vehicles via an SLD or a speed limiting function within a vehicle system that controls engine fuel feed.

**Key Definitions**

*   **Limitation speed (V)**: The maximum vehicle speed such that its design or equipment does not permit a response after a positive action on the accelerator control.
*   **Set speed (Vset)**: The intended mean vehicle speed when operating in a stabilized condition.
*   **Stabilized speed (Vstab)**: The mean vehicle speed when operating under specified test conditions.
*   **Maximum speed (Vmax)**: The maximum speed reached by the vehicle in the first half period of the response curve during testing.
*   **Speed Limitation Device (SLD)**: A device whose primary function is to control the fuel feed to the engine to limit vehicle speed.
*   **Unladen mass**: The mass of the vehicle in running order without crew, passengers, or load, but with a full fuel tank and usual tools/spare wheel.

**General Technical Requirements (Applicable across Parts I, II, III)**

The core requirements for the speed limiting function or SLD include:
1.  **Inviolability**: The set/limitation speed must not be capable of being increased or removed temporarily or permanently on vehicles in use. Protection against unauthorized adjustment or interruption of energy supply must be provided (e.g., via seals, special tools). A failure mode analysis to the first failure is required.
2.  **Non-interference with Service Brakes**: The speed limitation function/SLD must not actuate the vehicle's service braking system. A permanent brake (e.g., retarder) may only operate *after* the fuel feed has been restricted to the minimum position.
3.  **Accelerator Override**: The device must not affect road speed if a positive accelerator action is applied when running at the set speed. Normal accelerator control for gear changing is permitted.
4.  **Safety**: No malfunction or unauthorized interference shall result in engine power exceeding that demanded by the driver's accelerator position.
5.  **Multiple Controls**: The function must operate regardless of which accelerator control (if more than one reachable from the driver's seat) is used.
6.  **Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC)**: Must operate satisfactorily in its electromagnetic environment without causing unacceptable disturbance.
7.  **Checkability**: Procedures must exist to check the function while the vehicle is stationary (e.g., for production conformity or periodic inspection).
8.  **Continuous Operation**: All necessary components must be energized whenever the vehicle is being driven.

**Approval and Administrative Provisions**

*   **Application & Approval Process**: Defined for each Part, involving submission of documentation and a representative sample (vehicle or SLD) to the technical service. The competent authority verifies arrangements for conformity of production control before granting approval.
*   **Approval Number & Mark**: A unique approval number is assigned. An international approval mark (an encircled "E" with country code, regulation number "89R", approval number, and a rectangle showing the set speed/range) must be affixed to approved vehicles/SLDs. Vehicles may also require an interior plate stating "SPEED LIMITER FITTED" and calibration details.
*   **Modifications & Extension**: Modifications to an approved type must be notified. The authority may confirm, require further tests, or refuse approval extension.
*   **Conformity of Production (CoP)**: The holder of approval must ensure effective quality control and that production conforms to the approved type. Competent authorities conduct periodic inspections (normally biennial).
*   **Penalties & Discontinuation**: Approval may be withdrawn for non-conformity. The authority must be notified if production is definitively discontinued.

**Testing Requirements (Annex 5)**

Performance is verified through tests, which can be conducted on a test track, chassis dynamometer, or (under specific conditions) an engine test bench. Key test methods include:
*   **Acceleration Test**: Vehicle accelerated from 10 km/h below Vset. Criteria specify limits for Vstab (≤ Vset + tolerance), Vmax (≤ Vstab + 5%), rate of speed change, and stabilization time.
*   **Steady Speed Test**: Vehicle driven at full acceleration up to a steady speed over a test basis. Criteria relate to Vstab and consistency between test runs.
*   **Endurance Tests**: SLDs are subjected to conditioning cycles (ambient temperature, high/low temperature, salted atmosphere, vibration) to verify durability and that the set speed remains unchanged.

**Annexes**
*   **Annexes 1-3**: Communication forms for the approval/extension/refusal/withdrawal of approval for Part I, II, and III respectively.
*   **Annex 4**: Examples of approval mark arrangements for SLDs (Model A) and vehicles (Models B & C).
*   **Annex 5**: Detailed test procedures and performance requirements for speed limitation and endurance (as summarized above).
