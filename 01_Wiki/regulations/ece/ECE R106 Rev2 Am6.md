# UN Regulation No. 106 (Revision 2, Amendment 6)

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of pneumatic tyres for agricultural vehicles and their trailers**

*Date of entry into force: 29 December 2018*
*Authentic and legal binding text: ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2018/57*

## 1. Scope
This Regulation covers new pneumatic tyres designed primarily, but not only, for agricultural and forestry vehicles (power-driven vehicles in category T1), agricultural machines (power-driven and trailed) and agricultural trailers, and identified by speed category symbols corresponding to speeds of 65 km/h (speed symbol "D") and below.

It does not apply to tyre types designated primarily for other purposes, such as:
(a) Earth-moving equipment;
(b) Industrial and lift trucks.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Type of agricultural tyre**: Tyres which do not differ in essential characteristics such as manufacturer's name, tyre-size designation, category of use, structure, speed category symbol, load capacity index, and tyre cross-section.
*   **Categories of use**: Include Tractor - steering wheel; Tractor - drive wheel - standard tread; Tractor - drive wheel - special tread; Implement - traction; Implement - trailer; Implement - mixed applications; Forestry machines – standard tread; Forestry machines – special tread; Construction applications (IND).
*   **Structures**: Diagonal (bias-ply), bias-belted, radial-ply.
*   **Tyre-size designation**: Shows nominal section width (mm), nominal aspect ratio, structure indicator ("-"/"D", "R", "B"), nominal rim diameter, and optional suffixes (e.g., "IMP", "FRONT", "IND", "IF", "VF", "CFO", "CHO").
*   **Service description**: Association of a load capacity index with a speed category symbol.
*   **Speed category symbols**: A2 (10 km/h), A4 (20 km/h), A6 (30 km/h), A8 (40 km/h), B (50 km/h), D (65 km/h).
*   **Load-capacity index (LI)**: A number indicating the load the tyre can carry (see Annex 4 for list).
*   **Special tread**: Tyre designed for better grip in marshy areas.
*   **Improved Flexion (IF) / Very High Flexion (VF) Tyre**: Tyre structure with a carcass more resistant than that of a corresponding standard tyre.
*   **NHS (Not for highway service)**: Tyre primarily for use outside public roads, suitable for temporary/incidental use on public roads.
*   **SS**: Differentiates tyres for off-highway vehicles (e.g., skid-steer loaders).

## 3. Markings
Tyres submitted for approval shall bear markings on both sidewalls and on one sidewall only.

**Markings on both sidewalls (3.1):**
*   Manufacturer's name or brand name/trademark.
*   Trade description/commercial name (if not same as brand name).
*   Tyre-size designation.
*   Indication of structure (optional "RADIAL" for radials, "BIAS-BELTED" for bias-belted).
*   Service description (for implement tyres, supplemented with relevant application symbol; for mixed applications, two service descriptions).
*   Supplementary service description (if applicable).
*   Inscription "DEEP" (or "R-2", "LS-3", "HF-3", "HF-4") for special tread tyres.
*   Inscriptions "F-1", "F-2", or "F-3" for tractor steering wheel tyres not marked "FRONT".
*   Inscriptions "LS-1", "LS-2", "LS-3", or "LS-4" for log-skidder tyres.
*   Inscription "IMPLEMENT" for implement tyres not marked "IMP".
*   Word "TUBELESS" if designed for use without an inner tube.
*   Pictogram with inscription "... bar MAX." (or "… kPa MAX") for maximum inflation pressure for bead seating (see Annex 11).
*   Inscription "R-3" for tractor drive wheel tyres with shallow tread.
*   Inscription "R-4" for construction application tyres not marked "IND".
*   Inscriptions "HF-1", "HF-2", "HF-3", or "HF-4" for high-flotation tyres.
*   Indication of inflation pressure for measurements and tests, preceded by "@" or "at" (e.g., "@ 240 kPa").

**Markings on one sidewall only (3.2):**
*   Date of manufacture (four digits: week and year).
*   A free space for the approval mark.

All markings must be clearly and legibly moulded during manufacture.

## 4. Application for Approval
Submitted by the manufacturer or representative, specifying details including tyre-size designation, manufacturer info, category of use, structure, speed category, load-capacity index, tyre/rim configuration, applicable rims, inflation pressures, and factor X (for theoretical rim width). A technical file and/or sample may be required.

## 5. Approval
*   Approval granted if requirements met.
*   An approval number is assigned.
*   Notice communicated via form in Annex 1.
*   An international approval mark (circle with "E", country number, regulation number "106R", dash, approval number) must be affixed to conforming tyres (see Annex 2).

## 6. Requirements
*   **Section width (6.1)**: Calculated by formula `S = S1 + K(A - A1)`. For sizes in Annex 5, values are given in tables.
*   **Outer diameter (6.2)**: Calculated by formula `D = d + 2H`. For sizes in Annex 5, values are given in tables.
*   **Tolerances for section width (6.3)**: Overall width must not exceed calculated section width by more than: Radial: +5%; Diagonal: +8%. For Annex 5 sizes, use specified percentages.
*   **Tolerances for outer diameter (6.4)**: Must be within `Dmin` and `Dmax` calculated using coefficients `a` and `b` which vary by category of use and structure (see table in para. 6.4.2). For Annex 5 sizes, use specified values.
*   **Test procedures (6.5)**:
    *   Dimensions measured as per Annex 6.
    *   Resistance to burst tested as per Annex 8. Pass criteria: no tread, ply, or cord separation; no broken beads or cords.
    *   Load/speed performance tested as per Annex 9. Pass criteria: no tread, ply, or cord separation; no broken cords. Chunking is acceptable.

## 7. Modification of Tyre Type and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority. Changes to tread pattern do not necessitate repeating tests. Extensions are communicated via Annex 1 form.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Production must conform to the approved type. Type Approval Authority verifies control methods at least once every two years.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met. Withdrawal is communicated via Annex 1 form.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
Manufacturer must inform the Type Approval Authority, which then notifies other parties via Annex 1 form.

## 11. Names and Addresses of Technical Services, etc.
Contracting Parties communicate details of Technical Services, test laboratories, and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat. Manufacturer laboratories may be designated as approved test laboratories.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval, extension, refusal, withdrawal, or discontinuation.
*   **Annex 2**: Arrangement of the approval mark.
*   **Annex 3**: Arrangement of tyre markings (with examples for different tyre categories and minimum marking heights).
*   **Annex 4**: List of load capacity indices (LI) and corresponding maximum masses (kg).
*   **Annex 5**: Tables of theoretical rim, outer diameter and nominal section width for specific tyre size designations, covering:
    *   Table 1: Agricultural steering wheels - Normal and low section sizes.
    *   Table 2: Drive wheel tyres for agricultural tractors - Normal section sizes.
    *   Table 3: Drive wheel tyres for agricultural tractors - Low section series (78-series).
    *   Table 4: Drive wheel tyres for agricultural tractors - Low section series (IF/VF series).
    *   Table 5: Agricultural implement tyres - Normal section sizes.
    *   Table 6: Agricultural implement and construction applications tyres - Low section sizes.
    *   Table 7: Agricultural high flotation tyres.
    *   Table 8: Log-Skidder Tyres for Forestry Machines – Code-Designated Sizes.
    *   Table 9: Tyres for construction applications (industrial tractors).
    *   Table 10: (Referenced in para. 2.18.12.1) Tyres for construction applications with "SS" or "NHS" marking.
*   **Annex 6**: Test method for measuring tyre dimensions.
*   **Annex 7**: Variation of load capacity with speed (tables).
*   **Annex 8**: Test procedure to assess tyre resistance to bursting.
*   **Annex 9**: Load/speed test procedure.
*   **Annex 10**: Tyre classification code (optional marking identifying category of use and tread pattern).
*   **Annex 11**: Example of the pictogram for maximum inflation pressure for bead seating.
*   **Appendix 1**: Explanatory figure of tyre cross-section.
