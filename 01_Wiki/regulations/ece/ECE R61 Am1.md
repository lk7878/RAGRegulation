**Amendment 1 to UNECE Regulation No. 61**

**Date of Entry into Force:** 10 October 2006

This document constitutes Amendment 1 to UNECE Regulation No. 61, which contains uniform provisions concerning the approval of commercial vehicles with regard to their external projections forward of the cab's rear panel.

**Specific Amendments:**

1.  **Paragraph 1.1.:** A reference to a new footnote 1/ is inserted. The new footnote 1/ defines vehicle categories N1, N2, and N3 as per Annex 7 to the Consolidated Resolution on the Construction of Vehicles (R.E.3), document TRANS/WP.29/78/Rev.1/Amend.2 as last amended by Amend.4.

2.  **Paragraph 2.5.:** A reference to a new footnote 2/ is inserted. The new footnote 2/ clarifies that the use of the option described in paragraph 2.5 does not modify the scope of the Regulation.

3.  **Paragraph 4.4.1., Footnote 1/:** Renumbered as footnote 3/ and amended to update the list of country codes. The updated list includes codes for Contracting Parties to the 1958 Agreement, such as:
    *   1 for Germany, 2 for France, 3 for Italy, 4 for the Netherlands, 5 for Sweden, 6 for Belgium, 7 for Hungary, 8 for the Czech Republic, 9 for Spain, 10 for Serbia and Montenegro, 11 for the United Kingdom, 12 for Austria, 13 for Luxembourg, 14 for Switzerland, 16 for Norway, 17 for Finland, 18 for Denmark, 19 for Romania, 20 for Poland, 21 for Portugal, 22 for the Russian Federation, 23 for Greece, 24 for Ireland, 25 for Croatia, 26 for Slovenia, 27 for Slovakia, 28 for Belarus, 29 for Estonia, 31 for Bosnia and Herzegovina, 32 for Latvia, 34 for Bulgaria, 36 for Lithuania, 37 for Turkey, 39 for Azerbaijan, 40 for The former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia, 42 for the European Community, 43 for Japan, 45 for Australia, 46 for Ukraine, 47 for South Africa, 48 for New Zealand, 49 for Cyprus, 50 for Malta, 51 for the Republic of Korea, 52 for Malaysia, and 53 for Thailand. Subsequent numbers are to be assigned to new Contracting Parties in the order of their accession to the Agreement.
