# UN Regulation No. 80 (Revision 3)

## 1. Scope
1.1. This Regulation applies to:
    (a) Passenger seats for forward-facing installation in vehicles of categories M2 and M3, of Classes II, III and B;
    (b) Vehicles of categories M2 and M3 of Classes II, III and B in respect of their passenger seat anchorages and seat installation.
    (c) It does not apply to rearward-facing seats or to any head restraint fitted to these seats.
1.2. At the request of the manufacturer, vehicles of category M2 approved to Regulation No. 17 shall be deemed to meet the requirements of this Regulation.
1.3. Vehicles where some seats benefit from the derogation provided in paragraph 7.4. to Regulation No. 14 shall be approved to this Regulation.
1.4. The installation of side-facing seats shall be prohibited in vehicles of categories M2 (of class II, III and B) and M3 (of class II, III and B) with the exception of vehicles of category M3 (of class II, III and B) of a technically permissible maximum laden mass exceeding 10 tonnes provided the requirements of paragraph 7.4. are met.
1.5. Paragraph 1.4. shall not apply to ambulances or to vehicles intended for use by the armed services, civil defence, fire services and forces responsible for maintaining public order.

## 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation, key definitions include:
- **Approval of a seat**: Approval of a seat type as a component in relation to the protection of the occupants of forward-facing seats with regard to their strength and the design of the seat backs.
- **Approval of a vehicle**: Approval of a vehicle type with regard to the strength of the parts of the vehicle structure to which seats are to be secured, and with regard to the installation of seats.
- **Seat type**: Seats which do not differ essentially with respect to characteristics likely to affect their strength and their aggressiveness (e.g., structure, shape, dimensions, materials, attachment fittings).
- **Vehicle type**: Vehicles which do not differ essentially in respect of constructional features relevant to this Regulation and the type(s) of type-approved seat(s) fitted.
- **Forward-facing seat**: A seat which can be used while the vehicle is in motion and which faces towards the front of the vehicle (vertical plane of symmetry of the seat forms an angle of less than ±10° with the vertical plane of symmetry of the vehicle).
- **Side-facing seat**: A seat which can be used whilst the vehicle is in motion and which faces towards the side of the vehicle (vertical plane of symmetry of the seat forms an angle of 90° (±10°) with the vertical plane of symmetry of the vehicle).
- **Individual seat, Double seat, Row of seats**: Definitions based on the number of seated passengers accommodated.
- **Anchorage**: A part of the floor or of the body of a vehicle to which a seat may be fixed.
- **Manikin**: A test dummy corresponding to specifications for HYBRID II or III for forward-facing seats, or to specifications for side impact dummy according to Regulation No. 95, Annex 6 for side-facing seats.
- **Reference zone**: The space between two vertical longitudinal planes, 400 mm apart and symmetrical with respect to the H-point, defined for impact assessment.

## 3. Application for Approval
3.1. The application for approval of a seat shall be submitted by the seat manufacturer or by his duly accredited representative.
3.2. The application for approval of the vehicle shall be submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or by his duly accredited representative.
3.3. The application shall be accompanied by detailed descriptions and drawings of the seat (including attachment fittings, adjustment, displacement and locking systems) or vehicle anchorages.
3.4. The following shall be submitted to the technical service:
    - Two seats representative of the type to be approved (for seat approval).
    - A part of the vehicle structure (for vehicle approval).

## 4. Approval
4.1. Approval of a seat type shall be granted if it meets the requirements of paragraph 5.
4.2. Approval of a vehicle type shall be granted if it meets the requirements of paragraphs 6 and 7.
4.3. An approval number shall be assigned. Its first two digits indicate the series of amendments incorporating the most recent major technical amendments at the time of issue.
4.4. Notice of approval, extension, or refusal shall be communicated to the Parties to the 1958 Agreement.
4.5. An international approval mark shall be affixed to every approved seat or vehicle, consisting of a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the country which granted approval, and the Regulation number followed by "R", a dash, and the approval number.
4.6. The approval mark shall be clearly legible and indelible.
4.7. The mark shall be placed on the seat(s) or on/near the vehicle's data plate.
4.8. Examples of approval mark arrangements are given in Annex 3.

## 5. Requirements for Seats
5.1. Each type of forward-facing seat shall be subject to the test requirements of either Appendix 1 (dynamic test) or Appendices 5 and 6 (static test) at the request of the manufacturer.
5.2. The tests passed shall be recorded in the approval communication form (Annex 1).
5.3. Every adjustment and displacement system shall incorporate an automatic locking system.
5.4. The adjustment and locking systems are not required to be in full working order after the test.
5.5. Static test in Appendix 5 is not permitted if the seat is attached to the vehicle structure by means of any clamping without mechanical fixation.
5.6. A head restraint complying with UN Regulation No. 25 (as amended by the 04 series) shall be mounted on every outboard front seat in every vehicle of category M2 with a maximum mass not exceeding 3,500 kg.

## 6. Requirements for Seat Anchorages of a Vehicle Type
6.1. The anchorages shall be capable of withstanding:
    - Either the test described in Appendix 2.
    - Or, if a seat is mounted on the part of the vehicle structure being tested, the tests prescribed in Appendix 1. The seat need not be an approved seat provided it satisfies specific requirements.
6.2. Permanent deformation, including breakage, is permissible provided the prescribed force has been sustained throughout the prescribed period.
6.3. When there is more than one type of anchorage on a vehicle, each variant shall be tested.
6.4. One test may be used to approve simultaneously a seat and a vehicle.
6.5. For vehicles of category M3, seat anchorages shall be deemed to comply if the safety-belt anchorages of the corresponding seating positions are fitted directly to the seats and comply with Regulation No. 14.

## 7. Requirements for Installation of Seats in a Vehicle Type
7.1. All forward-facing seats installed shall be approved to paragraph 5 and subject to conditions regarding reference height (≥1 m) and H-point height differentials.
7.2. When approved to Appendix 1 (dynamic test), Test 1 and 2 apply with specified exceptions (e.g., if no seat behind, or if seat behind has a compliant 3-point belt).
7.3. When approved to Appendices 5 and 6 (static test), all tests apply with similar exceptions.
7.4. The installation of side-facing seats is subject to conditions including a reference height ≥1 m, specific H-point alignment and spacing (450-725 mm), and safeguarding by a forward vehicle part (e.g., partition, wall) meeting the requirements of Appendix 7.

## 8. Conformity of Production
8.1. Seats and/or vehicles approved shall be so as set out in paragraphs 5, 6, and 7.
8.2. Suitable production controls shall be carried out to verify compliance, including checking dimensions and quality control procedures.
8.3. The competent authority may verify conformity control methods and carry out tests; normal frequency is once a year.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
9.1. Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met.
9.2. Withdrawal of approval shall be notified to other Contracting Parties using the prescribed communication forms.

## 10. Modification and Extension of Approval
10.1. Every modification shall be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further tests.
10.2. Confirmation or refusal of approval shall be communicated.
10.3. Extension of approval shall be assigned a series number and communicated.

## 11. Production Definitively Discontinued
The holder of the approval shall inform the authority if manufacture ceases; the authority shall inform other Parties.

## 12. Transitional Provisions
Detailed provisions specify the applicability and phasing out of approvals under different series of amendments (01, 02, 03, 04), including dates for granting new approvals, cessation of validity of old approvals, and acceptance of type-approvals.

## 13. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Parties shall communicate the names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## Appendices
- **Appendix 1**: Test procedures for seats (dynamic test), anchorages, and installation of side-facing seats. Includes requirements for occupant retention, injury criteria (HIC, ThAC, FAC, RDC, VC, PSPF, APF), and seat strength. Describes test setup, manikin positioning, and impact simulation.
- **Appendix 2**: Test procedure for anchorages of a vehicle (static test). Specifies test apparatus and procedure for applying a force.
- **Appendix 3**: Measurements to be made, specifying instrumentation standards (ISO 6487:1987) and channel frequency classes (CFC) for various measurements (trolley deceleration, head acceleration, thorax acceleration/deflection, femur force, abdomen force, pelvis force).
- **Appendix 4**: Determination of acceptability criteria for frontal impact (HIC, ThAC, FAC) and side impact (HIC, RDC, VC, APF, PSPF).
- **Appendix 5**: Static test requirements and procedure for seats. Includes requirements for occupant retention (displacement limits), injury prevention (radius of curvature), and seat strength. Describes test apparatus (cylindrical surfaces) and procedure for applying forces.
- **Appendix 6**: Energy absorption characteristics of the rear part of seat backs. Refers to test per Regulation No. 21, Annex 4.
- **Appendix 7**: Requirements for safeguarding passengers in side-facing seats. Specifies distance limits, vehicle part dimensions, and energy absorption requirements. Allows for an alternative dynamic test.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Communication form for approval of a seat type.
- **Annex 2**: Communication form for approval of a vehicle type.
- **Annex 3**: Examples of arrangements of approval marks for seats and vehicles.
- **Annex 4**: Procedure for determining the "H" point and the actual torso angle for seating positions in motor vehicles (references Consolidated Resolution R.E.3). Includes appendices on the 3-D H machine, reference system, and reference data.
