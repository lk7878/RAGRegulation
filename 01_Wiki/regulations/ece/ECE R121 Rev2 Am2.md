# UN Regulation No. 121 - Revision 2 - Amendment 2

**Supplement 2 to the 01 series of amendments**
**Date of entry into force:** 19 July 2018

**Note:** This document is meant purely as a documentation tool. The authentic and legally binding texts are: ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2017/116 and ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2017/116/Corr.1.

## Amendments

### Paragraph 5.5.1.3 to 5.5.1.5
Amended to read:
- **5.5.1.3.** The tell-tales for the brake system malfunction, headlamp driving beam, direction indicator and safety belts for front seating row shall not be shown in the same common space.
- **5.5.1.4.** If tell-tale for the brake system malfunction, headlamp driving beam, direction indicator or safety belts for front seating row is displayed in a common space it shall displace any other symbol in such common space if the underlying condition exists for its activation.
- **5.5.1.5.** With the exception of tell-tales for the brake system malfunction, headlamp driving beam, direction indicator or safety belt malfunction, the information may be cancellable automatically or by the driver. The tell-tale for safety belt reminder may be cancellable under the conditions laid down in UN Regulation No. 16.
