# UN Regulation No. 92 (Revision 1, Amendment 3)

**Replacement exhaust silencing systems for motorcycles**

## 1. Scope
This UN Regulation applies to non-original replacement exhaust silencing systems for vehicles of categories L1, L2, L3, L4 and L5.

## 2. Definitions
- **2.1. "Non-original replacement exhaust silencing system (NORESS) or components of this system"**: A system of a type different from that fitted to the vehicle on approval or extension of approval, intended for use only as a replacement.
- **2.2. "Non-original replacement exhaust silencing system component"**: One of the various components which together form the exhaust silencing system.
- **2.3. "Non-original replacement exhaust silencing systems of different types"**: Systems differing significantly in trade name/mark, material characteristics/shape/size, operating principles, or component combination.
- **2.4. "NORESS or component thereof"**: Any part of the exhaust silencing system intended for use on a vehicle other than the original type fitted during type approval per UN R9, R41, or R63.
- **2.5. "Approval of a NORESS or component(s) thereof"**: Approval of the whole or part of a silencing system adaptable to specified vehicle type(s) regarding sound level limitation.
- **2.6. "Vehicle type"**: Vehicles not differing in essential respects such as engine type, drive train, and exhaust silencing system arrangement.
- **2.7. "Rated engine speed (n_rated)"**: The engine speed at which the engine develops its rated maximum net power.

## 3. Application for approval
- **3.1.** Submitted by manufacturer or accredited representative.
- **3.2.** Requires documents in triplicate: description of intended vehicle type(s), description and drawings of the NORESS, specifications.
- **3.3.** Technical Service may request samples of NORESS and original system, and a representative test vehicle meeting specified sound level conditions.

## 4. Markings
- **4.1.** Each NORESS component (excluding pipes/fittings) must bear the manufacturer's trade name/mark and commercial designation.
- **4.2-4.3.** Markings must be legible, indelible, visible when fitted. The NORESS must be labelled with approved vehicle type(s).
- **4.4-4.5.** A component may carry multiple approval numbers. Packaging/label must include manufacturer details and list of intended vehicle models.
- **4.6.** Manufacturer must provide mounting instructions, handling instructions, and a component list.

## 5. Approval
- **5.1.** Granted if requirements of paragraph 6 are met.
- **5.2.** An approval number is assigned. Its first two digits indicate the series of amendments.
- **5.3.** Notice communicated via form in Annex 1.
- **5.4-5.5.** An international approval mark (circle with "E", country code, regulation number "92R", and approval number) must be affixed, legible and indelible.
- **5.6.** Example in Annex 2.

## 6. Specifications
- **6.1. General specifications**: Design and construction must ensure compliance under normal use, corrosion resistance, maintained ground clearance, safe temperatures, no sharp edges, adequate clearances, and tamper-resistance compatible with maintenance.
- **6.2. Specifications regarding sound levels**: Acoustic efficiency verified per UN R9, R41, or R63. Sound levels with NORESS must not exceed those measured with the original system.
- **6.3. Additional requirements**:
    - **6.3.1. Tampering protection**: Construction must not permit easy removal of silencing parts; removal must cause permanent damage.
    - **6.3.2. Multi-mode NORESS**: Must meet requirements in all operating modes. Reported sound levels are from the highest sound mode.
    - **6.3.3. Prohibition of defeat devices**: No intentional alteration/device solely for passing tests that is not operational during typical on-road use.
    - **6.3.4. Additional sound emission provisions (ASEP)**: Applies if designed for vehicles type-approved under UN R41 with ASEP. Specifies testing requirements, including back-to-back tests with original system, for NORESS with/without selectable modes/variable geometries. Requires manufacturer's statement of compliance (Annex 4) and, for adjustable systems, additional documentation for approval authority.
- **6.4. Measurement of vehicle performance**: Vehicle performance with NORESS must be comparable to original system (±5% max power and engine speed). Test methods: engine or vehicle dynamometer.
- **6.5. Additional provisions regarding fibrous materials**: Use permitted only if requirements in Annex 3 are met.
- **6.6. Evaluation of pollutant emissions**: Vehicle with NORESS must fulfil the pollution requirements of its type approval.

## 7. Modification and extension of approval
- **7.1.** Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further tests.
- **7.2-7.4.** Procedure for extending approval to other vehicle types. Extension communicated with a series number.

## 8. Conformity of production
Procedures per Appendix 2 of the 1958 Agreement. Approved NORESS must conform to the approved type. Holder ensures tests are carried out. Authority verifies control methods biennially. Conformity criteria linked to sound level limits in UN R9, R41, R63.

## 9. Penalties for non-conformity of production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements not met. Withdrawal communicated to other Contracting Parties via Annex 1 form.

## 10. Production definitively discontinued
Holder must inform approval authority, which notifies other Contracting Parties.

## 11. Names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties communicate these to the UN Secretariat.

## 12. Transitional provisions
- **12.1.** From entry into force of 02 series, no refusal of approvals under 02 series.
- **12.2.** 12 months after entry into force, only grant approvals meeting 02 series.
- **12.3.** 24 months after, only grant extensions meeting 02 series.
- **12.4.** Approvals under preceding series remain valid.

## Annexes
- **Annex 1**: Communication form (Parts A for R41 vehicles, B for R9/R63 vehicles).
- **Annex 2**: Example of approval marks.
- **Annex 3**: Requirements for fibrous absorbent materials (asbestos-free, retention methods, testing).
- **Annex 4**: Statement of Compliance with the Additional Sound Emission Provisions (ASEP).
