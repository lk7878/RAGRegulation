# UN Regulation No. 27 (Revision 2) - Advance Warning Triangles

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to certain advance warning devices intended to be on board vehicles and to be placed on the carriageway in order to signal, by day and at night, the presence of a halted vehicle.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Advance warning triangle:** The device in the form of an equilateral triangle.
    *   **Type 1:** Comprised of a separate retro-reflecting device and separate fluorescent material.
    *   **Type 2:** Comprised of a single fluorescent retro-reflecting material.
*   **Type of triangle:** Triangles which do not differ in essential respects such as trade name/mark, optical characteristics, and distinctive geometrical/mechanical design features.
*   **Retro-reflecting device:** An assembly comprising one or more retro-reflecting optical units.
*   **Fluorescent material:** A material which, when excited by daylight, exhibits photo-luminescence ceasing shortly after excitation.
*   **Coefficient of luminous intensity (CIL):** The luminous intensity reflected divided by the illumination of the retro-reflecting device for given angles.
*   **Fluorescent retro-reflecting material:** A material with retro-reflecting properties which also exhibits fluorescence when excited by daylight.

## 3. Application for Approval
The application must be submitted by the holder of the trade name/mark or representative, accompanied by dimensional drawings, technical description, assembly instructions, samples of the triangle and material, and other required documentation.

## 4. Markings
Every advance warning triangle and its protective cover must bear the indelible and legible trade name or mark of the applicant and provide adequate space for the approval mark.

## 5. Approval
*   Approval is granted if submitted samples meet the requirements.
*   An approval number is assigned. Its first two digits indicate the series of amendments incorporating the most recent major technical amendments at the time of approval (e.g., 04 for the 04 series).
*   An international approval mark must be affixed, consisting of a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the distinguishing number of the country granting approval, the Regulation number (27), the letter "R", and the approval number.

## 6. General Specifications
*   The triangle must be open at the centre and comprise a red border of an outer retro-reflecting strip and an inner fluorescent strip (Type 1), or use a fluorescent retro-reflecting material (Type 2).
*   It must retain prescribed characteristics and ensure satisfactory functioning in normal use.
*   Optical units and parts must not be easily disassembled and must provide good stability.
*   In use, the front face must be vertical (axis within 5° of base plane).
*   The front face must be easy to clean.
*   The triangle and support must not present sharp edges.
*   It must be accompanied by a protective cover (or equivalent means) and assembly instructions.

## 7. Particular Specifications
### 7.1. Shape and Dimensions
*   Theoretical side length: 500 ± 50 mm.
*   **Type 1:** Retro-reflecting strip width: 25-50 mm. Fluorescent surface area ≥ 315 cm².
*   **Type 2:** (Fluorescent retro-reflecting material) strip width: 50-85 mm.
*   Side of open centre: minimum 70 mm.
*   Distance from supporting surface to lower side of triangle: ≤ 300 mm.

### 7.2. Colorimetric Specification
*   **Retro-reflecting devices (night-time colour):** Must be red in mass. Trichromatic coordinates of reflected flux must be within specified limits (see Table 1 in OCR).
*   **Fluorescent materials (daytime colour):** Colour coordinates in new condition must be within specified area (see Table 2 in OCR).
*   **Luminance factor** (including reflection and fluorescence):
    *   Type 1: ≥ 30%
    *   Type 2: ≥ 25%

### 7.3. Photometric Specifications
*   Minimum **Coefficient of Luminous Intensity (CIL)** values are specified for various illumination angles (β1 vertical, β2 horizontal) and angles of divergence (20' and 1°30'). See table in OCR Page 9.
*   CIL uniformity and diversity of luminance requirements are specified to ensure the triangular shape remains discernible.

## 8. Test Procedure
Every advance warning triangle and its cover must meet the requirements of the checks and tests described in **Annex 5**. Key tests include:
*   Colour tests for retro-reflecting devices and fluorescent materials.
*   Determination of luminance factor.
*   Measurement of CIL.
*   Clearance to ground test.
*   Mechanical solidity test.
*   Heat and low-temperature resistance test.
*   Water test.
*   Resistance to fuels test.
*   Stability against wind test.
*   Resistance to penetration of water test.
*   Weather-resistance test of fluorescent materials.

## 9. Modifications and Extension of Approval
Modifications to an approved type must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may confirm approval or require further tests.

## 10. Conformity of Production
Approved triangles must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. Compliance is verified per paragraphs 6, 7, and 8. Minimum requirements for control procedures and sampling are set out in **Annexes 7 and 8**. The Type Approval Authority may verify control methods periodically.

## 11. Penalties for Non-conformity
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met. Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 12. Production Definitely Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority upon ceasing manufacture, who will notify other Parties.

## 13. Technical Services and Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate the names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN Secretariat.

## 14. Transitional Provisions
*   From the entry into force of the 04 series, no Party shall refuse to grant approvals under the 03 series.
*   36 months after the entry into force of the 04 series, Parties shall grant approvals only if the triangle meets the 04 series requirements.
*   Existing approvals granted before the 04 series remain valid indefinitely.
*   Extensions of approvals to preceding series shall not be refused.

## Annexes
The regulation includes detailed annexes covering:
*   **Annex 1:** Communication form for approval notifications.
*   **Annex 2:** Arrangements of the approval mark.
*   **Annex 3:** Shape and dimensions of the triangle and support.
*   **Annex 4:** Determination of road surface roughness ("sandy beach" method).
*   **Annex 5:** Detailed test procedures.
*   **Annex 6:** Method for measurement of the CIL.
*   **Annex 7:** Minimum requirements for conformity of production control.
*   **Annex 8:** Minimum requirements for sampling by an inspector.
*   **Annex 9:** Colour fastness to artificial light (Xenon-arc lamp test).
*   **Annex 10:** Measurement geometry for colour and luminance factor of fluorescent retro-reflective materials.
