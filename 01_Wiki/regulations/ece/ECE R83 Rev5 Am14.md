**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to the emission of pollutants according to engine fuel requirements**

This document constitutes **Addendum 82 – UN Regulation No. 83, Revision 5 - Amendment 14** (Supplement 14 to the 07 series of amendments). It was issued on **15 September 2022** and entered into force on **22 June 2022**. The authentic and legally binding text is referenced as **ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2021/133**.

This regulation falls under the framework of the **Agreement Concerning the Adoption of Harmonized Technical United Nations Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts...** (Revision 3, including amendments effective 14 September 2017).

**Key Amendments:**

The document specifies amendments to several annexes of UN Regulation No. 83:

*   **Annex 4a, Paragraph 5.1. (Test procedure for vehicle road load):**
    *   The primary procedure is described in Appendix 7a.
    *   **Alternative measures are permitted:**
 *   **(a)** If the road load was already determined per **UN GTR No. 15 (WLTP procedures)**, the methodology in Appendix 7b may be used.
 *   **(b)** If a road load was already determined per Appendix 7a, simulation of other vehicle configurations (with same body shape or transmission) may be used, subject to approval of the methodology by the Type Approval Authority.
    *   These procedures are not required if the chassis dynamometer load is set based on the vehicle's reference mass.

*   **Annex 6, Paragraph 5.2. (Crankcase pressure measurement):**
    *   Pressure must be measured at an appropriate location.
    *   It is recommended to measure at the dip-stick hole, if feasible.

*   **Annex 8, Paragraph 5.2.11. (Testing of four-wheel drive vehicles):**
    *   A four-wheel drive vehicle shall be tested in a **two-wheel drive mode**.
    *   The total road force for dynamometer setting is determined while operating the vehicle in its **primary designed driving mode**.
    *   **At the manufacturer's request**, a four-wheel drive vehicle shall be tested in its **primary drive mode of operation**.
