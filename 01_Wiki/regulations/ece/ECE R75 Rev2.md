**Regulation No. 75: Uniform provisions concerning the approval of pneumatic tyres for motor cycles and mopeds**

1. Scope
2. Definitions
3. Markings
4. Application for approval
5. Approval
6. Requirements
7. Modifications of the type of pneumatic tyre and extension of approval
8. Conformity of production
9. Penalties for non-conformity of production
10. Production definitely discontinued

**Annexes**
1. Communication concerning the approval or extension or refusal or withdrawal of approval or production definitely discontinued of a type of pneumatic tyre for motor cycles and mopeds pursuant to Regulation No. 75
2. Arrangement of the approval mark
3. Arrangement of tyre markings – Example of the markings to be borne by types of tyres placed on the market after the entry into force of this Regulation
4. Load capacity index/maximum mass correspondence

7. Procedure for load/speed performance tests
8. Tyre load capacities at various speeds
9. Test procedure for the dynamic growth of tyres

**1. Scope**
This Regulation applies to new pneumatic tyres for vehicles of category L1, L2, L3, L4 and L5. However, it does not apply to tyre types designed exclusively for the "off-road" use, which are marked "NHS" (Not for Highway Service) and to tyre types designed exclusively for competitions.

**2. Definitions**
Key definitions include:
*   **Type of pneumatic tyre:** A category of pneumatic tyres which do not differ in essential respects such as manufacturer, tyre size designation, category of use, structure, speed category, load capacity index, and tyre cross-section.
*   **Structures:** Diagonal (bias ply), bias belted, radial, and reinforced.
*   **Tyre-size designation:** Shows nominal section width (S1), nominal aspect ratio (except for certain types), and a conventional number "d" denoting the nominal rim diameter.
*   **Speed category:** Defined by symbols (e.g., B=50 km/h, S=180 km/h, V=240 km/h, W=270 km/h). Tyres suitable for speeds above 240 km/h are identified by "V" or "Z" in the size designation.
*   **Load capacity index:** A figure associated with the maximum load a tyre can carry at its rated speed.
*   **Maximum load rating:** The maximum mass the tyre is rated to carry, with specific derating rules for speeds above 210 km/h.

**3. Markings**
Tyres submitted for approval must bear markings including: trade name/mark, tyre size designation, structure indication (e.g., "R" for radial), speed category symbol, load-capacity index, "TUBELESS" if applicable, "REINFORCED" if applicable, date of manufacture (4-digit week/year), and specific inscriptions for snow tyres ("M+S"), multiservice tyres ("MST"), or moped tyres ("MOPED"). Tyres for speeds above 240 km/h have additional marking requirements. All markings and the approval mark must be moulded on and clearly legible.

**4. Application for approval**
The application, submitted by the trade name holder, must specify details such as tyre-size designation, trade name, category of use, structure, speed category, load-capacity index, intended use (with/without tube), reinforcement status, overall dimensions, applicable rims, test pressures, and for high-speed tyres, the maximum permitted speed and corresponding load capacity. It must be accompanied by sketches/photographs of the tread pattern and tyre envelope, and either a test report or samples.

**5. Approval**
If requirements are met, approval is granted with an assigned approval number. Notice is communicated to contracting parties via a form (Annex 1). An international approval mark (a circle containing "E" and the country code, plus "75R-{approval number}") must be affixed to conforming tyres.

**6. Requirements**
*   **Dimensions:** Specifies formulas and tolerances for section width and outer diameter, with reference values provided in Annex 5. Measurement procedures are in Annex 6.
*   **Load/Speed Performance Test:** Tyres must undergo a test per Annex 7. After the test, there must be no tread, ply, or cord separation, chunking, or broken cords. Outer diameter growth must be within ±3.5%, and overall width must not exceed specified limits.
*   **Dynamic Growth Test:** Applicable tyres that pass the load/speed test must also undergo a dynamic growth test per Annex 9 to check dimensional stability at high speed.

**7. Modifications and extension of approval**
Modifications must be notified. The authority may accept them or require further testing. Tread pattern changes do not necessitate retesting. Extensions for high-speed tyres for different max speeds/loads require a new test report.

**8. & 9. Conformity of production and penalties**
Production must conform to the approved type. Verification frequency is at least once every two years per facility. Approval can be withdrawn for non-conformity.

**10. Production definitely discontinued**
The approval holder must inform the authority if production ceases, who then notifies other parties.

**11. Names and addresses**
Parties must communicate details of their technical services and administrative departments to the UN Secretariat. They may use manufacturers' laboratories or laboratories in other contracting parties' territories.

**Annexes (Summarized)**
*   **Annex 1:** Model communication form for approval actions.
*   **Annex 2:** Example of the approval mark arrangement.
*   **Annex 3:** Example of tyre markings layout.
*   **Annex 4:** Table correlating Load Capacity Index (A) with Maximum Mass in kg (B).
*   **Annex 5:** Extensive tables of tyre size designations and corresponding dimensions (section width, overall width, overall diameter min/max) for various tyre types (motor cycles, mopeds, derivatives, American sizes).
*   **Annex 6:** Method for measuring pneumatic tyres (mounting, inflation, conditioning, measurement).
*   **Annex 7:** Detailed procedure for the load/speed endurance test (preparation, test drum, load application, speed steps, duration).
*   **Annex 8:** Table showing the percentage variation in load carrying capacity at various speeds for different speed symbols and rim diameter codes.
*   **Annex 9:** Test procedure for determining the dynamic growth of tyres under centrifugal force at maximum speed, including the definition of an enveloping curve.
