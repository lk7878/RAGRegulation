# UN Regulation No. 138 (Revision 1)
**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of Quiet Road Transport Vehicles with regard to their reduced audibility**

## 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to electrified vehicles of categories M and N1 which can be propelled in the normal mode, in reverse or at least one forward drive gear, without an internal combustion engine operating, in respect to their audibility.

## 2. Definitions
Key definitions include:
*   **Approval of a vehicle:** Approval of a vehicle type with regard to sound.
*   **Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System (AVAS):** A component or set of components installed in vehicles with the primary purpose to fulfil the requirements of this Regulation.
*   **Vehicle type:** A category of motor vehicles which does not differ essentially in specified respects affecting sound emission.
*   **Frequency Shift:** The variation of the frequency content of the AVAS sound as a function of the vehicle speed.
*   **Electrified vehicle:** A vehicle with a powertrain containing at least one electric motor or electric motor-generator. Sub-types include Pure Electric Vehicle (PEV), Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV), Fuel Cell Vehicle (FCV), and Fuel Cell Hybrid Vehicle (FCHV).
*   **Mass in running order:** The mass of the vehicle under specified conditions.
*   **Pause function:** A mechanism to enable the driver to halt the operation of an AVAS (prohibited by this Regulation).

## 3. Application for Approval
The application must be submitted by the manufacturer or accredited representative, accompanied by required documents describing the vehicle type, engine(s), and AVAS components (if applicable).

## 4. Markings
Components of the AVAS (if applicable) must bear the trade name/mark of the manufacturer and a designated identification number. Markings must be clear and indelible.

## 5. Approval
*   Approval is granted if the vehicle type meets the specifications.
*   An approval number is assigned.
*   An international approval mark must be affixed to every conforming vehicle.

## 6. Specifications
### 6.1. General Specifications
The vehicle must fulfil the acoustic requirements specified.

### 6.2. Acoustics Characteristics
The sound emitted is measured per **Annex 3**. The operational speed range is >0 km/h up to and including 20 km/h.

#### 6.2.1. Constant Speed Tests (10 km/h and 20 km/h)
The vehicle must emit a sound that:
*   Has a minimum overall sound pressure level (see Table 2).
*   Has at least two of the specified one-third octave bands with minimum levels (see Table 2). At least one band must be at or below 1600 Hz.

#### 6.2.2. Reversing Test
The vehicle must emit a sound with a minimum overall sound pressure level (see Table 2, column 5).

#### 6.2.3. Frequency Shift
At least one tone within the specified frequency range must vary proportionally with speed by an average of at least **0.8% per 1 km/h** in the speed range from 5 km/h to 20 km/h (forward direction).

#### 6.2.5. Driver Selectable Sounds
Permitted if each sound complies with the Regulation's provisions.

#### 6.2.6. Pause Function
**Prohibited.**

#### 6.2.7. Maximum Sound Level for AVAS
A vehicle equipped with an AVAS must not emit an overall sound level of more than **75 dB(A)** when driving forward (measured at 2 m).

#### 6.2.8. Minimum Sound Levels
The sound levels, rounded to the nearest integer, must meet or exceed the values in **Table 2**.

## 7. Modification and Extension of Approval
Modifications must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which may require further testing.

## 8. Conformity of Production
Vehicles must be manufactured to conform to the approved type. The Type Approval Authority verifies control methods.

## 9. Penalties for Non-Conformity of Production
Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met.

## 10. Production Definitively Discontinued
The approval holder must inform the Type Approval Authority.

## 11. Transitional Provisions
Specifies timelines for the application of the original version vs. the 01 series of amendments (e.g., original version approvals not accepted after 1 September 2021).

## 12. Names and Addresses of Technical Services and Authorities
Contracting Parties must communicate relevant details to the UN Secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Communication form and Technical Information Document for approval.
*   **Annex 2:** Examples of the arrangement of the approval mark.
*   **Annex 3:** **Methods and instruments for measuring the sound made by motor vehicles.** This is the core test procedure annex, detailing:
    *   Instrumentation requirements (sound level meters, calibrators).
    *   Test site specifications (outdoor per ISO 10844:2014 or indoor hemi-anechoic).
    *   Meteorological conditions and background noise correction criteria.
    *   Vehicle test conditions (state of charge, tyre pressure, operating mode).
    *   Detailed test procedures for:
 *   Constant speed tests (forward motion or simulated).
 *   Reversing tests (in motion or simulated).
 *   Frequency shift analysis (5 methods outlined).
    *   Data compilation and reporting requirements.
    *   Appendix with figures and flowcharts illustrating test setups and decision logic.
