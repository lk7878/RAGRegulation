**Amendment Content**

This document constitutes **Amendment 3** to **Revision 3** of **UN Regulation No. 50**.

**Key Amendments:**

1.  **Paragraph 2.2. ("...lamps of different types")** - Amended to clarify that lamps differing **only by trade name or mark** but produced by the **same manufacturer** are considered the **same type**.

2.  **New Paragraph 3.2.4.** - Introduces a simplified approval process for a lamp type that differs **only by trade name or mark** from an already approved type. Requires:
    *   A manufacturer's declaration of identity (except for trade name/mark).
    *   Two samples bearing the new trade name/mark or equivalent documentation.

3.  **New Paragraph 3.2.5.** - Specifies that for approval of lamps equipped with **non-replaceable filament lamp(s) or light source module(s)**, the documentation per the new **paragraph 6.6.** must be submitted.

4.  **Paragraph 4.3. (Marking)** - Amended to require that lamps with **non-replaceable light sources or light source module(s)** bear the marking of the rated voltage or voltage range.

5.  **Paragraph 4.4.3. (Marking)** - Amended similarly to paragraph 4.3.

6.  **Paragraph 6. (General specifications)** - Amended to state that the general and individual specifications (and referenced annexes) from **UN Regulations Nos. 53 or 74** (and their applicable amendments) apply to this Regulation, pertinent to the specific lamp and intended vehicle category.

7.  **New Paragraph 6.6.** - Requires that for **non-replaceable filament lamp(s) or light source module(s)**, the type approval application must include a report from the light source manufacturer, acceptable to the Type Approval Authority, demonstrating compliance with the requirements of **paragraph 4.11. of IEC 60809, Edition 3**.

8.  **New Paragraphs 10.2. to 10.2.2.2. (Conformity of Production)** - Introduce specific conformity of production checks for **non-replaceable filament lamp(s) or light source module(s)**:
    *   **10.2.1:** Requires a manufacturer's report demonstrating compliance with lifetime (and colour endurance for coated lamps) per **IEC 60809 Ed.3, para. 4.11**.
    *   **10.2.2:** At production checks, the approval holder must demonstrate the use of the specified light source.
    *   **10.2.2.2:** If doubt exists regarding lifetime/colour endurance compliance, conformity shall be checked as per **IEC 60809 Ed.3, para. 4.11**.
