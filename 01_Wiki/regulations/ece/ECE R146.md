# UN Regulation No. 146

**Uniform provisions concerning the approval of motor vehicles and their components with regard to the safety-related performance of hydrogen-fuelled vehicles of categories L1, L2, L3, L4 and L5**

*Date of entry into force as an annex to the 1958 Agreement: 02 January 2019*
*This document is meant purely as documentation tool. The authentic and legal binding text is: ECE/TRANS/WP.29/2018/68.*

## 1. Scope
This Regulation does not cover post-crash safety requirements of road vehicles and applies to:
1.1. **Part I** - Compressed hydrogen storage systems for hydrogen-fuelled vehicles of categories L1, L2, L3, L4 and L5 on their safety-related performance.
1.2. **Part II** - Specific components for compressed hydrogen storage systems for hydrogen-fuelled vehicles of categories L1, L2, L3, L4 and L5 on their safety-related performance.
1.3. **Part III** - Hydrogen-fuelled vehicles of categories L1, L2, L3, L4 and L5 incorporating compressed hydrogen storage system on its safety-related performance.

*Note: This Regulation does not cover the electrical safety of electric power train, the material compatibility and hydrogen embrittlement of the vehicle fuel system.*

## 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation, the following definitions shall apply:
*   **2.1. "Burst disc"**: The non-reclosing operating part of a pressure relief device which, when installed in the device, is designed to burst at a predetermined pressure to permit the discharge of compressed hydrogen.
*   **2.2. "Check valve"**: A non-return valve that prevents reverse flow in the vehicle fuel line.
*   **2.3. "Compressed hydrogen storage system (CHSS)"**: A system designed to store hydrogen fuel for a hydrogen-fuelled vehicle and composed of a pressurized container, pressure relief devices (PRDs) and shut off device(s) that isolate the stored hydrogen from the remainder of the fuel system and its environment.
*   **2.4. "Container" (for hydrogen storage)**: The component within the hydrogen storage system that stores the primary volume of hydrogen fuel.
*   **2.5. "Date of removal from service"**: The date (month and year) specified for removal from service.
*   **2.6. "Date of manufacture" (of a compressed hydrogen container)**: The date (month and year) of the proof pressure test carried out during manufacture.
*   **2.7. "Enclosed or semi-enclosed spaces"**: The special volumes within the vehicle (or the vehicle outline across openings) that are external to the hydrogen system (storage system, fuel cell system and fuel flow management system) and its housings (if any) where hydrogen may accumulate (and thereby pose a hazard), as it may occur in the passenger compartment, luggage compartment and space under the hood.
*   **2.8. "Exhaust point of discharge"**: The geometric centre of the area where fuel cell purged gas is discharged from the vehicle.
*   **2.9. "Fuel cell system"**: A system containing the fuel cell stack(s), air processing system, fuel flow control system, exhaust system, thermal management system and water management system.
*   **2.10. "Fuelling receptacle"**: The equipment to which a fuelling station nozzle attaches to the vehicle and through which fuel is transferred to the vehicle. The fuelling receptacle is used as an alternative to a fuelling port.
*   **2.11. "Hydrogen concentration"**: The percentage of the hydrogen moles (or molecules) within the mixture of hydrogen and air (equivalent to the partial volume of hydrogen gas).
*   **2.12. "Hydrogen-fuelled vehicle"**: Any motor vehicle that uses compressed gaseous hydrogen as a fuel to propel the vehicle, including fuel cell and internal combustion engine vehicles. Hydrogen fuel for passenger vehicles is specified in ISO 14687-2: 2012 and SAE J2719: (September 2011 Revision).
*   **2.13. "Luggage compartment"**: The space in the vehicle for luggage and/or goods accommodation, bounded by the roof, hood, floor, side walls, being separated from the passenger compartment by the front bulkhead or the rear bulkhead.
*   **2.14. "Manufacturer"**: The person or body responsible to the approval authority for all aspects of the type approval process and for ensuring conformity of production. It is not essential that the person or body is directly involved in all stages of the construction of the vehicle, system or component which is the subject of the approval process.
*   **2.15. "Maximum allowable working pressure (MAWP)"**: The highest gauge pressure to which a pressure container or storage system is permitted to operate under normal operating conditions.
*   **2.16. "Maximum fuelling pressure (MFP)"**: The maximum pressure applied to compressed system during fuelling. The maximum fuelling pressure is 125 per cent of the Nominal Working Pressure.
*   **2.17. "Nominal working pressure (NWP)"**: The gauge pressure that characterizes typical operation of a system. For compressed hydrogen gas containers, NWP is the settled pressure of compressed gas in fully fuelled container or storage system at a uniform temperature of 15 °C.
*   **2.18. "Pressure relief device (PRD)"**: A device that, when activated under specified performance conditions, is used to release hydrogen from a pressurized system and thereby prevent failure of the system.
*   **2.19. "Rupture" or "burst"**: Both mean to come apart suddenly and violently, break open or fly into pieces due to the force of internal pressure.
*   **2.20. "Safety relief valve"**: A pressure relief device that opens at a preset pressure level and can re-close.
*   **2.21. "Service life" (of a compressed hydrogen container)**: The time frame during which service (usage) is authorized.
*   **2.22. "Shut-off valve"**: A valve between the storage container and the vehicle fuel system that can be automatically activated; which defaults to the "closed" position when not connected to a power source.
*   **2.23. "Single failure"**: A failure caused by a single event, including any consequential failures resulting from this failure.
*   **2.24. "Thermally-activated pressure relief device (TPRD)"**: A non-reclosing PRD that is activated by temperature to open and release hydrogen gas.
*   **2.25. "Type of hydrogen storage system"**: An assembly of components which do not differ significantly in such essential aspects as: (a) The manufacturer's trade name or mark; (b) The state of stored hydrogen fuel; compressed gas; (c) The nominal working pressure (NWP); (d) The structure, materials, capacity and physical dimensions of the container; and (e) The structure, materials and essential characteristics of TPRD, check valve and shut-off valve, if any.
*   **2.26. "Type of specific components of hydrogen storage system"**: A component or an assembly of components which do not differ significantly in such essential aspects as: (a) The manufacturer's trade name or mark; (b) The state of stored hydrogen fuel; compressed gas; (c) The sort of component: (T) PRD, check-valve or shut-off valve; and (d) The structure, materials and essential characteristics.
*   **2.27. "Vehicle type" with regard to hydrogen safety**: Vehicles which do not differ in such essential aspects as: (a) The manufacturer's trade name or mark; and (b) The basic configuration and main characteristics of the vehicle fuel system.
*   **2.28. "Vehicle fuel system"**: An assembly of components used to store or supply hydrogen fuel to a fuel cell (FC) or internal combustion engine (ICE).

## 3. Application for approval
*   **3.1. Part I: Application for approval of a type of the compressed hydrogen storage system.** Submitted by the manufacturer or authorized representative. Model information document in Annex 1, Part 1-I. Sufficient number of representative systems submitted for testing.
*   **3.2. Part II: Application for approval of a type of specific component for compressed hydrogen storage system.** Submitted by the manufacturer or authorized representative. Model information document in Annex 1, Part 1-II. Sufficient number of representative components submitted for testing.
*   **3.3. Part III: Application for approval of a vehicle type.** Submitted by the vehicle manufacturer or authorized representative. Model information document in Annex 1, Part 1-III. Sufficient number of representative vehicles submitted for testing.

## 4. Approval
*   **4.1. Granting of type approval.** Granted if the submitted hydrogen storage system, specific component, or vehicle meets the requirements of Part I, II, or III respectively.
*   **4.2. Approval number.** Assigned to each approved type. First two digits indicate the series of amendments.
*   **4.3. Notice of approval.** Communicated to Contracting Parties using forms in Annex 1, Part 2.
*   **4.4. Approval mark.** Consists of a circle surrounding the letter "E" followed by the country distinguishing number, and the Regulation number followed by "R", a dash and the approval number.
*   **4.5. Multiple approvals.** If a vehicle conforms to multiple UN Regulations, additional symbols are placed in vertical columns.
*   **4.6. Marking requirements.** The mark must be clearly legible and indelible, placed on the vehicle data plate, container, or specific component as applicable.

## 5. Part I – Specifications of the compressed hydrogen storage system
Specifies requirements for the compressed hydrogen storage system (CHSS), consisting of a high-pressure storage container and primary closure devices (TPRD, check valve, automatic shut-off valve). A container shall be metal liner reinforced with a resin impregnated continuous filament (fully wrapped) and with a water capacity of 23 litres or less. All new systems shall have a NWP of 70 MPa or less and a service life of 15 years or less.

The hydrogen storage system shall meet the following performance test requirements (detailed test procedures in Annex 3):
*   **5.1. Verification tests for baseline metrics**
    *   5.1.1. Baseline initial burst pressure
    *   5.1.2. Baseline initial pressure cycle life
*   **5.2. Verification test for performance durability (Hydraulic sequential tests)**
    *   5.2.1. Proof pressure test
    *   5.2.2. Drop (impact) test
    *   5.2.3. Surface damage test
    *   5.2.4. Chemical exposure and ambient-temperature pressure cycling tests
    *   5.2.5. High temperature static pressure test
    *   5.2.6. Extreme temperature pressure cycling
    *   5.2.7. Residual proof pressure test
    *   5.2.8. Residual strength burst test
*   **5.3. Verification test for expected on-road performance (Pneumatic sequential tests)**
    *   5.3.1. Proof pressure test
    *   5.3.2. Ambient and extreme temperature gas pressure cycling test
    *   5.3.3. Extreme temperature static gas pressure leak/permeation test
    *   5.3.4. Residual proof pressure test (hydraulic)
    *   5.3.5. Residual strength burst test (hydraulic)
*   **5.4. Verification test for service terminating performance in fire**
*   **5.5. Requirements for primary closure devices** (TPRD, check valve, shut-off valve) must be type-approved per Part II.

**5.6. Labelling:** A permanent label on each container must include: manufacturer's name, serial number, date of manufacture, MFP, NWP, type of fuel (e.g., "CHG"), date of removal from service, and number of cycles used in testing. Date of removal from service shall not be more than 15 years after date of manufacture.

## 6. Part II – Specifications of specific components for the compressed hydrogen storage system
*   **6.1. TPRD requirements:** Must meet performance requirements for pressure cycling, accelerated life, temperature cycling, salt corrosion resistance, vehicle environment, stress corrosion cracking, drop and vibration, leak, bench top activation, and flow rate tests (detailed in Annex 4).
*   **6.2. Check valve and automatic shut-off valve requirements:** Must meet performance requirements for hydrostatic strength, leak, extreme temperature pressure cycling, salt corrosion resistance, vehicle environment, atmospheric exposure, electrical tests (for shut-off valves), vibration, stress corrosion cracking, and pre-cooled hydrogen exposure tests (detailed in Annex 4).
*   **6.3. Marking:** At least MFP and type of fuel (e.g., "CHG") must be marked on each component in a clearly legible and indelible manner.

## 7. Part III – Specifications of a vehicle fuel system incorporating the compressed hydrogen storage system
Specifies requirements for the vehicle fuel system, which includes the compressed hydrogen storage system (type-approved per Part I), piping, joints, and components.

**7.1. In-use fuel system requirements:**
*   **7.1.1. Fuelling receptacle:** Must prevent reverse flow, be labelled (fuel type, MFP, NWP, container removal date), be mounted for positive nozzle locking and protection, and not be mounted within external energy absorbing elements or in compartments where hydrogen could accumulate.
*   **7.1.2. Over-pressure protection for the low pressure system:** The system downstream of a pressure regulator must be protected against overpressure.
*   **7.1.3. Hydrogen discharge systems:**
    *   *7.1.3.1. Pressure relief systems:* Specifies requirements for vent line outlets and discharge directions for storage system TPRDs and other pressure relief devices to avoid directing gas into enclosed spaces, towards ignition sources, braking components, hydrogen containers, or passengers.
    *   *7.1.3.2. Vehicle exhaust system:* At the point of discharge, hydrogen concentration shall not exceed 4% average by volume during any moving 3-second interval, and not exceed 8% at any time.
*   **7.1.4. Protection against flammable conditions: single failure conditions:** Specifies requirements to prevent hydrogen leakage from accumulating in passenger/luggage compartments or enclosed spaces. Requires a warning at 3.0% concentration and automatic shut-off valve closure at 4.0% concentration in enclosed/semi-enclosed spaces.
*   **7.1.5. Fuel system leakage:** The hydrogen fuelling line downstream of the main shut-off valve(s) shall not leak at NWP.
*   **7.1.6. Tell-tale signal warning to driver:** Specifies requirements for a visual warning signal (yellow for system malfunction, red for hydrogen detection ≥3.0%) visible to the driver under all conditions.

**7.2. Installation of the hydrogen container on-board a vehicle:**
*   The vehicle fuel system must withstand specified accelerations (43.5g forward/rearward; 63g left/right).
*   **7.2.1. Container Displacement:** Containers must remain attached and within installed locations after acceleration tests.
*   **7.2.2. Additional installation requirements:** Containers and primary closure devices must be protected from direct contact with road surface during turnover, from other parts during collision, and from direct sunlight.

## 8. Modification of type and extension of approval
*   **8.1.** Any modification must be notified to the Type Approval Authority, which decides if a new approval is needed, or if it constitutes a "revision" or "extension".
*   **8.2.** Confirmation or refusal of an extension is communicated to Contracting Parties.
*   **8.3.** A serial number is assigned to each extension communication.

## 9. Conformity of production
*   **9.1.** Approved products must be manufactured to conform to the approved type.
*   **9.2.** The Type Approval Authority may verify conformity control methods, normally once every two years.
*   **9.3. Additional requirements for compressed hydrogen storage system production control:**
    *   9.3.1. Every container must undergo a proof pressure test ≥150% NWP.
    *   9.3.2. **Batch testing:** For each batch (max 200 cylinders or one shift), at least one container must undergo a rupture test and one an ambient temperature pressure cycling test. Specific sampling frequency and retesting procedures are defined, including relaxation provisions based on successful test history.

## 10. Penalties for non-conformity of production
Approval may be withdrawn if conformity of production requirements are not met. Withdrawal must be notified to other Contracting Parties.

## 11. Production definitively discontinued
If production of an approved type ceases completely, the approval holder must inform the authority, which notifies other Contracting Parties.

## 12. Names and addresses of Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities
Contracting Parties shall communicate the names and addresses of responsible Technical Services and Type Approval Authorities to the UN secretariat.

## Annexes
*   **Annex 1:** Information documents and communication forms for type approval.
*   **Annex 2:** Arrangements of the approval marks.
*   **Annex 3:** Test procedures for the compressed hydrogen storage system.
*   **Annex 4:** Test procedures for specific components (TPRD, check valve, automatic shut-off valve) for the compressed hydrogen storage system.
*   **Annex 5:** Test procedures for vehicle fuel system incorporating the compressed hydrogen storage system.
