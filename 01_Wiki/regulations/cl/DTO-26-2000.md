**Article 1. Scope of Application**
This decree applies to light motorized vehicles defined by Supreme Decree No. 211 and medium-sized motorized vehicles defined by Supreme Decree No. 54, subject to the security elements specified in the following article, in the manner and according to the obligation defined in Article 3.

**Article 2. Definitions of Security Elements**
For the purposes of this decree, the following security elements are defined:
1.  **Seat belt:** Element intended to prevent occupants from moving from their seats in the event of an impact.
2.  **Safety glass for windshields:** Glass that, upon impact, does not produce sharp edges and maintains reasonable transparency for driver visibility.
3.  **Rear window defroster:** Device intended to prevent fogging of the rear window.
4.  **Headrest:** Element located on the back of seats or constituting its upper projection, intended to limit the backward movement of the occupant's head.
5.  **Interior rear view mirror with day/night adjustment:** Mirror with two positions (day and night), the latter being anti-glare.
6.  **Seat anchor:** Components or system that fixes the seat to the vehicle structure.
7.  **Retractable steering column:** Column that, in case of impact, can retract to avoid injuring the driver.
8.  **Air Bag System:** Supplemental restraint system to seat belts. In the event of a collision or serious crash, it automatically inflates a flexible structure to limit the severity of contact between an occupant's body and the vehicle interior. Can be front, side body, head side, among others.
9.  **Anti-lock braking system (ABS):** System that, under certain braking situations, prevents wheel lock-up, facilitating driver control.
10. **Occupant protection system:** Set of structural bodywork elements that provide protection to occupants in the event of a collision, shock, or rollover.
11. **Seat belt pretensioner:** System that, in a collision, improves the effectiveness of the seat belt to protect the occupant.
12. **Seat Belt Tension Limiter:** Seat belt component or feature that controls belt tension to regulate forces applied to occupants.
13. **Folding rearview mirror:** Mirror that, in case of a blow, yields in the opposite direction to the vehicle's movement.
14. **Seat Belt Use Reminder System:** System that notifies the driver when an occupant is not using the seat belt.
15. **Anchorages for child safety systems or seats:** Parts of the vehicle structure, seat, or other vehicle part to which child safety systems or seats must be attached.
16. **Electronic stability program (ESP):** Electronic control function that improves a vehicle's dynamic stability.
17. **Advanced Emergency Braking System (AEB):** System capable of automatically detecting an imminent frontal collision and activating the braking system to slow the vehicle, to avoid or mitigate the collision.
18. **Blind Spot Detector (BSD):** System that warns drivers, via audible or visual alert, of vehicles in adjacent lanes that may not be seen.
19. **Intelligent Speed Assistant (ISA):** System that helps the driver maintain the ideal speed for the road environment by providing specific and appropriate information.
20. **Lane Keeping Assistant (LKA):** System that helps the driver maintain a safe vehicle position relative to lane or road limits, at least when the vehicle leaves or is about to leave the lane and there is an imminent collision risk.

**Article 3. Mandatory Security Elements**
All vehicles referred to in Article 1 must be equipped with the elements indicated in numbers 1) to 10), and 13) to 16) of Article 2.
*   For elements 3) (rear window defroster) and 5) (day/night mirror), the requirement applies only to vehicles with a rear window.
*   For element 8) (Air Bag System), the requirement applies regarding frontal airbags.
*   For element 14) (Seat Belt Use Reminder System), the requirement applies to all seats in light vehicles. For medium-sized vehicles, it applies only to driver and passenger seats.
*   These obligations are enforceable unless vehicles are built or equipped with other equivalent elements that meet the objectives of Article 2.
*   Vehicles intended for passenger transport with more than 9 seats (including driver) and vehicles complying with regulations indicated in Article 6 are exempt from the obligation related to number 10) (Occupant protection system).
*   Vehicles intended for passenger transport with more than 9 seats (including driver) and cargo vehicles with only one row of seats are excluded from the obligation contained in number 15) (Anchorages for child safety systems).

**Modalities for Mandatory Elements:**
a)  **Seat belt (1):** Mandatory for front and rear seats. Must meet characteristics of Decree No. 30 of 1985. For seats adjacent to doors, must be three-point. For intermediate rear seats, seats adjacent to sliding doors, facing backwards, folding seats and equivalents, belts may be two-point. Same for rear seats of half-cab pickup trucks and convertibles.
b)  **Headrest (4):** Mandatory in all seats where a three-point seat belt must be worn, according to letter a) above. Must meet conditions specified.
c)  Compliance with security elements in Article 2 will consider the scope of application of the rules referred to in Article 6.

**Article 4. Interior Materials**
The interior of vehicle compartments referred to in Article 1 must be made of materials that hinder flame propagation in case of fire.

**Article 5. Brake Systems**
The Ministry will establish special safety conditions that the brake systems of vehicles referred to in Article 1 must meet.

**Article 6. Applicable Standards**
The standards that must be met by the elements mentioned in Article 2, or their equivalents, are those established by:
*   The Federal Code of Regulations of the United States of America, or
*   The Security Directives of the European Economic Community, or
*   The Security Regulations defined by the European Economic Commission of the United Nations, Brazil, Japan, Korea, or
*   The National Standard of the People's Republic of China (GB or GB/T),
as determined by the Ministry.

**Article 7. Ministry Resolution**
The Ministry will, within sixty days after the publication of this decree, issue a resolution indicating the specific rules for compliance referred to in Article 6, regarding elements 1) to 7) of Article 2.

**Article 8. Optional Security Elements**
Optional for all vehicles referred to in Article 1 are the security elements indicated in:
*   Number 8) regarding side body and head side airbags.
*   Numbers 11), 12).
*   Number 14) regarding the seat belt use reminder system for the rear seats of medium-sized vehicles.
*   Numbers 17) to 20) of Article 2.
If light motorized vehicles (as defined) come equipped with one or more optional elements and are intended to be marketed or advertised with that characteristic, manufacturers, shipowners, importers or their representatives must be able to prove to interested parties that they comply with Article 6 rules, having a certificate available for the Ministry. They must also place a sign on the vehicles listing all optional elements, indicating which ones the vehicle has and lacks, as determined by the Ministry.

**Article 9. Entry into Force**
This decree will be in force ten months after the publication of the resolution referred to in Article 7, for all vehicles indicated in this decree that request their first registration in the National Registry of Motorized Vehicles after its entry into force.

**Transitory Articles**
*   **Transitory Article 1:** Three months after publication, vehicle models conforming to its regulations may be submitted for approval.
*   **Transitory Article 2:** The optional nature of the air bag (element 8) for light passenger vehicles applies only until dates referred to in the supreme decree regulating mandatory air bag use.
