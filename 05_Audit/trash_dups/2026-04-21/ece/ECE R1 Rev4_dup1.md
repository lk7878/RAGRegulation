**Regulation No. 2**
UNIFORM PROVISIONS CONCERNING THE APPROVAL OF INCANDESCENT ELECTRIC LAMPS FOR HEADLAMPS EMITTING AN ASYMMETRICAL PASSING BEAM OR A DRIVING BEAM OR BOTH
(Transitional provisions)

## REGULATION NO. 1

### 1. SCOPE
This Regulation applies to motor vehicle headlamps which may incorporate lenses of glass or plastic material.

### 2. DEFINITIONS
2.1. "Lens" means the outermost component of the headlamp (unit) which transmits light through the illuminating surface.
2.2. "Coating" means any product or products applied in one or more layers to the outer face of a lens.
2.3. Headlamps of different "types" are headlamps which differ in such essential respects as:
    2.3.1. The trade name or mark;
    2.3.2. The characteristics of the optical system;
    2.3.3. The inclusion of additional components capable of altering the optical effects by reflection, refraction or absorption; and/or deformation during operation;
    2.3.4. Suitability for right-hand or left-hand traffic or for both traffic systems;
    2.3.5. Ability to provide a passing beam or a driving beam or both;
    2.3.6. The materials constituting the lenses and coating, if any.

### 3. APPLICATION FOR APPROVAL
3.1. The application for approval shall be submitted by the owner of the trade name or mark or by his duly accredited representative. It shall specify:
    * Whether the headlamp is intended to provide both a passing beam and a driving beam or only one of these beams;
    * Whether, if the headlamp is intended to provide a passing beam, it is designed for both left-hand and right-hand traffic or for either left-hand or right-hand traffic only.
3.2. The application shall be accompanied, in respect of each type of headlamp, by:
    3.2.1. Drawings in triplicate in sufficient detail to permit identification of the type.
    3.2.2. A brief technical specification.
    3.2.3. Two samples of the type of headlamp.
    3.2.4. For the test of plastic material of which the lenses are made:
        * Thirteen lenses (six of which may be replaced by material samples).
        * A reflector to which the lenses can be fitted.
3.3. The materials making up the lenses and coatings, if any, shall be accompanied by the test report of their characteristics if already tested.
3.4. The competent authority shall verify effective control of conformity of production before type approval is granted.

### 4. MARKINGS
4.1. Headlamps submitted for approval shall bear the trade name or mark of the applicant.
4.2. They shall comprise, on the lens and on the main body, spaces of sufficient size for the approval mark and additional symbols.
4.3. For headlamps designed for both right-hand and left-hand traffic, the two settings shall be marked by the capital letters R and D, and L and G, respectively.

### 5. APPROVAL
5.1. **General**
    5.1.1. Approval shall be granted if all samples satisfy the provisions.
    5.1.2. A single international approval mark may be used for grouped, combined or reciprocally incorporated lamps satisfying multiple Regulations.
    5.1.3. An approval number shall be assigned to each type approved.
    5.1.4. Notice of approval actions shall be communicated to Parties applying the Regulation via a form per Annex 1.
    5.1.5. An approval mark shall be affixed to every conforming headlamp.
5.2. **Composition of the approval mark**
    The approval mark consists of:
    * An international approval mark (circle with "E" and country distinguishing number, followed by the approval number).
    * Additional symbol(s):
        * For left-hand traffic only: a horizontal arrow pointing right.
        * For both traffic systems: a horizontal double-headed arrow.
        * For passing beam only: letter "C".
        * For driving beam only: letter "R".
        * For both beams: letters "CR".
        * For plastic lens: letters "PL" near the beam symbols.
        * Specific operating mode and permitted voltage markings as required.
5.3. **Arrangement of the approval mark**
    Examples are given in Annex 5. Specific rules apply for independent lamps, and grouped/combined/reciprocally incorporated lamps.

### 6. GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS
6.1. Each sample shall conform to photometric specifications.
6.2. Headlamps shall retain prescribed characteristics under normal use and vibration.
6.3. Headlamps shall have an adjustment device, with exceptions for certain assemblies.
6.4. Filament lamp fixing components shall ensure correct positioning.
6.5. Headlamps for both traffic systems shall have two precise, differentiated setting positions.
6.6. Complementary tests per Annex 4 to ensure stability of photometric performance in use.
6.7. Conformity verified visually and by test fitting.
6.8. Plastic lens headlamps tested per Annex 7.

### 7. ILLUMINATION
7.1. Headlamps shall provide adequate illumination without dazzle (passing beam) and good illumination (driving beam). Checked on a vertical screen at 25 m with a standard 12V filament lamp (40W/450 lm passing, 45W/700 lm driving).
7.2. **Passing Beam Requirements:**
    * Must produce a sharp "cut-off".
    * Adjustment: Horizontal part of cut-off is 25 cm below the horizontal plane through the focus on the screen.
    * Illumination values at specified points/zones (I, II, III, IV) must meet the table in para. 6.3 (different for right-hand and left-hand traffic). Values also specified for zones "A" and "B".
7.3. **Driving Beam Requirements:**
    * Point HV within isolux 90% of maximum illumination (Emax).
    * Emax ≥ 32 lux.
    * Horizontal illumination requirements specified.
7.4. Measurements taken with a photo-electric cell (useful area within a 65 mm square).

### 8. GAUGING OF DISCOMFORT
The discomfort caused by the passing beam shall be gauged (subject of a recommendation).

### 9. STANDARD HEADLAMP
A headlamp is deemed standard if it:
9.1. Satisfies approval requirements.
9.2. Has an effective diameter ≥ 160 mm.
9.3. Provides illumination within 90% of max limits and 120% of min limits prescribed for the passing beam.

### 10. CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
Every headlamp bearing an approval mark must conform to the approved type and meet photometric conditions. Verification per Annex 3 and Annex 4 (para. 3), and Annex 7 (para. 3) if applicable.

### 11. PENALTIES FOR NON-CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
11.1. Approval may be withdrawn if requirements are not met or products are non-conforming.
11.2. Withdrawal shall be notified to other Contracting Parties via Annex 1 form.

### 12. MODIFICATION AND EXTENSION OF APPROVAL OF A TYPE OF HEADLAMP
12.1. Modifications must be notified. Authority may require further tests.
12.2. Confirmation/refusal communicated per para. 5.1.4.
12.3. Extension number assigned and communicated.
12.4. Approvals granted before 18 March 1986 remain valid.

### 13. PRODUCTION DEFINITELY DISCONTINUED
If manufacture ceases, the approval holder shall inform the granting authority, which notifies other Parties via Annex 1 form.

### 14. NAMES AND ADDRESSES OF TECHNICAL SERVICES AND ADMINISTRATIVE DEPARTMENTS
Parties shall communicate the names and addresses to the UN Secretariat.
