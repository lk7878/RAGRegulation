- **Annex 7, Appendix 4 (Test Procedure Excerpt):**
    - Fix the plate and fold back the free end of the tape at 90°. Apply force in such a manner that the separation line between the tape and the plate is perpendicular to this force and perpendicular to the plate.
    - Pull to unstick at a speed of 300 mm/s ± 30 mm/s and record the force required.
    - **Results:** The five values obtained shall be arranged in order and the median value taken as the result of the measurement. This value shall be expressed in Newtons per centimetre of width of the tape.

- **Regulation No. 2:**
    - **Transitional Provisions:**
        1.1. No new type approval shall be granted under this Regulation after the date of entry into force of the 03 series of amendments (9 March 1986).
        1.2. Type approvals granted before 9 March 1986 shall remain valid.
        1.3. However, the contracting parties applying this Regulation may, from the date of entry into force of the 03 series of amendments, prohibit the fitting of category R2 filament lamps approved under this Regulation if they do not meet the requirements of Regulation No. 37.
